h2 Introduction
p The aim of this guide is to provide a basic overview of how to get consistent kills at Amascut, the Devourer. Due to the boss' high defence levels, demanding DPS checks and mechanical complexity, contents of this guide assume you have at least made it to the end of Stage 1 of Upgrade guides for your chosen style. As Amascut is enrage-based with fixed enrages of 100, 500, 750, 1000, 2000 and 4000%, mechanics and strategies may differ based on enrage. For the purposes of this guide, 100% enrage will be utilized.
h3 Disclaimer
p This guide, and its contents thereof, assume you are getting started with Amascut, and does not necessarily cover meta strategies.

h3 Attack Rotation
p ⬥ This rotation assumes Amascut is not phased by reaching her hitcap
p ⬥ 3 auto attacks → Tri-colour Attack → Rapid swipes + 2 auto attacks → Summon Scarabs → 1 auto attack + Rapid swipes + 1 auto attack → Soul Crush → 2 auto attacks + Rapid swipes → Triple Strike → 3 auto attacks → Tri-colour Attack → Rapid swipes + 2 auto attacks → Hard phase (Amascut enters P2 or P4 depending on which phase the players were on)
