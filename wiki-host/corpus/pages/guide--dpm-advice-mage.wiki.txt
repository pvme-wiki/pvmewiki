<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="core-magic-rotation"><nowiki>&gt; </nowiki><ins><nowiki>Core Magic Rotation</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]</h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This section covers rotations using core style abilities before later unlocks are obtained</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>See </nowiki><ins><strong><nowiki>Advanced Magic Rotations</nowiki></strong></ins><nowiki> section further down for info on magic rotations using best-in-slot gear</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>A typical Magic rotation uses the strongest Basic Abilities available to build adrenaline, and spends it on the strongest available Enhanced Abilities, Ultimate Abilities, or Special Attacks</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• While doing this, we must think about the following </nowiki><strong><nowiki>key considerations:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Basic ability patterns </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063111104757760.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Buff usage </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Spell effects </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090783567902.png?v=1 <nowiki>incitefear</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="basic-pattern"><ins><nowiki>Basic Pattern</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>If Greater Concentrated Blast </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869285393223254107.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]<nowiki> is not unlocked, use the regular version </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1087373968731811880.png?v=1 <nowiki>conc</nowiki>]<nowiki> instead</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Greater Concentrated Blast </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869285393223254107.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]<nowiki> should form the foundation of your rotation</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Try to follow </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063111104757760.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]<nowiki> with strong abilities that can critically strike </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833391702017.png?v=1 <nowiki>dbreath</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809978966037.png?v=1 <nowiki>wm</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809664262179.png?v=1 <nowiki>omni</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>The basic pattern uses </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869285393223254107.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]<nowiki> often in blocks of 3 abilities</nowiki></strong><nowiki>:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>[</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869285393223254107.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]<nowiki> → Ability → Ability </nowiki><strong><nowiki>]</nowiki></strong><nowiki> → Repeat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When building adrenaline, fill the empty slots with strong Basic Abilities:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Dragon Breath </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833391702017.png?v=1 <nowiki>dbreath</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Sonic Wave </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063115709972651.png?v=1 <nowiki>sonic</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Combust </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063107908702482.png?v=1 <nowiki>combust</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Then spend adrenaline on Enhanced Abilites, Ultimate Abilities, and Special Attacks:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Such abilities are often categorically referred to as </nowiki><strong><nowiki>spenders</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Omnipower </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809664262179.png?v=1 <nowiki>omni</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Wild Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809978966037.png?v=1 <nowiki>wm</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Asphyxiate </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Lasts long enough to fill both empty slots between </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063111104757760.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]<nowiki> uses</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This pattern helps ensure that your cooldowns are always cycling to allow for a consistent flow of strong abilities</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="sunshine"><ins><nowiki>Sunshine</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>It is assumed that either the Planted Feet perk </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921595489488927.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshinepf</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Greater Sunshine unlock has been obtained </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> for the purposes of this guide</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Sunshine </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> is the main buff to prioritise whenever there is an opportunity to deal damage</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The exception to this is when the fight would end too quickly, or mechanics occur, and you can't benefit from the full duration</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Ideally, enter boss fights and important phases with 100% adrenaline so you can activate it immediately</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Sunshine </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki>, continue following the basic pattern while fitting in your most powerful spenders:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎● Omnipower </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809664262179.png?v=1 <nowiki>omni</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎● Wild Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809978966037.png?v=1 <nowiki>wm</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎● Asphyxiate </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Keep in mind that Damage-over-Time (DoT) abilities such as Combust </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063107908702482.png?v=1 <nowiki>combust</nowiki>]<nowiki> are not affected by </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> and most other damage buffs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ While waiting for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> cooldown, you do not want to needlessly camp 100% adrenaline, and can start building when </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> is nearly ready to use again</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="other-buffs"><ins><nowiki>Other Buffs</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Besides Sunshine, Magic has multiple buffing abilities that are meant to supplement and enhance its core rotation</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Tsunami</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809995874304.png?v=1 <nowiki>tsunami</nowiki>]<nowiki> grants adrenaline per critical strike and is most often used alongside </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Synergises with abilities that have built-in critical strike chance boosts </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063111104757760.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809978966037.png?v=1 <nowiki>wm</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/536257336130404372.png?v=1 <nowiki>smoketendrils</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Sonic Wave</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063117211668584.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsonic</nowiki>]<nowiki> is typically used to save adrenaline while setting up </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809995874304.png?v=1 <nowiki>tsunami</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• It can also be used on spenders </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809978966037.png?v=1 <nowiki>wm</nowiki>]<nowiki> to help maintain high adrenaline while waiting on </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> cooldown</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Runic Charge</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063122345361562.png?v=1 <nowiki>runic_charge</nowiki>]<nowiki> can flexibly enhance different basic abilities but is most commonly used for:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• (1) </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063122345361562.png?v=1 <nowiki>runic_charge</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063117211668584.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsonic</nowiki>]<nowiki> on either </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809995874304.png?v=1 <nowiki>tsunami</nowiki>]<nowiki> to allow for more immediate adrenaline during the buff window</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• (2) </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063122345361562.png?v=1 <nowiki>runic_charge</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063111104757760.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]<nowiki> on either </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809664262179.png?v=1 <nowiki>omni</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809978966037.png?v=1 <nowiki>wm</nowiki>]<nowiki> for consistent critical strikes on your highest damaging abilities</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Asphyxiate</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]<nowiki> provides a critical strike damage boost after its full channel, often stacked with other buffs to maximise burst damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="spell-effects"><ins><nowiki>Spell Effects</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Magic spells can be clicked in the spellbook or right-clicked on an ability bar to be set to autocast, which 'selects' them to use with your abilities, like picking your ammo for a ranged weapon</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>When using abilities, stronger autocast spells can increase your ability damage and provide additional effects</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Autocast either Exsanguinate </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090745950258.png?v=1 <nowiki>exsanguinate</nowiki>]<nowiki> or alternatively Incite Fear </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090783567902.png?v=1 <nowiki>incitefear</nowiki>]<nowiki> if you plan to use Tsunami </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809995874304.png?v=1 <nowiki>tsunami</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Otherwise, use the highest rank of blood </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537338981747261446.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloodbarrage</nowiki>]<nowiki> / air </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/543465115870035999.png?v=1 <nowiki>airsurge</nowiki>]<nowiki> spell available or Crumble Undead </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1351696446646390824.png?v=1 <nowiki>crumbleundead</nowiki>]<nowiki> if applicable</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Any damaging spell can be dragged onto a hotbar slot to act as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063104146411713.png?v=1 <nowiki>magicbasic</nowiki>]<nowiki> although you will gain the secondary effect of that spell when used rather than the secondary effect of your autocast</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• It is recommended to do so to maintain a consistent hit timing for your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063104146411713.png?v=1 <nowiki>magicbasic</nowiki>]<nowiki>, as certain spells such as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090783567902.png?v=1 <nowiki>incitefear</nowiki>]<nowiki> hit slower than others</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This guide will use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063104146411713.png?v=1 <nowiki>magicbasic</nowiki>]<nowiki> to denote basic attacks, although typically </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537338981747261446.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloodbarrage</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/914077639784935424.png?v=1 <nowiki>emeraldaurora</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/574292444791963659.png?v=1 <nowiki>rubyaurora</nowiki>]<nowiki> are chosen for faster hit timing and useful effects</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Smoke Cloud </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090641879050.png?v=1 <nowiki>smokecloud</nowiki>]<nowiki> should be prioritised on bosses that last over a minute, especially once further critical strike boosts are unlocked</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="area-of-effect-aoe"><ins><nowiki>Area-of-Effect (AoE)</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Chain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063105182269460.png?v=1 <nowiki>chain</nowiki>]<nowiki> / Greater Chain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257440278512537740.png?v=1 <nowiki>gchain</nowiki>]<nowiki> is the default option for dealing with AoE scenarios as magic</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Longer range AoE targeting than other options while being flexible in terms of adrenaline management</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use a powerful ability on the main target like </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257440278512537740.png?v=1 <nowiki>gchain</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809664262179.png?v=1 <nowiki>omni</nowiki>]<nowiki> or high hitting special attacks such as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1266417538045317121.png?v=1 <nowiki>roarofawakening</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ If the main target dies before the followup ability is cast, the effect will be lost</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Can be used to briefly stun multiple targets with stun abilities such as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809655873556.png?v=1 <nowiki>impact</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Magma Tempest </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/902209626509025290.png?v=1 <nowiki>magmatempest</nowiki>]<nowiki> deals high damage when targets are expected to survive for a longer period of time</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Targeted variant </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/924741973858996284.png?v=1 <nowiki>magmatempesttarget</nowiki>]<nowiki> is good for setting up damage before enemies have spawned</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Is not classified as DoT damage, allowing it to benefit from Sunshine </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> and other damage buffs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Can mostly finish ticking if used between </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063106914779166.png?v=1 <nowiki>gchain</nowiki>]<nowiki> cooldown</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="runic-charge-and-adrenaline-renewal"><ins><nowiki>Runic Charge and Adrenaline Renewal</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063122345361562.png?v=1 <nowiki>runic_charge</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/736298121704767538.png?v=1 <nowiki>adrenrenewal</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>In previous example rotations we have been using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063122345361562.png?v=1 <nowiki>runic_charge</nowiki>]<nowiki> on </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063117211668584.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsonic</nowiki>]<nowiki> to guarantee more adrenaline after </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809995874304.png?v=1 <nowiki>tsunami</nowiki>]</strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This sacrifices a use on </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063111104757760.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]<nowiki> that we're unable to use on </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809664262179.png?v=1 <nowiki>omni</nowiki>]<nowiki> during the first </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]<nowiki> buff</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>In reality, there are multiple considerations that allow us to optimise our </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063122345361562.png?v=1 <nowiki>runic_charge</nowiki>]<nowiki> uses</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Both </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063122345361562.png?v=1 <nowiki>runic_charge</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833391702017.png?v=1 <nowiki>dbreath</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be consistently skipped in the opener when </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/736298121704767538.png?v=1 <nowiki>adrenrenewal</nowiki>]<nowiki> is available</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Preparing </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063122345361562.png?v=1 <nowiki>runic_charge</nowiki>]<nowiki> ~10s before </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> allows it to be used on </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063117211668584.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsonic</nowiki>]<nowiki> as usual while having it come up back up soon after the first </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In general, plan to use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063122345361562.png?v=1 <nowiki>runic_charge</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki><strong><nowiki>as early as possible</nowiki></strong><nowiki> as long as it won't be consumed on the wrong ability</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This allows you to potentially find spots to squeeze in additional uses</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="temporal-anomaly"><ins><nowiki>Temporal Anomaly</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1357545000577011782.png?v=1 <nowiki>temporalanomaly</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Due to its random nature, Temporal Anomaly (TA) </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1357545000577011782.png?v=1 <nowiki>temporalanomaly</nowiki>]<nowiki> is typically not written into rotations. However, using TA resets correctly can still result in improved kill times on average, even if the base rotation you work with is relatively static.</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The value of most resets primarily comes down to the cooldown of the ability were you to use it again</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For example, a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809995874304.png?v=1 <nowiki>tsunami</nowiki>]<nowiki> reset is not particularly useful because it needs to be back up by the next </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> (which cannot reset off TA), and casting it again runs the risk of de-syncing your major cooldowns</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>TA reset considerations</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809664262179.png?v=1 <nowiki>omni</nowiki>]<nowiki> Best reset, allows additional casts during </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869284271595069451.png?v=1 <nowiki>fsoa</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> that isn't otherwise possible</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]<nowiki> With 4-piece </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1400500746377625790.png?v=1 <nowiki>tumekenrobetop</nowiki>]<nowiki>, can do the second cast earlier to get extra abilities under the buff near the end of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809978966037.png?v=1 <nowiki>wm</nowiki>]<nowiki> Deals good damage, can replace the next </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063111104757760.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]<nowiki> or filler ability if a reset occurs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/536257336130404372.png?v=1 <nowiki>smoketendrils</nowiki>]<nowiki> Also allows additional casts under </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869284271595069451.png?v=1 <nowiki>fsoa</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> that isn't otherwise possible, though generally un-needed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257440278512537740.png?v=1 <nowiki>gchain</nowiki>]<nowiki> in AoE-heavy scenarios</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ It's important to recognize that many TA resets will not be particularly useful in the context that they occur, don't feel obligated to use every one immediately. Often times, just prioritizing </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809664262179.png?v=1 <nowiki>omni</nowiki>]<nowiki> leads to the most </nowiki><strong><nowiki>consistent</nowiki></strong><nowiki> gains from TA, though fishing for more resets from abilities like </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]<nowiki> can lead to higher peaks.</nowiki></p>
