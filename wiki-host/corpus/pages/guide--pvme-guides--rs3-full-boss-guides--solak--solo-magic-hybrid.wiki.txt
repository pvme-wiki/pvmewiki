</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This strategy successfully rootskips 75-80% of the time if executed properly.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• A successful rootskip is done by getting Solak's HP down to ~1510K or lower at </nowiki><strong><nowiki>~45s</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If you don't meet this threshold it's advised to ignore the rootskip attempt entirely.</nowiki></p>
