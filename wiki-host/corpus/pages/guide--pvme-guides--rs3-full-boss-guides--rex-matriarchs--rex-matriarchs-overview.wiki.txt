<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The Rex Matriarchs are four dinosaur bosses found on Anachronia</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Each Matriarch has its own attack style, weakness and mechanics</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ While the Matriarchs share a boss instance, only 3/4 share an arena</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Osseous' arena is accessed via teleportation device near the entrance, only after completion of the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Osseous_Rex_quest <nowiki>Osseous Rex quest</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Primarily attacks with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/944649693500154006.png?v=1 <nowiki>poisonicon</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vulnerable to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> (+27% hit chance with stab)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vulnerable to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> (+27% hit chance with arrows)</nowiki></p>
