[[PvME|PvME guide index]]

<span id="dragonkin-laboratory-basic"></span><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/EA7wP1w.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>ED2 is considered the most beginner friendly Elite Dungeon. The requirements to feasibly solo it both in terms of gear and skill are generally considered low. However, it still has considerable amount of challenging components which can result in fast KOs if the player is not careful.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Mechanics with high KO potential include but are not limited to:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Astellarn's neutron star</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Astellarn's black hole combined with celestial rain</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Verak Lith's fire tornados</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Verak Lith's egg bomb</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Black Stone Dragon's purple smoke/fire</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Black Stone Dragon's ability to attack semi-randomly using all 3 styles</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Black hands' heavy magic autos and small shadow hands</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use zerker aura </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643505179378974748.png?v=1 <nowiki>reckless</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643505153709965322.png?v=1 <nowiki>mani</nowiki>]<nowiki> variants for the dungeon. Otherwise, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643505653079343144.png?v=1 <nowiki>vamp</nowiki>]<nowiki> is fine for learning.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="astellarn-the-first-celestial-info"><nowiki>Astellarn, the First Celestial (Info)</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/YzDTPVB5wy.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The first boss you encounter. The boss has many mechanics that deal typeless damage so be prepared to eat. It uses hard hitting but slow auto attacks in all 3 styles, but primarily uses a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> breath attack which is very easy to flick.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>

</nowiki><div id="block-mechanics"><u><nowiki>Mechanics</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Attack Rotation</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Astellarn's starting attack pattern is:
    • 2-3 autos → Pulsar → ~2 autos → Black hole → 1-2 autos → Neutron Star → Repeat
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Celestial rain</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ When Astellarn falls below 200k HP, she starts taking drastically decreased damage and spawns celestial rain which affects a quadrant of the arena at any point of time, dealing rapid typeless damage which ramps up. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/M6UbQli.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Avoid standing in it always.
​
</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/M6UbQli.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Pulsar</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Astellarn summons a pulsar with 20000 HP near you, dealing periodic typeless damage. Kill this as soon as possible once it spawns. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/0UabHmn.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Using basics together with a powerful threshold such as </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/0UabHmn.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]<nowiki> to deal with it.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Wyrmhole</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Astellarn places a blue bar above your head. When it expires a black wyrmhole is dropped at the player location which deals rapid typeless damage. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/ZVNJasC.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Focus on placing it along these lines to prevent it from being blocked out by the later rain mechanic. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/ZVNJasC.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/wlaz77A.jpg <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Neutron Star</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ This will follow the player around and must be lured into the Wyrmhole, stabilizing it, turning white, and giving increased damage to Astellarn. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/zHIuF3t.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Do not let it touch you to avoid taking 4000+ damage.
        ⬩ Immediately teleport out and restart the fight if the star explodes like that.
    • The timing of this mechanic is not fixed so be extra careful of it and react accordingly.
​
</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/zHIuF3t.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</div>

</nowiki><h3 id="astellarn-the-first-celestial-fight-tips"><nowiki>Astellarn, the First Celestial (Fight tips)</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Do not use your damage boosting ultimates </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921597813137518.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswiftpf</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921595489488927.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshinepf</nowiki>]<nowiki>, but instead igneous abilities </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/959089455283728386.png?v=1 <nowiki>deadshotigneous</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/958989794757730307.png?v=1 <nowiki>tsunamiincite</nowiki>]<nowiki> and powerful thresholds for the first 50k HP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921597813137518.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswiftpf</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921595489488927.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshinepf</nowiki>]<nowiki> after dropping the wyrmhole as you will deal most of your damage after luring the neutron star.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stand out of her melee range so you only have to flick </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> autos.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ It is recommended to drop the wyrmhole between Astellarn and the west wall:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/t693lnb.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Keep hitting Astellarn before the Neutron Star spawns to build adren and as poison damage is unaffected by her damage reduction.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You can </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334377648130.png?v=1 <nowiki>divert</nowiki>]<nowiki> after using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921597813137518.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswiftpf</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921595489488927.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshinepf</nowiki>]<nowiki> while waiting for the Neutron Star to spawn.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If you fail to lure the Neutron Star into the Wyrmhole, teleport out and restart the fight.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="verak-lith-info"><ins><nowiki>Verak Lith (Info)</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/A2EOjdXdyH.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Verak Lith is the second boss of the dungeon, he has sizeable HP and mechanics that can shred your HP if handled improperly. He uses heavy </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> autos.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>

</nowiki><div id="block-mechanics--2"><u><nowiki>Mechanics</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Attack Rotation</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Verak does the following attack rotation:
    • 4 Auto attacks → Spire → 4 Auto attacks → Ricochet → 4 Auto attacks → Spire → 4 Auto Attacks → Nosedive/Hurricanes → Repeat
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Spire</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Verak Lith summons a spire with 15000 HP shortly after a warning message, dealing about 2000 melee damage to the player. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/afn857t.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
​
</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/afn857t.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Ricochet</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Verak sends out a projectile that lands near the player after a few seconds. The element of this projectile can be poison, ice, or shock. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/1c5I5Ar.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • The correct way to deal with this mechanic is to pick up the projectile and interact with a different type of egg to disperse it before the bar fills up.
    • Missing the projectile will deal 4000 damage and missing the dispersal can deal 9000+ damage.
    • Players may want to miss the projectile and either </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/1c5I5Ar.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541278264393729.png?v=1 <nowiki>debil</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> it.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Nosedive</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ A message will appear on the screen mentioning that a dragon is nosediving. An arrow will show up near the player to indicate the direction of approach. Shortly after, a thin line of dragonbreath will be fired along the line. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/66ACLwS.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • This can deal over 8000 magic damage and can be avoided by simply moving out of the way. However, the arrow will adjust it's position based on the player's movement for a while so it is recommended to learn the timing on this mechanic and move as it goes off.
    • This can also be dealt with by using </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/66ACLwS.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMage</nowiki>]<nowiki> and tanking it.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Hurricanes</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Verak says 'Let the flames melt the flesh from your bones' and engulfs the area around the player in fire. This will deal no damage but after a short delay, hurricanes will spawn in the area that deal extremely high </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage, lasting for about 20 seconds. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/uv37IW8.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/uv37IW8.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> or run away from the area accordingly.
​
</nowiki></div>

A 3x3 zone of spikes erupts around the player's initial position after a short delay, dealing 5000+ melee damage, draining all adrenaline and applying a debuff that reduces all adrenaline gain by 50% for a short duration if it hits. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/4ppYV4M.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>

        ⬩ </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/jBFHmJu.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/662249620579155968.png?v=1 <nowiki>dommine</nowiki>]<nowiki> help greatly as they explode for 10,000 damage each.

This is followed by a trail of small black hands which moves towards the player, dealing rapid 2000+ damage if stood on. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/5MAYT2U.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
        ⬩ The small hands will never reach the player if they on any of these 4 spots. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/5MAYT2U.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/Oa1qT6q.png <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>

    • Onyx dragon - Heals the dragon for 4000 HP.
    • Hydrix dragon - Instantly drains all adrenaline and applies a debuff that halves adrenaline for about 15 seconds.
    • Be careful of this especially when fighting multiple dragons.
    • Avoid these specs by stepping 2 tiles away from your original location before the spikes land.

It will then repeat this every 6 auto attacks. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/tITMd30.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
