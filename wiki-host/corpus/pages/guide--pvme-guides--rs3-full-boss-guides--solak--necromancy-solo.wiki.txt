</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Necromancy </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki> has the benefit of conjures, good aoe abilities, and high hitcaps to make quick work of Solak. The rotation as written has an element of RNG from critical strikes, Impatient perk procs and general boss mechanics.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This is an advanced </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki> guide to Grandmaster role (sub 5:15) solo Solak, assuming strong underlying knowledge of all Solak mechanics.</nowiki></p>
