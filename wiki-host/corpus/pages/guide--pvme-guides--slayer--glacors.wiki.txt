<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Glacors are profitable monsters that can be fought on or off task effectively, usually for their Slayer collection log items, or due to consistent profit from making </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/881962727705280512.png?v=1 <nowiki>armadylbattlestaff</nowiki>]<nowiki> and opening </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869284271452479488.png?v=1 <nowiki>eldertrovewent3</nowiki>]<nowiki> if fought at the Elder God Wars Dungeon.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The ability to be assigned Glacors can be unlocked at any Slayer master after completion of Ritual of the Mahjarrat, which also unlocks access to the Glacor Cave.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="location-and-where-to-stand"><ins><nowiki>Location and where to stand</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Glacors can be found at the Elder God Wars Dungeon, Glacor Cave, and Frozen Plateau (Wilderness), but are best killed at the Elder God Wars Dungeon due to dropping </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869284271452479488.png?v=1 <nowiki>eldertrovewent3</nowiki>]<nowiki> and being generally easier to AFK.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The Glacor Front of the EGWD can be accessed quickly using a boss portal leading to Arch-Glacor.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>There are two viable spots to kill Glacors, which each having pros and cons:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/55ufEq0vvx.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ This spot allows for the consistent killing of 2 Glacor spawns.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The long spawn time of Glacors can cause a lot of downtime which may cause issues with maintaining </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/KrGtsWEcVF.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ This spot allows for the consistent killing of 3 Glacor spawns.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This location is more gear/luck dependant as maintaining max KPH requires fast killing of the Glacors and good Enduring Glacyte luck.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Glacytes further North will be killed by some AoE abilities, which may cause them to drop hard to reach </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869284271452479488.png?v=1 <nowiki>eldertrovewent3</nowiki>]<nowiki> as well as being less efficient XP per Glacor kill while on task.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="afk-magic">[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki><ins><nowiki>AFK Magic</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Magic is the only viable style to AFK Glacors due to Dragon Breath </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833391702017.png?v=1 <nowiki>dbreath</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Greater Chain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063106914779166.png?v=1 <nowiki>gchain</nowiki>]<nowiki> being able to hit the spawned Glacytes while targetting the Glacor.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: When starting your hour or killing too slow, you may take the aggro of 2 Glacors at once, be sure to click on the one your character can actually attack, as one will be unable to be damaged</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583429936958930964.png?v=1 <nowiki>torment</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158468907008.png?v=1 <nowiki>affliction</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Sustain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/928221126360969226.png?v=1 <nowiki>powderofpenance</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1494069332584693810.png?v=1 <nowiki>penanceaspect</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/648976643687317532.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovlsalve</nowiki>]<nowiki> is active.</nowiki></p>
