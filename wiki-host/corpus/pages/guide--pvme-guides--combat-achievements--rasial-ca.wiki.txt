</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide will go over how to complete the Combat achievements for Rasial, and assumes you are already familiar with the basic mechanics of the encounter which can be found here </nowiki>[[PvME/Rasial/Rasial|<nowiki>Rasial</nowiki>]]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="positioning"><ins><nowiki>Positioning</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/z4ifLrUqck.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ The tile shown above serves as a safespot for the ghost wall mechanic.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This tile is ideal as it keeps you 5 tiles away for quicker </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For the soul obelisks, you may run 4 tiles west (toward obelisk), and 2-4 east.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Doing this allows </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]<nowiki> to kill one of the soul obelisks.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ This is only recommended if you have the ruthless perk.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For an in-game example of the correct tile being used, </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/N8u6ViKvDL.mp4 <nowiki>click here</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/N8u6ViKvDL.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</p>

</nowiki><h2 id="rasial-master-speed-killer"><ins><nowiki>Rasial Master Speed Killer</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This rotation is </nowiki><strong><nowiki>NOT</nowiki></strong><nowiki> recommended for standard kills, only for the achievement.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The setup takes extra time, thus lowering average kills per hour.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This rotation seeks to consistently hit under 1 minute kill times.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Will be slower without Innate Mastery enhancement </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1266418218264694954.png?v=1 <nowiki>shardofgenesisessence</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Requires </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689502804720615441.png?v=1 <nowiki>undeadslayerperk</nowiki>]<nowiki> on one gear piece.</nowiki></p>
