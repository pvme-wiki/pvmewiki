</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide will go over how to complete the Grandmaster speed kill Combat achievement for Beastmaster Durzag.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="general-information"><ins><nowiki>General Information</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The base must avoid being melee'd by Beastmaster Durzag so that he does not spawn the 3rd pet. They can do this by running between corners of the room and using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> to avoid melee swipes.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The base should be the only person to pre-vuln Beastmaster Durzag to avoid other members getting aggro.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
