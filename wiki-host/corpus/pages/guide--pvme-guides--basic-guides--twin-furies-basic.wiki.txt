<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="general-information"><ins><nowiki>General Information</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The Twin Furies are located in the Heart of Gielinor.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The duo consists of </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Nymora</nowiki></strong><nowiki> in the East and </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Avaryss</nowiki></strong><nowiki> in the West.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Entering the encounter requires 40 kill count, or 20 kill count with enough reputation.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Level 80 Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> is required to create an instance, which you can boost with any ranged potion / overload </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689554436179361864.png?v=1 <nowiki>overload</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The Twin Furies share one health pool of 200,000 LP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode, the fight adds lava bombs alongside the standard mechanics.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="fight-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Fight Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Poison </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/944649693500154006.png?v=1 <nowiki>poisonicon</nowiki>]<nowiki> splats may appear, but they do </nowiki><strong><nowiki>not</nowiki></strong><nowiki> deal damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="attack-rotation"><ins><nowiki>Attack Rotation</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Normal Mode: </nowiki><em><nowiki>Avaryss' Surge</nowiki></em><nowiki> → auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Rain Death</nowiki></em><nowiki> → auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Burn Phase</nowiki></em><nowiki> → auto attacks → Repeat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Hard Mode: 2 auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Avaryss' Surge + Lava Bombs</nowiki></em><nowiki> → auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Ceiling Collapse + Lava Bombs</nowiki></em><nowiki> → auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Channeled Bomb + Lava Bombs</nowiki></em><nowiki> → auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Blue Lava Bomb + Lava Bombs</nowiki></em><nowiki> → auto attacks → Repeat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="shared-health-pool"><ins><nowiki>Shared Health Pool</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Avaryss and Nymora share one health pool</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When they are close together, AoE abilities can hit both twins and effectively damage the shared health pool twice</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Hit caps do </nowiki><strong><nowiki>not</nowiki></strong><nowiki> apply to the Twin Furies' health pool, so some high-damage hits may deal more damage than the visible hitsplats suggest</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use AoE abilities when both twins are close together e.g., </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532878969438210.png?v=1 <nowiki>cane</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306391265284.png?v=1 <nowiki>bombard</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809145706319892.png?v=1 <nowiki>spectralscythe</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063106914779166.png?v=1 <nowiki>gchain</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ High-damage combinations such as Chaos Roar </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644356671737966.png?v=1 <nowiki>chaosroar</nowiki>]<nowiki> → Overpower </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/959089455640215633.png?v=1 <nowiki>overpowerigneous</nowiki>]<nowiki> will also go through the hit cap</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="avaryss-surge-example"><ins><nowiki>Avaryss' Surge</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/NWTHSDg.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/NWTHSDg.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Avaryss jumps to a wall, then charges across the arena</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This repeats 4 times</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Avaryss always travels in cardinal directions, such as north to south or west to east</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode, the dive can hit up to around 4,000 Melee damage, stun for 3 seconds, and disable overhead prayers</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode, attacking Avaryss mid-jump can reflect 90% of the damage taken</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Move out of Avaryss' path after she jumps to a new wall</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode, continually move to avoid being pushed and stunned</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Avoid attacking Avaryss mid-jump in Hard Mode</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="ceiling-collapse-example"><ins><nowiki>Ceiling Collapse</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/QIMa9WS.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/QIMa9WS.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Nymora jumps to a random square and causes a ceiling collapse</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode, Nymora yells &quot;we will purge them all!&quot; before the collapse</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode, this deals rapid Ranged damage up to around 1,000 damage every 1.8 seconds</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stand in the 3x3 area around Nymora to negate the mechanic</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use defensive abilities if needed while moving into position</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="channeled-bomb-examples-explosion"><ins><nowiki>Channeled Bomb</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/N7ceSRh.mp4 <nowiki>(Examples)</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/N7ceSRh.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/CJkO32O.mp4 <nowiki>(Explosion)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/CJkO32O.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The twins move to the centre of the arena and channel an attack</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If the channel finishes, it deals 4,500 damage in Normal Mode</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode, this can deal up to around 8,000 damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ During this mechanic, the twins take 2x damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You take typeless damage during the channel, which can be offset by Soul Split </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use AoE abilities while the twins are close together</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Melee can use Cleave </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532878616985610.png?v=1 <nowiki>cleave</nowiki>]<nowiki>, Hurricane </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532878969438210.png?v=1 <nowiki>cane</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Quake </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879506309150.png?v=1 <nowiki>quake</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Necromancy can use Spectral Scythe </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809145706319892.png?v=1 <nowiki>spectralscythe</nowiki>]<nowiki>, Spectral Scythe II </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809149690916945.png?v=1 <nowiki>spectralscythe2</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Spectral Scythe III </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809152043925505.png?v=1 <nowiki>spectralscythe3</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Move or Surge </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> to the edge of the arena before the explosion </nowiki><strong><nowiki>or</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Block the explosion with defensive abilities</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="lava-bombs-hm-only"><ins><nowiki>Lava Bombs (HM Only)</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ A lava bomb spawns alongside each main mechanic</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Lava bombs bounce around the arena 5 times before disappearing</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Green arrows show where the lava bomb will land</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If you are within the 5x5 landing area, you take 3,000-4,000 hard typeless damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After 2 bounces, a second lava bomb spawns</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Watch the green arrows and move away from the landing area</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Keep moving while handling the main mechanic</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Avoid pathing into Avaryss' jump path while dodging lava bombs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="blue-lava-bomb-hm-only"><ins><nowiki>Blue Lava Bomb (HM Only)</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ A blue lava bomb bounces around the arena 3 times</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Green arrows show where the blue lava bomb will land</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If avoided, it homes in on the player and deals 6,000-7,000 hard typeless damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If the player takes the bomb, it also hits both twins for the same damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Intentionally take the blue lava bomb to damage both twins</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Prepare defensives or healing before taking the hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Avoid overlapping the blue bomb with other dangerous mechanics where possible</nowiki></p>
