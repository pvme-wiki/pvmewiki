<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>A being of pure chaos that resides to the west of the Rogues' Castle located in deep Wilderness. The Chaos Elemental is a beginner-friendly boss with simple mechanics meant to disorient the player more than harm them.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>How to Get There</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>WARNING: THIS BOSS IS LOCATED IN THE WILDERNESS, BE SURE TO HAVE PVP TOGGLED </nowiki><em><nowiki>OFF</nowiki></em><nowiki> BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO FIGHT IT.</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The level 50 Wilderness Obelisk is located east of the Chaos Elemental, easily accessed with the Portable Obelisk </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137735861937655900.png?v=1 <nowiki>portableobelisk</nowiki>]<nowiki>, Obelisk Shards, or any of the other Wilderness Obelisks.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• These options will teleport the player to a random Wilderness Obelisk unless they have the hard Wilderness area tasks completed.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The teleport levers located in Edgeville </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1032552323903402024.png?v=1 <nowiki>edgevillelode</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Ardougne </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1032552315724517507.png?v=1 <nowiki>ardougnelode</nowiki>]<nowiki> teleport the player to the Deserted Keep, located west of the Chaos Elemental.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Discord</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>The Chaos Elemental's basic attack, made up of green particles that deal either </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage to the player. His other attacks are variations of this move with different effects.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Madness</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ The Chaos Elemental launches a multicoloured attack. Upon hit, the player's main hand weapon and up to 3 other pieces of equipped gear will be unequipped and placed in their inventory.
    • If the player's inventory is full, the Chaos Elemental will not use this attack
    • </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/gNKxb57aQz.mp4 <nowiki>Click to view an example of Madness</nowiki>]<nowiki>.
</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/gNKxb57aQz.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Confusion</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ The Chaos Elemental launches an attack made of fire. Upon hit, the player will be teleported upwards of 20 tiles away from Chaos Elemental in a random direction.
    • The player will stop attacking the boss after being teleported, be sure to click on the Chaos Elemental once this happens to resume attacking.
    • </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/8OQ7OtVZhR.mp4 <nowiki>Click to view an example of Confusion</nowiki>]<nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="tips-and-tricks"><ins><nowiki>Tips and Tricks</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> hit of Discord is the most accurate, making it best to either Protect from Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1052551456835768351.png?v=1 <nowiki>protectfrommagic</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Soul Split </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> throughout the fight.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Prioritize using powerful threshold abilities, only using basic abilities to build up to stronger ones, or when better options are on cooldown.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> examples of powerful abilities are Snap Shot </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535534127131394088.png?v=1 <nowiki>snap</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Rapid Fire </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki>, with abilities like Dazing Shot </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541307142307860.png?v=1 <nowiki>dazing</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Piercing Shot </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258538450944.png?v=1 <nowiki>piercing</nowiki>]<nowiki> being your weaker basics. Death's Swiftness </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921434633744455.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> is a very powerful ability, but should only be used near the start of the fight, if you find yourself with </nowiki><em><nowiki>too much</nowiki></em><nowiki> adrenaline.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For Necromancy </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki> build up to abilities like Volley of Souls </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Finger of Death </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434801938432010.png?v=1 <nowiki>fingerofdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> with your weaker abilities, Soul Sap </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809140476031057.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsap</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Touch of Death </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809175980810380.png?v=1 <nowiki>touchofdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki>, using your Basic Attack </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1482849360462086244.png?v=1 <nowiki>necrobasic</nowiki>]<nowiki> when all else is on cooldown.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If possible, keep your inventory full of items to avoid the Madness special attack, Chaos Elemental's drops can be used to fill any blank spaces.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ An NPC named Edward, located outside the Dragonkin Laboratory, south-east of the level 50 Wilderness Obelisk, can be used as a bank to store any drops the player receives and resupply for longer trips.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Due to the Chaos Elemental being located in the Wilderness, all Wilderness based bonuses granted by the Infernal Puzzle Box </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994189299681607800.png?v=1 <nowiki>infernalpuzzlebox</nowiki>]<nowiki> work against this boss.</nowiki></p>
