</nowiki><h2 id="fight-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Fight Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥Stay on the east-side of Pthentraken to avoid pulling Orikalka's aggression</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You should attack with Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Arrows give a +27% hit chance boost</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Any other style will only deal insignificant damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="attack-rotation"><ins><nowiki>Attack Rotation</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>3x </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Converging Lightning</nowiki></strong><nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Storm Column</nowiki></strong><nowiki> →</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>3x </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Converging Lightning</nowiki></strong><nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> →</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Repeat</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="auto-attacks-magic-example-ranged-example"><ins><nowiki>Auto Attacks</nowiki></ins><nowiki> (</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/ubFr4GP6Je.mp4 <nowiki>Magic example</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/ubFr4GP6Je.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/NVUYvi752x.mp4 <nowiki>Ranged example</nowiki>]<nowiki>)</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/NVUYvi752x.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Pthentraken uses Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> auto attacks.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If lower Defence </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250979987525.png?v=1 <nowiki>defence</nowiki>]<nowiki> pray against Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmage</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1052551456835768351.png?v=1 <nowiki>protectfrommagic</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> once you are comfortable surviving the mechanics</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="converging-lightning-example"><ins><nowiki>Converging Lightning</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/rW2FSiGUoG.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/rW2FSiGUoG.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Pthentraken summons 3-5 lightning beams around the arena. These beams move towards Pthentraken and deal heavy Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage if you stand in them (up to 5000-5500 damage every 2 ticks).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Either:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Wear Insulated boots </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841409588876279819.png?v=1 <nowiki>insulatedboots</nowiki>]<nowiki> to reduce damage taken</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Avoid standing in the lightning beams as they move towards Pthentraken</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="storm-column-overcharged-example"><ins><nowiki>Storm Column / Overcharged</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/3EeRovZD36.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/3EeRovZD36.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> An overhead bar appears above the player. When the bar empties, a 3x3 Storm Column appears on the player's tile, dealing rapidly increasing typeless damage, up to 3600 damage every 2 ticks. It also gives the </nowiki><em><nowiki>Overcharged</nowiki></em><nowiki> buff.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>While </nowiki><em><nowiki>Overcharged</nowiki></em><nowiki> is active, your hits on Pthentraken deal an additional Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> hit for 5% of the original damage, and Surge </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> has a 1-tick cooldown.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Move away from the Storm Column as soon as it appears, like in the example video</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Moving too early may make the Storm Column appear at your new location</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
