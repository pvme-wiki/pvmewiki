</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Flesh-hatcher Mhekarnahz is a stalker boss located in the Wilderness and requires 40 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1003947795541143632.png?v=1 <nowiki>dungeoneering</nowiki>]<nowiki> to fight. Unlike other </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Stalker_creatures_(Slayer_assignment) <nowiki>stalker creatures</nowiki>]<nowiki>, 71 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> is </nowiki><strong><nowiki>not</nowiki></strong><nowiki> required. This encounter is aimed at lower levels and introduces movement as a mechanic which players must react to and avoid.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Auto-attack</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Does 4 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> auto-attacks between mechanics
    • The start of the encounter only has 3
    • </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/gptIeL96KN.mp4 <nowiki>Click to view an example of the auto-attack</nowiki>]<nowiki>
</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/gptIeL96KN.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Targeted 3x3 Square</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Shoots a 3x3 attack towards the player that inflicts </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage
    • Getting hit will cause a short </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1102897815073603685.png?v=1 <nowiki>bindicon</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289428492369.png?v=1 <nowiki>stunicon</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/7AkOQn7ylg.mp4 <nowiki>Click to view an example of the targeted 3x3 square</nowiki>]<nowiki>
</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/7AkOQn7ylg.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Waves</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Unleashes 3 consecutive waves which cover specific parts of the arena
    • They occur back-to-back in a random order and deal typeless damage
    • They cover the outer, middle, and inner sections of the arena respectively
    • </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/CHLqUALsSc.mp4 <nowiki>Click to view an example of the 3 waves</nowiki>]<nowiki>
</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/CHLqUALsSc.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Minions</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Spawns 3 warped flesh sacs with bars above their heads
    • If a bar fills, spawns a warped seeker
    • Boss is immune while minions are alive
    • </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/EkfOuHcVyS.mp4 <nowiki>Click to view an example of the minions</nowiki>]<nowiki>
</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/EkfOuHcVyS.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</div>
