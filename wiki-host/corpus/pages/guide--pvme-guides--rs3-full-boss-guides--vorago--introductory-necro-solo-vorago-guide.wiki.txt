<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki> guide is meant to take someone who knows the </nowiki>[[PvME/Vorago/Overview|<nowiki>Vorago Basic</nowiki>]]<nowiki> mechanics in group, and let them solo. This introductory guide will not explicitly state every minute detail of how things work; and focus on the easier four weeks: </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Teamsplit</nowiki></strong><nowiki>, </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Ceiling Collapse</nowiki></strong><nowiki>, </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Scopulus</nowiki></strong><nowiki>, and </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Purple Bomb (The End)</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The way to get better at Vorago is just to fail over and over, and learn each time. It is okay if it takes a while, nothing worth doing is easy.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="general-notes"><ins><nowiki>General Notes</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When learning a hellhound </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/921410226475925514.png?v=1 <nowiki>hellhoundpouch</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be very useful</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Bring an ice nihil pouch </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/854475442164334592.png?v=1 <nowiki>icenihil</nowiki>]<nowiki> to switch to at the end of P4. Accuracy at Vorago is very important, and the nihil will help give you the extra push you need on p5.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Between every phase all of your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139452791878856805.png?v=1 <nowiki>necrosis</nowiki>]<nowiki> stacks, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139453152169558046.png?v=1 <nowiki>residualsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki>, and conjures are removed. There is no reason to save them because of this.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ There is an </nowiki>[[PvME/Combat reference/Alt1|<nowiki>Alt1</nowiki>]]<nowiki> plugin called </nowiki><code><nowiki>Vorago Tag</nowiki></code><nowiki> ‎that helps with target cycle (tc) timing when starting out. Recommended, especially for Teamsplit p5.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Some common road blocks:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Soulsplit flicking, and prayer flicking between </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmage</nowiki>]<nowiki> on p2 and p4 for Vorago's attacks. Vorago has a chance every auto-attack while in Melee Distance (MD) to either blue bomb or melee smack </nowiki><strong><nowiki>or both!</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• How to solo clear bleeds.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Phase 5.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="prefight"><ins><nowiki>Prefight</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>You take a hit for 70% of your HP 10s after challenging.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809209782698024.png?v=1 <nowiki>darkness</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Can affect melee hits, and has a small effect on blue bombs.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Do </nowiki><strong><nowiki>not</nowiki></strong><nowiki> conjure spirits until </nowiki><strong><nowiki>after</nowiki></strong><nowiki> you climb. If they attack Vorago while you climb, he will start to throw blue bombs.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]<nowiki> the tick prior to being hit.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Takes some learning, but halves the damage taken.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="phase-1"><ins><nowiki>Phase 1</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Once you drop-down into phase 1, activate </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641337999170207763.png?v=1 <nowiki>excal</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmage</nowiki>]<nowiki> and spam click the gap in the wall to start climbing.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Once at the top, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> and then go into a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> rotation.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At around 30k damage you can </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809121983336548.png?v=1 <nowiki>invokedeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> + click to jump at the same time. That way when you hit the ground you can just (tc) and kill.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809121983336548.png?v=1 <nowiki>invokedeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> after jumping risks you getting hit by a blue bomb once down.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vorago's bombs can still hit you while jumping so </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmage</nowiki>]<nowiki> before clicking to jump.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You cannot switch prayers during the jump animation.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><h2 id="phase-5"><ins><nowiki>Phase 5</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This phase has mechanics and requires you to do damage. You can get really good at either one or learn to get decent at both.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanical outline:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>1)</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Each tick you take damage, Vorago pushes you back </nowiki><strong><nowiki>one</nowiki></strong><nowiki> tile.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306353778689.png?v=1 <nowiki>cade</nowiki>]<nowiki> block the damage, you will not get pushed.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>2)</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Each 20k damage you do, you push Vorago back </nowiki><strong><nowiki>one</nowiki></strong><nowiki> tile.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Damage from </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809168368148490.png?v=1 <nowiki>splitsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki> does </nowiki><strong><nowiki>NOT</nowiki></strong><nowiki> contribute to push so it should not be used.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>3)</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Every time Vorago moves a tile, he will stall his next attack slightly.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>4)</nowiki></strong><nowiki> After you have pushed Vorago eight tiles from his starting spot, you can maul </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/736524968174682132.png?v=1 <nowiki>maul</nowiki>]<nowiki> him if you are in MD.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Do not stand MD unless clearing bleeds or at the end of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/867678153883516958.png?v=1 <nowiki>cadeturtling</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Whenever Vorago melee swipes he has a chance of throwing a blue bomb, which can generate an extra tile of push against you.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Teamsplit and Ceiling, as well as Purple Bomb and Scopulus, share the same phase 5 mechanics and rotation.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The below rotations are to be completed in one Vorago cycle (weekly spec - reflect - bleeds - repeat):</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• To minimise pushback, early </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/867678153883516958.png?v=1 <nowiki>cadeturtling</nowiki>]<nowiki> to cover Vorago's blue bombs and reflect</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• To maximise push, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> at the end of reflect and do a lot of damage during bleeds (or the weekly spec).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ To clear bleeds, you should stand MD and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258832052231.png?v=1 <nowiki>escape</nowiki>]<nowiki> first bleed, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> second bleed and run MD, and then </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258832052231.png?v=1 <nowiki>escape</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> back the third bleed.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> is to maintain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]<nowiki> bouncing. If they are not up, just run back closer. When clearing bleeds, stand in the middle of the room, MD to Vorago. This will lead to the most consistent results.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1285852547453685781.png?v=1 <nowiki>numbingroot</nowiki>]<nowiki> Can be used if you fail to clear a bleed but at the cost of 10 adrenaline. While that doesn't seem like much, phase 5 snowballs due to Vorago stalling his attack every time you push, and so should only be used in the case of messing up the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258832052231.png?v=1 <nowiki>escape</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258832052231.png?v=1 <nowiki>escape</nowiki>]<nowiki> method</nowiki></p>

The </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/867678153883516958.png?v=1 <nowiki>cadeturtling</nowiki>]<nowiki> should occur right before the second blue bomb hits you and hopefully will last through reflect which gets you to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]<nowiki> crit though, you will likely generate enough stall that the last 1-2 autos of Vorago will not be under </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306353778689.png?v=1 <nowiki>cade</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vorago's first bleed can occur right before the end of reflect, so you should aim to not build with damaging abilities at that time.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Hence the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809194423160883.png?v=1 <nowiki>commandskeleton</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> at the end of reflect.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Once you get to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki>, a simple rotation follows which should generate more than enough push.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Depending on push, after the second </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434801938432010.png?v=1 <nowiki>fingerofdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> you might be able to just use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809234922934282.png?v=1 <nowiki>omniguard</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1482849360462086244.png?v=1 <nowiki>necrobasic</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809262194294864.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathguard70</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/736524968174682132.png?v=1 <nowiki>maul</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339234111848463.png?v=1 <nowiki>ingen</nowiki>]<nowiki> is written into the example rotations, but is not necessary at all. It will just bring you from ~87% to 100% hit chance on that first </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]</em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="p5-multi-cycle"><nowiki>P5 multi-cycle</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>If the phase extends longer than the specified rotations above:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For the second cycle you can use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809234922934282.png?v=1 <nowiki>omniguard</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> at the start of bleeds and do an </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809234922934282.png?v=1 <nowiki>omniguard</nowiki>]<nowiki> rotation, then build adren for 3rd cycle.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For the third cycle aim to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/867678153883516958.png?v=1 <nowiki>cadeturtling</nowiki>]<nowiki> the first blue bomb after weekly spec, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> after reflect and dps during bleeds + next weekly spec, then after reflect build to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/867678153883516958.png?v=1 <nowiki>cadeturtling</nowiki>]<nowiki> and repeat.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Cumulative </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1176968330582700174.png?v=1 <nowiki>invokelordofbones</nowiki>]<nowiki> stacks will help a lot here.</nowiki></p>
