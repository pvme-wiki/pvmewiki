<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The aim of this guide is provide a basic overview of how to get consistent kills at Nex, Angel of Death with a low-tier geared team. While writing this guide, we aimed to keep each preset within the confines of tier 1 of </nowiki>[https://pvme.io/gear/ <nowiki>Upgrade guides</nowiki>]<nowiki>. However due to the endgame nature of the boss, this has not been totally possible. Keeping this in mind, we have instead aimed to provide presets that are as low-tiered as possible without consistent strategies becoming unviable.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="disclaimer"><nowiki>Disclaimer</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide is made with the idea of getting started at AoD, and does not cover meta strategies. For those please refer to the main guides in </nowiki>[https://pvme.io/guides/?q=angel%20of%20death <nowiki>Angel of Death guides</nowiki>]<nowiki>. For a detailed description of mechanics, please refer to </nowiki>[[PvME/Nex: Angel of Death/Angel Of Death 7s — Mechanics|<nowiki>Mechanics</nowiki>]]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Phase 2</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Build adrenaline while Nex is unattackable.
​⬥ Once she is damageable again use strong abilities on her until she reaches close to 1.8m hp.
​⬥ Help kill the Glacies and Cruor amalgamations. Ideally use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809995874304.png?v=1 <nowiki>tsunami</nowiki>]<nowiki> during this phase on one of the amalgamations.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Phase 3</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line">[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921119381463070.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> and build adrenaline until you are able to use your strong abilities such as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809978966037.png?v=1 <nowiki>wm</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809664262179.png?v=1 <nowiki>omni</nowiki>]<nowiki>. Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869285393223254107.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]<nowiki> as much as possible in between.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Pillars</nowiki></u><nowiki>

<div><div><u><nowiki>Phase 2</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Run to your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833358016512.png?v=1 <nowiki>deto</nowiki>]<nowiki> spot and lose target to centre it. Instructions on losing target provided below:
    • Drop </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/656844963522281473.png?v=1 <nowiki>dummy</nowiki>]<nowiki> → any basic ability on </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/656844963522281473.png?v=1 <nowiki>dummy</nowiki>]<nowiki> → disassemble </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/656844963522281473.png?v=1 <nowiki>dummy</nowiki>]<nowiki> → any offensive ability → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833358016512.png?v=1 <nowiki>deto</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • If using queueing replace any offensive ability with an eof spec instead
​⬥ Throw a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/655341074235129858.png?v=1 <nowiki>vulnbomb</nowiki>]<nowiki> at the amalgamation spot just before it spawns. Release </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833358016512.png?v=1 <nowiki>deto</nowiki>]<nowiki> on </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580167371365548042.png?v=1 <nowiki>aod</nowiki>]<nowiki> once amalgamations spawn and use strong abilities till she is almost at 1.8m hp.
    • It is beneficial to use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833391702017.png?v=1 <nowiki>dbreath</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809995874304.png?v=1 <nowiki>tsunami</nowiki>]<nowiki> while standing behind the amalgamation for this step, so as to help phase </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580167371365548042.png?v=1 <nowiki>aod</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki><em><nowiki>and</nowiki></em><nowiki> kill the amalgamation.
​⬥ Kill your amalgamation.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Phase 3</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Once your minion has spawned and is aggro'd onto you, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> across the centre of the arena to lure it to the centre.
​⬥ Once this is done, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921119381463070.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> and build adrenaline until you are able to use your strong abilities such as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809978966037.png?v=1 <nowiki>wm</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809664262179.png?v=1 <nowiki>omni</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/867678154369400862.png?v=1 <nowiki>impactflank</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/867678153611018261.png?v=1 <nowiki>deepimpactflank</nowiki>]<nowiki>. Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869285393223254107.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]<nowiki> as much as possible in between.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Pillars</nowiki></u><nowiki>

<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> to the first pillar just before </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580167371365548042.png?v=1 <nowiki>aod</nowiki>]<nowiki> reaches 600k hp.
​⬥ Use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/655341074235129858.png?v=1 <nowiki>vulnbomb</nowiki>]<nowiki> on each pillar, then kill them using your strong abilities as available.
​⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/756236265472524418.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofacceleration</nowiki>]<nowiki> if necessary to move between pillars or to the middle to pick up bombs. Aim to exit the 3rd pillar with 100% adren or close to it.

Hammer should use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339234111848463.png?v=1 <nowiki>ingen</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641670143197446182.png?v=1 <nowiki>swh</nowiki>]<nowiki> Special Attack as the first ability on the boss. 
</nowiki></div>

<div><div><u><nowiki>Phase 2</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Build adren and Residual Souls </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139453152169558046.png?v=1 <nowiki>residualsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki> with basics while </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580167371365548042.png?v=1 <nowiki>aod</nowiki>]<nowiki> is undamageable.
​⬥ Command Skeleton </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809194423160883.png?v=1 <nowiki>commandwarrior</nowiki>]<nowiki> before the boss becomes damageable and use strong abilities to phase it.
​⬥ AOD </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580167371365548042.png?v=1 <nowiki>aod</nowiki>]<nowiki> will phase at 1.8m hp. 
​⬥ The Necromancy base will kill the amalgamations using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809121983336548.png?v=1 <nowiki>invokedeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809140476031057.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsap</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]<nowiki> (assuming Soulbound Lantern </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809230573441064.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulboundlantern</nowiki>]<nowiki> and 3/4+ Residual Souls </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139453152169558046.png?v=1 <nowiki>residualsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki>).
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Phase 3</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Lure your minion by surging through </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580167371365548042.png?v=1 <nowiki>aod</nowiki>]<nowiki>.
​⬥ This is a good time to use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/736298121704767538.png?v=1 <nowiki>adrenrenewal</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809175980810380.png?v=1 <nowiki>touchofdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> →  </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809140476031057.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsap</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809194423160883.png?v=1 <nowiki>commandwarrior</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]<nowiki>.
​⬥ Once minions are lured, you can use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809140476031057.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsap</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434801938432010.png?v=1 <nowiki>fingerofdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809175980810380.png?v=1 <nowiki>touchofdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> to kill the minions and do good damage to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580167371365548042.png?v=1 <nowiki>aod</nowiki>]<nowiki>.
</nowiki></div>

<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> to the first pillar just before </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580167371365548042.png?v=1 <nowiki>aod</nowiki>]<nowiki> reaches 600k hp.
​⬥ Kill the pillars using your strong abilities as available, such as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]<nowiki> (which can be bounced between pillars if timed correctly).

​⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/756236265472524418.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofacceleration</nowiki>]<nowiki> if necessary to move between pillars or to the middle to pick up bombs. Aim to exit the 3rd pillar 4/5 Residual Souls </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139453152169558046.png?v=1 <nowiki>residualsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki> so you can use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]<nowiki> on </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580167371365548042.png?v=1 <nowiki>aod</nowiki>]<nowiki>.
