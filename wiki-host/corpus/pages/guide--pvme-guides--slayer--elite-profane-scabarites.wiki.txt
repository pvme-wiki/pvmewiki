</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Elite_Profane_Scabarite <nowiki>Elite Profane Scabarites</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1333926854666621019.png?v=1 <nowiki>profanescabarite</nowiki>]<nowiki> are slayer monsters requiring 87 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> to kill. </nowiki><h2 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Elite Profane Scabarites can be fought in the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Temple_of_Isolation <nowiki>Temple of Isolation</nowiki>]<nowiki> or the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Sunken_Pyramid <nowiki>Sunken Pyramid</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
