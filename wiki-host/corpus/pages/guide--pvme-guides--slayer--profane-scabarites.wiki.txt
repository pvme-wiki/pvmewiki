</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Profane Scabarites </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1333926854666621019.png?v=1 <nowiki>profanescabarite</nowiki>]<nowiki> are slayer monsters requiring 87 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> to kill. They can be killed off-task, or with either a &quot;Scabarites&quot; or &quot;Profane Scabarites&quot; task for on-task bonuses.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
