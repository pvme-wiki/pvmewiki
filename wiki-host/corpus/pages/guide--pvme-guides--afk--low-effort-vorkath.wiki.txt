</nowiki><h3 id="the-fight"><nowiki>The fight</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This method requires few inputs to work, but they must be done in order for it to be successful.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>There is still a low chance of death which can be fully avoided by using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> to avoid Zemouregal's bone spikes attack.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Enter Instance → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1166094935066423348.png?v=1 <nowiki>conjurearmy</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809121983336548.png?v=1 <nowiki>invokedeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> → Target </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1278853696159088833.png?v=1 <nowiki>vorkath</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/655341074235129858.png?v=1 <nowiki>vulnbomb</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339922019516416.png?v=1 <nowiki>undeadslayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1176968204883603497.png?v=1 <nowiki>ballista</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Upkeep </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/655341074235129858.png?v=1 <nowiki>vulnbomb</nowiki>]<nowiki> and press </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339922019516416.png?v=1 <nowiki>undeadslayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> on cooldown.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Press </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1176968204883603497.png?v=1 <nowiki>ballista</nowiki>]<nowiki> whenever it is available, however make sure that it is available to be pressed for when Vorkath flies @ 750k hp.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If you press </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1176968204883603497.png?v=1 <nowiki>ballista</nowiki>]<nowiki> late or not at all, you can use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]<nowiki> to survive the flight damage and use the spare </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/921410226475925514.png?v=1 <nowiki>hellhoundpouch</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Cast </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/938475261366784070.png?v=1 <nowiki>prismofrestoration</nowiki>]<nowiki> after vorkath flies to keep your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/921410226475925514.png?v=1 <nowiki>hellhoundpouch</nowiki>]<nowiki> alive.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641337999170207763.png?v=1 <nowiki>excal</nowiki>]<nowiki> after Vorkath flies.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Walk out of the green/purple poison pools if they spawn beneath you.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Target Zemouregal after Vorkath takes flight, and re-target Vorkath when he lands.</nowiki></p>
