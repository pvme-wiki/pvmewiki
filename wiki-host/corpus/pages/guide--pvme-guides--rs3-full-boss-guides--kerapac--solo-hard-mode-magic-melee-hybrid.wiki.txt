</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This is an </nowiki><strong><nowiki>advanced guide</nowiki></strong><nowiki> which assumes a strong underlying knowledge of all mechanics and information presented in </nowiki>[[PvME/Kerapac/Kerapac HM Overview|<nowiki>Kerapac HM Basic</nowiki>]]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This method aims to consistently hit sub-1:40 kill times.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>It is </nowiki><strong><nowiki>not</nowiki></strong><nowiki> required to have a </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Nodon_dragonkin_slayer_assignment <nowiki>Nodon dragonkin</nowiki>]<nowiki> slayer assignment for this method to work, but it will improve your consistency with the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_helmet_stand <nowiki>Slayer helmet stand</nowiki>]<nowiki> boost </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285340460449792.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayerhelm</nowiki>]</p>
