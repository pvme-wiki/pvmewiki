Beastmaster Durzag (BM) is the first of two raids bosses. Because of the straightforward nature of the fight, this is often the first group pvm experience for many players.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="overview-of-the-fight"><ins><nowiki>Overview of the Fight</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The fight begins by clearing airuts and chargers.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Beware of Airuts' punishing melee attacks.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When the mob counter reaches 22 the first pet, Cormes, will spawn beside the western gong.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Stand slightly SE of this gong and attack the pet.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Protect melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> if attacked by melee airuts, or step away and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Kill airuts and chargers until the countdown reaches 0.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At this point, team members should position themselves near the centre of the arena.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Base lures BM to the SE corner.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Pet Tank 2 provokes the pet ensuring it faces away from the team.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• NC and BUSC clear chargers lining the arena perimeter.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The DPS attack Pet 2 until it reaches 100k health.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• They should then switch to Pet 3, attacking until it reaches ~100k lifepoints.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Kill pets when BM is almost 750k LP. If BM still has high health (~900k), lower BM's lifepoints to 750k before finally killing the pets.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Once pets die, focus on BM.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><div id="block-bmpetmechanics"><nowiki>BM Pet Mechanics
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><div><div><div><nowiki>Attack effects
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Ranged attacks (Krar) drain run energy, stunning you if run energy reaches 0%.
⬥ Mage attacks (Tuz) apply a bleed.
⬥ Melee attacks (all pets if in melee distance) apply stacks which increase damage received.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><nowiki>General advice
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Provoke </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> pets promptly and position to face away from the dps pile.
⬥ Ensure pets are </nowiki><strong><nowiki>not</nowiki></strong><nowiki> in the south-east quadrant with BM.
⬥ Pets can randomly 'jump', targeting other players.
    • </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> the pet back and lure it far from BM, keeping it facing away from the DPS pile. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/SdALHPU.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>.
⬥ Cycling defensives (</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/SdALHPU.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541278264393729.png?v=1 <nowiki>debil</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki>) is advised to mitigate damage taken.
</nowiki></div>

<nowiki>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pets are split between two players: Pet Tank 1/3 and Pet Tank 2</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The numbers refer to the respective order in which the pets spawn.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The first pet is always Cormes, the second pet can be either Tuz or Krar.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For Cormes, stand north of the western gong and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> as soon as it spawns.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Protect from Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488317046812.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectRange</nowiki>]<nowiki> and DPS the pet until it dies.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/nqu7oF0.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pet 2 spawns with BM. Position yourself just east of the northern gong, drop your DPS ultimate and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> the pet as soon as it spawns.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When Pet 2 reaches 500k LP, the third pet will spawn in a random corner.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Pet Tank 1/3 should </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> to draw the pet away from BM, keeping it facing away from the DPS pile.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/e4U8Wvb.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="north-chargers-nc"><ins><nowiki>North Chargers (NC)</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Draws aggression of the northern chargers and kills them.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Shortly before the mob counter reaches 0, make your way to the eastern gong.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The perimeter of the arena is lined with cages which fill with chargers once BM spawns.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Start north of the eastern gong, move counter-clockwise.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Click each cage to free the charger.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Take a wide turn around the northern gong so chargers do not get trapped behind the pet. Example: </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/A1bDenb.mp4 <nowiki>https://img.pvme.io/images/A1bDenb.mp4</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/A1bDenb.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Once you collect all the northern chargers, move along the west wall to the SW corner.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Along with the BUSC, kill chargers with AOE abilities.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Rangers should bring Mechanised Chinchompas </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641669268722810881.png?v=1 <nowiki>mechchin</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Magers should prioritise AOE abilities (e.g. </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159194259467.png?v=1 <nowiki>corruptblast</nowiki>]<nowiki>)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Necromancers should utilise </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809140476031057.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsap</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809142376058910.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulstrike</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After clearing chargers, NC becomes a DPS for the rest of the fight.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/KdwdbkC.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="backup-south-chargers-busc"><ins><nowiki>Backup / South Chargers (BUSC)</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Clears southern chargers and helps the Base Tank with tanking BM.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ To clear chargers, start south of the eastern gong and move clockwise to the SW corner, clicking cages. Example: </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/KE8qKSu.mp4 <nowiki>https://img.pvme.io/images/KE8qKSu.mp4</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/KE8qKSu.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• AOE chargers down with the NC.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Rangers should bring Mechanised Chinchompas </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641669268722810881.png?v=1 <nowiki>mechchin</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Magers should prioritise AOE abilities (e.g. </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159194259467.png?v=1 <nowiki>corruptblast</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833391702017.png?v=1 <nowiki>dbreath</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833358016512.png?v=1 <nowiki>deto</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/958989794757730307.png?v=1 <nowiki>tsunamiincite</nowiki>]<nowiki>).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Necromancers should utilise </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809142376058910.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulstrike</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After clearing chargers, go directly east of the southern gong.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Drop your DPS ultimate and attack BM. It is safest to Protect Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> until the end of the fight.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When BM reaches 750k LP </nowiki><strong><nowiki>OR</nowiki></strong><nowiki> one of the pets dies (whichever happens first), </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> BM.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Aim to always have a defensive up as BM hits especially hard during this stage of the fight.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Eventually the base will provoke BM away from you.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• In longer kills, your stacks will clear. You should then </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> back BM. Repeat this process with the base until the end of the kill.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><div id="block-bmmechanics"><nowiki>BM Mechanics
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ BM attacks his main target with a 3x3 melee AOE attack.
    • All players </nowiki><em><nowiki>not directly behind him</nowiki></em><nowiki> are hit with a ranged auto attack
⬥ Applies a stack to his primary target (up to a max of 15) per auto.
    • This increases the damaged received by 6.6% per stack.
⬥ BM resets your defensives at 5, 10, and 15 stacks.
    • Defensives are reset for 3 seconds, 6 seconds, and after every auto, respectively.
⬥ Freedom </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> is used to halve stacks.
    • This halves stacks, rounded down (e.g. using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> at 9 stacks brings them down to 4).
⬥ BM throws out explosive magic bombs. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/t0iiVH5.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Bombs put defensives on cooldown for 3 seconds.
        ⬩ Click bombs to defuse them.
        ⬩ Deflect Magic </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/t0iiVH5.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMage</nowiki>]<nowiki> halves damage from them.
⬥ BM can drag players into melee distance randomly. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/9Ubda9SJwG.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Use </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/9Ubda9SJwG.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and run away quickly
</nowiki></div>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When BM spawns, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> him and proceed to the SE corner. Example: </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/CCknDQP.mp4 <nowiki>https://img.pvme.io/images/CCknDQP.mp4</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/CCknDQP.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/6zUuEsq.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Once in the corner, drop your DPS ultimate and attack BM.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After the BUSC provokes BM away from you, your stacks will eventually clear. When stacks clear, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> back and cycle through your defensive abilities.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/KgFimmi.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After BM reaches 750k hp, DPS him down as fast as possible.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The Base and BUSC should 'juggle' BM to enable each other to clear stacks and maintain good HP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• All other team members are now purely DPS until BM dies.</nowiki></p>
