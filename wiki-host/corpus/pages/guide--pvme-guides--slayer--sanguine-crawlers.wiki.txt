</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Sanguine_crawler <nowiki>Sanguine crawlers</nowiki>]<nowiki> are a low level Slayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> mob found on top of the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Blighted_Cave <nowiki>Blighted Cave</nowiki>]<nowiki>, located in Havenhythe. They are mainly killed for the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_collection_log#Havenhythe <nowiki>Havenhythe collection log</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Sanguine crawlers can be fought on top of the Blighted Cave, located in Havenhythe.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Blighted Cave</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/hZs8OVaOEw.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ Teleport to Silverquill, the Dreadhog via a boss portal, exit the cave and head north.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• May alternatively use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1084799722780360704.png?v=1 <nowiki>portablefairyring</nowiki>]<nowiki> and teleport to </nowiki><code><nowiki>BKS</nowiki></code><nowiki> ‎then run northeast.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285651387613194.png?v=1 <nowiki>oldakcoil</nowiki>]<nowiki> as shown above, your character will constantly be moving.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="method-afk-melee"><ins><nowiki>Method: AFK Melee</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]</h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Before loading your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285651387613194.png?v=1 <nowiki>oldakcoil</nowiki>]<nowiki> and starting the session, check the following:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/648976643687317532.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovlsalve</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689525476158472288.png?v=1 <nowiki>weppoison</nowiki>]<nowiki> are active at all times.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583429936606347280.png?v=1 <nowiki>turmoil</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159416557573.png?v=1 <nowiki>malevolence</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Sustain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/928221126360969226.png?v=1 <nowiki>powderofpenance</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1494069332584693810.png?v=1 <nowiki>penanceaspect</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159416557573.png?v=1 <nowiki>malevolence</nowiki>]<nowiki> you will need extra prayer sustain, use prayer potion drops as needed.</nowiki></p>
