</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide lists the recommended Action Bars to use in Revolution combat mode (see section </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Configuring Revolution+</nowiki></strong><nowiki> later in this guide for more info).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The different combinations of bars are:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Single-target </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137770977506369596.png?v=1 <nowiki>singletarget</nowiki>]<nowiki> (e.g., most bosses)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Multi-target </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137770974431944835.png?v=1 <nowiki>multitarget</nowiki>]<nowiki> (e.g., slayer mobs)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ 2-handed (only one weapon can be equipped)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Dual-wield (where two weapons can be equipped, one in each hand)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>If a combination is not displayed, it means the bar scored poorly in tests and is not worth using.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="disclaimer"><ins><nowiki>Disclaimer</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>No revolution bar is perfect for every situation - unless you have accessibility needs, we strongly recommend learning full manual to gain total control over which abilities are fired when. You can find more information in </nowiki>[[PvME/Learning to boss/Revo To Full Manual|<nowiki>Revo To Full Manual</nowiki>]]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Variances may include:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Single-target </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137770977506369596.png?v=1 <nowiki>singletarget</nowiki>]<nowiki> bars performing differently based on duration of the fight</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Testing was conducted at both War's Retreat dummies and Vindicta</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Multi-target </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137770974431944835.png?v=1 <nowiki>multitarget</nowiki>]<nowiki> bars performing differently based on the number of enemies surrounding you, and their life points</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Multi-target testing was conducted at Abyssal Beasts in the Sunken Pyramid</nowiki></p>
