<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: Use the </nowiki><ins><strong><nowiki>Table of Contents</nowiki></strong></ins><nowiki> in this page’s contents navigation to find the information you are looking for.</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Some general notes:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The game runs on an internal tick system: </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1t (tick) = 0.6s (seconds)</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The minimum time between abilities is called the global cooldown: </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1 gcd = 3t = 1.8s</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Auto attacks will only give adrenaline if they hit (splash = no adrenaline gain).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Debuff spells like Vuln </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537349530551582720.png?v=1 <nowiki>Vuln</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Enfeeble </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/567727986409734145.png?v=1 <nowiki>Enfeeble</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Smoke Cloud </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090641879050.png?v=1 <nowiki>smokecloud</nowiki>]<nowiki> do not give adrenaline.</nowiki></p>
