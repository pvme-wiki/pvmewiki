</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide covers the perks we suggest for your gear and how to obtain them.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>When you see symbols in a listed perk (+ and X):</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ + represents an item with </nowiki><strong><nowiki>multiple gizmos slots</nowiki></strong><nowiki>:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• E.g., </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073088769982475.png?v=1 <nowiki>p6</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689502339891331093.png?v=1 <nowiki>as1</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712074245202772009.png?v=1 <nowiki>as4</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1214416336797564959.png?v=1 <nowiki>eruptive2</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ X - represents where you have </nowiki><strong><nowiki>a choice of perks</nowiki></strong><nowiki>:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• E.g., </nowiki><em><nowiki>'Biting 4 X'</nowiki></em><nowiki> you could choose between 9 different perks to go with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073087809617931.png?v=1 <nowiki>biting4</nowiki>]<nowiki> depending on your preference</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• These combinations are listed in the later sections of this guide</nowiki></p>
