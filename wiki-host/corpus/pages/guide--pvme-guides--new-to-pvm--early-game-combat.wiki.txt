</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Early game combat quests to help you gain some levels whilst being immersed in the lore</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Some training suggestions (these are not mandatory)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ An introduction to War's Retreat and some further quest information relating to combat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="some-key-tips-before-you-start"><ins><nowiki>Some key tips before you start</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Hit chance i.e. your damage scaling when attacking monsters can be viewed as a percentage on the monster's HP bar. The higher the number, the greater your damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Wearing &quot;off-style&quot; armour e.g. a melee weapon with magic armour severely impacts your hit chance in a negative manner! Be sure to match your armour combat style with your weapon combat style.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Attack </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250472476758.png?v=1 <nowiki>attack</nowiki>]<nowiki> affects melee accuracy and its level is what determines what melee weapons you can equip. Strength </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250879324165.png?v=1 <nowiki>strength</nowiki>]<nowiki> determines your damage dealt with melee weapons.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The best defence is a good offence: weapon upgrades take precedence over armour upgrades!</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You will level quickly in the early levels - if you feel like you can survive without issues, skip an armour upgrade and save your gold for the following one</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When using Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> you should regularly upgrade your auto-cast to the highest available spell tier. You can find out a spell's damage level cap by checking its tooltip.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><em><nowiki>For example: Air strike's damage cap is at level 16 - you can upgrade to Air bolt at level 17.</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If you need help, don't struggle in silence! You can ask questions in the help channels of:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The official Runescape Discord </nowiki>[https://discord.com/invite/rs <nowiki>invite link (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/723915055976153138 <nowiki>Affiliates (external Discord)</nowiki>]</p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ One of the first useful unlocks to aim for is the tier 3 </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Infernal_Puzzle_Box <nowiki>Infernal Puzzle Box</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994189299681607800.png?v=1 <nowiki>infernalpuzzlebox</nowiki>]<nowiki>, which prevents your adrenaline from draining when outside of combat</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You can find the box in </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/The_Zamorakian_Undercity#Getting_there <nowiki>The Zamorakian Undercity</nowiki>]<nowiki> specifically </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Infernal_Puzzle_Box#mapFullscreen <nowiki>here</nowiki>]<nowiki>. Completing the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Daughter_of_Chaos/Quick_guide <nowiki>Daughter of Chaos</nowiki>]<nowiki> quest (40 Archaeology </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/941211288665739274.png?v=1 <nowiki>archaeology</nowiki>]<nowiki> and 40 Divination </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1042453694735712286.png?v=1 <nowiki>divination</nowiki>]<nowiki>) unlocks tier 2.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• To unlock tier 3, complete the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Civil_War_I_(miniquest)/Quick_guide <nowiki>Civil War I</nowiki>]<nowiki> miniquest, and the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Gross_Misconduct <nowiki>Gross Misconduct achievement</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Two quests also worth mentioning are </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/The_Temple_at_Senntisten/Quick_guide <nowiki>The Temple at Senntisten</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Plague%27s_End/Quick_guide <nowiki>Plague's End</nowiki>]<nowiki>. The first unlocks curses, which are basically stronger prayers. The second unlocks better overloads and combination potions.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Note that quests are more rewarding the sooner you do them, due to how level XP scaling works</nowiki></p>
