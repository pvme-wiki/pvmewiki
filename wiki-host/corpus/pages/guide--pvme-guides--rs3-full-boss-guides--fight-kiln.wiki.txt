<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Fight_Kiln <nowiki>Fight Kiln</nowiki>]<nowiki> is a wave-based encounter which requires completion of </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/The_Elder_Kiln <nowiki>The Elder Kiln</nowiki>]<nowiki> quest and a one-time sacrifice of a fire cape. There are 37 waves which become increasingly difficult as the fight progresses.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Completing the fight rewards you with a cape for your most used style </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1153768065427853354.png?v=1 <nowiki>kilnmage</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/602582045553590274.png?v=1 <nowiki>KilnMelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1153772314215858247.png?v=1 <nowiki>kilnnecro</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/602582045541269567.png?v=1 <nowiki>KilnRanged</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Each cape can be used to craft its respective igneous variant </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1153772938252787793.png?v=1 <nowiki>igneouskalmej</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/902209626404192316.png?v=1 <nowiki>igneouskalket</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139200072438005851.png?v=1 <nowiki>igneouskalmor</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/902209626404171786.png?v=1 <nowiki>igneouskalxil</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ These are combined into the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/902209626479685734.png?v=1 <nowiki>igneouskalzuk</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="preset-suggestions"><ins><nowiki>Preset Suggestions</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://presets.pvme.io/?id=AToCog7o6tN7tyjnbfpN <nowiki>All styles</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki>
</nowiki>[https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/preset-images/o/images%2FAToCog7o6tN7tyjnbfpN.png?alt=media <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gear and inventory can get switched around based on what you have and how experienced you are</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• As long as you have a pickaxe in your toolbelt, you will be able to kill Dills using your Melee weapon</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Obsidian armour reduces damage taken in the Fight Kiln and saves inventory space if not bringing switches</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Tri-bridding is the best way to deal with the Fight Kiln. At the minimum, you want a 2H weapon of every style</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975012160759550042.png?v=1 <nowiki>tokkulzo</nowiki>]<nowiki> provides a 10% damage boost against the mobs but requires tokkul to upkeep</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Bring a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513201065877831680.png?v=1 <nowiki>rov</nowiki>]<nowiki> if the passive version is not unlocked</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="the-fight"><ins><nowiki>The Fight</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="waves-1-9"><nowiki>Waves 1-9</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>There will be mostly rangers with an increasing number of magers and meleers so use Melee and switch to kill the others</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Wave 2</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Invulnerability crystal spawn</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Wave 4</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Restoration crystal spawn</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Wave 5</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Dill spawn</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Wave 9</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: All rangers</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854004678657.png?v=1 <nowiki>zerk</nowiki>]<nowiki> on the last ranger to have it for Jad</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="wave-10"><nowiki>Wave 10</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/cibB2kx.jpg <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Jad</nowiki></strong><nowiki> spawns </nowiki><strong><nowiki>southwest</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ 1 ranger also spawns southeast</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="wave-11-19"><nowiki>Wave 11-19</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>There will mostly be magers with an increasing number of meleers and rangers so use Ranged and switch to kill the others</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Wave 14</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Invulnerability crystal spawn</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Wave 16</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Restoration crystal spawn</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Wave 19</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Dill spawn (switch to Melee for Dill)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Go northeast, kill the Dill then go southwest and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921597813137518.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswiftpf</nowiki>]<nowiki> killing all the magers, use remainder on Jad</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="wave-20"><nowiki>Wave 20</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Jad</nowiki></strong><nowiki> spawns </nowiki><strong><nowiki>southwest</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ 1 mager also spawns southeast</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="waves-21-29"><nowiki>Waves 21-29</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>There will mostly be meleers with an increasing number of magers and rangers so use Magic and switch to kill the others</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Wave 26</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Invulnerability crystal spawn</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Wave 28</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Restoration crystal spawn</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Wave 28</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: 6 Dills spawn with a mager (kill mager then swap to Melee)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Wave 29</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921595489488927.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshinepf</nowiki>]<nowiki> and kill meleers, use remainder on Jad</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Wave 33</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Use Ranged</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ 1 meleer spawns northwest, and one ranger southwest</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ 1 meleer spawns northwest, 1 ranger southwest, and 1 mager southeast</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="wave-37-har-aken"><nowiki>Wave 37 (Har-Aken)</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/n4B2Z2KLAw.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ Spawns tentacles that deal Magic or Ranged hits. Build/heal on these when he is not on the surface</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ He never spawns on northern side of the platform</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Spawns after ~72 seconds and is up for ~30 seconds</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921595489488927.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshinepf</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854004678657.png?v=1 <nowiki>zerk</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921597813137518.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswiftpf</nowiki>]<nowiki> as he surfaces</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Apply </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537349530551582720.png?v=1 <nowiki>Vuln</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/655341074235129858.png?v=1 <nowiki>vulnbomb</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Spam </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/651079281882955787.png?v=1 <nowiki>spiritualprayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> if </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/703581275453128714.png?v=1 <nowiki>ripperpouch</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/703581275155464203.png?v=1 <nowiki>ripperscroll</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="tips-and-tricks"><ins><nowiki>Tips and Tricks</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="general"><nowiki>General</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You can use restoration crystals in emergencies and to extend </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159085207555.png?v=1 <nowiki>onsl</nowiki>]<nowiki> on Har-Aken if needed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Make sure you have </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005552211847295076.png?v=1 <nowiki>signoflife</nowiki>]<nowiki> up just in case because the hits start to add up very quickly</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> for the majority of the fight and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmage</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488317046812.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectrange</nowiki>]<nowiki> as needed, don't forget </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• On wave 19 there are a lot of magers so using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki> can help</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Always use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmage</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488317046812.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectrange</nowiki>]<nowiki> when fighting Jads</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Invulnerability crystals do not protect you from Dill special attacks, instead use restoration crystals</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
