</nowiki><h3 id="introduction"><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This basic guide is designed to streamline players who are starting Vorago duos, and contains some information for Trio, such as the TL5 responsibilities. The strategies in this guide are safe and easy relative to Vorago. Note though, that Vorago is not an easy boss in general, and will likely take multiple attempts before you get a kill.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Each weekly rotation has its own guide in the Vorago basic collection. This snapshot does not track the current weekly rotation.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="misc-important-vorago-facts"><ins><nowiki>Misc Important Vorago Facts</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ BT refers to Bomb Tank, not Base Tank. Stands furthest away from Vorago to take bombs.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This is a trio+ role.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• They perform the climb in Phase 1, and take the 5th bleed when applicable during Phase 2 and Phase 4.</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ As long as the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/974504834394898522.png?v=1 <nowiki>turtling3</nowiki>]<nowiki> isn't on a weapon/shield, you can switch back immediately.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="phase-1"><ins><nowiki>Phase 1</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="for-both-players"><nowiki>For Both Players</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vorago's attack rotation</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/EOpDHbN.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Three types of auto attack at random: Melee swipe, magic Blue Bomb, or both at the same time</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Auto attack blue bombs home in on the furthest player from Vorago, deal AoE magic damage, and always hit.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Melee swipes deal AoE melee damage around Vorago and around the Base Tank. They can splash.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Vorago does not melee swipe if the player with aggro is not in melee distance.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Red Bomb deals 2000 damage, comes with a non-homing Blue Bomb, clears defensives and puts them on cooldown</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Thrown at the furthest player similar to Blue Bombs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For each player that is close to the bomb tank when the red bomb hits, an additional 1k damage occurs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Move at least 4 tiles away from where you were stood to avoid the non-homing blue.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The phase can only end when someone has successfully jumped on Vorago to release the piece of the Maul of Omens.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If Vorago's hp goes under about 13k before this, his HP will &quot;reset&quot; somewhere between 80-100k</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="base-tank"><nowiki>Base Tank</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Prioritise tagging Vorago ASAP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If Trio, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> as you tag.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Bring Vorago to the east of the red line in the image below. Once he is there use your main damaging abilities </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921119381463070.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921434633744455.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswift</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/yhVlG5p6no.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Expect a few blue bombs. Prayer flick and use defensives as necessary. </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> provides a big heal.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Once the BT has voked, stand directly south of the middle of Vorago and voke back at around 50k HP left.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="top-lure-the-bomb-tank-in-duo"><nowiki>Top Lure (The Bomb Tank in duo)</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Climb speed is paramount to survival</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Less damage is taken by you and your base on faster climbs.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use surge and dive as frequently as possible.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> Vorago from two north and two west of the jump spot and do not move until Vorago has finished moving.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• There is no leniency on what tile to stand on for this mechanic. If you mess up, the base must revoke vorago to the east and you must try again.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/gXlab4c.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ ‎ ‎Once Vorago stops moving, do a basic DPS rotation</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ ‎ ‎You must have at least one inventory space free for the Maul of Omens piece to be able to jump.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ ‎ ‎Jump at &lt;50k HP. Make sure to be high/full HP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Food cannot be eaten during climb animations, but bombs can continue to land and stack damage upon animation finish. Make sure to maintain high HP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Do not risk death trying to save food. It is expected to use some this phase.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><h2 id="phase-2"><ins><nowiki>Phase 2</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="for-both-players_1"><nowiki>For Both Players</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vorago's attack rotation:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/pPNE2a2.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Phase 2 is largely time gated. Focus on mechanics.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• One gravity orb spawns every reflect. Two are needed to progress. Missing orbs will result in additional cycles.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use defensives to avoid food usage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ More advanced Vorago guides will minimise Vorago's movement on this phase, but while learning the focus should be on surviving and dealing with mechanics properly.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vorago's Bleeds break defensives (i.e. take them off. So if you have resonance / cade up, it will remove them + you will receive damage.)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Appears as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879376023572.png?v=1 <nowiki>dismember</nowiki>]<nowiki> on debuff bar</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• First bleed of the phase has no animation; it occurs during Vorago's spawning animation.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Reflect (Link)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>DO NOT ATTACK ON REFLECT</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Vorago is immune to damage during this time</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Damage dealt to Vorago instead goes to someone on the team</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Spam click Vorago during Bring Him Down (BHD), then deal damage to free the maul piece.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• BHD damage threshold is a collective 50k.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use high damaging thresholds, but refrain from using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921119381463070.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921434633744455.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> as you will want them for next phase.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="phase-4"><ins><nowiki>Phase 4</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="for-both-players_3"><nowiki>For Both Players</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vorago's attack rotation:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/hVrOm2k.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Phase 4 is time gated. Focus on mechanics.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 3 Waterfalls are required to progress the phase. Lower Vorago's HP to under 30k before the last Waterfall.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Waterfall</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Spawns in one of the four corners. Run behind it quickly, or take up to 10k damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Some tiles look safe but are not. If unsure, run all the way into the corner.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Clears active defensives and puts them on cooldown.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Clone skip</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Massive time save if done both times</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• See role sections below for each player's responsibilities</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><ins><nowiki>No unique special attack this week. It is replaced by a Red Bomb.</nowiki></ins></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="base-tank_3"><nowiki>Base Tank</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Clone skip</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Base Tank walks Vorago to the wall</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Turn run off, spam click Vorago + surge out of the Waterfall, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki>, walk under Vorago, click near the wall in the direction that Vorago walks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The BT may be out of bomb range, which means any potential bombs go to the Base Tank instead.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Eat to high HP in the Waterfall, prayer flick </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmage</nowiki>]<nowiki> for Blue Bombs, and use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> as needed.</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>

</nowiki><h2 id="phase-5"><ins><nowiki>Phase 5</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="for-both-players_4"><nowiki>For Both Players</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vorago's attack rotation:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/gg1VugJ.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Reverse tug-of-war mechanic</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 20k damage against Vorago pushes him east 1 square</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Each tick of damage received from Vorago he pushes west 1 square</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Any damage mitigated from </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/867678153883516958.png?v=1 <nowiki>cadeturtling</nowiki>]<nowiki> negates any pushback</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Push Vorago all the way to the east, move melee distance, and click the Maul of Omens</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ PB</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Vorago turns all players into explosive Vitali, with a timer above their head.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When timer hits 0, a purple bomb explodes on the player. This is AoE, so avoid standing near your partner.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Vorago will do 5 auto attacks before reflect.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Standing on opposite walls largely allows you to ignore this special.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Base Tank hugs the South wall, while Bomb Tank hugs the North wall. Players should only move away from their respective walls to clear Vorago's bleeds.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Bleeds</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 3 bleeds with that are randomly assigned</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Vorago does not throw blue bombs during bleeds.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Taking damage from bleeds results in Vorago pushing further west. Prioritise clearing them over DPS.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Bleeds are cleared by distance from the tile the bleed was received, not distance from Vorago in this phase.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Move melee distance + go to the center of the arena. That way you can properly </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258832052231.png?v=1 <nowiki>escape</nowiki>]<nowiki> to remove bleeds</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The safest way to clear bleeds in case you get all 3 is to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258832052231.png?v=1 <nowiki>escape</nowiki>]<nowiki> ‎ ‎the first bleed and then run back melee distance, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> second bleed. </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258832052231.png?v=1 <nowiki>escape</nowiki>]<nowiki> the third bleed.</nowiki></p>
