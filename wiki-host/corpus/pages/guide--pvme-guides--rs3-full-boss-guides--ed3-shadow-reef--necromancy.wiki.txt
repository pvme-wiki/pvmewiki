<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="introduction"><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Rotations are written for each of the three ED3 bosses and the monsters in between. The rotations can be adapted as required.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Boss rotations assume you have the </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Invigorating 4</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073087859949570.png?v=1 <nowiki>invig4</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Impatient 4</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073088204013640.png?v=1 <nowiki>imp4</nowiki>]<nowiki> perks on your armour.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ It's recommended to set your anti-spam delay for your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> to 1 via the Lectern at Anachronia.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You can Surge </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Bladed Dive </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854281764884.png?v=1 <nowiki>bd</nowiki>]<nowiki> a tick before each gate unlocks to save a tick on every entry.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ It is assumed that Darkness </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809209782698024.png?v=1 <nowiki>darkness</nowiki>]<nowiki> is used and reapplied where required to mitigate the damage taken.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ It is assumed that </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1166094935066423348.png?v=1 <nowiki>conjurearmy</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809128136388819.png?v=1 <nowiki>lifetransfer</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434409913634816.png?v=1 <nowiki>commandghost</nowiki>]<nowiki> is used to keep conjures alive as required depending on the speed of your clear (as such, this isn't always specified within the rotations).</nowiki></p>

Genocidal </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689503091539705870.png?v=1 <nowiki>genocidal</nowiki>]<nowiki> can also provide a damage buff while on an undead task, but this isn't recommended.</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Alternatively, use Shadow's Grace </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/895999229737185280.png?v=1 <nowiki>shadowsgrace</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
