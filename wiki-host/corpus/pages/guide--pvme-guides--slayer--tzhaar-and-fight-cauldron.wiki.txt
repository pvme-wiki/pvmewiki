</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>TzHaar creatures are killed for the Ring of Stone drop, which is their rarest item for the Karamja slayer log. This method utilises legacy combat mode. The method is basically just running around the TzHaar City and killing every TzHaar monster you see. If you happen to get the Ring of Stone early, the Fight Cauldron minigame is your best way of obtaining the rest of the items you're missing.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="overview-of-the-fight-cauldron"><ins><nowiki>Overview of the Fight Cauldron</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Fight Cauldron is a safe minigame unlocked after the Grandmaster quest </nowiki><strong><ins><nowiki>The Brink of Extinction</nowiki></ins></strong><nowiki>. Within the Fight Cauldron minigame, players battle with unlimited waves of TokHaar monsters who wish to test their might, and that of their opponent.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You will need a total of 228 obsidian shards to make every piece of obsidian equipment. On average, this will take 66 kills in the Cauldron.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Full Obsidian armour is required for the TokHaar-Mok achievement.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Fight Cauldron is also the fastest way to obtain weapon/armour drops for the Karamja slayer log.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="mechanics-to-watch-for"><ins><nowiki>Mechanics to watch for</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Hot floor sections</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Keep an eye on the floor. Standing on a hot section of the floor will deal damage equal to 20% of your life points every game tick, which can kill you quickly.</nowiki></p>
