</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>There are three species of gemstone dragons - Dragonstone dragons, Onyx dragons, and Hydrix dragons.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ To kill them </nowiki><strong><nowiki>off-task</nowiki></strong><nowiki> in gemstone mines, you need to buy KC through these following uncut gems:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643507025602543638.png?v=1 <nowiki>dstone</nowiki>]<nowiki> - Allows 2 kills each</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643411078763511828.png?v=1 <nowiki>uncutonyx</nowiki>]<nowiki> - Allows 400 kills each</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1010485542456135730.png?v=1 <nowiki>uncuthydrix</nowiki>]<nowiki> - Allows 4000 kills each</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If killing them in the wilderness </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1023150757148622921.png?v=1 <nowiki>mandrith</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Dragonkin Laboratory </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583430542326759435.png?v=1 <nowiki>bsd</nowiki>]<nowiki>, payment is not needed.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Due to their low profitability but relatively high </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> EXP rates, they </nowiki><strong><nowiki>should be only be killed on-task</nowiki></strong><nowiki> for slayer EXP and heavily improved rare drop rates.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• All presets hence assume </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285340460449792.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayerhelm</nowiki>]<nowiki> buffs are active on the Anachronia helmet stand.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For active methods, ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> is preferred with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641669268722810881.png?v=1 <nowiki>mechchin</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/971025697042755584.png?v=1 <nowiki>jasdragonbanearrow</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For AFK methods </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090453135382.png?v=1 <nowiki>animatedead</nowiki>]<nowiki> is better, although </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> methods are still available.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Besides autos and standard dragonbreath attacks, each species of gemstone dragon has a special attack which fires out a set of </nowiki><strong><nowiki>gemstone spikes</nowiki></strong><nowiki>, creating a 3x3 area centred around the player after a delay, dealing high </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage. Additionally, gemstone spikes have the following effects:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Dragonstone Dragon: Halves the duration of antifires and super antifires.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This </nowiki><strong><nowiki>does NOT work only</nowiki></strong><nowiki> on extended super antifires.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Onyx Dragon: Heals the dragon by up to 5000 LP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Hydrix Dragon: Drains adrenaline to 0 and halves adrenaline gain for 15 seconds.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="desirable-drops"><nowiki>Desirable Drops</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Besides the Draconic Visage (1/10000) which most dragons drop, different dragons drop the different pieces of Gemstone Armour (T80 hybrid, unaugmentable) required for the Karamja slayer log:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Dragonstone Dragon: Gemstone Gloves and Boots</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Onyx Dragon: Gemstone Helmet and Greaves</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Hydrix Dragon: Gemstone Hauberk</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ All Gemstone dragons have a static droprate for Armour pieces at a rate of 1/2500 off-task and 1/1000 on-task.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For Dragonstone and Onyx Dragons this is then 50% chance to be either of the pieces they can drop.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><nowiki>Location and How to Get There</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gemstone dragons are located below the Shilo Village Mines. A ladder leads to the cavern beside Kelhar, which requires Karamja Gloves 3 or above to access.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The teleport option on Karamja Gloves 3 or higher is the fastest way to access the cavern.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ They are also found in the Wilderness Dungeon just south of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1023150757148622921.png?v=1 <nowiki>mandrith</nowiki>]<nowiki>. The quickest way to get there is by teleporting to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1023150757148622921.png?v=1 <nowiki>mandrith</nowiki>]<nowiki> using the Slayer Cape </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975284344123953164.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayercape</nowiki>]<nowiki>, walk south and down the ladder.</nowiki></p>
