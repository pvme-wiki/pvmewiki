</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>

</nowiki><h3 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get There</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1027212539441975296.png?v=1 <nowiki>celestialdragon</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be found in the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Dragontooth_Island_resource_dungeon <nowiki>Dragontooth Island resource dungeon</nowiki>]<nowiki> after the question </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/One_of_a_Kind <nowiki>One of a Kind</nowiki>]<nowiki> is complete.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>You can get there by:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Option 0 → 3 on the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Dungeoneering_cape <nowiki>Dungeoneering cape</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/976732922545586246.png?v=1 <nowiki>dgcape</nowiki>]<nowiki> (option 0 → 3).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Using a </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Max_guild_portal <nowiki>Max guild portal</nowiki>]<nowiki> tuned to the Radiant Wisp colony.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Teleport to Dragontooth Island using the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Big_Book_o%27_Piracy <nowiki>Big Book o' Piracy</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You can also go to Port Phasmatys (Ectophial teleport or charter a boat) and speak with the Ghost captain while wearing a ghostspeak amulet.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="method-afk-ranged"><ins><nowiki>Method: AFK Ranged</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]</h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Place your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869283142350041098.png?v=1 <nowiki>cannon</nowiki>]<nowiki> in the middle of the room and ensure </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1084799717726228530.png?v=1 <nowiki>autoretaliate</nowiki>]<nowiki> is active.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>With this </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> method trips are only limited by your supplies (e.g. </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289831800882.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovl</nowiki>]<nowiki> and cannonballs).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583429936665198592.png?v=1 <nowiki>anguish</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159018098713.png?v=1 <nowiki>desolation</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Sustain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975765831248130079.png?v=1 <nowiki>demonhornnecklace</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975284344526602240.png?v=1 <nowiki>bonecrusher</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289831800882.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovl</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/864492695997513748.png?v=1 <nowiki>superantifire</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689525476158472288.png?v=1 <nowiki>weppoison</nowiki>]</p>
