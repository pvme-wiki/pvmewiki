<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Revenants <nowiki>Revenants</nowiki>]<nowiki> are a popular type of Wilderness monster which may be found in the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Forinthry_Dungeon <nowiki>Forinthry Dungeon</nowiki>]<nowiki>. They're mainly killed for the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Boss_collection_log#Chaos_Elemental <nowiki>Chaos Elemental collection log</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_collection_log#Wilderness <nowiki>Wilderness collection log</nowiki>]<nowiki>, and the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Revenant_Spirit <nowiki>Revenant Spirit</nowiki>]<nowiki> which is used to unlock the Revenant pet as well as its skins; this drop gets increasingly rarer the more skins you have.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After the Wilderness update killing revenants became substantially safer, as players can now opt-out of PvP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Using a </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Forinthry_bracelet#Effects <nowiki>Forinthry bracelet</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1023152061350678549.png?v=1 <nowiki>forinthrybracelet</nowiki>]<nowiki> will make revenants </nowiki><strong><nowiki>NOT</nowiki></strong><nowiki> be aggressive.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This effect will persist for an hour and only count down while in the Forinthry Dungeon.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Even if it was used years ago, the effect may still be active.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Revenants are located in the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Forinthry_Dungeon <nowiki>Forinthry Dungeon</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The quickest way to get there is via the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1009419838764109834.png?v=1 <nowiki>wildernesssword</nowiki>]<nowiki> Forinthry Dungeon teleport.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Alternatively you may use the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1032552297231822878.png?v=1 <nowiki>wildernesslode</nowiki>]<nowiki> and run west to the level 17 Wilderness entrance.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="method-active-magic"><ins><nowiki>Method: Active Magic</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The active Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> method consists of killing revenants using </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Legacy_Mode#Combat <nowiki>Legacy Combat Mode</nowiki>]<nowiki> one at a time following specific routes, this can be replicated using Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> with an augmented </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Revenant_bane_2h_sword <nowiki>Revenant bane 2h sword</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/859483155210567741.png?v=1 <nowiki>bane2h</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The knight and dragon are the fastest spawning revenants; use routes that prioritise killing them as soon as they respawn, as they have the highest drop rates for uniques.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/hAJb1MB50g.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869283142350041098.png?v=1 <nowiki>cannon</nowiki>]<nowiki> is positioned to kill the southern revenant goblins and imp, occasionally being able to kill the pyrefiend, and lowering the dark beast and demon such that they can be consistently one-shot.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/mjtXJs8Zua.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Red</nowiki></strong><nowiki> and </nowiki><strong><nowiki>green</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Main routes to take when killing revenants between knight and dragon spawns.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When going from knight to dragon, kill 3-5 revenants. When going from dragon to knight, kill 2-3 revenants.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Blue</nowiki></strong><nowiki> and </nowiki><strong><nowiki>yellow</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Potential detour routes for red and green routes respectively depending on revenant movement.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure Legacy Combat Mode is active.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/648976643687317532.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovlsalve</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689525476158472288.png?v=1 <nowiki>weppoison</nowiki>]<nowiki> are active at all times.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Upkeep </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1494069332584693810.png?v=1 <nowiki>penanceaspect</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535616247807868938.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloodblitz</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The healing from the spell alone should negate the need for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583429936958930964.png?v=1 <nowiki>torment</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158468907008.png?v=1 <nowiki>affliction</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1351696446646390824.png?v=1 <nowiki>crumbleundead</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1351696446646390824.png?v=1 <nowiki>crumbleundead</nowiki>]<nowiki> Crumble Undead with Soul Split </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki>, Holy Scarab </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/929773040273862656.png?v=1 <nowiki>holyscarab</nowiki>]<nowiki> and any overload with prayer restore to restore prayer</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="method-low-effort-magic"><ins><nowiki>Method: Low Effort Magic</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The low effort Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> method consists of killing revenants using </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Legacy_Mode#Combat <nowiki>Legacy Combat Mode</nowiki>]<nowiki> to AFK until they are no longer aggressive. When this happens you will follow a specific route killing revenants along the way in order to reset their aggro.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/LlbDHRTBgQ.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869283142350041098.png?v=1 <nowiki>cannon</nowiki>]<nowiki> will solo kill the dragon.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You AFK kill the dark beast, ork, demon, cyclops, pyrefiend, and 2 goblins.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/5K8rvWazPb.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Blue</nowiki></strong><nowiki> → </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Green</nowiki></strong><nowiki> → </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Yellow</nowiki></strong><nowiki> → </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Blue (back to start)</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Every ~10 minutes follow the route shown above: take the outer loop to the dragon → knight → dragon → start.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Make sure to reach the marked northwesternmost tile in order to properly reset aggro.</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
