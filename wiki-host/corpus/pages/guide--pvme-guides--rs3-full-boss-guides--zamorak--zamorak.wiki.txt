</nowiki><h2 id="fight-overview"><ins><nowiki>Fight Overview</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Zamorak has 6 phases that are progressed by depleting his health bar and charging the 6 edicts, or pads, located around the arena. During each phase he will cycle through 3 health bar colours, each with their own effects that dictate the progression of the fight:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/XvTHsFcxSC.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Grey HP:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> The health bar Zamorak starts with and regains between each phase</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Zamorak will have accelerated 4-tick attack speed and deal 50% more damage, but </nowiki><strong><nowiki>won't use special attacks</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Cannot be fully depleted through damage and will constantly regenerate itself at an increasing rate</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Starts at a soft cap that can regenerate itself up to a hard cap of double the starting value</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ The soft cap will increase with uninterrupted healing at the current cap</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ A healing tick can only restore </nowiki><ins><nowiki>up to</nowiki></ins><nowiki> the cap if </nowiki><strong><nowiki>current grey HP</nowiki></strong><nowiki> is lower than it</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Damage is calculated before healing on the same tick, even a tiny amount of damage can prevent the cap from increasing</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ DoT effects like </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063107908702482.png?v=1 <nowiki>combust</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478062910113714297.png?v=1 <nowiki>dismember</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159433682403201044.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloat</nowiki>]<nowiki> are very effective at preventing the soft cap from increasing</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• At 900% enrage, the regeneration increase will </nowiki><strong><nowiki>carry over between grey HP phases</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Upon activating a pad, all remaining </nowiki><strong><nowiki>grey HP</nowiki></strong><nowiki> will be converted into </nowiki><strong><nowiki>red HP</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• An </nowiki><strong><nowiki>increased grey HP soft cap</nowiki></strong><nowiki>, or hard cap, never carries over between </nowiki><strong><nowiki>grey HP</nowiki></strong><nowiki> phases and always resets to the initial soft cap</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/TZEQHE85wb.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Red HP:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> The health bar Zamorak gains upon the player activating a pad</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Zamorak will have accelerated 4-tick attack speed and deal 50% more damage, and </nowiki><strong><nowiki>will do his next special attack every 11 auto attacks</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ The </nowiki><strong><nowiki>first mechanic in red HP</nowiki></strong><nowiki> will carry over attacks from the previous </nowiki><strong><nowiki>green HP</nowiki></strong><nowiki> phase, see the </nowiki><ins><strong><nowiki>Special Attack Order</nowiki></strong></ins><nowiki> section for more information</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Must be depleted by dealing damage and will be followed by </nowiki><strong><nowiki>green HP</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/GRgpCe9WAg.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Green HP:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Zamorak's </nowiki><strong><nowiki>main health bar</nowiki></strong><nowiki>, and is split into 6 sections representing each phase</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Zamorak will have 5-tick attack rate and deal normal damage, and </nowiki><strong><nowiki>will do his next special attack every 5 auto attacks</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ The </nowiki><strong><nowiki>first mechanic in green HP</nowiki></strong><nowiki> on each phase will happen after </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1 auto attack</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Upon depletion of each section, Zamorak will </nowiki><strong><nowiki>regain Grey HP and start the </nowiki><em><nowiki>Channelers</nowiki></em><nowiki> mechanic</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Overall, the progression of the first 6 phases can be summarised as:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Grey HP &gt; charge pad &gt; deplete red HP &gt; deplete green HP section &gt; </nowiki><em><nowiki>Channelers</nowiki></em><nowiki> + grey HP &gt; repeat</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Zamorak's </nowiki><strong><nowiki>green HP</nowiki></strong><nowiki> scales between 300,000 and 1,600,000 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>constitution</nowiki>]<nowiki> per player, capping at 1740% enrage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Phases 1-5 each take up 16% of the total </nowiki><strong><nowiki>green HP</nowiki></strong><nowiki> bar, while phase 6 takes up 20%</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At 100%+ enrage, Zamorak will gain an additional 7th phase that will be explained in its own section</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="special-attacks"><ins><nowiki>Special Attacks</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Channelers</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Automatically triggered upon phasing Zamorak from </nowiki><strong><nowiki>green HP</nowiki></strong><nowiki> into </nowiki><strong><nowiki>grey HP</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• A message will appear saying </nowiki><strong><code><nowiki>Zamorak begins to draw power and energy from something within Infernus.</nowiki></code></strong><nowiki> , this will spawn a Chaos Witch Channeler in Infernus</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Players must enter Infernus with the extra action button </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1073955377537876089.png?v=1 <nowiki>realmmovement</nowiki>]<nowiki> and defeat the witch before being able to charge the next pad</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The witch has 33,000 health </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>constitution</nowiki>]<nowiki> + 132 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>constitution</nowiki>]<nowiki> for every 10 enrage increase</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• It is advised to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> before </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> towards the witch since it is able to stun</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• At 500%, Chaos Witch Channeler spawns with 50% adrenaline and their first ability is </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• At 1000%+, Chaos Witch Channeler spawns with 100% adrenaline and their first ability is </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809664262179.png?v=1 <nowiki>omni</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Flames of Zamorak</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Zamorak will yell </nowiki><strong><code><nowiki>This world will burn.</nowiki></code></strong><nowiki> and slam into the ground, dealing 2 melee hits and spawning a line of black smoke between himself and a player</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The melee slam deals more damage the closer the player is to Zamorak, and more smoke will be spawned the further the player is</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Picking up the smoke by walking over it will start a timer over the player that deals typeless damage at the end</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ The damage increases and gains additional </nowiki><strong><nowiki>defence piercing</nowiki></strong><nowiki> the more smoke that is picked up</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ If the smoke is left on the ground, Zamorak will gain stacks reducing his damage taken</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><h2 id="hexes-and-edicts"><ins><nowiki>Hexes and Edicts</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Charge Pads</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The arena has 6 pads that you can stand on to charge.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When it is fully charged, Zamorak will stop attacking for a while and your adrenaline bar will be refilled. After this, you will gain an </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Edict</nowiki></strong><nowiki> while Zamorak gains a </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Hex</nowiki></strong><nowiki> depending on the pad charged.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You will become unable to charge pads during the </nowiki><em><nowiki>Channelers</nowiki></em><nowiki> mechanic. To continue charging the next pad, you must enter Infernus </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1073955377537876089.png?v=1 <nowiki>realmmovement</nowiki>]<nowiki> and kill the witch present.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Hexes and Edicts</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Every time a pad is charged, all players and Zamorak will gain a stack of currently active stacking edicts and hexes, respectively</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This makes it such that earlier pad effects become stronger as the fight progresses</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Note that pads 3 and 5 don't have stacking effects</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ </nowiki><em><nowiki>E.g. if you activate pads 2 → 4 → 1 → 3 → 6 → 5, once 5 is activated rune 2 will have 6 stacks, rune 4 will have 5 stacks, rune 1 will have 4 stacks, and rune 6 will have 2 stacks</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/pAOYodBn8H.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
 ‎‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>During phase 7, all stacking edicts and hexes have their stack amounts reversed</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ </nowiki><em><nowiki>E.g. Using the pad order in the above example, phase 7 will act as if the pad order were 5 → 6 → 3 → 1 → 4 → 2</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/tOouJ4IzhG.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ A calculator has been made to gauge the effects of each activated Edict &amp; Hex: </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Calculator:Combat/Rune_pads <nowiki>https://runescape.wiki/w/Calculator:Combat/Rune_pads</nowiki>]</p>

</nowiki><h2 id="phase-7"><ins><nowiki>Phase 7</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/WavFFAD1rN.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
At 100% enrage, phase 7 is introduced. Zamorak will enter Infernus and force the player in along with them.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When the phase starts:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The pad order from phases 1-6 is reversed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Zamorak starts immune to damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• A Chaos Demon will spawn which should be killed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Zamorak sends 2 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> hits to all players</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="rune-calls"><nowiki>Rune Calls</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ An amount of runes (2 in solo, otherwise equal to team size in group) will appear before Zamorak and stay there temporarily, </nowiki><strong><nowiki>the order in which they appear is the order they must be defeated in</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At the same time Zamorak will begin charging a </nowiki><strong><nowiki>new red HP bar, referred to as the chaos bar</nowiki></strong><nowiki>, that determines the damage of upcoming mechanics</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994206048334393385.png?v=1 <nowiki>smiterune</nowiki>]<nowiki> may be shown twice</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Killing a rune will delay Zamorak from charging for 9 ticks (4 ticks at 1600%+ enrage)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Killing a rune out of order will deal heavy damage to all players</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Runes uber heal after they are defeated, be careful not to kill it twice.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The rate of charge increases with enrage and caps at 8850%</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After the last rune is defeated Zamorak stops charging, becomes damageable, and uses in this order:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• [1] A single moderate </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• [2] A large chaos damage hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Soft typeless damage that is </nowiki><strong><nowiki>equivalent to the chaos bar</nowiki></strong><nowiki> that charges while killing runes</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Make sure your team is spread out as it does damage in a small area around each player</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Has a base </nowiki><strong><nowiki>defence piercing</nowiki></strong><nowiki> of 50%</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• [3] 4 additional small chaos damage hits</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Deals damage based on a portion of the chaos bar and the amount of time it was allowed to charge for</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Does not have base defence piercing but is </nowiki><strong><nowiki>affected by Twinshot</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If Zamorak is not defeated before becoming immune on the 4th small chaos hit:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• [4] Another Chaos Demon spawns</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• [5] Another moderate </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• [6] Another large chaos damage hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• [7] Runes are called</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Resets to [1] once last rune is defeated</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Zamorak will have 100,000 to 250,000 health </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>constitution</nowiki>]<nowiki> per player during phase 7, capping at 1000% enrage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The Chaos Demon is the same as during the </nowiki><em><nowiki>Infernal Tomb</nowiki></em><nowiki> mechanic and will have 18,750 to 70,000 health </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>constitution</nowiki>]<nowiki>, capping at 1470% enrage</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><code><nowiki>= floor(Iteration * Chaos_bar/100)</nowiki></code><nowiki> ‎where </nowiki><code><nowiki>Iteration</nowiki></code><nowiki> ‎refers to how many times the Charge value has increased (this gets delayed when killing a rune)</nowiki></p>

    • Killing the first rune </nowiki><strong><em><nowiki>immediately</nowiki></em></strong><nowiki> is imperative as Zamorak's red bar stops charging for 10 ticks (5 ticks at 1600+ enrage) following each rune death. The red bar directly correlates to the damage you receive.

</nowiki><h2 id="enrage-based-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Enrage-Based Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Zamorak's damage and HP scales with enrage, and additional mechanics are introduced at specific enrage thresholds:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>100%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Phase 7 is introduced</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>500%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> During </nowiki><em><nowiki>Infernal Tomb</nowiki></em><nowiki>, all players must be on their rune at the same time to exit Infernus</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>500%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> During </nowiki><em><nowiki>Channelers</nowiki></em><nowiki>, Chaos Witch Channeler spawns with 50% adrenaline and the first ability is Asphyxiate </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>750%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> During </nowiki><em><nowiki>Infernal Tomb</nowiki></em><nowiki>, demons will move faster towards the portal</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>900%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> During </nowiki><em><nowiki>Channelers</nowiki></em><nowiki>, </nowiki><strong><nowiki>grey HP</nowiki></strong><nowiki> healing ramp-up no longer resets between phases</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1000%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> During </nowiki><em><nowiki>Channelers</nowiki></em><nowiki>, Chaos Witch Channeler spawns with 100% adrenaline and their first ability is Omnipower </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809664262179.png?v=1 <nowiki>omni</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1000%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> On phase 7, red bar charge is no longer reset between cycle(s)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1000%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> On phase 7, Zamorak's life points cap at 250,000 per player</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1470%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> The life points of chaos demons cap at 70,000 while on Infernus</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1500%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> During the Adrenaline Cage attack, the targeted player will have their Devotion </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki> deactivated</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1600%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Zamorak's stagger after killing a rune on Phase 7 reduced from 10 to 5 ticks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1740%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> For phases 1-6, Zamorak's starting life points cap at 1,600,000 per player</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>3500%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> On phase 7, the life points the runes revive with are capped at 60,000</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="helpful-tips"><ins><nowiki>Helpful Tips</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The </nowiki><strong><nowiki>grey HP</nowiki></strong><nowiki> soft cap can't increase on the ticks Zamorak takes damage, meaning DoT abilities such as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063107908702482.png?v=1 <nowiki>combust</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478062910113714297.png?v=1 <nowiki>dismember</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159433682403201044.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloat</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be used before entering Infernus </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1073955377537876089.png?v=1 <nowiki>realmmovement</nowiki>]<nowiki> to prevent meaningful amounts of healing</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ As Infernal Tomb starts, you can </nowiki><ins><nowiki>on tick</nowiki></ins><nowiki> stun Zamorak by using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> as he assigns runes and using a stun as your character is lifted. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/URj54bk2bF.mp4 <nowiki>Example clip here.</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/URj54bk2bF.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This will greatly reduce the damage dealt to players</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You can double stun by stalling one and releasing it with another, this counts as two stuns and lets you cover one missed stun</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You can stack </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/536256663641128971.png?v=1 <nowiki>stormshards</nowiki>]<nowiki> to use with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/536256673904328704.png?v=1 <nowiki>Shatter</nowiki>]<nowiki> during phase 7</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At high enrages the base can use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/567727986409734145.png?v=1 <nowiki>enfeeble</nowiki>]<nowiki> to reduce the damage dealt by autos</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809209782698024.png?v=1 <nowiki>darkness</nowiki>]<nowiki> also reduces a lot of damage since Zamorak uses a lot of autos</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ring of Death </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159462825984.png?v=1 <nowiki>rod</nowiki>]<nowiki> effect clears upon entering phase 7</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Many debuffs such as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1404885709034229922.png?v=1 <nowiki>flameboundrival</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090641879050.png?v=1 <nowiki>smokecloud</nowiki>]<nowiki> carry over into phase 7</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You may </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/543478434509357098.png?v=1 <nowiki>Cept</nowiki>]<nowiki> other players and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258538582017.png?v=1 <nowiki>immort</nowiki>]<nowiki> if multiple players will take a fatal attack at the same time</nowiki></p>
