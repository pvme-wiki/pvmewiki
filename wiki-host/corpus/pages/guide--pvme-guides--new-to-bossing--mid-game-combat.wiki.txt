<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>This guide is intended as a guide for players looking to progress towards the tougher challenges RS3 PvM offers.</nowiki></strong><nowiki> It covers skill goals to set on your PvM journey, how to improve your damage and useful unlocks for your PvM experience.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="preparation"><ins><nowiki>Preparation</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Before you begin, it can be helpful to get a few things out of the way:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Set up a good interface - see </nowiki>[[PvME/Getting started/Interface Guide|<nowiki>Interface Guide</nowiki>]]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Learn about the different combat modes (Revolution and Full Manual) - </nowiki>[[PvME/Learning to boss/Revo To Full Manual|<nowiki>Revo To Full Manual</nowiki>]]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Have a peek at the </nowiki>[[PvME/Learning to boss/Bossing Path|<nowiki>Bossing Path</nowiki>]]<nowiki> that you can work on as you learn PvM.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="initial-goals"><ins><nowiki>Initial Goals</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Having an idea of some of the things you want to aim for initially can help with planning, which we cover here. </nowiki><strong><nowiki>None of these things are strictly necessary, but are recommended to make your bossing learning curves an easier and more enjoyable experience.</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="bossing"><ins><nowiki>Bossing</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If you are particularly interested in bossing, </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Soul_Reaper <nowiki>reaper tasks</nowiki>]<nowiki> are a daily activity where Death asks you to kill a certain boss. This is a good way to experience different bosses.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You can also check out </nowiki>[[PvME/Learning to boss/Bossing Path|<nowiki>Bossing Path</nowiki>]]<nowiki> to set some bossing goals.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Basic guides for most bosses in Runescape can be found in </nowiki>[https://pvme.io/guides/?category=Boss%20fundamentals <nowiki>Boss fundamentals</nowiki>]<nowiki>. These guides have beginner preset recommendations, an overview of the boss's mechanics, and suggestions for how to deal with them.</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="improving-damage-output"><ins><nowiki>Improving Damage Output</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>In RuneScape, the best defence is a great offence. Improving your damage output will significantly increase your chances of success at whichever boss or slayer task you're trying to accomplish. There are little things you can do to optimise once you reach that stage, but initially there are three core ways to increase your damage output:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Upgrading your gear setup</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Assuming a starting point of T70 weapons and T70 armour, </nowiki>[https://pvme.io/gear/ <nowiki>Upgrade guides</nowiki>]<nowiki> will help you upgrade your gear progressively for each combat style.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Improving your DPS rotation</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For strong revolution bars with various degrees of ability unlocks, check out </nowiki>[[PvME/Combat reference/Revolution Bars|<nowiki>Revolution Bars</nowiki>]]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For learning to switch to full manual gameplay, check out </nowiki>[[PvME/Learning to boss/Revo To Full Manual|<nowiki>Revo To Full Manual</nowiki>]]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For learning how to start improving your DPM, check out </nowiki>[https://pvme.io/guides/?category=Damage%20%26%20rotations <nowiki>Damage and rotation guides</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For help and advice, the Helpers and Super Helpers can answer your questions in </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/656898197561802760 <nowiki>PvM help (external Discord)</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Useful permanent unlocks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Permanent unlocks have a multitude of benefits ranging from adrenaline refunds on abilities to enchantments on weapons/gear. For a full list of recommendations, check out </nowiki>[[PvME/Gear upgrades/Permanent Unlocks|<nowiki>Permanent Unlocks</nowiki>]]</p>
