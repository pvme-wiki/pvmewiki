</nowiki><p><nowiki>Notes:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Loading into Fort Forinthry is very intensive. The following reduces load times</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Reducing draw distance, and turning off textures.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When you use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> towards Zemouregal, hover your mouse over the northwest part of the minimap, so you can quickly </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki>. It is a bit difficult to click on Zemouregal when quickly using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> though, so keep that in mind. (needs investigation)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ballista timing assumes you perfectly </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> → (tc) + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki>. If you are late in any way, the timings will be different, so exercise caution.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1448070604937891921.png?v=1 <nowiki>amhej</nowiki>]<nowiki> actually does matter for a lot of damage. It's imperative to treat </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1448697873603498045.png?v=1 <nowiki>varanussmercy</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> as a switch, not </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1448070604937891921.png?v=1 <nowiki>amhej</nowiki>]<nowiki> as a switch.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1448697873603498045.png?v=1 <nowiki>varanussmercy</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/779048041088024606.png?v=1 <nowiki>dragonclaw</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be switched</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/603979368850653216.png?v=1 <nowiki>dba</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki>, it's recommended to spec immediately if Vorkath is around 860k the moment you use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/536257336130404372.png?v=1 <nowiki>smoketendrils</nowiki>]<nowiki>. This allows you to build up more adrenaline immediately after DBA spec while ensuring a perfect P2.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Keep </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1179716261681307728.png?v=1 <nowiki>zemouregalsnexus</nowiki>]<nowiki> on until you use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334377648130.png?v=1 <nowiki>divert</nowiki>]<nowiki>, then swap to the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1449363594495135776.png?v=1 <nowiki>nodonspikeharness</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If you end up using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879334080517.png?v=1 <nowiki>overpower</nowiki>]<nowiki> when Zemouregal has around 70k LP left, move </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/796989663051120640.png?v=1 <nowiki>gmaul</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> up and use it with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879334080517.png?v=1 <nowiki>overpower</nowiki>]<nowiki> to instantly KO Zemouregal.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Replace </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/756236265472524418.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofacceleration</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]<nowiki> for safer kills, and use it after </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879250456578.png?v=1 <nowiki>gbarge</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Tectonic works almost just as well, but will be noticeably slower by a couple seconds.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Sometimes can </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/883134308146098227.png?v=1 <nowiki>lengmh</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/796989663051120640.png?v=1 <nowiki>gmaul</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> if 3+ stacks and ~70k health.</nowiki></p>
