<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="general-information"><ins><nowiki>General Information</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vindicta &amp; Gorvek is a multi-phase fight in the Heart of Gielinor.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Normal Mode, Vindicta starts at 200,000 LP and mounts Gorvek at 100,000 LP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode, Vindicta starts at 300,000 LP and mounts Gorvek at 150,000 LP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After Vindicta is defeated in Hard Mode, Gorvek continues the fight alone with 300,000 LP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Level 80 Attack </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250472476758.png?v=1 <nowiki>attack</nowiki>]<nowiki> is required to create an instance, which you can boost with any attack potion.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="fight-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Fight Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="attack-rotation"><ins><nowiki>Attack Rotation</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Normal Mode:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ P1: 2 auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Hurricane</nowiki></em><nowiki> → 2 auto attacks / </nowiki><em><nowiki>Ranged Attack</nowiki></em><nowiki> → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Dragonfire</nowiki></em><nowiki> → 3 auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Hurricane</nowiki></em><nowiki> → Repeat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ P2: 1 auto attack → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Ranged Attack</nowiki></em><nowiki> → 1 auto attack → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Dragonfire Wall</nowiki></em><nowiki> → Repeat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Hard Mode:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ P1: 2 auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Hurricane</nowiki></em><nowiki> → 3 auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Dragonfire</nowiki></em><nowiki> → 3 auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Hurricane</nowiki></em><nowiki> → Repeat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ P2: 1 Melee auto attack → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Ranged Attack</nowiki></em><nowiki> → 1 Melee auto attack → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Fly + Dragonfire Wall</nowiki></em><nowiki> → Repeat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ P3: 10 rapid Melee auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Fly + Dragonfire Wall</nowiki></em><nowiki> → Fly back to the middle → Repeat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="auto-attacks"><ins><nowiki>Auto Attacks</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vindicta's auto attacks deal Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode Phase 3, Gorvek attacks rapidly with Melee auto attacks, hitting up to around 4,500 damage per hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray against Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki><strong><nowiki>or</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> once you are comfortable surviving mechanics</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode Phase 3, use defensive abilities if you are taking too much damage from Gorvek's rapid attacks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-1-hurricane-example"><ins><nowiki>Phase 1 - Hurricane</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/1tatA2X.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/1tatA2X.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vindicta uses a 5x5 Melee AoE when you are within melee distance</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If you are outside melee distance, </nowiki><em><nowiki>Hurricane</nowiki></em><nowiki> is replaced by a Ranged attack instead</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use Devotion </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki> to mitigate the damage </nowiki><strong><nowiki>or</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Tank the attack</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stay outside melee distance if you want to force the Ranged attack instead</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-1-ranged-attack-example"><ins><nowiki>Phase 1 - Ranged Attack</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/Y43i9Dnwrr.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/Y43i9Dnwrr.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vindicta fires a single Ranged hit if you are outside melee distance</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode, this deals moderate Ranged damage to a random target</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Briefly switch to pray against Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488317046812.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectrange</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-1-dragonfire-example"><ins><nowiki>Phase 1 - Dragonfire</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/TG1pugT.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/TG1pugT.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vindicta raises her arms to signal Gorvek</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gorvek fires a straight line of flames across the arena</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This deals rapid Magic damage if stood in</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Move out of the fire</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Avoid standing directly on the dragonfire line, as the damage hits rapidly</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-2-mounted-gorvek"><ins><nowiki>Phase 2 - Mounted Gorvek</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vindicta mounts Gorvek once she reaches the phase transition threshold</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Normal Mode, this happens at 100,000 LP</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode, this happens at 150,000 LP</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Once mounted, Vindicta and Gorvek gain a new attack pattern</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Prepare for </nowiki><em><nowiki>Ranged Attack</nowiki></em><nowiki> and </nowiki><em><nowiki>Dragonfire Wall</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Keep moving out of dragonfire lines while continuing to damage the boss</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-2-dragonfire-wall-example"><ins><nowiki>Phase 2 - Dragonfire Wall</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/flnK9zh.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/flnK9zh.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gorvek flies to a random corner and breathes a straight line of flames across the arena</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This works the same as </nowiki><em><nowiki>Phase 1 - Dragonfire</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Move out of the fire </nowiki><strong><nowiki>or</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Surge </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> towards Gorvek and past the fire</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-2-ranged-attack-example"><ins><nowiki>Phase 2 - Ranged Attack</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/h5uwYYH.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/h5uwYYH.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gorvek fires a high-damage Ranged hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use Resonance </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> to heal from the hit </nowiki><strong><nowiki>or</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray against Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488317046812.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectrange</nowiki>]<nowiki> and use Devotion </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-3-gorvek-hm-only"><ins><nowiki>Phase 3 - Gorvek (HM Only)</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After Vindicta is defeated, Gorvek continues the fight alone with 300,000 LP</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gorvek uses rapid Melee auto attacks, then flies away to create a dragonfire wall before returning to the middle</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray against Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> during Gorvek's rapid auto attacks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use defensive abilities if needed, as the Melee hits can deal heavy damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Be ready to move when Gorvek flies away for </nowiki><em><nowiki>Dragonfire Wall</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-3-dragonfire-wall-hm-only"><ins><nowiki>Phase 3 - Dragonfire Wall (HM Only)</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gorvek flies away and breathes a straight line of flames across the arena</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This works the same as </nowiki><em><nowiki>Phase 1 - Dragonfire</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode Phase 3, dragonfire lines stay active until three are present</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When the next dragonfire wall is used, the oldest line despawns</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Move out of the fire</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Track active dragonfire lines and avoid boxing yourself in</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use Surge </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Dive </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> to reposition safely</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
