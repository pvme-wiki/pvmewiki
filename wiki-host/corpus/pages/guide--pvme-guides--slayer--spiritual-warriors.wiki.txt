<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Spiritual warriors have four different variants, each resembling their respective god within the God Wars Dungeon. They're mostly similar, except that Armadylean spiritual warriors </nowiki><strong><nowiki>can't</nowiki></strong><nowiki> be attacked with melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This guide will focus on killing spiritual warriors in the Sunken Pyramid, as this location yields the highest kills per hour</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Other methods of killing spiritual warriors, such as in the God Wars Dungeon will not be discussed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ They can be assigned as a slayer assignment </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> by Jacquelyn, The Raptor, Vannaka, Sumona, and Laniakea</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• They count as spiritual warriors, ghosts, and undead slayer tasks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Armadylean spiritual warriors also count as aviansie slayer tasks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The best place to kill spiritual warriors is in the Sunken Pyramid due to how spread out they are in other locations. You can teleport there with a teleport to house tablet, or with a Slayer Codex </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1097517249499254875.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayercodex</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Spiritual warriors will only spawn as the version you captured</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="method-magic-and-melee"><ins><nowiki>Method: Magic and Melee</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Spiritual warriors can be AFK'd for extended periods with minimal inputs required</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• It is possible to AFK them with only one input every 10 minutes</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Sacrifice </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513201065907322880.png?v=1 <nowiki>sacrifice</nowiki>]<nowiki> is used to help sustain your life points</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ They're aggressive to you if you're not wearing any armour for their respective faction; however, using aggression potions </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/925794592199147581.png?v=1 <nowiki>aggressionpotion</nowiki>]<nowiki> will net you a higher kph and requires less inputs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If you're using melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> you can't have Armadylean spiritual warriors</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
