<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide covers active strategies and rotations for the Rex Matriarchs. There are two main strategies:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><strong><nowiki>Hybrid killing multiple Matriarchs</nowiki></strong></li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Higher KPH at the cost of significantly higher effort</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Only applies to Rathis/Orikalka/Pthentraken (Osseous has a separate arena)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><strong><nowiki>Camping a single Matriarch</nowiki></strong></li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Simpler and uses only 1 combat style at the cost of lower KPH</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="strategy-1-hybrid-killing-multiple-matriarchs"><ins><nowiki>Strategy 1: Hybrid killing multiple Matriarchs</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Alternate between a pair of Matriarchs to minimise wasted time during respawns. It is suggested to follow the rotations listed for each boss in the below section </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Strategy 2: Camping a single Matriarch</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Top tips:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If killing Rathis, you can target Rathis from range while moving towards her, and use defensives to build 100% adrenaline for your opening </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854004678657.png?v=1 <nowiki>zerk</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://youtu.be/PfhJG00n61s <nowiki>(example)</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If killing Rathis/Pthentraken, ensure you travel at the edge of the arena to prevent aggression from Orikalka</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
