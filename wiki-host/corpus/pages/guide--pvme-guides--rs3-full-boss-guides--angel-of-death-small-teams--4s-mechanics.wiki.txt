<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This is a mechanics guide for 4-man Nex, Angel of Death. It is assumed that you know how to deal with mechanics in 7-man AoD, described in </nowiki>[[PvME/Nex: Angel of Death/Angel Of Death 7s — Mechanics|<nowiki>Mechanics</nowiki>]]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Due to the meta heavily favouring ranged at the time of writing, this guide will assume that the team consists of </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Ranged Minion Tanks</nowiki></strong><nowiki>. The base can either Necro or Ranged.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The current rotations can </nowiki><strong><nowiki>consistently reaver skip</nowiki></strong><nowiki> if executed correctly.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Dealing with mechanics in 4-man AoD is slightly different to how they are dealt with in 7-mans. The following summarizes the major mechanics of the fight and how to deal with them.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-1-3000000-2100000-hp"><nowiki>Phase 1 (3,000,000 - 2,100,000 HP)</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Elements</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Quadrant with elements:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Tank it as you would in larger teams.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Quadrant with reavers:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Throw sticky bombs </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/655341074306301964.png?v=1 <nowiki>stickybomb</nowiki>]<nowiki> at the reavers until AoD hits her phase hp.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Sticky bombs last 6s / 10t.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Before getting bound by consecutive sticky bombs, the reaver will always walk at least 1 tile.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: Fast teams should not need to tank elements and do not require sticky bombs as you should consistently reaver skip.</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-2-2100000-1800000-hp"><nowiki>Phase 2 (2,100,000 - 1,800,000 HP)</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Handle mechanics exactly the same as in Phase 1.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-3-1800000-600000-hp"><nowiki>Phase 3 (1,800,000 - 600,000 HP)</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Smoke Pool</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• All tanked pools taken deal 14k damage spread evenly among the people inside.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Base is to tank the first pool making use of a vitality potion </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]<nowiki>. If the base is using ranged, the Fumus Minion Tank may instead handle the first pool with a vitality potion </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If there are any other pools, they have to be tanked by at least 2 people both with &gt;7k hp.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ice</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Assist the team member who is trapped ice by doing damage on the ice to free them.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ All other mechanics work the same as in 7s.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="dummy-placement"><ins><nowiki>Dummy Placement</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Important Notes</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Dummies </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/656844963522281473.png?v=1 <nowiki>dummy</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki><strong><nowiki>CANNOT</nowiki></strong><nowiki> be placed during Nex's </nowiki><em><nowiki>elements</nowiki></em><nowiki> special attack since squares are blocked for placement</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Familiars </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/703581275453128714.png?v=1 <nowiki>ripperpouch</nowiki>]<nowiki> block dummies, they are 2x2</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Conjures </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1166094935066423348.png?v=1 <nowiki>conjurearmy</nowiki>]<nowiki> block dummies, they are 1x1</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Prisms </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/938475261366784070.png?v=1 <nowiki>prismofrestoration</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1016651066684756070.png?v=1 <nowiki>prismofloyalty</nowiki>]<nowiki> block dummies</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Make sure before placing a dummy that nothing blocks it</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Call your familiars</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Move your conjures (Target the Glacies Amalgamation)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Don't place prisms near where dummies are supposed to be placed</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="minion-lure"><ins><nowiki>Minion Lure</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>To lure the minions using 3 minion tanks (UG Tank, C Tank and F Tank) players need to do the movement correctly.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ UG Tank will do the following:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> at the Fumus detonation spot and move to the center line to avoid elements</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Move behind the umbra amalgamation (1) while targeting the amalgamation</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Pre vuln umbra, and surge north after umbra spawns (to 2).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Vuln glacies, and surge through nex to lure (to 3).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• After luring, you may move to the center line to be closer to first pillar (4).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ C Tank will do the following:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> at the Umbra detonation spot and move to the center line to avoid elements</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Surge the minion through nex as you would in a 7-man team at the timing listed in rotations</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ F Tank will do the following:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> at the Umbra detonation spot and move to the center line to avoid elements</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Surge the minion through nex as you would in a 7-man team at the timing listed in rotations</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Move back into after minion is fully lured</nowiki></p>
