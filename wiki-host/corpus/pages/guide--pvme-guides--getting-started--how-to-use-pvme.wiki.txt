<span id="welcome-to-pvme"></span><p><nowiki>PvM Encyclopedia is a collection of resources for RuneScape combat. This guide introduces you to how our guide and text channels are organised and presented.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="getting-involved-in-pvme"><nowiki>Getting Involved in PvME</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>It's important to note that </nowiki><strong><nowiki>PvME is open source</nowiki></strong><nowiki> and </nowiki><strong><nowiki>everyone who wants to contribute is welcome</nowiki></strong><nowiki>. Regardless of your combat skill level, the editing team would love to have you! Read </nowiki>[[PvME/Contributing/Intro To Editing|<nowiki>Introduction to editing</nowiki>]]<nowiki> for contributor setup.</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="pvm-help"><ins><nowiki>PVM Help</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Link: </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/656898197561802760 <nowiki>PvM help (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/1021152606455873686 <nowiki>PvM help forums (external Discord)</nowiki>]</em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Ask all questions related to PVM or combat in these channels frequented by </nowiki><span><nowiki>Helper (community role)</nowiki></span><nowiki>s. They will likely know the answers to your questions or direct you to the relevant guide.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Some tips on channel usage:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ask quick questions </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/656898197561802760 <nowiki>PvM help (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki>. When there's an ongoing conversation which may bury your question, try using a thread or post it in </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/1021152606455873686 <nowiki>PvM help forums (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Make a forum post in </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/1021152606455873686 <nowiki>PvM help forums (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki> for complex questions requiring large amounts of information, e.g. preset help, understanding fundamentals of a style or boss.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>To help with commonly asked questions, we introduced commands. These should be used in the </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/534563158304620564 <nowiki>Bot commands (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki> channel to provide some quick information on a variety of topics. A list of commands can be found in </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/996854552353583185 <nowiki>Command info (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="vod-review"><ins><nowiki>VOD Review</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Link: </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/1019648841965916220 <nowiki>VOD reviews (external Discord)</nowiki>]</em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/1019648841965916220 <nowiki>VOD reviews (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki> allows you to submit recordings of your PVM encounters for others to review and give constructive, personalised, and judgement-free feedback. It is a great way to improve your PVM.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>To make a forum post in </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/1019648841965916220 <nowiki>VOD reviews (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki>:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Name it appropriately, e.g. &quot;&lt;Boss&gt; (&lt;Style&gt;) - RSN&quot;.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Include relevant background information and/or questions.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Include a link to your video recording.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="bossing-path"><ins><nowiki>Bossing Path</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Link: </nowiki>[[PvME/Learning to boss/Bossing Path|<nowiki>Bossing Path</nowiki>]]</em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The PvME bossing path provides an avenue to progress your skills and reach higher-tier PvM goals. It helps set objectives based on learning stages to measure your progression. It's perfect if you're new to bossing and unsure of where to start or looking for objectives to facilitate advancing your current abilities.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="the-mastery-system"><ins><nowiki>The Mastery System</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Link: </nowiki>[[PvME/Get involved/Mastery|<nowiki>Mastery</nowiki>]]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[[PvME/Get involved/Feats|<nowiki>Feats</nowiki>]]</em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The PvME Mastery System is a great way to gauge your PvM progress. It provides meaningful goals to shoot for as you learn new content or hone your skills at familiar encounters. There are multiple tiers of mastery ranks, each one granting its own unique role colour for all to see.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="pvme-boss-guides"><ins><nowiki>PvME Boss Guides</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The focus of our community is providing the best text guides possible for every encounter and players of all skill levels. Described below is the general composition of our guides. Use each guide’s On this page contents to navigate its sections quickly.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="suggested-presets"><nowiki>Suggested Presets</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ PvME provides one or more suggested preset(s) in every strategy guide.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Historically, presets were presented as images with a link to a full breakdown of the items used.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Since the creation of </nowiki>[https://pvme.github.io/preset-maker/ <nowiki>https://pvme.github.io/preset-maker/</nowiki>]<nowiki>, PvME is in the process of converting presets to a more condensed format, complete with familiars, relics, and more.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Examples of both formats can be found below.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Presets </nowiki><em><nowiki>usually</nowiki></em><nowiki> </nowiki><ins><nowiki>DO NOT</nowiki></ins><nowiki> need to be strictly copied.</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Downgrading a little bit is usually fine.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If a boss guide that has strict gear requirements that </nowiki><strong><nowiki>must</nowiki></strong><nowiki> be adhered to, it will be clearly stated.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>If unsure, ask questions relating to presets and their contents in </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/656898197561802760 <nowiki>PvM help (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Presets in full boss guides generally assume best in slot perks listed in </nowiki>[[PvME/Invention perks/Perks|<nowiki>Perks</nowiki>]]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Presets in basic and AFK boss guides generally assume basic perks listed in </nowiki>[[PvME/Invention perks/Perks|<nowiki>Perks</nowiki>]]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Downgrading your preset from the recommended may mean that rotations will need some extra improvisation.</nowiki></strong></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="mechanics-suggested-ability-rotations"><nowiki>Mechanics &amp; Suggested Ability Rotations</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Following basic information and preset suggestions, guides will generally mention:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Specific boss mechanics.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• DPS ability rotations.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>The rotations listed in full boss guides are designed for optimal kills!</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Some considerations to take when following these are:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Abilities used to deal with specific mechanics </nowiki><strong><nowiki>should be adhered to strictly</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Most DPS rotations are generally only suggestions and need not be followed exactly</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Feel free to adapt them as you see fit!</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Due to RNG you may need to improvise these rotations based on damage dealt, adrenaline etc.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Many of our guides make use of abbreviations. Check them out in this document: </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Glossary <nowiki>https://runescape.wiki/w/Glossary</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
