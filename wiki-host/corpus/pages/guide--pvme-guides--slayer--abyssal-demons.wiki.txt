</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Abyssal demons offer high experience rates while being completely afk. Being that they don't deal much damage, they're a great option for lower level players.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="desirable-drops"><ins><nowiki>Desirable drops</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Abyssal Demons drop the Abyssal whip </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/860891494805536769.png?v=1 <nowiki>abyssalwhip</nowiki>]<nowiki>, wand </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841409588590805043.png?v=1 <nowiki>abyssalwand</nowiki>]<nowiki> and orb </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/860891495041073152.png?v=1 <nowiki>abyssalorb</nowiki>]<nowiki> which are required for the Slayer Log. They also drop alchables and hard clues at a decent rate.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If killed in the Wilderness, they can also drop </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1006481325076787240.png?v=1 <nowiki>revenantdropenhancer</nowiki>]<nowiki> which can be helpful for Ultimate Slayer hunters.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The best spot for abyssal demons is located at the level 18 Wilderness obelisk (Graveyard of Shadows). The fastest way to reach the location is using the portable obelisk or an obelisk shard.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Level 18 Wilderness</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/MtWr7YkgwP.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ Place </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869283142350041098.png?v=1 <nowiki>cannon</nowiki>]<nowiki> on yellow tile as shown below (2E of stump NE of obelisk) and stand on red tile (2N and 2W of stump SE of obelisk).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If your character gets walked off the marked tile, step back on it, and click the nearest demon.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
