<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The Barrows Brothers </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513222840162582541.png?v=1 <nowiki>dharokhead</nowiki>]<nowiki> were originally composed of six wights, two more members were added after completing the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Ritual_of_the_Mahjarrat <nowiki>Ritual of the Mahjarrat</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Kindred_Spirits <nowiki>Kindred Spirits</nowiki>]<nowiki> quests.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This guides aims to get you started on doing Barrows runs, and will focus on the original six brothers.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• It will be assumed that you're using Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> and the entry-level preset.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ War's Retreat is used for easy access to the Barrows Brothers </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513222840162582541.png?v=1 <nowiki>dharokhead</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• War's Retreat Teleport </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1032540079589163049.png?v=1 <nowiki>warsretreatteleport</nowiki>]<nowiki> is unlocked via getting 10 boss kills (any boss).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Using High Contrast Mode lets you see which door is clickable when in the tunnels.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Right-click the World Map to toggle it on.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><div id="block-thebrothers"><u><nowiki>The Brothers</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ As this is an older encounter, there aren't many mechanics to be wary of.
    • Each brother has a mechanic, which can mostly be ignored.
        ⬩ Dharok increases his damage dealt, the lower his life points get.
    • Being underground will periodically drain your prayer points.
    • The catacombs are a maze, with a simple puzzle at the end.
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>The Catacombs</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Searching one of the sarcophagi will place you in a random corner of the catacombs.
    • This sarcophagus is random, and normally reserved for last.
⬥ The catacombs are composed of a series of tunnels, in the shape of a 3x3 grid, with a treasure room in the center.
    • The path to the center is randomly generated.
    • Using High Contrast Mode makes finding the correct path easier.
⬥ The door leading to the center of the catacombs will have one of four, </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/The_Barrows_Brothers/Strategies#The_tunnels <nowiki>simple puzzles</nowiki>]<nowiki>, which always have the same solution.
</nowiki></div>

</nowiki><h3 id="order-the-brothers-should-be-killed"><nowiki>Order the Brothers Should be Killed</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Dharok → Ahrim → Karil → Guthan → Torag → Verac</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Protect from Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1052551452788269127.png?v=1 <nowiki>protectfrommelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> on Dharok, Guthan, Torag, and Verac.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Protect from Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1052551456835768351.png?v=1 <nowiki>protectfrommagic</nowiki>]<nowiki> on Ahrim.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Protect from Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1052551455078367313.png?v=1 <nowiki>protectfrommissiles</nowiki>]<nowiki> on Karil.</nowiki></p>
