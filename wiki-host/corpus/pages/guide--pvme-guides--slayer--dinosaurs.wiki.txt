</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ There are 4 types of dinosaurs (slayer monster), all of which are found on Anachronia:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Feral dinosaurs </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107322882261072.png?v=1 <nowiki>feraldinosaur</nowiki>]<nowiki> - 90 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Venomous dinosaurs </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107319166107689.png?v=1 <nowiki>venomousdinosaur</nowiki>]<nowiki> - 105 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Brutish dinosaurs </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107321087103179.png?v=1 <nowiki>brutishdinosaur</nowiki>]<nowiki> - 99 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Ripper dinosaurs </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107317450641438.png?v=1 <nowiki>ripperdinosaur</nowiki>]<nowiki> - 114 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Dinosaurs are only assigned by Laniakea </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975284345411600426.png?v=1 <nowiki>laniakea</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki><strong><nowiki>CANNOT</nowiki></strong><nowiki> be placed in the player owned dungeon.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ All of them attack with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This makes the best style to use against them </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki>, followed by </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> should not be used for any dinosaur tasks.</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Needed for T3 Slayer Lodge for Slayer Helmet </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285340460449792.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayerhelm</nowiki>]<nowiki> stand</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Blowpipe pieces </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/925794591830056961.png?v=1 <nowiki>upgradedboneblowpipe</nowiki>]<nowiki> - 1/6,000 on-task, 1/20,000 off-task</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Totem of auras middle </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643507594811670538.png?v=1 <nowiki>vitalitytotem</nowiki>]<nowiki> - 1/300</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Totem of intimidation base </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643507594794631198.png?v=1 <nowiki>intimidationtotem</nowiki>]<nowiki> - 1/300</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="venomous-dinosaurs"><ins><nowiki>Venomous Dinosaurs</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Venomous dinosaurs are the go-to for dinosaur tasks as they have the highest kills per hour of any dinosaur.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="stats"><nowiki>Stats</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896050370281523.png?v=1 <nowiki>combat</nowiki>]<nowiki> XP per kill : 1378 + 454.7 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>Constitution</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> XP per kill : 1332</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Optimal kills per hour: 480</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Optimal </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> XP per hour : ~640k</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><nowiki>Location and How to Get There</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Venomous Dinosaurs are located on the northwestern part of Anachronia, easily accessed by using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1354420110772928512.png?v=1 <nowiki>nwanachronia</nowiki>]<nowiki> and heading east.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Place the cannon </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869283142350041098.png?v=1 <nowiki>cannon</nowiki>]<nowiki> on the yellow square and stand on the green square shown in the image below. This effectively aggros 5 dinosaurs. This works for all three methods.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If a dinosaur is getting stuck on the bone to the south while using Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki>, stand 1 tile east.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="method-afk-magic"><ins><nowiki>Method: AFK Magic</nowiki></ins></h2>
