</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Wyverns are one of the 4 raptor mobs, and are known for their unique environmental effects and highly damaging attacks. They offer decent </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> XP, and are a consistent source of GP due to their wyvern bone drops.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The Dragon slayer perk and sigil </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689502927731163170.png?v=1 <nowiki>dragonslayerperk</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339921814126594.png?v=1 <nowiki>dragonslayer</nowiki>]<nowiki>, along with any other dragon-slaying related items such as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/971025697042755584.png?v=1 <nowiki>jasdragonbanearrow</nowiki>]<nowiki>, do </nowiki><strong><nowiki>NOT</nowiki></strong><nowiki> work on wyverns.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="desirable-drops"><nowiki>Desirable Drops</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Wyverns have a rich drop table with a guaranteed drop of Wyvern bones, a valuable item and an ingredient for super prayer potions.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><nowiki>Location and How to Get There</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Living Wyverns are found in 2 locations:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><nowiki>The Asgarnian Ice Dungeon, accessed via a rope in the south of the Skeletal Wyvern room.</nowiki></li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎a. The easiest way to get there would be via dungeoneering cape teleport </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/976732922545586246.png?v=1 <nowiki>dgcape</nowiki>]<nowiki> to the Frost dragon dungeon (0,8)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><strong><nowiki>Northwest of Ghorrock, accessed via the Ghorrock teleport (ancient spellbook)</nowiki></strong></li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎a. This is the best location as it benefits from the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994189299681607800.png?v=1 <nowiki>infernalpuzzlebox</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1006121672790773770.png?v=1 <nowiki>jasboost</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎b. Wyverns are also attackable from areas without the chill mechanic, making it viable to be stationary and AFK in this location.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>

</nowiki><div><nowiki>

</nowiki><div><nowiki>



</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Wyrm breath</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Wyverns have a breath attack different from that of dragons, and requires </nowiki><strong><nowiki>wyrmfire</nowiki></strong><nowiki> protection.
    • Use wyrmfire potions or elder overload salves to block all damage from this attack.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Cavern chill</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Wyverns reside in a habitat which slowly increases the player's cold percentage.
    • The higher the chill percentage, the lower the poison damage taken by the player. No poison damage will be taken by players with ~70% chill.
    • At ≥25% chill, wyvern attack damage increases to 2500 from around 1300.
    • At ≥90% chill, players will be periodically stunned at 8-10s per stun, </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Never let your chill reach this stage.</nowiki></strong><nowiki>
    • Fires throughout the wyvern habitats and be lit, standing within 2 tiles of them periodically lowers the player's chill percentage.
    • </nowiki><strong><nowiki>This mechanic is avoided completely by standing outside of area affected by cavern chill in the wilderness.</nowiki></strong><nowiki>
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Wyrm poison</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Wyverns can inflict poison damage based on the percentage of </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Cavern chill</nowiki></strong><nowiki> every 4-5 ticks.
    • Antipoison++ halves the damage, but every tick of poison decreases potion duration.
    • </nowiki><strong><nowiki>This mechanic is avoided completely by standing outside of area affected by cavern chill in the wilderness.</nowiki></strong><nowiki>
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Varied attacking</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Wyverns can attack with all 3 styles, alongside their breath attack.
    • They will attack with primarily </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> if the player stands in melee range.
    • Hence, either stand in melee distance and use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> or camp </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> and out heal their damage through high DPS.
</nowiki></div>
</div>

</nowiki><h3 id="active-strategy"><nowiki>Active Strategy</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If doing them actively with best-in-slot gear, stand close to the most north-western fire in the wilderness spot (refer to example vod), and use target cycle to target wyverns within range. Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/662249620579155968.png?v=1 <nowiki>dommine</nowiki>]<nowiki> whenever </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644352871714836.png?v=1 <nowiki>gsunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> is used along with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/736298121704767538.png?v=1 <nowiki>adrenrenewal</nowiki>]<nowiki> if available.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257440278512537740.png?v=1 <nowiki>gchain</nowiki>]<nowiki> combos opportunistically on any wyverns that wander close to each other.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When eating solid food, such as sharks dropped by wyverns, try to eat them in the tick between the end of a kill and the start of the next kill when you have no target, as this does not reduce adrenaline.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If doing wyverns with lesser gear, an </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285651387613194.png?v=1 <nowiki>oldakcoil</nowiki>]<nowiki> may be used to aggro a limited amount (2-3) of wyverns which makes it more manageable to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki>, as shown </nowiki>[https://youtu.be/2jCihwc-Bd0 <nowiki>here</nowiki>]</p>
