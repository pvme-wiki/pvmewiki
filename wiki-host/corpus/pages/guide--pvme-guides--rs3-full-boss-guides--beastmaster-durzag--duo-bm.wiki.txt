</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide assumes that you already have an understanding of mechanics of the fight - </nowiki>[[PvME/Beastmaster Durzag/Bm Overview|<nowiki>Bm Basic</nowiki>]]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="the-strategies"><nowiki>The Strategies</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ There are two main strategies to duo BM:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>AoE stack strategy</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - stacking and tanking everything at once, dealing damage with AoEs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Entangle strategy</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - uses entangle to get the 2nd pet stuck, reducing damage taken</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="general-notes"><nowiki>General Notes</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ It will be significantly easier if the base tank uses </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289319964763.png?v=1 <nowiki>staffoflight</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> , especially if they are tanking pets at the same time for the AoE strategy.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Cryptbloom </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1334336168363561041.png?v=1 <nowiki>cryptbloomtop</nowiki>]<nowiki> with animate dead </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090453135382.png?v=1 <nowiki>animatedead</nowiki>]</p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="aoe-stack-strategy"><ins><nowiki>AoE Stack Strategy</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stand by the north gong where the 2nd pet spawns, and ideally deal damage to both the pets and BM using AoE attacks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="stacking-phase"><nowiki>Stacking Phase</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/axiYyam.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stay in melee distance to the pet to only take melee damage whilst stacking the enemies.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Walk the pet/BM to stack them for AoEs if necessary.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Focus on killing one of the pets, particularly if stacking BM on the pet fails</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/938475261366784070.png?v=1 <nowiki>prismofrestoration</nowiki>]<nowiki> Prisms of restoration may not be able to spawn if surrounded by enemies - place the prism before starting, and extend it during the fight</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The 3rd pet and chargers will spawn once the 2nd pet's health is lowered sufficiently - continue to deal damage with AoEs.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="one-pet-dead"><nowiki>One Pet Dead</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Base tank - run BM to avoid taking too much damage; stacks are only received from melee hits. Using emerald aurora </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/914077639784935424.png?v=1 <nowiki>emeraldaurora</nowiki>]<nowiki> for additional damage reduction is helpful.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ DPS player - finishes off the remaining pet. provoke </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> and tank the pet, using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> again if it jumps onto the base tank.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="both-pets-dead"><nowiki>Both Pets Dead</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ DPS player helps to kill BM - the DPS player can act as the backup tank, allowing the base tank to do their damage rotation.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Try not to run BM too far from the non-tank player's damage-boosting ultimate.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="entangle-strategy"><ins><nowiki>Entangle Strategy</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Uses entangle </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/567727987274022924.png?v=1 <nowiki>ent</nowiki>]<nowiki> to manipulate the 2nd pet, preventing it from attacking until you are ready to kill it.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This strategy aims to use chargers to block the 2nd pet.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Therefore, BM must not be in combat until you are ready to have it summon the chargers and 3rd pet.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="pet-2-lure"><nowiki>Pet 2 Lure</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/pqwlV4r.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: Do </nowiki></em><em><nowiki>not</nowiki></em><em><nowiki> aggro BM</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>1) Stand at the closest charger cage that is south of the west gong as the fight starts.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>2) Wait for pet to jump to you → cast entangle </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/567727987274022924.png?v=1 <nowiki>ent</nowiki>]<nowiki> on pet.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>3) Do not attack, move to the southwest corner of the arena and wait for the pet to jump again.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/cxQhHvK.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>4) Attack the pet; lower its health to around 25k.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="pet-is-lowered-getting-it-stuck"><nowiki>Pet is Lowered - Getting it Stuck</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>1) Run towards the middle to aggro BM, cast reflect </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> whilst doing this</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>2) Run back towards the pet - cast entangle </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/567727987274022924.png?v=1 <nowiki>ent</nowiki>]<nowiki> on the pet as BM comes close, and use freedom </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> or anticipate </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> as you will be stunned by the 3rd pet</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>3) Wait for chargers to spawn - just as the chargers start to move, surge </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> east and go to the southeast corner</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/6gwUuPF.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If done correctly, the entangle will prevent the pet from moving until the chargers get into position. Once in position, the chargers will block the pet.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="southeast-corner"><nowiki>Southeast Corner</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Lower the 3rd pet/BM as you see fit.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Base tank - run BM when the 3rd pet dies to avoid taking too much damage; stacks are only received from melee hits. Using emerald aurora </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/914077639784935424.png?v=1 <nowiki>emeraldaurora</nowiki>]<nowiki> for additional damage reduction is helpful.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ DPS player finishes off the pet that is stuck.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="killing-bm"><nowiki>Killing BM</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ DPS player can act as the backup tank, allowing the base tank to do their damage rotation.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Try not to run BM too far from the non-tank player's damage-boosting ultimate.</nowiki></p>
