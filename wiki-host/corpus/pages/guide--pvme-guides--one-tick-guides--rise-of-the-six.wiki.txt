</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide will cover the basic strategy to get a tick perfect kill at Rise of the Six in a 4 man team.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For general 1 tick kill information, and some useful tips and tricks make sure to read </nowiki>[[PvME/One-tick guides/General Tips|<nowiki>General Tips</nowiki>]]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This method requires everyone to use Necromancy, everyone on the team should be at least level 110 or higher, the higher the easier the 1 tick will be.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="rotation"><ins><nowiki>Rotation</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Do the entire rotation on the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/656844963522281473.png?v=1 <nowiki>dummy</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Turning off revolution </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1482849360462086244.png?v=1 <nowiki>necrobasic</nowiki>]<nowiki> will help.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Build 12 Necrosis Stacks with the following rotation:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/656844963522281473.png?v=1 <nowiki>dummy</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809175980810380.png?v=1 <nowiki>touchofdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809175980810380.png?v=1 <nowiki>touchofdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1482849360462086244.png?v=1 <nowiki>necrobasic</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1482849360462086244.png?v=1 <nowiki>necrobasic</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The person starting the fight should signal they are ready with an ability such as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki>, the starter will also have to do it slightly differently. They also must have </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> bound somewhere on their actionbar.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Starter Rotation is as follows:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: make sure you press 1 + click </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> 4 ticks after </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]</em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Signal with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339922019516416.png?v=1 <nowiki>undeadslayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> → click Disrupt Shadow portal → space → 1 + click </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> in the same tick.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Once the person who is starting has signalled they are about to use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]<nowiki> do the following rotation 3 ticks after their signal:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339922019516416.png?v=1 <nowiki>undeadslayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> → wait 4 ticks → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809243143766118.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathguard90</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="positioning"><ins><nowiki>Positioning</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Place the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/656844963522281473.png?v=1 <nowiki>dummy</nowiki>]<nowiki> 1 West / East of the middle brother (blue squares in image below).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ On each side, a player will stand 1 North and 1 South of the middle brother. This helps distinguish between players (green squares in image below).</nowiki></p>
