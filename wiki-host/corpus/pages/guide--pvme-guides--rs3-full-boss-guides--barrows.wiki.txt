<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The Barrows Brothers </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513222840162582541.png?v=1 <nowiki>dharokhead</nowiki>]<nowiki> are mainly camped by high-level players in an effort to obtain the Graverobber title. The brothers are relatively weak, with the exception of Linza.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="useful-unlocks"><ins><nowiki>Useful Unlocks</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Completing all </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Morytania_Set_Tasks_-_Hard <nowiki>Hard Morytania achievements</nowiki>]<nowiki>:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Doubles rune drops</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Allows you to bank loot directly from the chest</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Completing all </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/War%27s_Blessing_1 <nowiki>Easy Combat Mastery Achievements</nowiki>]<nowiki>:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Portals will permanently appear in corner &amp; central rooms of the underground crypt</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Teleporting via these portals circumvents the need to to solve puzzles or open doors</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Bringing a Dragon/Barrows defender allows for additional defender components. Corrupted sigils can be dissasembled for undead components</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
