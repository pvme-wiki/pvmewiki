<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Capsarius <nowiki>Capsarius</nowiki>]<nowiki> are the healers of the Order of Ascension. They are mainly killed for Ascension members Slayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> tasks, the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_collection_log#Feldip_Hills <nowiki>Feldip Hills collection log</nowiki>]<nowiki>, and Ascension Keystones in the Sunken Pyramid.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This guide will focus on killing Capsarius in the Sunken Pyramid, as this location yields the highest kills per hour.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Capsarius have weak attacks, don't heal each other, and have low health which makes them very easy to farm.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ascension members can be assigned as a Slayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> assignment by Duradel, Kuradal, Morvran, and Laniakea.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If killing Capsarius in the Monastery of Ascension use the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1032552303024152578.png?v=1 <nowiki>oogloglode</nowiki>]<nowiki> and run northwest.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Only use this location while collecting souls.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If killing Capsarius in the Sunken Pyramid use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1097517249499254875.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayercodex</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Relocating your house to Menaphos and using a teleport to house tablet also works.</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="method-legacy-ranged"><ins><nowiki>Method: Legacy Ranged</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Toggle on </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Legacy_Mode#Combat <nowiki>Legacy Combat Mode</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Place 5 Capsarius in the small room of the Sunken Pyramid.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Toggle the scavenge settings on your legendary to only pick up desired drops.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Not doing this results in an increased chance at missing keystone drops.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/t6Us9dgqjv.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ Position yourself in the center of the small room.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689551388576972805.png?v=1 <nowiki>aggrovl</nowiki>]<nowiki> is active at all times.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Activate your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159546712074.png?v=1 <nowiki>cruelty</nowiki>]</p>
