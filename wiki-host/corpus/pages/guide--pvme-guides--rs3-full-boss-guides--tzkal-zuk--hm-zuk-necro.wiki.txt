</nowiki><h2 id="introduction-and-preface"><ins><nowiki>Introduction and preface</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This is an </nowiki><strong><nowiki>advanced guide</nowiki></strong><nowiki> which assumes a strong underlying knowledge of all mechanics and information presented in </nowiki>[[PvME/TzKal-Zuk/Tzkal Zuk Overview|<nowiki>Tzkal Zuk Basic</nowiki>]]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The rotations used are examples and can be adapted depending on Adrenaline, Residual Soul stacks </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139453152169558046.png?v=1 <nowiki>residualsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Necrosis Stacks </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139452791878856805.png?v=1 <nowiki>necrosis</nowiki>]<nowiki>. The lures generally require moving depending on which monsters are blocked prior to using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Notes:</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><em><nowiki>Players should try to follow this guide as best as possible. If you find that you are slower at moving between luring positions then you should try to always be hitting an enemy while running</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><em><nowiki>You should upkeep </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809209782698024.png?v=1 <nowiki>darkness</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1166094935066423348.png?v=1 <nowiki>conjurearmy</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434409913634816.png?v=1 <nowiki>commandghost</nowiki>]<nowiki> at all times for a reduction in damage taken, healing and increased damage dealt</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><em>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809118283976814.png?v=1 <nowiki>greaterboneshield</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1179716261681307728.png?v=1 <nowiki>zemouregalsnexus</nowiki>]<nowiki> should be used as defensives will be vital during the encounter, such as to use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki> on challenge wave 15 and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> in the conduit phase</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><em>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643148943856762890.png?v=1 <nowiki>mahj</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643505652865695767.png?v=1 <nowiki>invigaura</nowiki>]<nowiki> are viable alternatives, however, they are less consistent. </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1233722649834684416.png?v=1 <nowiki>equilibriumaura</nowiki>]<nowiki> offers consistency, cheap upkeep cost, longer duration, more </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1166094935066423348.png?v=1 <nowiki>conjurearmy</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage, and more </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434409913634816.png?v=1 <nowiki>commandghost</nowiki>]<nowiki> healing</nowiki></em></p>
