</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Zemouregal &amp; Vorkath is a boss encounter consisting of two bosses; Zemouregal and Vorkath. To access this encounter, the players must progress through the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Battle_of_Forinthry <nowiki>Battle of Forinthry</nowiki>]<nowiki>. Completion of the quest unlocks the normal mode and hardmode encounters.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="normal-mode-specifics"><nowiki>Normal Mode specifics</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Every place of power is protected by an undead giant, kill it to disrupt the beam.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Disrupting the places of power disables some of Zemouregal's abilities during the encounter, or starts the encounter.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Northern location: minion spawns.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Northwestern location: Zemouregal's Spikes.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Western location: Starts the encounter.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><div id="block-mechanics"><u><nowiki>Mechanics</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Power meter</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Displays the connection between Zemouregal and Vorkath, affecting damage absorption and damage dealt by Vorkath.
    • Each next phase increases it by net 25%.
    • Empowered zombies can increase it by 50%. 
    • Power meter disappears when vorkath leaves at zero lifepoints.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Dragonfire Breath</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Vorkath charges up and deals a big magic hit. 
    • Damage is reduced by </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/864492695997513748.png?v=1 <nowiki>superantifire</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/648976643687317532.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovlsalve</nowiki>]<nowiki>
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Zonal Ice Breath</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Vorkath takes to the air and spews ice on his location that spreads outward, after which he comes back down. 
    • Can deal three </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> hits and one MD </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> slam. 
        • Can be avoided by moving away from Vorkath, also protecting </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583430259064569856.png?v=1 <nowiki>summoning</nowiki>]<nowiki> familiars.
    • The </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> hits damage Zemouregal and all minions caught within it.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Poison pools</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Vorkath will stand on his hind legs and spew into the air, causing pools that hit hard typeless damage to appear. 
    • Phase 2: Green poison pools, hitting up to 3k.
    • Phase 3: Purple poison pools, hitting up to 5k.
        •  Every hit taken increases Zemouregal's Empowerment debuff by 5%, increasing damage taken.
    • Can be accompanied by the voiceline: </nowiki><strong><code><nowiki>Spew your venom, my malformed pet!</nowiki></code></strong><nowiki>
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Fly</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Vorkath flies up high to freeze the whole arena by air strike. If not stopped, all in the arena will be hit ~15k </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage, killing </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583430259064569856.png?v=1 <nowiki>summoning</nowiki>]<nowiki> familiars.
    • Using the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1176968204883603497.png?v=1 <nowiki>balista</nowiki>]<nowiki> (extra action button) while Vorkath is up will shoot Vorkath down, preventing the attack and dealing a 10k hit to Vorkath.
        • Can fail if done right before Vorkath returns!
    • </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306353778689.png?v=1 <nowiki>cade</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be used alternatively.
    • Can be accompanied by the voiceline: </nowiki><strong><code><nowiki>Cast my glorious vengeance down upon them!</nowiki></code></strong><nowiki> or </nowiki><strong><code><nowiki>Cut them down without mercy!</nowiki></code></strong><nowiki>
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Power Meter and Damage Reduction</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ The power meter increases Vorkath's damage dealt by auto attacks as well as mechanics.
⬥ While Vorkath is around, Zemouregal takes no or reduced damage.
    • Every phase transition Zemouregal gets hit 10k by Vorkath, reducing his damage reduction by 10% (100%→90%→80%).
⬥ If Zemouregal gets hit while shielded, a green orb is sent to Vorkath, giving him damage absorption based on the damage dealt on Zemouregal.
    • Vorkath's damage absorption is multiplied by the power meter % and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537349530551582720.png?v=1 <nowiki>vuln</nowiki>]<nowiki>.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Minions</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Zemouregal summons two minions, one of each type.
    • This is a time-based mechanic which resets on phasing.
    • Combat minions (36k </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>constitution</nowiki>]<nowiki> per player) 
        • Skeleton ranger
        • Zombie warrior
        • Skeleton knight
        • Phantom Spellcaster
    • Support minions (6k </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>constitution</nowiki>]<nowiki> per player) 
        • Ghost Healer, heals Vorkath 1000 lifepoints.
        • Zombie Shielder, gives Vorkath a shield, reducing incoming damage.
        • Empowering Zombie, adds +50% to the power meter temporarily.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Bone Spikes</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Zemouregal makes a dust cloud appear on  every player's location, where 4.2s later spikes shoot out of the ground.
    • This is a time-based mechanic which resets on phasing.
    • Can deal up to 8k soft typeless damage to every player in the 7x7 area.
    • Can be avoided by moving away from your location.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>

<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Zemouregal uses soul sap </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809140476031057.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsap</nowiki>]<nowiki>, gaining one </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139453152169558046.png?v=1 <nowiki>residualsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki>.
