</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>A powerful three-headed dragon residing north-west of the Edgeville lodestone. The King Black Dragon (KBD) </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513213018054852648.png?v=1 <nowiki>kbd</nowiki>]<nowiki> is an iconic lower-level boss suitable for players with tier 50-70 gear. His attacks involve dealing with high-damage Dragonfire and other elemental breath attacks.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="tips-and-tricks"><ins><nowiki>Tips and Tricks</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The King Black Dragon's mechanics are mostly accounted for via good preparation such as bringing protection from dragonfire. During the fight you'll be largely focused on dealing good damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If you have access to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1052551452788269127.png?v=1 <nowiki>protectfrommelee</nowiki>]<nowiki>, use it and stand in melee distance.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Don't forget to use any damage-boosting prayers you have available!</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Your options for dealing damage will vary greatly depending on your gear and unlocked abilities. The below are guidelines, assuming no expensive unlocks.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Build adrenaline (and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139452791878856805.png?v=1 <nowiki>necrosis</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139453152169558046.png?v=1 <nowiki>residualsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki> for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki>) and use strong threshold abilities when available:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki>: wild magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809978966037.png?v=1 <nowiki>wm</nowiki>]<nowiki> and asphyxiate </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Try combust </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833098100745.png?v=1 <nowiki>comb</nowiki>]<nowiki> and stepping under KBD!</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki>: assault </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532855191928842.png?v=1 <nowiki>assault</nowiki>]<nowiki> and destroy </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879330148352.png?v=1 <nowiki>destroy</nowiki>]<nowiki> or hurricane </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532878969438210.png?v=1 <nowiki>cane</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Try Slaughter </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879237873666.png?v=1 <nowiki>slaughter</nowiki>]<nowiki> and stepping under KBD!</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki>: snapshot </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535534127131394088.png?v=1 <nowiki>snap</nowiki>]<nowiki> and rapid fire </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]</p>
