<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="definitions"><ins><nowiki>Definitions</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Within these guides there are two levels of AFK you will see mentioned:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>AFK</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ These do not usually require any attention to the game</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You may still need to interact infrequently, for example: clicking to prevent lobbying, drinking potions, using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513200949016264727.png?v=1 <nowiki>enhancedexcalibur</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/656426717505650708.png?v=1 <nowiki>ancientshard</nowiki>]<nowiki>, collecting loot, healing familiars with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/938475261366784070.png?v=1 <nowiki>prismofrestoration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Alt1 can usually be used to notify you of these</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Low Effort</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ These usually do require you to pay attention to the game, and are instead used to heavily reduce inputs required to kill a boss</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In addition to those listed above under AFK, example interactions include: queuing an ability before the fight, repositioning every kill, banking between kills, moving out of the way of mechanics, eating food at low health</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="top-tips-using-alt1s-afk-warden"><ins><nowiki>Top tips: Using Alt1's AFK Warden</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>To set up alt1 AFK Warden you can include several useful warnings.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ HP Alert</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Prayer Alert</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Loot Beam</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Inventory Full</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Animate Dead</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Overload</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Weapon Poison</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Summoning Familiar HP Alert</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Kerapac Kill</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Lobby timer</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Depending on the boss you are doing, you can activate different ones of these, and set the HP threshold to a suitable level for the risk of your setup. Doing this will allow you to eat / excal to stop a death.</nowiki></p>
