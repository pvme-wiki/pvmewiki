</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Binding_contract_(blood_reaver) <nowiki>Blood Reaver binding contracts</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/839903693837959228.png?v=1 <nowiki>reaverpouch</nowiki>]<nowiki> are a good alternative to bossing for generating GP and a necessary evil for ironmen who wish to pursue many of the strategies outlined in PVME.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The below method assumes you have already prepared or collected your blank Binding Contracts from either:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Making them yourself after the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Dagon_Bye <nowiki>Dragon Bye</nowiki>]<nowiki> quest</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Getting from </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Ancient_casket <nowiki>Ancient Caskets</nowiki>]<nowiki> (in stacks of 50)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="positioning"><ins><nowiki>Positioning</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/ZtFaoGl6zk.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ Inside Nex's encampment, stand in the south-middle area</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You will be dragged around as you are aggro'd</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Occasionally a Blood Reaver may walk away - you may wish to click them manually (or wait for it to wander back into range)</nowiki></p>
