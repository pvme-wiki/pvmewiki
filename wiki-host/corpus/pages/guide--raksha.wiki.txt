</nowiki><h2 id="general-knowledge"><ins><nowiki>General Knowledge</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Raksha fight consists of 4 phases, with 200k HP for each phase (400k if duo).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Prayer flicking is crucial and mandatory as Raksha is able to use all three combat styles.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Raksha uses a special attack after every 4 normal attacks during phases 1-3, and after every 2 normal attacks during phase 4.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073088769982475.png?v=1 <nowiki>p6</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1214416401561952296.png?v=1 <nowiki>er4</nowiki>]<nowiki> on </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/839903893177106454.png?v=1 <nowiki>lanispear</nowiki>]<nowiki> to ensure 1k minimum damage with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854281764884.png?v=1 <nowiki>bd</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• To see if you meet the minimum damage requirement, go to dummies at War's Retreat, set them to minimum hit mode, and use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/839903893177106454.png?v=1 <nowiki>lanispear</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854281764884.png?v=1 <nowiki>bd</nowiki>]<nowiki> with your Raksha gear.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If you did not meet the requirement, use to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583429936606347280.png?v=1 <nowiki>turmoil</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159416557573.png?v=1 <nowiki>Malevolence</nowiki>]<nowiki> while using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854281764884.png?v=1 <nowiki>bd</nowiki>]</p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="raksha-the-shadow-colossus-miniquest"><nowiki>Raksha, the Shadow Colossus (Miniquest)</nowiki></h3>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="recommendation"><ins><nowiki>Recommendation</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Due to the nature of Raksha being very prayer-flick reliant, you may find it helpful to practice prayer flicking with nothing but a weapon until death, while doing damage and dealing with mechanics.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Repeat this until you get the hang of prayer flicking under pressure.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: if you are in melee distance, Raksha will not use the same style auto-attack back-to-back</nowiki></em></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="mechanics-summary"><ins><nowiki>Mechanics Summary</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phases-1-3"><nowiki>Phases 1-3</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><ins><nowiki>Rockfall</nowiki></ins></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Happens between phases 1-3 transitions.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 5x5 rocks fall from the ceiling that deal heavy typeless damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Small rubbles indicate the spots rocks will fall on.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Move out of the way before rocks hit the floor.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Utilise </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><ins><nowiki>Tail Swipe and Charge</nowiki></ins></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Tail Swipe</nowiki></strong><nowiki> → If the player is in melee distance → Raksha spins around in a 3x3 radius.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Charge</nowiki></strong><nowiki> → If the player is outside of melee distance → Raksha charges towards the player.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Getting hit by either of those attacks results in heavy typeless damage and a stun.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Remain in melee distance to avoid getting a Charge attack.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Run out of spin radius once Raksha starts the Tail Swipe animation.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><ins><nowiki>Shadow Bomb</nowiki></ins></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Raksha stuns the player before launching a shadow bomb that explodes and leaves a poison cloud in a 3x3 radius.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Raksha launches 3 bombs in phase 3.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> beforehand to protect against the stun.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Move out of the bomb's radius before it lands.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><ins><nowiki>5X Shockwave</nowiki></ins></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Raksha walks to the middle of the room and unleashes 5 strong magic attacks.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Phase Raksha before he can reach this special attack.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Restart the fight if you receive said special attack.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><ins><nowiki>Mind Poison</nowiki></ins></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Raksha immobilizes the player and spawns 2-5 orbs that deal constant typeless damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Failure of expelling the orbs will result in heavy typeless damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Click the orbs to expel them.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Clicking the orbs does </nowiki><strong><nowiki>not</nowiki></strong><nowiki> interrupt combat or channeled abilities.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><ins><nowiki>Shadow Anima Pools</nowiki></ins></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Harmless 1k HP pools that spawn in 4 different areas.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• During phase 3, Raksha absorbs anima from said pools.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ At the start of phase 3, kill all pools with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/839903893177106454.png?v=1 <nowiki>lanispear</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/602581988599398400.png?v=1 <nowiki>Laceration</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854281764884.png?v=1 <nowiki>bd</nowiki>]<nowiki> before Raksha can absorb any anima.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>DO NOT</nowiki></strong><nowiki> use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854281764884.png?v=1 <nowiki>bd</nowiki>]<nowiki> during global cooldown as it will deal no damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854281764884.png?v=1 <nowiki>bd</nowiki>]<nowiki> on the pool highlighted in green (using it on the pools in red will not guarantee all pools in the vicinity being cleared)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/4hSPQGW.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><ins><nowiki>Minion Spawn</nowiki></ins></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Exclusive to phase 3.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Raksha spawns a 10k HP melee minion during Mind Poison.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> against minion when Raksha isn't attacking, and focus on phasing Raksha.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="phase-4"><nowiki>Phase 4</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Raksha heals up to 400k HP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><ins><nowiki>Tail Swipe and Shadow Bomb</nowiki></ins></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Tail Swipe</nowiki></strong><nowiki> → If the player is in melee distance.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Shadow Bomb</nowiki></strong><nowiki> → If the player is outside of melee distance.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Stand anywhere on the red lines to force Tail Swipes.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/Tw4qZsT.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><ins><nowiki>Erebus Charge</nowiki></ins></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Exclusive to phase 4.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Raksha stops attacking and covers himself in a blue dome.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Deal enough damage to break the dome.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ The dome's health scales with how much anima Raksha previously absorbed from phase 3.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><ins><nowiki>Inner Power</nowiki></ins></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Extra action button, exclusive to phase 4.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Available after Erebus Charge special attack.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Upon activation, increase your damage by 10% for each shadow anima you collected.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><ins><nowiki>Shadow Anima</nowiki></ins></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Exclusive to phase 4.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• After destroying the blue dome during Erebus Charge, collectable shadow anima drop on the ground.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Collect 6-12 anima before activating Inner Power.</nowiki></p>
