<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>When Nex spawns for the first time in an instance she will be praying </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMage</nowiki>]<nowiki> for the majority of the kill, making Ranged the meta for fast consistent kills. After every kill you will teleport out of the instance and re-enter in order to force Nex to always be praying </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMage</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: It is strongly advised to complete the </nowiki></em><em><nowiki>Ritual of the Mahjarrat</nowiki></em><em><nowiki> quest in order to get access to the Stone of Jas </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1006121672790773770.png?v=1 <nowiki>jasboost</nowiki>]<nowiki> boost, which gives a +5% damage boost in GWD1 (including Nex). This can be done via Fairy ring code D.K.Q.</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689501908628799488.png?v=1 <nowiki>mob</nowiki>]<nowiki> should be combo'd with either </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073087809617931.png?v=1 <nowiki>biting4</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478090603026710648.png?v=1 <nowiki>ultimatums4</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689502339891331093.png?v=1 <nowiki>as1</nowiki>]<nowiki> is not recommended with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073088769982475.png?v=1 <nowiki>p6</nowiki>]<nowiki> here as it can proc on the Umbra phase transition and null the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1006119102814887957.png?v=1 <nowiki>perfectequilibrium</nowiki>]<nowiki> hit.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Due to this, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073088769982475.png?v=1 <nowiki>p6</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712244800924942396.png?v=1 <nowiki>ruthless1</nowiki>]<nowiki> is recommended instead.</nowiki></p>
