</nowiki><h2 id="general-information"><ins><nowiki>General Information</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Osseous has 350,000 LP, across a single fight phase.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Osseous is immune to Ranged, Melee and Magic damage.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="fight-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Fight Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Avoid using Darkness </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809209782698024.png?v=1 <nowiki>darkness</nowiki>]<nowiki> at Osseous as it will often negate your defensive abilities, for example using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> on a big hit to heal you may not work.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="attack-rotation"><ins><nowiki>Attack Rotation</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>2x auto → 1x </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> → 1x auto → </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Minion Summon</nowiki></strong><nowiki> →</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>3x auto → 1x </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Minion Launch</nowiki></strong><nowiki> → 2x auto &gt; </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Osseous’ Roar</nowiki></strong><nowiki> →</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="auto-attacks"><ins><nowiki>Auto Attacks</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Osseous attacks primarily with Necromancy damage, with some Ranged attacks.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If camping prayers, use a Necromancy protection prayer (</nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809111178813471.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectnecromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137941136397324448.png?v=1 <nowiki>protectfromnecro</nowiki>]<nowiki>)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> once you are comfortable surviving mechanics</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="minion-summon-example"><ins><nowiki>Minion Summon</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/QGYxu2jdtk.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/QGYxu2jdtk.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Osseous slams the ground, dealing heavy melee damage and summoning baby skeletons that can heal her.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> to heal from the melee hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If Resonance is unavailable, pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1052551452788269127.png?v=1 <nowiki>protectfrommelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Alternatively, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be used to reduce damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ignore the skeletons initially and prepare for the next mechanic</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="minion-launch-example"><ins><nowiki>Minion Launch</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/YJ6LKwQinf.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/YJ6LKwQinf.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Osseous throws minions at you, stunning you and spawning additional skeletons.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stay targeted on Osseous</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Clear the skeletons using any of these approaches:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434801938432010.png?v=1 <nowiki>fingerofdeath</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809191231295549.png?v=1 <nowiki>commandzombie</nowiki>]<nowiki> (after the stun)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Three casts of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809145706319892.png?v=1 <nowiki>spectralscythe</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Target and kill any remaining skeletons to prevent explosion damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="osseous-roar-example"><ins><nowiki>Osseous’ Roar</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/Hti5nPqCgd.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/Hti5nPqCgd.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> You are pulled into melee distance and bound in place.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure all skeletons are cleared before this mechanic</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Prepare defensives if damage overlaps with other mechanics</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="munch-example"><ins><nowiki>Munch</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/txcpikDFS4.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/txcpikDFS4.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Osseous binds you and prepares a high-damage bite attack.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Deal damage to fill the bar above Osseous</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use any damage-negating defensive, like </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334377648130.png?v=1 <nowiki>divert</nowiki>]<nowiki> to completely block</nowiki></p>
