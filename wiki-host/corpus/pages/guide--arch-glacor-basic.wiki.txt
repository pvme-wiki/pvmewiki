<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="general-information"><ins><nowiki>General Information</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Arch-Glacor can be fought in Normal Mode (NM) or Hard Mode (HM)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• NM can be fought in a group, but HM can only be fought solo</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Arch-Glacor's life points vary based on mode and enrage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Glacor </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107344935923743.png?v=1 <nowiki>glacor</nowiki>]<nowiki> slayer assignment applies to NM only</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="fight-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Fight Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Arch-Glacor randomly cycles through the 5 mechanics listed below</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Each mechanic can only occur once per cycle, meaning a mechanic can </nowiki><em><nowiki>only</nowiki></em><nowiki> occur back-to-back if it is the last mechanic of a cycle and the first mechanic of the next</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In NM, mechanics can be toggled on or off to learn the fight, except for Creeping Ice</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In HM, all mechanics are enabled and enrage increases with every kill, with difficulty increasing too</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="auto-attacks-ranged-example-magic-example"><ins><nowiki>Auto Attacks</nowiki></ins><nowiki> (</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/XZgiuE5XNB.mp4 <nowiki>Ranged example</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/XZgiuE5XNB.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/BtHNY8ND13.mp4 <nowiki>Magic example</nowiki>]<nowiki>)</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/BtHNY8ND13.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Arch-Glacor primarily uses Magic and Ranged auto-attacks, with stronger style-swapping auto-attacks during </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Flurry</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray against the correct attack style </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmage</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488317046812.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectrange</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541278264393729.png?v=1 <nowiki>debil</nowiki>]<nowiki> if taking heavy damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="glacyte-minions-example"><ins><nowiki>Glacyte Minions</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/kSB6UcI.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/kSB6UcI.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Arch-Glacor becomes immune and summons 4 Glacyte minions. Each minion drops an unstable core on death.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>At 250%+ HM enrage, a tougher Bolstered Glacyte also spawns (however, if this is the first mechanic in the fight, this Bolstered Glacyte does </nowiki><em><nowiki>not</nowiki></em><nowiki> spawn).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use area of effect attacks to clear the minions </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809149690916945.png?v=1 <nowiki>spectralscythe2</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063106914779166.png?v=1 <nowiki>gchain</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063454173532242.png?v=1 <nowiki>magmatemptest</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532878969438210.png?v=1 <nowiki>cane</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879283879977.png?v=1 <nowiki>gflurry</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061867288100885.png?v=1 <nowiki>ricochet</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306391265284.png?v=1 <nowiki>bombard</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Click the unstable cores only once </nowiki><strong><nowiki>all</nowiki></strong><nowiki> minions are dead</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="flurry-example"><ins><nowiki>Flurry</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/0CU0qXf.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/0CU0qXf.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Arch-Glacor's auto-attacks hit harder and rotate combat styles, with its Melee hit the strongest.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray against each combat style accordingly </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMage</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488317046812.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectRange</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMelee</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541278264393729.png?v=1 <nowiki>debil</nowiki>]<nowiki> for additional damage reduction</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334377648130.png?v=1 <nowiki>divert</nowiki>]<nowiki> on melee swipes where appropriate</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="pillars-of-ice-example"><ins><nowiki>Pillars of Ice</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/omGXX0a.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/omGXX0a.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Creeping Ice forces you to one end of the arena, then moving beams cross the arena. Contact with a beam deals heavy damage and stuns you </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289428492369.png?v=1 <nowiki>stunicon</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Move around the beams using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Watch the example above for one safe path through the mechanic</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In HM, an additional beam appears after the second beam</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="exposed-core-example"><ins><nowiki>Exposed Core</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/j3d2YIxcr7.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/j3d2YIxcr7.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Arch-Glacor traps you in the centre of the arena, between its arms. The core deals increasing Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Kill </nowiki><em><nowiki>either</nowiki></em><nowiki> arm to escape</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use strong damaging abilities to break the arm quickly</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMage</nowiki>]<nowiki> to reduce damage if needed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="frost-cannon-example"><ins><nowiki>Frost Cannon</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/20mFMPA.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/20mFMPA.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Creeping Ice traps you at one end of the arena, then Arch-Glacor fires a beam dealing heavy Magic damage. In HM, the beam hits 3 times and the first hit stuns you </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289428492369.png?v=1 <nowiki>stunicon</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Prioritise blocking the Magic attack(s) with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> before the beam in HM to prevent the stun</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If you miss this, don't panic and use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Example rotations:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• In NM: use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> to heal from the single hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• In HM:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMage</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/9t9EdxK.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/9t9EdxK.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMage</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> the final hit </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/9PyGvb4.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/9PyGvb4.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</p>
