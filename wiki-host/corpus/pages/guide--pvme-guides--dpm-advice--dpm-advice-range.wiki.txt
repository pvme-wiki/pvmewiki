<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="core-ranged-rotation"><nowiki>&gt; </nowiki><ins><nowiki>Core Ranged Rotation</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]</h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This section covers rotations using core style abilities before later unlocks are obtained</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>See </nowiki><ins><strong><nowiki>Advanced Ranged Rotations</nowiki></strong></ins><nowiki> section further down for info on ranged rotations using best-in-slot gear</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>A typical Ranged rotation uses the strongest Basic Abilities available to build adrenaline, and spends it on the strongest available Enhanced Abilities, Ultimate Abilities, or Special Attacks</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• To achieve this, we must think about the following </nowiki><strong><nowiki>key considerations:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Basic ability patterns </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061867288100885.png?v=1 <nowiki>ricochet</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Buff usage </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Ammo effects </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/900758234527301642.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsporearrows</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="basic-pattern"><ins><nowiki>Basic Pattern</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>The basic pattern comboes Galeshot </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061864943616122.png?v=1 <nowiki>galeshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> into Rapid Fire </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> when both cooldowns are available</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061864943616122.png?v=1 <nowiki>galeshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be thought of as </nowiki><strong><nowiki>buffing </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> + 3 abilities of your choice</nowiki></strong><nowiki>, adding additional damage onto each hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Therefore, try to follow </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> with strong multi-hit abilities </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061867288100885.png?v=1 <nowiki>ricochet</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061855732666439.png?v=1 <nowiki>snapshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061860371828929.png?v=1 <nowiki>deadshot</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• [</nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061864943616122.png?v=1 <nowiki>galeshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> → 3 Abilities] → [Filler] → [</nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061864943616122.png?v=1 <nowiki>galeshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> → 3 Abilities] → repeat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ranged has other rotational synergies that help to fill empty ability slots</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Snipe </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061857486143498.png?v=1 <nowiki>snipe</nowiki>]<nowiki> + Piercing Shot </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061863723073636.png?v=1 <nowiki>piercingshot</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Imbue: Shadows </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061866193518744.png?v=1 <nowiki>imbueshadows</nowiki>]<nowiki> + Shadow Tendrils </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/642713547142332416.png?v=1 <nowiki>shadowtend</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When building adrenaline, fill with available Basic Abilities:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Ricochet </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061867288100885.png?v=1 <nowiki>ricochet</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Piercing Shot </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061863723073636.png?v=1 <nowiki>piercingshot</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Basic Attack </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061858622537809.png?v=1 <nowiki>rangedbasic</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Then spend adrenaline on Enhanced Abilites, Ultimate Abilities, and Special Attacks:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Such abilities are often categorically referred to as </nowiki><strong><nowiki>spenders</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Snap Shot </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061855732666439.png?v=1 <nowiki>snapshot</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Deadshot </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061860371828929.png?v=1 <nowiki>deadshot</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Snipe </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061857486143498.png?v=1 <nowiki>snipe</nowiki>]<nowiki> + Shadow Tendrils </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/642713547142332416.png?v=1 <nowiki>shadowtend</nowiki>]<nowiki> don't spend adrenaline but are still considered Enhanced Abilities</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If adrenaline is limited, prioritise having enough adrenaline for your next </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061864943616122.png?v=1 <nowiki>galeshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> combo</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ With these considerations, we can ensure that cooldowns are always cycling to allow for a consistent flow of strong abilities</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="deaths-swiftness"><ins><nowiki>Death's Swiftness</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>It is assumed that either the Planted Feet perk </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921597813137518.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswiftpf</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Greater Death's Swiftness unlock has been obtained </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> for the purposes of this guide.</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Death's Swiftness </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> is the main buff to prioritise whenever there is an opportunity to deal damage</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The exception to this is when the fight would end too quickly, or mechanics occur, and you can't benefit from the full duration</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Ideally, enter boss fights and important phases with 100% adrenaline so you can activate it immediately</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Death's Swiftness </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki>, continue following the basic pattern while fitting in your most powerful abilities:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• [</nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061864943616122.png?v=1 <nowiki>galeshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061867288100885.png?v=1 <nowiki>ricochet</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061860371828929.png?v=1 <nowiki>deadshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061855732666439.png?v=1 <nowiki>snapshot</nowiki>]<nowiki>] → [Fill w/ Basics and Spenders] → [</nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061864943616122.png?v=1 <nowiki>galeshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> → ...]</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ While waiting for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> cooldown, you do not want to needlessly camp 100% adrenaline, and can start building when </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> is nearly ready to use again</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="other-buffs"><ins><nowiki>Other Buffs</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Besides Death's Swiftness, Ranged has multiple supportive buffs that are meant to supplement and enhance its core rotation</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Imbue Shadows</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061866193518744.png?v=1 <nowiki>imbueshadows</nowiki>]<nowiki> grants adrenaline per hit and is extremely important to pair with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Synergises with multi-hit abilities </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061867288100885.png?v=1 <nowiki>ricochet</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061860371828929.png?v=1 <nowiki>deadshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> and is vital to maintain a consistent flow of adrenaline during your damage buffs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Keep in mind that Damage-over-Time (DoT) </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306294796299.png?v=1 <nowiki>corruptshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> does not count as a hit for the purposes of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061866193518744.png?v=1 <nowiki>imbueshadows</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Shadow Tendrils</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/642713547142332416.png?v=1 <nowiki>shadowtend</nowiki>]<nowiki> extends </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061866193518744.png?v=1 <nowiki>imbueshadows</nowiki>]<nowiki> duration and should always be used once during a full </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Deathspore Arrows</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/900758234527301642.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsporearrows</nowiki>]<nowiki> should be used to get a free </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki>, allowing for tremendously accelerated rotational setup</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 12 stacks can be obtained through a combination of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061867288100885.png?v=1 <nowiki>ricochet</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061863723073636.png?v=1 <nowiki>piercingshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> or with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> if necessary</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Start building </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/900758234527301642.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsporearrows</nowiki>]<nowiki> in advance of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> cooldown so as to not waste time once it's available</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Rare situations may not allow enough time to stack </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/900758234527301642.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsporearrows</nowiki>]<nowiki>, in which case a form of adrenaline potion </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/736298121704767538.png?v=1 <nowiki>adrenrenewal</nowiki>]<nowiki> can help with buff setup</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Because of ranged's reliance on multi-hit abilities for both damage and adrenaline, it is highly recommended to prioritise obtaining Greater Ricochet </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334812807238.png?v=1 <nowiki>grico</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Igneous Kal-Xil </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/902209626404171786.png?v=1 <nowiki>igneouskalxil</nowiki>]<nowiki> upgrades as early as possible in the style's progression</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="ammo-effects"><ins><nowiki>Ammo Effects</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Due to having considerably stronger and more consistent effects, </nowiki></em><em><nowiki>it is recommended to use bows + arrows</nowiki></em><em><nowiki> over crossbows + bolts in the current state of the game. The information in this guide will assume that the highest available tier of bow + arrows are being used.</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>When using abilities, stronger ammunition can increase your ability damage and provide additional effects</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use Ful Arrows </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/971025696958853180.png?v=1 <nowiki>fularrow</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Deathspore Arrows </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/900758234527301642.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsporearrows</nowiki>]<nowiki> together for general use</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use Black Stone Arrows </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/785031580149743617.png?v=1 <nowiki>blackstonearrow</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Wen Arrows </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/971025697046925362.png?v=1 <nowiki>wenarrows</nowiki>]<nowiki> when hit chance is needed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/971025697046925362.png?v=1 <nowiki>wenarrows</nowiki>]<nowiki> are otherwise equal to or worse than </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/971025696958853180.png?v=1 <nowiki>fularrow</nowiki>]<nowiki> unless boss mechanics provide downtime to build stacks for free</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Otherwise, use the highest tier of generic ammunition available or Jas Bane arrows </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/971025697042755584.png?v=1 <nowiki>jasdragonbanearrow</nowiki>]<nowiki> if applicable</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When using Pernix's Quiver </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/902209626450296892.png?v=1 <nowiki>pernixsquiver</nowiki>]<nowiki>, multiple keybind options are available that make ammo cycling easier</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• (1) </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Quiver Slot:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Drag the </nowiki><ins><nowiki>empty</nowiki></ins><nowiki> quiver slot from your gear interface onto an action bar to </nowiki><strong><nowiki>cycle ammo in any currently equipped quiver</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• (2) </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Specific Quiver:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Drag a specific quiver onto an action bar to </nowiki><strong><nowiki>either equip the quiver or, if already equipped, cycles its ammo</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• (3) </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Quiver ammo slot 1 + Quiver ammo slot 2:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Utility Abilities in the ranged ability book which </nowiki><strong><nowiki>cycles ammo to the selected slot</nowiki></strong></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="pvm-decision-making"><nowiki>&gt; </nowiki><ins><nowiki>PvM Decision Making</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061855732666439.png?v=1 <nowiki>snapshot</nowiki>]</h2>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="area-of-effect-aoe"><ins><nowiki>Area-of-Effect (AoE)</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Decimation Special Attack </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643848618477879328.png?v=1 <nowiki>decimation</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> is ranged's most powerful and versatile AoE tool</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Moderate 3-tile AoE search range with no cooldown, a minor adrenaline cost, and can be set up before enemies spawn</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Combo with powerful abilities like </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061860371828929.png?v=1 <nowiki>deadshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1446178203608809472.png?v=1 <nowiki>gloomfirebow</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> to quickly deal with AoE scenarios</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Every additional hit counts for the purposes of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061866193518744.png?v=1 <nowiki>imbueshadows</nowiki>]<nowiki> adrenaline gain, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/900758234527301642.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsporearrows</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994189289623662702.png?v=1 <nowiki>bolg</nowiki>]<nowiki> stack generation, and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061863723073636.png?v=1 <nowiki>piercingshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> reduction on </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061857486143498.png?v=1 <nowiki>snipe</nowiki>]<nowiki> cooldown</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ If </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061866193518744.png?v=1 <nowiki>imbueshadows</nowiki>]<nowiki> is not active, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061857486143498.png?v=1 <nowiki>snipe</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061863723073636.png?v=1 <nowiki>piercingshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be an adrenaline-efficient way to make use of the effect</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Most often used from an Essence of Finality amulet (EoF) </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/745279787471470713.png?v=1 <nowiki>eof</nowiki>]<nowiki> to allow use with higher tier weapons</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Bombardment </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306391265284.png?v=1 <nowiki>bombard</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be used to quickly deal with low life point targets</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Upfront damage that doesn't require a prior ability cast to set up, but has more restrictive 2-tile AoE search range</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Outclassed by </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643848618477879328.png?v=1 <nowiki>decimation</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> if enemies would live for 3 or more abilities, or are spread further apart from each other</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Low Priority or Highly Situational</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Ricochet </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061867288100885.png?v=1 <nowiki>ricochet</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334812807238.png?v=1 <nowiki>grico</nowiki>]<nowiki> has a large 5-tile AoE search range, but does low damage and is generally not used for AoE purposes</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Corruption Shot </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306294796299.png?v=1 <nowiki>corruptshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> also has a 5-tile AoE search range, but should only ever be used for that specific aspect of it</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Due to being DoT, does not benefit from damage buffs </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki>, but can deal moderate damage while under </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615618531937222657.png?v=1 <nowiki>ecb</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]</p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1446178203608809472.png?v=1 <nowiki>gloomfirebow</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> is overall better in most cases, but </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643848618553507843.png?v=1 <nowiki>dbow</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> is a cheaper early pickup and has a faster hit timing</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• On 5x5+ targets </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/626466665848242186.png?v=1 <nowiki>sgb</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be used flexibly for high on-demand damage</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="sustaining-with-ranged"><ins><nowiki>Sustaining with Ranged</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Ranged has to frequently lock itself out of healing </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615618531937222657.png?v=1 <nowiki>ecb</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> or inflict self-damage </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/642713547142332416.png?v=1 <nowiki>shadowtend</nowiki>]<nowiki> in order to maximise damage output</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ To plan around a lack of sustain, consider the following:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Prioritise lossless defensive and healing tools </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641337999170207763.png?v=1 <nowiki>excal</nowiki>]<nowiki> as your first resort</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Familiarise yourself with boss mechanics to anticipate periods of heavy or little incoming damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Optionally introduce </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615618531937222657.png?v=1 <nowiki>ecb</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> into rotations </nowiki><em><nowiki>after</nowiki></em><nowiki> becoming comfortable at a boss</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Leave a few abilities of downtime in between </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615618531937222657.png?v=1 <nowiki>ecb</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> to give yourself a chance to heal up</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ It is mainly important that it overlaps with your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061864943616122.png?v=1 <nowiki>galeshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> pattern</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Drop </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615618531937222657.png?v=1 <nowiki>ecb</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> if unable to maintain sufficient </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809168368148490.png?v=1 <nowiki>splitsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki> uptime for it to be worthwhile i.e. during </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994914159969976440.png?v=1 <nowiki>zamorak</nowiki>]<nowiki> twinshot</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Replace </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/642713547142332416.png?v=1 <nowiki>shadowtend</nowiki>]<nowiki> with a better spender if you don't actually need the extension on </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061866193518744.png?v=1 <nowiki>imbueshadows</nowiki>]</p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>A tip to quickly stack 12 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/900758234527301642.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsporearrows</nowiki>]<nowiki> at the start of a fight</nowiki></strong><nowiki> is to stall either </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334812807238.png?v=1 <nowiki>grico</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061860371828929.png?v=1 <nowiki>deadshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> in War's Retreat, then releasing with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334812807238.png?v=1 <nowiki>grico</nowiki>]<nowiki> in the boss instance</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="utilising-stalls-in-combat"><ins><nowiki>Utilising Stalls in Combat</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Multiple ranged abilities provide a duration-based buff that starts counting down when they are cast/released. Therefore, we can stall these abilities and release with the next ability to &quot;extend&quot; their buffs and gain other benefits.</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ranged usually has to exit its attack range in order to stall, which can be challenging depending on the situation</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Therefore, it will typically only be recommended to use these tricks if the boss allows you to</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258832052231.png?v=1 <nowiki>escape</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> make it easier to temporarily exit attack range</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Against stationary bosses </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1400943309576470589.png?v=1 <nowiki>amascuthead</nowiki>]<nowiki>, you can simply find where your maximum attack range is and take a step back to stall abilities</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Galeshot</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061864943616122.png?v=1 <nowiki>galeshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be stalled and released with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> to extend its buff duration to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> + 4 abilities rather than 3</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This is useful to gain a bit of extra damage, or a lot if you are planning to use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334812807238.png?v=1 <nowiki>grico</nowiki>]<nowiki> as the 4th ability</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>BoLG Special Attack</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994189289623662702.png?v=1 <nowiki>bolg</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be stalled to extend the tail-end on its duration by an extra ability</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This allows time for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1006119102814887957.png?v=1 <nowiki>perfectequilibrium</nowiki>]<nowiki> alignment usually in the second </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Otherwise, rotations typically have to pick between using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994189289623662702.png?v=1 <nowiki>bolg</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615618531937222657.png?v=1 <nowiki>ecb</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> first in </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki>, which each have their tradeoffs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
