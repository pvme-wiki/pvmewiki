</nowiki><h2 id="handling-grey-hp"><ins><nowiki>Handling Grey HP</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Tips to reduce grey HP regeneration:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Grey HP healing is based on the total amount of time spent in grey HP over the course of the entire fight. As such, you should always focus on </nowiki><strong><nowiki>charging the next pad as soon as possible</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/903244090953588787.png?v=1 <nowiki>ezk</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> in combination with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478062908767211640.png?v=1 <nowiki>chaosroar</nowiki>]<nowiki> should keep grey HP below 150k at all times.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ DoT abilities (</nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063107908702482.png?v=1 <nowiki>combust</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478062910113714297.png?v=1 <nowiki>dismember</nowiki>]<nowiki>) will partially prevent healing if grey HP is above 150k</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In order to do this phase correctly, aim for the following:</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Allows you to deal with the demon + witch at the same time, removing the need to re-enter Infernus again</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="troubleshooting"><ins><nowiki>Troubleshooting</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Struggling to get the desired spec order while following the listed rotations is usually an indication that too much time is spent inbetween phases</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Aim to get the next pad charged as quickly as possible from the moment Zamorak enters grey HP, allowing time for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869284271595069451.png?v=1 <nowiki>fsoa</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> to finish and using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478062908767211640.png?v=1 <nowiki>chaosroar</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/903244090953588787.png?v=1 <nowiki>ezk</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Efficient movement between pads is important, on some phases it's possible to already be on the next pad by the time you phase Zamorak and use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1073955377537876089.png?v=1 <nowiki>realmmovement</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You may want to flick </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/690976219612708954.png?v=1 <nowiki>arcanespiritshield</nowiki>]<nowiki> as you set up on each pad. Once the pad is charged you should be able to burst down Zamorak's red HP within 1-2 autos, keeping the incoming damage within manageable ranges.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If you fail to skip red HP mechs on phase 5 or 6, focus on following the alternative spec order from </nowiki>[[PvME/Zamorak/Zamorak|<nowiki>Zamorak</nowiki>]]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You can </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306353778689.png?v=1 <nowiki>cade</nowiki>]<nowiki> Flames of Zamorak on phase 5, and then </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> the smoke with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki> active</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If you get Chaos Blast on phase 6 red HP, you should be able to kill Zamorak before he releases the bomb, otherwise simply </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258538582017.png?v=1 <nowiki>immort</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Once entering phase 7, the kill is basically guaranteed, take it slow and safe if necessary</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You can take as long as you want setting up on phase 7 as the red HP bar is completely irrelevant</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Avoid getting close to the Chaos Demon, you could get hit by his melee swipe as you get hit by Zamorak's mage autos</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• There is almost no benefit from staying below 60% HP as you will already overkill Zamorak with the listed rotation</nowiki></p>
