</nowiki><h2 id="contributing-in-its-simplest-form"><ins><nowiki>Contributing in its Simplest Form</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Making a post in </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/1020853673317908500 <nowiki>Suggestions (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki>. Someone else can see that post and turn it into the edit you want to see added to PvME. For example:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pointing out typos/rotation changes in guides</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Writing down strategies for a guide that doesn't exist yet</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="the-first-steps-of-using-pvmes-editor-tools"><ins><nowiki>The First Steps of Using PvME's Editor Tools</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>These are the most commonly used tools that are used by editors who want to be a bit more involved in making their edits happen:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://pvme.io/guide-editor/ <nowiki>https://pvme.io/guide-editor/</nowiki>]<nowiki>:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This is the most important tool editors use. It lets them visualise what the text would look like in discord.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Check out </nowiki>[[PvME/Contributing/Guide Editor|<nowiki>Guide Editor</nowiki>]]<nowiki> for a tutorial on how to use it, or </nowiki>[https://youtu.be/cPWgpRLsqGU <nowiki>a youtube video showing off how easy it is to use</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You can load the guide's text content into the guide editor by using the </nowiki><code><nowiki>/files</nowiki></code><nowiki> ‎command in discord and searching for the guide you want to edit, and clicking on the guide editor link the bot responds with.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/771436827214086154 <nowiki>Guide media upload and request (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki>:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• A simple command </nowiki><code><nowiki>/upload</nowiki></code><nowiki> ‎is all it takes to turn your picture into a usable link.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
