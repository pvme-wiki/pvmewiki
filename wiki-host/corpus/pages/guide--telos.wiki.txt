</nowiki><h2 id="enrage-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Enrage Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>50%</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Virus is added on P2 and P3</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>100%</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Phase 5</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>150%</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Telos will freedom stuns and binds with a 50 tick cooldown</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>200%</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Telos HP caps at 600,000 on P1-4</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>250%</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Telos will freedom stuns and binds with a 31 tick cooldown, and P5 will be stun-immune after freedom for 10 ticks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>300%</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Telos HP caps at 200,000 on P5</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>500%</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: P4 font HP caps at 10,000</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1000%</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: P4 a rock will fall, P5 multiple rocks will fall, immortality no longer works P5, and Telos will freedom stuns and binds with a 16 tick cooldown</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The higher the enrage the stronger the damage Telos and golems deal.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><div id="block-phase1"><u><nowiki>Phase 1</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Auto-Attacks</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>Telos attacks with </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/e6LpKti.mp4 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> when the player is in melee distance, otherwise </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/e6LpKti.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/wMhIhgf.mp4 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki>. If you leave MD, Telos will do one mage auto before walking up to you again.
</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/wMhIhgf.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Tendrils</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Telos binds you in place, drains both your health and adrenaline, and heals himself for every hit the player takes. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/n92IQzM.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
        ⬩ Deal (3,000 + 10 x Enrage) damage to free yourself.
</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/n92IQzM.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Charge</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Telos charges at the player after a short delay, dealing soft-typeless damage. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/iUIAuba.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
        ⬩ Use </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/iUIAuba.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258832052231.png?v=1 <nowiki>escape</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> 1 tick after the text above Telos disappears.
        ⬩ Resort to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> if necessary.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Hold Still</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Telos hits the player with a normal attack followed by a large melee hit that stuns, then jumps to the spot the player was standing on. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/fKhoh6P.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • The normal attack can be either a melee or a magic hit, depending on how far the player is.
        ⬩ </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/fKhoh6P.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> beforehand to protect against the stun.
        ⬩ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> the large melee hit.
        ⬩ Move out of the jump radius.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Green Beam</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Spawns at a random location.
    • When the player is standing in the beam, they gain rapid adrenaline.
    • When Telos is standing in the beam, he attacks 1 tick faster.
        ⬩ Phase boss before beam comes out.
</nowiki></div>

</nowiki><div id="block-phase2"><u><nowiki>Phase 2</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Auto-Attacks</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>Same as phase one: melee when in MD, otherwise mage. Again, if you move away, Telos will do one mage auto then walk back into MD.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Black Beam</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Spawns on Telos.
    • When the player is standing in the beam, they deal and take 30% less damage.
    • When Telos is standing in the beam, the phase meter charges and he receives 20% less damage.
        ⬩ Pull Telos out of the beam and avoid standing in it unless necessary.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Magic Onslaught</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Telos stops moving and fires gradually stronger magic attacks until the phase meter is depleted.
    • Move Telos out of the beam before this attack starts so the bar doesn't fill too much.
    • If Telos starts this attack while in the beam, block it by standing in it.
        ⬩ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMage</nowiki>]<nowiki>, resort to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki> and/or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> if the meter was charged too much.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Virus</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ The player receives a coloured virus that deals constant damage.
        ⬩ Stand in the beam to cleanse the virus.
</nowiki></div>

<div><div><u><nowiki>Red Beam</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Spawns at a random location.
    • When the player is standing in the beam, they deal and take 30% increased damage.
    • When Telos is standing in the beam, the phase meter charges and he deals 30% increased damage.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Phase Meter and Golems</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Meter naturally decreases.
⬥ Meter increases when Telos is standing in the beam.
⬥ With 1 golem alive, the meter slowly decreases.
⬥ With 2 golems alive, the meter remains stationary (increases and decreases back and fourth).
⬥ With 3 golems alive, the meter increases.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Shockwaves and Drain</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ If meter is on the left side → </nowiki><strong><u><nowiki>Drain:</nowiki></u></strong><nowiki> Telos drains the player's adrenaline and prayer points. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/IrOrcBM.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ If meter is on the right side → </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/IrOrcBM.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]<strong><u><nowiki>Shockwaves:</nowiki></u></strong><nowiki> Rapid, hard hitting soft-typeless attacks. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/GNj9g8i.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
        ⬩ Phase Telos before he could use Shockwaves, otherwise resort to </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/GNj9g8i.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki>

</nowiki><div id="block-phase4"><u><nowiki>Phase 4</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Auto-Attacks</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>Telos attacks with melee when the player is in melee distance, otherwise magic. Telos will </nowiki><strong><nowiki>not</nowiki></strong><nowiki> re-enter MD if you move away from him on P4 after his first attack.
⬥ Phase 4 is split into 4 smaller sections, separated by 3 fonts.
    • Deal a quarter of Telos's starting phase HP to phase him into a font.
    • Once phased, you lose your target, Telos becomes untargetable, walks up to a font and spawns 3 different-coloured golems.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Golems</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Three different-coloured melee golems spawn at each font. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/Pqb71gY.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Red golems attacks are able to stun after their first 3 hits.
        ⬩ Utilise </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/Pqb71gY.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki>, resort to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> if you got stunned.
    • Red golems have normal attack range.
    • Green and black golems have halberd attack range.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Fonts</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ To block Telos's instakill, players must walk up to and charge the fonts with their HP, for a total of 5 hits while dealing with golems. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/mBqSukd.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
        ⬩ Utilise </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/mBqSukd.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> to reduce damage taken from fonts.
        ⬩ Relevant defensives do NOT work on the last hit of fonts.
        ⬩ Once the instakill is negated, all of your defensive abilities get placed on a 6s cooldown.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Font 3</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Wrap around the center of the room and wait for the three golems to spawn.
    • When the golems reach you, (tc) furthest golem → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/567727987274022924.png?v=1 <nowiki>ent</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159194259467.png?v=1 <nowiki>corruptblast</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki>
        ⬩ See example </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/dyeeCcK.mp4 <nowiki>here</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Charge the font and phase Telos into P5.
</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/dyeeCcK.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Weak Anima Bomb</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Telos fires a slow moving orb that deals soft-typeless damage. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/rOCPDcD.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • The player's overload timer is constantly drained after getting hit until cleansed.
        ⬩ Ideally skip the mechanic, otherwise block using </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/rOCPDcD.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki>
        ⬩ Cleanse the overload debuff by stepping into one of the fonts.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>So Much Power (SMP)</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Once phase meter is filled, the next special attack is replaced by an SMP. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/Ft9mmv4.mp4 <nowiki>Example</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ Telos fires a deadly soft-typeless attack paired with a normal attack (much like Hold Still).
        ⬩ Ideally skip the mechanic, otherwise block with </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/Ft9mmv4.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki>

</nowiki><div id="block-phase5"><u><nowiki>Phase 5</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Auto-Attacks</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>Telos only attacks with magic throughout this phase.
</nowiki><em><nowiki>Note: Telos attacks 1 tick faster if green beam is not blocked by the player.</nowiki></em><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Strategy</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Telos spawns 6 similar-coloured minions at the start of the phase.
⬥ Make your way to the green beam.
    • Lure golems properly.
        ⬩ To do this, always start by going to the left beam.
        ⬩ If side beams → enter the 2nd tile in the green beam.
        ⬩ If back beam → enter the 4th tile in the beam.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Rock</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ A single rock falls at certain times during the phase.
        ⬩ Utilise </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Virus</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ The player receives a random-coloured virus that deals constant damage.
    • To cleanse the virus stand in the beam of the same colour.
        ⬩ Utilise </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541278264393729.png?v=1 <nowiki>debil</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Phase Meter</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>

</nowiki><h2 id="stunning-guide"><ins><nowiki>Stunning Guide</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Mage has access to 3 different stun abilities:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809655873556.png?v=1 <nowiki>impact</nowiki>]<nowiki> Stuns for 1 GCD (3ticks/1.8s)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833139912724.png?v=1 <nowiki>deep</nowiki>]<nowiki> Stuns for 1 GCD + 2 ticks (5ticks/3s)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]<nowiki> Stuns for the first 3 hits. (5ticks/3s)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Chain these abilities however you see fit when stunning Telos.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If not 4-ticking with mage, ideally leave </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833139912724.png?v=1 <nowiki>deep</nowiki>]<nowiki> for last.</nowiki></p>
