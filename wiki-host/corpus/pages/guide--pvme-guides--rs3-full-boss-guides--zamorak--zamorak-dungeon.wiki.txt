</nowiki><h3 id="introduction"><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Zamorak, Lord of Chaos is the fifth and final instalment in the Elder God Wars. This guide will primarily focus on clearing the dungeon segment, for help defeating the boss visit </nowiki>[[PvME/Zamorak/Zamorak|<nowiki>Zamorak</nowiki>]]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="general-notes-for-senntisten-undercity-zamorak"><ins><nowiki>General Notes for Senntisten Undercity + Zamorak</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Lucky charms for the dungeon can be bought from Thoksdottir at Daemonheim</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ A </nowiki><strong><nowiki>LOT</nowiki></strong><nowiki> of mobs can be ignored throughout the dungeon, but note that </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Menders and Dark Wizards will heal mobs/drain your adrenaline</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The only required minibosses are the Jailer on the second floor (who teleports to different floors after 50k damage dealt x Team Size) and Eterna being the second boss before Zamorak.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ A Demon / Greater Demon slayer task will affect a great number of mobs present in the dungeon.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Kuradal offers the best chance for this task.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><h2 id="mefis-the-jailer"><ins><nowiki>Mefis the Jailer</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/sZ3U9n3.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Mefis the Jailer is the first boss of the dungeon, this boss is a typical &quot;mini-boss&quot; styled encounter, dealing magic auto-attacks and low upfront damage to the player.</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="eterna"><ins><nowiki>Eterna</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/wgsSSmr.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Eterna is the last boss of the dungeon, this boss has multiple high hitting mechanics and does alternating magic / ranged auto-attacks throughout the fight.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>

</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div></div>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>



</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Phase 1</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Deal damage until Eterna phases
    • Eterna will spawn a single Chaos Demon Savage after phasing, kill them to move into phase 2.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Phase 2</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Eterna re-appears with full HP along with spawning spiked-circles on the ground, deal damage until phase HP again to move on.
    • Circles can be dealt with by using defensives or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ Magic bombs will also spawn from the red portal in the center dealing low magic damage.
⬥ Two Chaos Demon Savages will spawn kill them to proceed to phase 3.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Phase 3</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Eterna re-appears with all mechanics listed previously, deal damage until she is dead to move on to the next room.
⬥ The red portal in the center fires a spout into the player, dealing no damage and placing a bar over their head.
    • When this bar depletes, a 5x5 area around the player will become engulfed in smoke, dealing more damage the longer you stand inside.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
