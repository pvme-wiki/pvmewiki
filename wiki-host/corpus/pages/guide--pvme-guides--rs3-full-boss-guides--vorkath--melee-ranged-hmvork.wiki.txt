</nowiki><h3 id="notes"><nowiki>Notes</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073088589627505.png?v=1 <nowiki>eq4</nowiki>]<nowiki> is used over </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073087809617931.png?v=1 <nowiki>biting4</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Loading into Fort Forinthry is very intensive, potentially losing </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/603979368850653216.png?v=1 <nowiki>dba</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> time. The following reduces load times:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Reducing draw distance to Low</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Turning off textures</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1448070604937891921.png?v=1 <nowiki>amhej</nowiki>]<nowiki> actually does matter for a lot of damage. It's imperative to treat </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/779048041088024606.png?v=1 <nowiki>dragonclaw</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> as a switch, not </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1448070604937891921.png?v=1 <nowiki>amhej</nowiki>]<nowiki> as a switch.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If you end up using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879334080517.png?v=1 <nowiki>overpower</nowiki>]<nowiki> when Zemouregal has around 70k HP left, move </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/796989663051120640.png?v=1 <nowiki>gmaul</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> up and use it with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879334080517.png?v=1 <nowiki>overpower</nowiki>]<nowiki> to instantly KO Zemouregal. Alternatively, if you get an early </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/902209626412560395.png?v=1 <nowiki>scriptureofful</nowiki>]<nowiki> proc and 3+ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1219291285358120990.png?v=1 <nowiki>primordialice</nowiki>]<nowiki> stacks for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/883134308146098227.png?v=1 <nowiki>lengmh</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki>, you can replace </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879334080517.png?v=1 <nowiki>overpower</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879439069184.png?v=1 <nowiki>punish</nowiki>]<nowiki> and two </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/796989663051120640.png?v=1 <nowiki>gmaul</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
