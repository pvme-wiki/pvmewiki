</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Soulgazers </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107315521257572.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulgazer</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be killed in the Wilderness, the stalker dungeon in Zamorak's hideout, and the Sunken Pyramid. The Sunken Pyramid is easier to loot in and has no cannonball cost, but the Wilderness spawns can drop </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1006481325076787240.png?v=1 <nowiki>revenantdropenhancer</nowiki>]</p>

</nowiki><h2 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Wilderness (level 27 Wilderness)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place and load the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869283142350041098.png?v=1 <nowiki>cannon</nowiki>]<nowiki> in the open area a few tiles east of the lava pool</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Northeast of the level 18 obelisk, accessible via </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137735861937655900.png?v=1 <nowiki>portableobelisk</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/qGhOmREHX2.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ Sunken Pyramid (south-eastern room, 5 spawns)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• In the small room, position yourself six tiles south of the northern statue (northeastern corner of the brazier's shadow)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You can teleport there with a teleport to house tablet, or with a Slayer Codex </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1097517249499254875.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayercodex</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="method-necromancy"><ins><nowiki>Method: Necromancy</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Soulgazers can be effectively AFK'd in the Sunken Pyramid and the Wilderness using Necromancy due to its high damage output and sustain from </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434409913634816.png?v=1 <nowiki>commandghost</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1494069423047442573.png?v=1 <nowiki>vampyrismaspect</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="sunken-pyramid"><nowiki>Sunken Pyramid</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Elite soulgazers should be focused when they spawn as they have KO potential when ignored</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmage</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137940996391452772.png?v=1 <nowiki>ruination</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Sustain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/656426717505650708.png?v=1 <nowiki>ancientshard</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/928221126360969226.png?v=1 <nowiki>powderofpenance</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289831800882.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovl</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689525476158472288.png?v=1 <nowiki>weppoison</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/925794592199147581.png?v=1 <nowiki>aggressionpotion</nowiki>]<nowiki> are active at all times, drinking a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/547603714681339915.png?v=1 <nowiki>superprayerrenewal</nowiki>]<nowiki> dose whenever you drink a weapon poison</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure that </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1494069423047442573.png?v=1 <nowiki>vampyrismaspect</nowiki>]<nowiki> is active at all times.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Set the autofire rate of your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/929773044296216666.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloodreaver</nowiki>]<nowiki> to 15</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="wilderness"><nowiki>Wilderness</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Elite soulgazers should be focused when they spawn as they have KO potential when ignored</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmage</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137941003895046284.png?v=1 <nowiki>sorrow</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137940996391452772.png?v=1 <nowiki>ruination</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Sustain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/656426717505650708.png?v=1 <nowiki>ancientshard</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/928221126360969226.png?v=1 <nowiki>powderofpenance</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289831800882.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovl</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689525476158472288.png?v=1 <nowiki>weppoison</nowiki>]<nowiki> are active at all times, drinking a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/547603714681339915.png?v=1 <nowiki>superprayerrenewal</nowiki>]<nowiki> dose whenever you drink a weapon poison</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Soulgazer's in the Wilderness are permanently aggressive, so </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/925794592199147581.png?v=1 <nowiki>aggressionpotion</nowiki>]<nowiki> is </nowiki><strong><nowiki>not</nowiki></strong><nowiki> required</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure that </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1494069423047442573.png?v=1 <nowiki>vampyrismaspect</nowiki>]<nowiki> is active at all times.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Set the autofire rate of your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/929773044296216666.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloodreaver</nowiki>]<nowiki> based on how much damage you're taking and calibrate accordingly</nowiki></p>
