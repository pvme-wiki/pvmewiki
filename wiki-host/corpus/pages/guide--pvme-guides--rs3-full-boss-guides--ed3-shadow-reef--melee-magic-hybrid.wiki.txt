<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Rotations below are more of suggestions to give an idea of AoE opportunities and ability prioritisation at different parts of the dungeon.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If you find yourself needing more abilities to clear mobs due factors such as the lack of adren or unlucky hits it is expected.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Target cycling and using basics is a good way to check and finish off leftover mobs as sometimes their bodies take a while to disappear after dying.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ It's recommended to set your anti-spam delay for your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> to 1 via the Lectern at Anachronia.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You can </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> a tick before each gate unlocks to save a tick on every entry.</nowiki></p>
