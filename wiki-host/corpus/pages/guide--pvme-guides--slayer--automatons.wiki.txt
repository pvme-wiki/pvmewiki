</nowiki><h2 id="strategy"><ins><nowiki>Strategy</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For Tracers and Generators the core strategy is the same: back yourself into the corner to minimize the chance of getting spec'd by 2 automatons at the same time and taking rapid, heavy damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For Guardians, who roam the middle of the room, it is best to stand near the doorway.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The special timer starts counting from the time when the automatons are damaged.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Be careful when using &quot;chaining&quot; abilities ( </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/867678153882861568.png?v=1 <nowiki>gchaincaroming</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334812807238.png?v=1 <nowiki>grico</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]<nowiki> ) as they will aggro a large number of enemies at once.</nowiki></p>
