<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>As a very old boss, Kalphite Queen is relatively unrewarding and relatively simple. Typically only done for the pet or collection log, there is no further reason for a high level account to do this boss. The only notable mechanic is her form change. She will spawn into the instance immune to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]<nowiki>. After the first 40k damage is dealt, she will change forms to her flying form, which is immune to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki>. More information available here: </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Kalphite_Queen <nowiki>https://runescape.wiki/w/Kalphite_Queen</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>If doing the boss on a low level account for a Reaper task, bring your best </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> equipment, along with some food and poison protection.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>If farming the boss for log on a high level account, you can do it actively for </nowiki><strong><nowiki>88+ KPH</nowiki></strong><nowiki> or AFK for </nowiki><strong><nowiki>40-45 KPH</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><h2 id="active-strategy"><ins><nowiki>Active Strategy</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You will be instance hopping between the uninstanced encounter and an instanced encounter.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This method will work better if you have a friend in the instance to eliminate the wait for the initial spawn.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Uninstanced encounter rotation:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>First form:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879250456578.png?v=1 <nowiki>gbarge</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532855191928842.png?v=1 <nowiki>assault</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/959089455640215633.png?v=1 <nowiki>overpowerigneous</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644356671737966.png?v=1 <nowiki>chaosroar</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879325822986.png?v=1 <nowiki>deci</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879330148352.png?v=1 <nowiki>destroy</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Use basics </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879376023572.png?v=1 <nowiki>dismember</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879577612298.png?v=1 <nowiki>sever</nowiki>]<nowiki> in between if entering with low adrenaline.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Waiting for second form spawn: Use basic on minion → Swap to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643846959454617610.png?v=1 <nowiki>sirenicmask</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615618531937222657.png?v=1 <nowiki>ecb</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334812807238.png?v=1 <nowiki>grico</nowiki>]<nowiki> minion → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259033378827.png?v=1 <nowiki>mds</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615618531937222657.png?v=1 <nowiki>ecb</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> if 100% adrenaline → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> minion</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Second form:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Auto + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/959089455283728386.png?v=1 <nowiki>deadshotigneous</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/626466665848242186.png?v=1 <nowiki>sgb</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334812807238.png?v=1 <nowiki>grico</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535534127131394088.png?v=1 <nowiki>snap</nowiki>]<nowiki> and/or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259033378827.png?v=1 <nowiki>mds</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Immediately climb out once KQ HP hits 0. KC and loot in the uninstanced encounter will register even if you are not in it.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• (tc) + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513201065513058306.png?v=1 <nowiki>Tuskas</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258538450944.png?v=1 <nowiki>piercing</nowiki>]<nowiki> minion + rejoin instanced encounter</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Instanced encounter rotation:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Before spawn (if entering on empty instance): </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854281764884.png?v=1 <nowiki>bd</nowiki>]<nowiki> for adren if needed.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ (tc) just after </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> runs out.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The first form and second form rotations are the same as above.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Upon KQ death, do your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> switch and (tc) + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]<nowiki> before climbing out if you're the only one in the encounter.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Climbing out immediately and leaving the instance empty before KQ finishes the dying animation will cause the kill to not count and no loot will register.</nowiki></strong><nowiki> This is avoided if you have a friend in the instance.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If missing pet, wait for the loot to appear first before climbing up.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• (tc) + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> → join uninstanced encounter</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
