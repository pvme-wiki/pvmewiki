</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide assumes you understand the basics of combat, equipment, and the RotS fight, including mechanics as explained in </nowiki>[[PvME/Rise of the Six/Overview|<nowiki>Rise Of The Six Basic</nowiki>]]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/739965637056659567.png?v=1 <nowiki>persistentrage</nowiki>]<nowiki> is a very crucial part of RotS, this relic allows you to start every kill at full adrenaline without </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/736298121704767538.png?v=1 <nowiki>adrenrenewal</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/656844963522281473.png?v=1 <nowiki>dummy</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At RotS the meta is to AoE the brothers down as fast as possible</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Most of the meta and strategy revolve around stacking and luring the brothers so that they can be killed at the same time</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="useful-unlocks"><ins><nowiki>Useful Unlocks</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Double </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854281764884.png?v=1 <nowiki>bd</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> unlocked is crucial for high kph</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Equipment with the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689501908628799488.png?v=1 <nowiki>mob</nowiki>]<nowiki> should also be used for this purpose</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Equipment with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689502804720615441.png?v=1 <nowiki>undeadslayerperk</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339922019516416.png?v=1 <nowiki>undeadslayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> for a flat 7% and 15% (for 10 seconds) respectively</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Both the perk and sigil work against the brothers</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Take note that </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797899945730244648.png?v=1 <nowiki>salveamulet</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/855865893808767016.png?v=1 <nowiki>ghosthunterhelm</nowiki>]<nowiki> do now work against the brothers</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="melee"><ins><nowiki>Melee</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/rPkClq0aEz.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ Solo should be consistently ~30 seconds if done correctly.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At 24 seconds you will get a message that says &quot;As there is no-one on one side of the shadow portal, it empowers the brothers to destroy everyone!&quot;</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Once this happens you can then kill the brothers on the side you start at</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• They will hop over and be targetable at 27 seconds</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You are required to have these buffs for this rotation to work:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Undead Slayer ability </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339922019516416.png?v=1 <nowiki>undeadslayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Undead Slayer perk </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689502804720615441.png?v=1 <nowiki>undeadslayerperk</nowiki>]</p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Undead Slayer task (reduced to 1) alongside Genocidal perk </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689503091539705870.png?v=1 <nowiki>genocidal</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Task will not deplete</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Fort Forinthry Guardhouse Slayer Upgrades</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Ghost Hunter Helm, Backpack, Legs </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/855865893808767016.png?v=1 <nowiki>ghosthunterhelm</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/855865893829476352.png?v=1 <nowiki>ghosthunterbag</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/855865893854511144.png?v=1 <nowiki>ghosthunterlegs</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Salve Amulet </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797899945730244648.png?v=1 <nowiki>salveamulet</nowiki>]</p>

</nowiki><h2 id="necromancy"><ins><nowiki>Necromancy</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Solo should be consistently sub 33.6 seconds if done correctly.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You wear </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787526151978614824.png?v=1 <nowiki>eof</nowiki>]<nowiki> before the hop so you don't overkill the brothers with the rotation.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At 24 seconds you will get a message that says &quot;As there is no-one on one side of the shadow portal, it empowers the brothers to destroy everyone!&quot;</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Once this happens you can then kill the brothers on the side you start at</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• They will hop over and be targetable at 27 seconds</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You should never start on the side in which Karil is on as he will delay the kill significantly</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The ideal brother rotation is: West - Ahrim, Guthan, Verac. East - Karil, Dharok, Torag</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Whilst waiting the 24 seconds you need to get 5 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139453152169558046.png?v=1 <nowiki>residualsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki> stacks</nowiki></p>
