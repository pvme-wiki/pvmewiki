</nowiki><h2 id="middle-path"><ins><nowiki>Middle Path</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-1_1"><nowiki>Phase 1</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Walk Araxxor against the web</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At 99% web burn, use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854281764884.png?v=1 <nowiki>bd</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> into phase 2</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-2_1"><nowiki>Phase 2</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Run across the acid pool and stand 3 tiles up the ramp</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Damage Araxxor until it has absorbed 53% acid</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854281764884.png?v=1 <nowiki>bd</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> up the platform and walk 1 tile west</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258131865633.png?v=1 <nowiki>nat</nowiki>]<nowiki> when the acid bar reaches approximately 24–19%</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Build to 100% adrenaline for phase 3</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
