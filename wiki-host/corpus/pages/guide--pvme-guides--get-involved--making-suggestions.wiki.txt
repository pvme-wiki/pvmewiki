<span id="welcome-to-suggestions"></span><p><nowiki>Here, we welcome any suggestions you want to make on improving the server! See below for steps and tips on making suggestions.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="categories"><ins><nowiki>Categories</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Guide suggestion</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - Suggestions for guide content or structure (e.g. updated rotations, deleting a redundant section).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Server suggestion</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - Suggestions for server operations or structure (e.g. improvements to the vod review process, shifting of channels).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/820143052227346442 <nowiki>Modmail (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki> for more sensitive topics.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Command suggestion</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - Suggestions about changing/introducing commands</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Typo Fix</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - Suggestions highlighting typos to be fixed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Mastery/Feats</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - Suggestions about changing/introducing feats or masteries.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="making-suggestions"><ins><nowiki>Making suggestions</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Below outlines the process and suggested steps of making suggestions.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><nowiki>Enter the title in the search bar and click </nowiki><strong><nowiki>&quot;New post&quot;</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/19lrC0CzsW.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><nowiki>Enter more details about suggestion (</nowiki><strong><nowiki>including pictures/vods can be very helpful</nowiki></strong><nowiki>) and mark your suggestion with relevant tags - </nowiki>[[PvME/Get involved/Making suggestions and editing guides#categories|<nowiki>Categories</nowiki>]]</li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/Alvvbay4ja.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><nowiki>Include as many details as possible, including but not limited to:</nowiki></li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎a. Why the suggestion is good.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎b. Possible limiting factors?</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎c. Alternative ways to implement your idea.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎d. Or even a mock-up of what it will look like once implemented.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/ynoKT4H2U6.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><nowiki>If your suggestion thread involved a long discussion, you can provide a (point-form) summary once the discussion concludes to facilitate implementation.</nowiki></li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/P0hUew9MR9.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><nowiki>If you see this message, it means an editor is working on implementing your suggestion! You can return to the thread to check on its progress in the future if you find that it's taking too long.</nowiki></li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎a. </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Remember, the fastest way to implement your ideas is always to edit PvME directly!</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Read </nowiki>[[PvME/Contributing/Intro To Editing|<nowiki>Introduction to editing</nowiki>]]<nowiki> for contributor setup.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/jfZMLxceyp.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>

</nowiki></ol>
