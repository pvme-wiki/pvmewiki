</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Ripper_Demon <nowiki>Ripper Demons</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107343123976284.png?v=1 <nowiki>ripperdemon</nowiki>]<nowiki> are one of the 4 Raptor specialty monsters, as such they boast mechanics that make engaging them more dangerous than normal mobs. They are mainly killed for Slayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> tasks, the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_collection_log#Kharidian_Desert <nowiki>Kharidian Desert collection log</nowiki>]<nowiki>, the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Parasitic_orb <nowiki>parasitic orb</nowiki>]<nowiki> which is used to unlock the Nipper demon pet, and for profit via creating </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/703581275453128714.png?v=1 <nowiki>ripperpouch</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ripper Demons can be assigned as a Slayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> assignment by Mandrith, Laniakea, Morvran, Kuradal, and The Raptor.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Demon cluster tasks also work, they may be assigned by Laniakea, and The Raptor.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Laniakea assigns almost </nowiki><strong><nowiki>twice</nowiki></strong><nowiki> as many.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This guide will focus on killing Ripper Demons via AOE methods as they yield the highest KPH; however, at lower levels it is suggested to kill them one at time in the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Ripper_Demon_cave <nowiki>Ripper Demon cave</nowiki>]<nowiki> southeast of the Al Kharid lodestone </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1032552328005419038.png?v=1 <nowiki>alkharidlode</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Bring your best Melee gear with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1130170689119326350.png?v=1 <nowiki>darklight</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513201294262009874.png?v=1 <nowiki>vampscrim</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The </nowiki><em><nowiki>Midday</nowiki></em><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Skyboxes_and_filters <nowiki>skybox</nowiki>]<nowiki> helps brighten the area.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Slaughter</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ When engaged from a distance, Ripper Demons will immediately teleport to the player and cast Slaughter </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879237873666.png?v=1 <nowiki>slaughter</nowiki>]<nowiki>, which deals triple damage if you move.
    • This makes them extremely easy to stack up for AoE damage.
    • This also incentivises staying at the same spot when AoEing.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Death from above</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Ripper Demons gain adrenaline whenever they autoattack.
    • When the bar fills up, they will jump and land where the player was standing shortly after, dealing up to 32,000 hard typeless damage resulting in an instant kill.
    • This can be avoided by moving away from your original location immediately upon seeing them jump.
    • </nowiki><strong><nowiki>When AoEing, good DPM is crucial to avoid this mechanic.</nowiki></strong><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Aggression</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ If the player is below 35% HP, all nearby Ripper Demons will immediately aggro to the player.
    • Usually, using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki> when low HP is desirable to prevent being stacked out.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Death spin</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ When killed, Ripper Demons will spin and hit any nearby players with weak rapid melee hits per tick in a 3x3 area, which can deal large damage if stood in.
    • This mechanic is the main reason </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073087507628035.png?v=1 <nowiki>enhdev4</nowiki>]<nowiki> are essential for killing rippers.
</nowiki></div>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>The Wilderness</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>If killing Ripper Demons in the Wilderness; they are located west of the Demonic Ruins / northeast of Red Dragon Isle, they are </nowiki><strong><nowiki>aggressive</nowiki></strong><nowiki> in this location. </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Ensure you disable PVP by speaking to </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Vala#Edgeville <nowiki>Vala</nowiki>]<nowiki> in Edgeville.</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>1) How to get there in order of speed:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎a) </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Annakarl_Teleport <nowiki>Annakarl Teleport (Ancient Magicks)</nowiki>]<nowiki> and run west, has a high chance of aggro'ing Kal'gerion demons </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1155600607504257024.png?v=1 <nowiki>kalgerion_demon</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>

<nowiki>

</nowiki><h2 id="method-low-effort-magic"><ins><nowiki>Method: Low Effort Magic</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> is very effective at damage reduction and as such is a strong choice for killing Ripper Demons. Stacking Animate Dead </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090453135382.png?v=1 <nowiki>animatedead</nowiki>]<nowiki>, Cryptbloom armour </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1334336169336373339.png?v=1 <nowiki>cryptbloomhelm</nowiki>]<nowiki>, Infernal Puzzle Box </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994189299681607800.png?v=1 <nowiki>infernalpuzzlebox</nowiki>]<nowiki>, Deflect Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> with Amulet of souls </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158359724056.png?v=1 <nowiki>aos</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Enhanced Devoted 4 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073087507628035.png?v=1 <nowiki>enhdev4</nowiki>]<nowiki>, should allow players to sustain themselves purely with Blood Barrage </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537338981747261446.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloodbarrage</nowiki>]<nowiki> healing alone.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Walk up and attack demons between steps. This is the most dangerous part of the method as the first few demons are the slowest to kill since </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285651387613194.png?v=1 <nowiki>oldakcoil</nowiki>]<nowiki> is not active yet and you have no </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712244800572883025.png?v=1 <nowiki>ruthless3</nowiki>]<nowiki> stacks. Keep track of the demons on you and look out for jumps (refer to example video below).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure Legacy Combat Mode is active.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583429936958930964.png?v=1 <nowiki>torment</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158468907008.png?v=1 <nowiki>affliction</nowiki>]</p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Autocast </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537338981747261446.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloodbarrage</nowiki>]<nowiki> and upkeep </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090453135382.png?v=1 <nowiki>animatedead</nowiki>]</p>
