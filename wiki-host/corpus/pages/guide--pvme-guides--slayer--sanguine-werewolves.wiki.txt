</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Sanguine_werewolf <nowiki>Sanguine werewolves</nowiki>]<nowiki> are a low level Slayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> mob found in </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Berylbrook <nowiki>Berylbrook</nowiki>]<nowiki>, located in Havenhythe. They are mainly killed for the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_collection_log#Havenhythe <nowiki>Havenhythe collection log</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Sanguine werewolves can be fought in </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Berylbrook <nowiki>Berylbrook</nowiki>]<nowiki> or the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Sunken_Pyramid <nowiki>Sunken Pyramid</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Berylbrook</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/mx0A7JlRmi.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ If killing sanguine werewolves in </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Berylbrook <nowiki>Berylbrook</nowiki>]<nowiki>, use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869283142350041098.png?v=1 <nowiki>cannon</nowiki>]<nowiki> as shown above.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• While standing on the path, line yourself up with the blue fish on the clothesline then place the cannon 2 tiles north and then move back.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You may have to get them unstuck from the clothesline for the first kill, thus allowing them to get pulled from the side by the cannon.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Sunken Pyramid</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/lh39WYpTE1.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ If killing sanguine werewolves in the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Sunken_Pyramid <nowiki>Sunken Pyramid</nowiki>]<nowiki>, use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1097517249499254875.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayercodex</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• May instead use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1534426786048249907.png?v=1 <nowiki>houseteleportfree</nowiki>]<nowiki> and select Menaphos as your exit destination.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place 5 Sanguine Werewolves in the small room, and position yourself 7 tiles south of the northern statue.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="method-berylbrook-afk"><ins><nowiki>Method: Berylbrook AFK</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Before loading your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869283142350041098.png?v=1 <nowiki>cannon</nowiki>]<nowiki> and starting the session, check the following:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583429936606347280.png?v=1 <nowiki>turmoil</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159416557573.png?v=1 <nowiki>malevolence</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Sustain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/928221126360969226.png?v=1 <nowiki>powderofpenance</nowiki>]<nowiki> and use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/933079947419983912.png?v=1 <nowiki>powderofprotection</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159416557573.png?v=1 <nowiki>malevolence</nowiki>]<nowiki> will require extra sustain.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/648976643687317532.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovlsalve</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689525476158472288.png?v=1 <nowiki>weppoison</nowiki>]<nowiki> are active at all times.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Upkeeping either </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1555704881321353257.png?v=1 <nowiki>superantisanguine</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1555705025194623026.png?v=1 <nowiki>extremeantisanguine</nowiki>]<nowiki> is required if using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1555692252213547139.png?v=1 <nowiki>havensilverhalberd2</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Also requires </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1487224784428335255.png?v=1 <nowiki>vampyrismgloves</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Upkeep </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1494069423047442573.png?v=1 <nowiki>vampyrismaspect</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Activate </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513201294262009874.png?v=1 <nowiki>vampscrim</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use armour perked with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073087713280033.png?v=1 <nowiki>dev4</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073087507628035.png?v=1 <nowiki>enhdev4</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Set </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Scavenge <nowiki>Scavenge</nowiki>]<nowiki> (legendary pet) to &quot;miscellaneous&quot; only.</nowiki></p>
