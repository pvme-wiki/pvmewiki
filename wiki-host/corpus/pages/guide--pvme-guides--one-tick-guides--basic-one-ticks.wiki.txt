<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="kril-tsutsaroth-general-graardor-commander-zilyana-and-king-black-dragon"><ins><nowiki>K'ril Tsutsaroth, General Graardor, Commander Zilyana and King Black Dragon</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide will cover the basic strategy to get your first 1 tick boss kills. These 1 tick kills are fairly consistent, and are a good entry point to make progress towards minimum tick feats roles.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ These Bosses have been grouped together due to them using the same setup and rotation. This works as they are in a similar HP range (40-60k), K'ril and Zilyana will need better luck or more granite mauls due to having slightly more hp.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You may have to incorporate a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/796989663051120640.png?v=1 <nowiki>gmaul</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> end with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339234111848463.png?v=1 <nowiki>ingen</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For general 1 tick kill information, and some useful tips and tricks make sure to read </nowiki>[[PvME/One-tick guides/General Tips|<nowiki>General Tips</nowiki>]]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="rotation_1"><ins><nowiki>Rotation</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This is the basic rotation for these speed kills, many other rotations can be done instead.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>War's Retreat Build</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Build 12 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139452791878856805.png?v=1 <nowiki>necrosis</nowiki>]<nowiki> stacks with the following rotation:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809175980810380.png?v=1 <nowiki>touchofdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809175980810380.png?v=1 <nowiki>touchofdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1482849360462086244.png?v=1 <nowiki>necrobasic</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1482849360462086244.png?v=1 <nowiki>necrobasic</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Off and wait 10 seconds to drop combat:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Equip </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641337999170207763.png?v=1 <nowiki>excal</nowiki>]<nowiki> + (tc) + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809243143766118.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathguard90</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> + click floor</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>In Instance</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At any point after entering the portal switch to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/568262896375824385.png?v=1 <nowiki>grim</nowiki>]<nowiki> and activate.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You only need the second rotation including </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/796989663051120640.png?v=1 <nowiki>gmaul</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> for Commander Zilyana.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339921814126594.png?v=1 <nowiki>dragonslayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339921675845633.png?v=1 <nowiki>demonslayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be used for extra damage on KBD / K'ril after </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809243143766118.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathguard90</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Wait until </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809243143766118.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathguard90</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> has 10 seconds or less on its cooldown then enter the instance:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Go through door + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> → wait until </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> has 11 seconds on its cooldown remaining (1s if mobile is on your gear) →</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ ( tc + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090641879050.png?v=1 <nowiki>smokecloud</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809243143766118.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathguard90</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> )</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ ( tc + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090641879050.png?v=1 <nowiki>smokecloud</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809243143766118.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathguard90</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/903244090953588787.png?v=1 <nowiki>ezk</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339234111848463.png?v=1 <nowiki>ingen</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/796989663051120640.png?v=1 <nowiki>gmaul</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki>)</nowiki></p>
