</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This is an advanced guide to hybrid solo AOD </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580167371365548042.png?v=1 <nowiki>aod</nowiki>]<nowiki> It is assumed you are familiar with the mechanics of the boss as well as having a </nowiki><strong><nowiki>strong sense of timing for prayer flicking</nowiki></strong><nowiki>. This guide covers strategies to maximize efficiency in this encounter. For a single kill for the CA, </nowiki>[[PvME/Nex: Angel of Death/Angel Of Death Small Teams — Necro Solo Overview|<nowiki>Necro Solo Basic</nowiki>]]<nowiki> is recommended instead due to ease of access and gentler learning curve.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="strategy-and-rotations"><ins><nowiki>Strategy and Rotations</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-1"><ins><nowiki>Phase 1</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Start with a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854004678657.png?v=1 <nowiki>berserk</nowiki>]<nowiki> rotation and aim to get </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580167371365548042.png?v=1 <nowiki>aod</nowiki>]<nowiki> to 2500-2600k hp before reavers spawn</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643848618477879328.png?v=1 <nowiki>decimation</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> to clear out the reavers </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/929773044296216666.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloodreaver</nowiki>]<nowiki>, utilising </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306563231790.png?v=1 <nowiki>bindingshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> to bind the reavers and simultaneously apply </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/785031580149743617.png?v=1 <nowiki>blackstonearrow</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Note that the </nowiki><strong><nowiki>reavers must be close enough</nowiki></strong><nowiki> for decimation </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643848618477879328.png?v=1 <nowiki>decimation</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> to reach them all, this is automatically timed if following the suggested rotation</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When using defensives, keep in mind that Nex's melee autoattacks will reset defensive cooldowns at 5, 10, and 15 enrage stacks, visible on your debuff bar</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ To prepare to phase Nex into phase 2, hold damage slightly before hitting 2.1 million health </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>constitution</nowiki>]<nowiki> and overphase with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879334080517.png?v=1 <nowiki>overpower</nowiki>]<nowiki>, pushing her somewhere into the 2060k-2080k range ideally</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This is to ensure aod </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580167371365548042.png?v=1 <nowiki>aod</nowiki>]<nowiki> is phased into p3 on the amalg </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]</p>

</nowiki><h3 id="phase-2"><ins><nowiki>Phase 2</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Phase 2 will begin as Nex hits 2.1 million health </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>constitution</nowiki>]<nowiki> and will be shortly followed by amalgamation </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/878739232447938670.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloodamalg</nowiki>]<nowiki> spawns, which will be cleared with Invoke Death </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809121983336548.png?v=1 <nowiki>invokedeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> and threads </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The </nowiki><strong><nowiki>objective is to kill all 4 amalgamations simultaneously and lure all minions into the center</nowiki></strong><nowiki> such that you can efficiently aoe them with decimation </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643848618477879328.png?v=1 <nowiki>decimation</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ideally phase into 1.8m with the second </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use a combination of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879250456578.png?v=1 <nowiki>gbarge</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879334080517.png?v=1 <nowiki>overpower</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532855191928842.png?v=1 <nowiki>assault</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478062908767211640.png?v=1 <nowiki>chaosroar</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/903244090953588787.png?v=1 <nowiki>ezk</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> , </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532878969438210.png?v=1 <nowiki>cane</nowiki>]<nowiki> to push nex into the 1820-1850k hp range</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Nex should be centred during this phase</nowiki></strong><nowiki> if you phased at the beginning of the elements special attack in phase 1, otherwise make sure to recenter her so that threads </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]<nowiki> will hit all amalgamations</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="phase-3"><ins><nowiki>Phase 3</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>After the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1446178203608809472.png?v=1 <nowiki>gloomfirebow</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> spam sequence, optionally tweak </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1006119102814887957.png?v=1 <nowiki>perfectequilibrium</nowiki>]<nowiki> stacks with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/642713547142332416.png?v=1 <nowiki>shadowtend</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061857486143498.png?v=1 <nowiki>snipe</nowiki>]<nowiki> and finish the rest of the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061862405931120.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswiftness</nowiki>]<nowiki> with a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061864943616122.png?v=1 <nowiki>galeshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> sequence. </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580167371365548042.png?v=1 <nowiki>aod</nowiki>]<nowiki> should be at about 1.2m hp by the end of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061862405931120.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswiftness</nowiki>]<nowiki>. The remaining hp should be dealt with using a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854004678657.png?v=1 <nowiki>berserk</nowiki>]<nowiki> rotation.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Mechanics in phase 3 will happen in a semi-random order, with the primary restrictions being that ice cannot happen first and the same mechanic cannot happen back-to-back:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Ice</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: You go into a 'stasis' mode where bled channels will get cancelled and you are no longer able to attack the boss</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> to use a few more damaging abilities before you are imprisoned, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879053062146.png?v=1 <nowiki>pulverise</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1508822612049002627.png?v=1 <nowiki>saradominbow</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be very helpful for sustain if you just tanked a pool right before being iced</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Disables defensives such as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> but not </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537341954392850442.png?v=1 <nowiki>shielddome</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537341954392850442.png?v=1 <nowiki>shielddome</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/938475261366784070.png?v=1 <nowiki>prismofrestoration</nowiki>]<nowiki> (if using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/839903693837959228.png?v=1 <nowiki>reaverpouch</nowiki>]<nowiki>) and make sure to keep prayer flicking</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Preparation </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258546970624.png?v=1 <nowiki>prep</nowiki>]<nowiki> isn't put on cooldown so </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be used before ice and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258546970624.png?v=1 <nowiki>prep</nowiki>]<nowiki> once you are imprisoned</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The timing for when ice ends is when the cooldown of your mobility abilities reach 4 seconds (they instantly become usable again right after ice ends)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If you phase into P4 while iced, use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> to avoid being frozen by glacies pillar while prayer flicking </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> against ice and boss autos.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Pool</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Must have &gt;14k(or 11.2k if </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/921410226475925514.png?v=1 <nowiki>hellhoundpouch</nowiki>]<nowiki>) health </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>constitution</nowiki>]<nowiki> to take the pool</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• DPS gear and boosts from bonfire / desert pantheon aura </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/982264010126819380.png?v=1 <nowiki>pantheonaura</nowiki>]<nowiki> + thermal spa </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689530593796423724.png?v=1 <nowiki>thermalflask</nowiki>]<nowiki> will put you &gt;12.8k maximum health </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>constitution</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Fortitude </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537341748180025365.png?v=1 <nowiki>fortitude</nowiki>]<nowiki> may be flicked on for up to 1000 additional maximum health </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>constitution</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Save </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>vitality</nowiki>]<nowiki> for second pools so that you enter pillar phase with high hp</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Be wary of an invisible autoattack as Nex finishes her initial animation of spawning the pool</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Recover from pool damage using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/903244090953588787.png?v=1 <nowiki>ezk</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061860371828929.png?v=1 <nowiki>deadshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1508822612049002627.png?v=1 <nowiki>saradominbow</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]</p>
