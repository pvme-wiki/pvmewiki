</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Vile_blooms <nowiki>Vile blooms</nowiki>]<nowiki> are a combination task of all plant monsters on Anachronia. The main reason to do them is for the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_collection_log#Lost_Lands_and_Dungeons <nowiki>Lost Lands and Dungeons</nowiki>]<nowiki> collection log, </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Skeka%27s_hypnowand <nowiki>Skeka's hypnowand</nowiki>]<nowiki> pieces, or to obtain their soul for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1097517249499254875.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayercodex</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vile blooms can be assigned as a Slayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> assignment by Laniakea.</nowiki></p>
