<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="role-information"><ins><nowiki>Role Information</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Mage/Melee should be limited to only free(sc) or hammer roles as well as only 1 per team for optimal performance. It provides strong damage for p1-3 and zaros but woll not attack minions p3 and severely lacks pillar damage.</nowiki></p>
