<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>In order to fight Rasial you must complete the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Alpha_vs_Omega <nowiki>Alpha vs Omega</nowiki>]<nowiki> quest. Rasial deals Necromancy and typeless damage, and can only be killed using Necromancy.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="positioning"><ins><nowiki>Positioning</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/z4ifLrUqck.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ The tile shown above serves as a safespot for the ghost wall mechanic.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This tile is ideal as it keeps you 5 tiles away for quicker </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For the soul obelisks, you may run 4 tiles west (toward obelisk), and 2-4 east.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Doing this allows </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]<nowiki> to kill one of the soul obelisks.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ This is only recommended if you have the ruthless perk.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For an in-game example of the correct tile being used, </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/N8u6ViKvDL.mp4 <nowiki>click here</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/N8u6ViKvDL.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</p>

</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Dive</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Before the last phase, Rasial uses </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> to avoid the player if the player stands within a 2 square radius (scythe range) of him.
    • It is not feasible to use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809145706319892.png?v=1 <nowiki>spectralscythe</nowiki>]<nowiki> against him.
    • Getting this mechanic may cancel </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]<nowiki> bounces.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Conjure familiars</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Rasial can conjure skeletal warriors </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434516658651147.png?v=1 <nowiki>conjureskeleton</nowiki>]<nowiki> and putrid zombies </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809200219684924.png?v=1 <nowiki>conjurezombie</nowiki>]<nowiki> to attack the player.
    • Unlike the player, he can conjure multiple of each.
    • It's recommended to ignore them throughout the fight.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Soul stacks</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Rasial can gain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139453152169558046.png?v=1 <nowiki>residualsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki> and use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809140476031057.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsap</nowiki>]<nowiki> like the player, and holds up to 5 stacks, shown by green orbs under his health bar.
    • At 5 stacks, he shouts </nowiki><strong><nowiki>'This is true power!'</nowiki></strong><nowiki> and uses </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]<nowiki>. Flick </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809111178813471.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectnecromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki> (+ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki> if needed) to avoid taking heavy damage.
    • The rotations outlined below should skip this mechanic consistently.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Soul obelisk</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ At 700k HP, Rasial summons 2 soul obelisks with 12,000 HP each.
    • They each have a bar which fills repeatedly. Upon filling, Rasial will get a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139453152169558046.png?v=1 <nowiki>residualsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki> stack.
    • It's recommended to ignore this mechanic and focus on DPSing the boss.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Spirit wall</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Rasial summons a wall of 7 ghosts behind the player which moves forward, dealing heavy typeless damage upon contact.
    • If using movement abilities </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> to avoid this stay within 5 tiles of Rasial for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]<nowiki>
​    • Standing next to the barrier will avoid ghosts entirely.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Spectral scythe</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Rasial pulls the player to him and uses </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809145706319892.png?v=1 <nowiki>spectralscythe</nowiki>]<nowiki>, shouting </nowiki><strong><nowiki>'I will reap your soul!'</nowiki></strong><nowiki>
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Phase 4</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ At 200K HP, Rasial jumps to the upper deck of the citadel and briefly becomes immune, remaining stationary and stopping all mechanics mentioned above.
    • Necrotic explosions will occur, following the player. If hit, players take heavy Necromancy damage. Rasial's auto attacks are also always empowered, dealing double damage and disabling protection prayers </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787523880281047040.png?v=1 <nowiki>antitheism</nowiki>]<nowiki>
</nowiki></div>

</nowiki><div id="block-optimisationnotes"><nowiki>Optimisation Notes

<nowiki>
</nowiki><div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]<nowiki> too early will result in the first bounce becoming immune.
    • The correct timing of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]<nowiki> is the </nowiki><strong><nowiki>same tick</nowiki></strong><nowiki> as the </nowiki><code><nowiki>Ha! No more games!</nowiki></code><nowiki> message.
⬥ May </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/543478434953822208.png?v=1 <nowiki>veng</nowiki>]<nowiki> for extra damage, such as explosions in P4.
⬥ Rasial can be lured near to one of the soul obelisks, allowing </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]<nowiki> to bounce to it and kill it, granting an additional ruthless stack and slowing his soul buildup.
⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797899945730244648.png?v=1 <nowiki>salveamulet</nowiki>]<nowiki> boost is applied on cast and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787526151978614824.png?v=1 <nowiki>eof</nowiki>]<nowiki> on hit, swapping between these amulets can boost your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809168368148490.png?v=1 <nowiki>splitsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage for major hits </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434663903899728.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathskulls</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434801938432010.png?v=1 <nowiki>fingerofdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Swapping to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787526151978614824.png?v=1 <nowiki>eof</nowiki>]<nowiki> before the pre-build allows conjures to spawn with higher ability damage.
