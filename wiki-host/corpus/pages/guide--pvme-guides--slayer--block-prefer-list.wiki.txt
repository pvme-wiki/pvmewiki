<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="additional-information"><ins><nowiki>Additional Information</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Players start with a only a few slots to block/prefer monsters, gaining 1 of each every 50 Quest Points achieved, up to a maximum of 8 slots to block and prefer monsters at 400 Quest Points.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Don't be afraid to skip undesired or bad assignments, as you can skip around half of the tasks given by Laniakea </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975284345411600426.png?v=1 <nowiki>laniakea</nowiki>]<nowiki> without losing many Slayer points overall, and they can be farmed with relative ease, making it unnecessary to force yourself to do any tasks you dislike.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The assignment given by your Slayer Master is </nowiki><strong><nowiki>not</nowiki></strong><nowiki> completely random, a system unique to each Slayer Master known as </nowiki><strong><nowiki>weighting</nowiki></strong><nowiki> is used to make some assignments more common than others, and may be a factor in what the player chooses to block/prefer.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The higher the weighting, the more common the assignment compared to others, the weighting for each task a Slayer Master has can be viewed on their page of the RuneScape Wiki.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• As we can see from the image below, Jacquelyn </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975284345369665577.png?v=1 <nowiki>jacquelyn</nowiki>]<nowiki> has a slightly higher chance to assign us Cows, Goblins, or Zombies.</nowiki></p>
