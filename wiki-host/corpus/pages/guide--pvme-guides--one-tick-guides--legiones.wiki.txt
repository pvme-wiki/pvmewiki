<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide will cover the basic strategy to get a tick perfect kill at legios (3.6s). This 1 tick kill is fairly consistent, and a fairly easy 1t to make progress towards minimum tick feats roles.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For general 1t kill information, and some useful tips and tricks make sure to read </nowiki>[[PvME/One-tick guides/General Tips|<nowiki>General Tips</nowiki>]]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This kill requires being on a </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer#Social_Slayer <nowiki>Co-op slayer assignment</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• To do this it is recommended to both get the task individually and then join a social slayer group by using an enchanted gem on the other person.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After getting the task, both go to the legio you wish to kill, and have one person enter. The other will get a popup asking if they want to join.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Quickly kill the spawned legio, and then you can setup for the 1t kill, bring multiple keys and dummies to do multiple attempts in the instance.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This can be done at any legio.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For this 1t, one person will be the </nowiki><strong><nowiki>damage dealer</nowiki></strong><nowiki> and one person will </nowiki><strong><nowiki>start the fight</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="preset-suggestions"><ins><nowiki>Preset Suggestions</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://presets.pvme.io/?id=HK6RglCdwLR1tbLtBZ2w <nowiki>Ranged - Basic</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Many items can be downgraded and still get 1t kills, however the more downgrades that are made the </nowiki><strong><nowiki>more attempts will be needed on average</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For info on how to use Berserker blood essence </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1132205017546358794.png?v=1 <nowiki>berserkerbloodess</nowiki>]<nowiki> see </nowiki>[[PvME/One-tick guides/General Tips|<nowiki>General Tips</nowiki>]]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The person starting the fight will need keys, food, and some prayer restoration to protect from magic if the 1t is not done successfully.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​
</nowiki>[https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/preset-images/o/images%2FHK6RglCdwLR1tbLtBZ2w.png?alt=media <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="rotation"><ins><nowiki>Rotation</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This is the basic rotation for this speed kills, while many other rotations can be done instead.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921434633744455.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> → build to 100% adren → s </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1215929540908679208.png?v=1 <nowiki>zammybow</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/736298121704767538.png?v=1 <nowiki>adrenrenewal</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/655341074235129858.png?v=1 <nowiki>vulnbomb</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> to legio spawn tile → ( tc → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/856635090641879050.png?v=1 <nowiki>smokecloud</nowiki>]<nowiki> + r </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1215929540908679208.png?v=1 <nowiki>zammybow</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1215929540908679208.png?v=1 <nowiki>zammybow</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> )</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Inputs within ( ) should be done in the right order, in the same tick</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Within a short time of tc, use your blood essence as described in </nowiki>[[PvME/One-tick guides/General Tips|<nowiki>General Tips</nowiki>]]</em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The person starting the fight should do so by pre clicking the door, and then pressing start </nowiki><strong><nowiki>3 ticks</nowiki></strong><nowiki> after the damage dealer uses dive.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="positioning"><ins><nowiki>Positioning</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Make sure to be stood under thee legio spawn when target cycling and releasing your abilities, as legiones have damage reduction based on distance from them, and you deal 110% damage while stood under. It is useful to check the spawn position when you first enter the instance.</nowiki></p>
