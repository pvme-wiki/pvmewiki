<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Solo Hardmode Vorago is one of the hardest PvM challenges in the game and will require good improvisation skills. Therefore, a strong understanding of the combat system is required.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ A basic understanding of the boss and P10/11 pushback is assumed and strong knowledge of solo normal mode P5 will translate well to hardmode and save time learning</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• It is recommended to read </nowiki>[[PvME/Vorago/Introductory Necro Solo Vorago Guide|<nowiki>Introductory Necro Solo Vorago Guide</nowiki>]]<nowiki> first to make sure you have a base knowledge of solo Vorago</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• It is assumed you have 6/6 solo NM Vorago before attempting this</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ A good goal before starting is to solo all normal mode weeks before 2nd spec</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="phase-3-phase-8-overview"><ins><nowiki>Phase 3 - Phase 8 Overview</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The P3-P8 written below </nowiki><strong><nowiki>focuses on surviving even if substantial time is lost</nowiki></strong></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ For example, the blue right before you bomb stack usually lands after reflect starts. This is the optimal bomb to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]</p>

</nowiki><h3 id="stacking-bombs"><ins><nowiki>Stacking Bombs</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Anytime you are melee distance to Vorago, you run a chance of either getting a blue or a melee hit + blue, so stacks are not perfectly consistent</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>3 auto stack:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Consists of a 9-11 tile blue → 2-3 tile blue → md auto to potentially negate 2 autos worth of pushback</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• So, stand 9-11 tiles away, wait for the first blue to release, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> into 2-3 tile range, wait for the second blue to release, then walk md</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This requires the player to not attack the boss in order to prepare for a 9-11 square auto. Also, pushing the boss during a triple stack will ruin it</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For solo hm in particular, this is useful for P10/P11 reflects that are not barricaded </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306353778689.png?v=1 <nowiki>cade</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/VFBW3xK.mp4 <nowiki>Source media</nowiki>]<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>2 auto stack:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Consists of a 2-3 tile blue → md auto to potentially negate 1 auto worth of pushback</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If done outside of a reflect, take this time to use weaker abilities to build adrenaline. Pushing the boss while trying to stack will stagger the auto timings</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For solo hm in particular, this is useful for P10/P11 to stack 1st and 2nd autos of reflect if close to instakill ledge</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/PNOiPlW.mp4 <nowiki>Source media</nowiki>]<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>PB stack:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Consists of a PB landing + blue to potentially negate 1 auto worth of pushback</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Can go a step further and combine this with other stack methods to negate even more pushback</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>It is very important to maul at a specific time rather than whenever Vorago is in maul spot</nowiki></strong></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Maul on reflect to be able to deal with bleeds effectively P11</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Stall out this phase to be able to reach 100% hitchance </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1176968330582700174.png?v=1 <nowiki>invokelordofbones</nowiki>]<nowiki> before going into P11 to be able to deal with vits</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Very large damage window after broken teamsplit to do a full </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> rotation</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306353778689.png?v=1 <nowiki>cade</nowiki>]<nowiki> during PB such that it lasts through reflect</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Mauling on reflect and baiting out the red bomb the start of P11 is the easiest</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• It is hard to maul on TS</nowiki></p>
