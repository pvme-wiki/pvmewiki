</nowiki><h2 id="general-information"><ins><nowiki>General Information</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Araxxor can be found in the Araxyte lair, just south of Port Phasmatys</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The fight can be done solo or duo (drop rates are reduced in duo)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="fight-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Fight Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The fight consists of four phases: </nowiki><em><nowiki>Araxxor</nowiki></em><nowiki> in P1–3, followed by </nowiki><em><nowiki>Araxxi</nowiki></em><nowiki> in P4</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ P2 has three possible paths:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Top/1 path (&quot;Minions&quot;)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Middle/2 path (&quot;Acid&quot;)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Bottom/3 path (&quot;Darkness&quot;)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Only 2 of the 3 paths are open at any point, changing every 4 days</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You can use the Beasts tab to view the current path rotation</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You can change this at any time with </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/War's_Blessing_3 <nowiki>War's Blessing 3</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Using Soul Split </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> slightly heals the boss when it hits you</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ As enrage increases, as does damage from the below attack mechanics</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Enrage increases by +20% per kill, to a maximum fight start of 300%</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Enrage resets to 0% each day, or by using a pheromone </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643122483641188372.png?v=1 <nowiki>pheromone</nowiki>]<nowiki> at a bank</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="attack-rotation"><ins><nowiki>Attack Rotation</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>P1–3: 5x auto → Random special attack → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Repeat</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Path mechanics happen during P2 depending on the open path:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Top Path: </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Minion Spawn</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Middle Path: </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Bomb Spider</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Bottom Path: </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Spotlight</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>P4: Auto attacks → </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Putrid Wave</nowiki></strong><nowiki> at 25,000 LP</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="auto-attacks"><ins><nowiki>Auto Attacks</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Araxxor can spawn using Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki>, Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> auto attacks. This style is random in public instance, and based on your main-hand weapon in a private instance.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Forcing an attack style</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If fighting Araxxor in a public instance, keep a </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Araxyte_pheromone <nowiki>Araxyte pheromone</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643122483641188372.png?v=1 <nowiki>pheromone</nowiki>]<nowiki> in your inventory</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Araxxor spawns in the style weak to your equipped main-hand style, e.g., it will deal Ranged damage if you are holding a sword</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Once spawned, it will retain this attack style for the rest of the fight</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Equipping a necromancy weapon at time of spawn is random, as this style has no weakness</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray against Araxxor's combat style if taking too much damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> once you are comfortable surviving mechanics</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Be ready for a special attack after every 5 auto attacks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="cleave-example"><ins><nowiki>Cleave</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/A2rkoT1.gif <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Araxxor swipes in front of itself, dragging you into melee distance and dealing heavy typeless damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stay away from Araxxor's front</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> to prevent the drag</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If dragged in, move away quickly</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="cocoon-example"><ins><nowiki>Cocoon</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/QfbT4nH.gif <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Araxxor traps you in a cocoon, dealing damage over time and stopping you from using defensives.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Spam-click to break free</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> reduce the number of clicks required</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="web-shield-example"><ins><nowiki>Web Shield</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/2gfprsH.gif <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Araxxor hides under a web, healing and reflecting damage back to you.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stop attacking immediately</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> to heal from one reflected hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> if needed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="egg-bomb-example"><ins><nowiki>Egg Bomb</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/FXooXiP.gif <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Araxxor launches a slow bomb at you. If it does not hit the eggs, extra spiders spawn.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Move onto the eggs so the bomb destroys them</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Move away after the explosion</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="top-path-minion-spawn-example"><ins><nowiki>(Top Path) Minion Spawn</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/J5SMqvv.gif <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Araxxor summons spiders. Some special spiders either reflect damage from Araxxor or heal Araxxor.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stop attacking Araxxor when spiders spawn</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Kill Mirrorback and Pulsing spiders first</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ignore or clear the other spiders as needed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If unsure, wait for the special spider to spawn before attacking again</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="middle-path-bomb-spider-example"><ins><nowiki>(Middle Path) Bomb Spider</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/mYTxw9X.gif <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> A bomb spider chases you and deals very high typeless damage if it reaches you.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Run away until its timer expires</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> if needed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="bottom-path-spotlight-example"><ins><nowiki>(Bottom Path) Spotlight</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/XXnmQsO.gif <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> The arena becomes dark except for one safe light spot. Standing in the dark deals increasing typeless damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Move into the light</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> to reach it quickly</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-4-araxxi-example"><ins><nowiki>Phase 4 - Araxxi</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/76WZHlY.gif <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Araxxi attacks with Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> outside melee distance.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>At 25,000 LP, Araxxi spawns a bouncing black blob called Putrid Wave, which deals heavy damage over time.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Prayer flick </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmage</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488317046812.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectrange</nowiki>]<nowiki> at higher enrages</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Deal burst damage to finish the kill quickly</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Move each time the black blob hits you</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Keep moving to avoid the blob entirely if needed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
