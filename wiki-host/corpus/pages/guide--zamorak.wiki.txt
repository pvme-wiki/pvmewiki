</nowiki><div id="block-specialattacks"><u><nowiki>Special Attacks</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>These are mechanics which will happen as you go around charging pads.
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Channelers</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ The message </nowiki><code><nowiki>Zamorak begins to draw power and energy from something within Infernus.</nowiki></code><nowiki> appears and a witch spawns in infernus which you must kill to charge the next pad.
    • Press extra ability button.
    • </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> towards and kill witch.
    • Press extra ability button to return to Zamorak. Then walk the next pad you want to charge.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Flames of Zamorak</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Zamorak says </nowiki><code><nowiki>The world will burn.</nowiki></code><nowiki> and slams his wings into the ground, dealing 2 heavy melee hits.
    • Step into melee distance of Zamorak, use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> (use defensives if low hp)
    • Generally, the smoke will go to the North Eastern most player.
    • If any black smoke is absorbed, use defensives to mitigate damage.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Chaos Blast</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Zamorak says </nowiki><code><nowiki>I will tear you asunder!</nowiki></code><nowiki> and wraps his wings around himself, with a blue bar appearing overhead.
    • He sends a red smoke after you after you stun him and deal a specified amount of damage. The longer he charges the attack the more powerful the red smoke hits.
    • Stun Zamorak to disrupt the blue bar.
⬥ He then says </nowiki><code><nowiki>Feel the rage of a god.</nowiki></code><nowiki>
    • Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> before the red smoke hits you.
​
 ⬥Fully channelled Chaos Blast releases with an auto that counts towards the 5 or 11 auto set. If a chaos blast is stunned and releases before the channel is finished, it will not release this auto and do the full set after the mechanic finishes.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Infernal Tomb</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Zamorak says </nowiki><code><nowiki>Step into the dark... meet your death.</nowiki></code><nowiki>, stunning you and sending to infernus while charging a blue bar.
    • He sends a red smoke after you after you stun him. The longer he charges the attack the more powerful the red smoke hits.
    • </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> to avoid stun, and as screen turns black use a stun.
        ⬩ You will only be hit with a weak attack shortly after entering infernus if done correctly.
    • </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> towards and kill the demon by using a strong threshold and basic.
    • Match overhead rune with one on top of a pad and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854281764884.png?v=1 <nowiki>bd</nowiki>]<nowiki> onto it.
        ⬩ Matching the wrong rune will make you take heavy damage.
    • </nowiki><em><nowiki>Note: If you didn't stun properly while entering, stun Zamorak ASAP after exiting infernus and stack defensives to mitigate a more powerful Chaos Blast. </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/URj54bk2bF.mp4 <nowiki>Example clip of proper stun timing here.</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/URj54bk2bF.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</em><nowiki>
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Adrenaline Cage</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Zamorak says </nowiki><code><nowiki>Chaos, unfettered!</nowiki></code><nowiki>, dropping all overhead prayers and hits the base with a few </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> attacks soon after.
    • Stand still. You will take additional damage when further away from the original tile where Zamorak cast the attack.
    • Base </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMage</nowiki>]<nowiki> as soon as possible.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Rune of Destruction</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Zamorak says </nowiki><code><nowiki>You're already dead.</nowiki></code><nowiki> and summons a ritual circle with an inner circle, an outer ring and a space in the middle.
    • A black sludge will travel along the space stunning and dealing small typeless hits to players.
    • Standing in the inner and outer rings will deal small typeless hits to the player.
    • Stay off the red, avoid black sludge.
    • Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> if you are in the black sludge to avoid getting stunned.
​
</nowiki></div>

</nowiki><h2 id="mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Charge Pads</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The arena has 6 pads that you can stand on to charge.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When it is full charged, Zamorak will stop attacking for a while and your adrenaline bar will be refilled. After this, you will gain an </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Edict</nowiki></strong><nowiki> while Zamorak gains a </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Hex</nowiki></strong><nowiki> depending on the pad charged.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You will become unable to charge pads when Zamorak says </nowiki><strong><code><nowiki>HEED MY CALL</nowiki></code></strong><nowiki>. To continue charging the next pad, you must enter Infernus and kill the mage present.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ This happens after charging every pad.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="enrage-based-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Enrage-Based Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Zamorak's damage and HP scales with enrage, and additional mechanics are introduced at specific enrage thresholds:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>100%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Phase 7 is introduced</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>500%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> During </nowiki><em><nowiki>Infernal Tomb</nowiki></em><nowiki>, all players must be on their rune at the same time to exit Infernus</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>500%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> During </nowiki><em><nowiki>Channelers</nowiki></em><nowiki>, Chaos Witch Channeler spawns with 50% adrenaline and the first ability is Asphyxiate </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>750%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> During </nowiki><em><nowiki>Infernal Tomb</nowiki></em><nowiki>, demons will move faster towards the portal</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>900%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> During </nowiki><em><nowiki>Channelers</nowiki></em><nowiki>, </nowiki><strong><nowiki>grey HP</nowiki></strong><nowiki> healing ramp-up no longer resets between phases</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1000%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> During </nowiki><em><nowiki>Channelers</nowiki></em><nowiki>, Chaos Witch Channeler spawns with 100% adrenaline and their first ability is Omnipower </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809664262179.png?v=1 <nowiki>omni</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1000%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> On phase 7, red bar charge is no longer reset between cycle(s)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1000%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> On phase 7, Zamorak's life points cap at 250,000 per player</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1470%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> The life points of chaos demons cap at 70,000 while on Infernus</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1500%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> During the Adrenaline Cage attack, the targeted player will have their Devotion </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki> deactivated</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1600%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Zamorak's stagger after killing a rune on Phase 7 reduced from 10 to 5 ticks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1740%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> For phases 1-6, Zamorak's starting life points cap at 1,600,000 per player</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>3500%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> On phase 7, the life points the runes revive with are capped at 60,000</nowiki></p>
