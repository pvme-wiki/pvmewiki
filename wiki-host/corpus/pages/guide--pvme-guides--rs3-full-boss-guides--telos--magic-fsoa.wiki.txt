<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide assumes a </nowiki><strong><nowiki>very</nowiki></strong><nowiki> strong understanding of everything found in </nowiki>[[PvME/Telos/Overview|<nowiki>Telos Basic</nowiki>]]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[[PvME/Telos/Telos — Stuns And Mechanics|<nowiki>Stuns And Mechanics</nowiki>]]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[[PvME/Telos/Telos — P5 Movement And Timing|<nowiki>P5 Movement And Timing</nowiki>]]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The rotations listed below are ideally used at 1000%+ enrage. However, they will generally work at lower enrage as well, and it is encouraged to learn these rotations as early as possible to build good habits and muscle memory.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="notable-enrage-checkpoints-and-changes-999"><nowiki>Notable Enrage Checkpoints and Changes (999+)</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1000%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> P4 one rock will fall, P5 multiple rocks will fall, immortality no longer works P5, and Telos will freedom stuns and binds with a 16 tick cooldown</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>2000%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> &quot;So you think you can guard?&quot; Achievement, aka Silver Warden</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>2700%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Tendrils damage required caps at 30,000</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>3300%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> The first prayer drain on P3 will smite you from full unless Telos touches the beam.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>4000%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Hard enrage cap; &quot;True Warden&quot; achievement, aka Gold Warden</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
