</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Sanctum_of_Rebirth <nowiki>Sanctum of Rebirth</nowiki>]<nowiki> is a boss dungeon set in the Underworld. Formerly owned by Amascut, three soul devourer bosses can be fought after completion of the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Soul_Searching <nowiki>Soul Searching</nowiki>]<nowiki> quest.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This is an advanced guide and assumes a deep understanding of the </nowiki><strong><nowiki>boss's rotations and mechanics</nowiki></strong><nowiki> for the </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Hard Mode</nowiki></strong><nowiki> version of the Sanctum of Rebirth, found in </nowiki>[[PvME/Sanctum of Rebirth/Sanctum HM Mechanics Overview|<nowiki>Sanctum HM Mechanics Overview</nowiki>]]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Only in Hard Mode is the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Shard_of_Genesis_Essence <nowiki>Shard of Genesis Essence</nowiki>]<nowiki> dropped, which upgrades Tier 95 weapons to Tier 100.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><em><nowiki>Note: the rotations in this guide are still being optimised, so you may need to improvise.</nowiki></em></strong></p>
<nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="vermyxs-attack-rotation"><ins><nowiki>Vermyx's Attack Rotation</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vermyx uses both </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/C31eiH1xcj.mp4 <nowiki>Melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/C31eiH1xcj.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/yDjvHTmoY8.mp4 <nowiki>Ranged</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/yDjvHTmoY8.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> auto-attacks, while Coilspawns only attack with Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Auto-attacks cause 2% armour break per successful hit.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Every auto-attack Vermyx's adrenaline bar fills by 50%, starting a new mechanic when full.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-1"><nowiki>Phase 1</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>3 autos → Moonstone Shards → 2 autos → Wyrmfire + 2 autos → repeat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-2"><nowiki>Phase 2</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>3 autos → Moonstone shards → 2 autos → Soul Bomb → 1 auto + bomb hit (in that order) → 1 auto → Wyrmfire → 8 autos + Scarab Healer → Moonstone Shards</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-3"><nowiki>Phase 3</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>3 autos → Scarab Healer (+ autos continue) → 3 autos → Moonstone shards → 2 autos → Soul Bomb → 1 auto + bomb hit (in that order) → 1 auto → Wyrmfire → 7 autos + Scarab Healer → Wyrmfire</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="t95100-rotation"><ins><nowiki>T95/100 Rotation</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This rotation seeks to consistently hit around ~1:30 kill times.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Sometimes you may not be able to build off of Coilspawns due to the limited range of Necromancy </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You can flank </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073088296157185.png?v=1 <nowiki>flank4</nowiki>]<nowiki> by standing right next to Vermyx's head.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stay in the center after </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> to not get attacked by Coilspawns from multiple sides, may stand on the green lines during phases 2 and 3 for a similar result.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="kezalams-attack-rotation"><ins><nowiki>Kezalam's Attack Rotation</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Kezalam uses both Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> auto-attacks.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Can be stunned (delays rotation).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Every auto-attack gives it 25% adrenaline, at 100% it uses its next mechanic. Adrenaline carries over between phases.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Kezalam starts at 100% adrenaline.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For phase 2 and 3, the first mechanic is determined by the last mechanic of the previous phase.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Kill Moonstone Obelisk → SB-Line → SB-Targeted → Unstable Scarabs → SB-Line → SB-Targeted → Moonstone Prison + Soul Bomb → repeat</nowiki></p>
