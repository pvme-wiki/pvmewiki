</nowiki><h2 id="armour"><ins><nowiki>Armour</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="general-armour-introduction"><nowiki>General Armour Introduction</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ There are three categories of armour:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Power</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: any armour with a style bonus</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Hybrid</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: anything stated as &quot;hybrid&quot;</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Tank</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: any other item (also has more armour than power / hybrid)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Armour tiers are based on the level required to equip them</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Equipping armour provides style and armour bonuses</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• These bonuses are based on armour category and tier</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Generally armours in the same category and tier will have equal bonuses</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Wearing off-style armour slots (excluding cape slot) provides an accuracy penalty based on armour bonus</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• &quot;Off-style&quot; is any style that does not match your equipped main hand / 2H weapon</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Hybrid gear incurs no penalty</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This penalty is near-doubled (0.8 → 1.5) when using the Necromancy combat style.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Some armour sets have </nowiki><strong><nowiki>set effects</nowiki></strong><nowiki> which may be useful</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="weapons"><ins><nowiki>Weapons</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="general-weapons-introduction"><nowiki>General Weapons Introduction</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The tier of a weapon is the listed equip requirement. Generally the damage and accuracy tier match that</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Exceptions include: </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/839903893177106454.png?v=1 <nowiki>lanispear</nowiki>]<nowiki> (T90 damage, T75 accuracy), GWD2 weapons (T80 damage, T90 accuracy), and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/915232154160594974.png?v=1 <nowiki>darkiceshard</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/915232153787334677.png?v=1 <nowiki>darkicesliver</nowiki>]<nowiki> (T88 damage, T85 accuracy)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For dual-wield weapons:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Overall damage tier is equal to 66% of the main hand's tier and 33% of the off-hand's tier</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Overall accuracy tier you have is equal to your main hand's tier fully.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For ammo'd </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> weapons and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> weapons:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Damage tier is equal to the minimum between the weapon tier and the ammo / spell tier</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ E.g. a T90 weapon with a T1 ammo / spell has T1 damage</nowiki></p>
