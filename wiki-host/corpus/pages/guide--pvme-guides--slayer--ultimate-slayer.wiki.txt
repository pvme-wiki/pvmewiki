</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>the Ultimate Slayer</nowiki></strong><nowiki> is a title obtained by completing every slayer collection log item. After the wilderness update, it became a popular goal for many players as PvP can now be turned off. This guide contains all the tips and tricks to make the grind as smooth as possible.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This made tasks like Lava Strykewyrms, Revenants and Ripper demons a very safe and hassle-free task.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ An overview of generally helpful slayer items/consumables/relics can be found in </nowiki>[[PvME/Slayer/Overview Of Slayer|<nowiki>Overview Of Slayer</nowiki>]]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Task specific guides can be found in </nowiki>[https://pvme.io/guides/?category=Slayer <nowiki>Slayer guides</nowiki>]</p>

</nowiki><div id="block-usefulitems"><u><nowiki>Useful Items</nowiki></u><nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>Items that will greatly assist the player's Ultimate Slayer journey.
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Consumables - Time savers</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Fellstalk_incense_sticks <strong><nowiki>Fellstalk incense sticks</nowiki></strong>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/690987265421213746.png?v=1 <nowiki>fellstalksticks</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Each potency level provides a </nowiki><strong><nowiki>x1.01</nowiki></strong><nowiki> multiplier to elite NPCs spawn chance, up to ~4% (tiers stack multiplicatively).
    • Important item for tasks such as: Ripper Demons, Edimmu and Rune Dragons.
⬥ </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_VIP_ticket <strong><nowiki>Slayer VIP ticket</nowiki></strong>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975284344597934120.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayervip</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Usually obtained via the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Travelling_merchant <nowiki>Travelling Merchant</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Cabbage_Facepunch_Bonanza <nowiki>Cabbage Facepunch Bonanza</nowiki>]<nowiki>.
    • You can also kill the beginner version of the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Giant_Mimic <nowiki>Giant Mimic</nowiki>]<nowiki> for ~30+ slayer VIP tickets per hour depending on luck.
⬥ </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Seeker's_charm <strong><nowiki>Seeker's Charm</nowiki></strong>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1027212537411932160.png?v=1 <nowiki>seekerscharm</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Offering them to the northern portal in the stalker dungeon increases the chance of elite soulgazers appearing from 1/50 to 1/10.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Consumables - QoL</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Lantadyme_incense_sticks <strong><nowiki>Lantadyme incense sticks</nowiki></strong>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/565726489404899368.png?v=1 <nowiki>lantasticks</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Extends potion timers up to +2 minutes at max potency - reduces the doses of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/648976643687317532.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovlsalve</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/925794592199147581.png?v=1 <nowiki>aggressionpotion</nowiki>]<nowiki> used.
⬥ </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Potion_reservoir <strong><nowiki>Potion reservoir</nowiki></strong>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/878739200407654431.png?v=1 <nowiki>potionreservoir</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Allows for potions with a timer to be automatically consumed - Helps the grind become more AFK.
⬥ </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Powder_of_penance <strong><nowiki>Penance powder</nowiki></strong>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/928221126360969226.png?v=1 <nowiki>powderofpenance</nowiki>]<nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="daily-slayer-mask-tasks"><ins><nowiki>Daily Slayer mask tasks</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ There are 2 main reasons to force 1 task per day with their respective slayer masks:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Easy points that adds up over time.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Removes the need to use lower tier slayer masters.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Most important:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Use slayer masks for monsters whose uniques are only obtainable on-task/have rates significantly boosted on task:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Ice strykewyrm </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107267915907166.png?v=1 <nowiki>icestrykewyrm</nowiki>]<nowiki> - Staff of light</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Desert strykewyrm </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107266313703566.png?v=1 <nowiki>desertstrykewyrm</nowiki>]<nowiki> - Focus sight</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Jungle strykewyrm </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107264459821066.png?v=1 <nowiki>junglestrykewyrm</nowiki>]<nowiki> - Hexcrest</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Airut </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107275562139648.png?v=1 <nowiki>airut</nowiki>]<nowiki> - Razorback gauntlets </nowiki><strong><nowiki>(Only on task)</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Abyssal demon </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107332478840873.png?v=1 <nowiki>abyssaldemon</nowiki>]<nowiki> - Abyssal wand and orb </nowiki><strong><nowiki>(Only on task)</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><strong><nowiki>Note:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Other slayer masks such as Mask of Kurask/Basilisks/Stone provide a boost in drop chance for their respective head items.</nowiki></em></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="task-prioritisation"><ins><nowiki>Task prioritisation</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>**Disclaimer! This section assumes the following:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You own the Slayer master cape </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975284344123953164.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayercape</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Laniakea </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975284345411600426.png?v=1 <nowiki>laniakea</nowiki>]<nowiki> is your main slayer master</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You are starting with mostly empty logs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Block/Prefer lists are based on task weighing and general level of annoyance of the task**</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The Slayer master cape </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975284344123953164.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayercape</nowiki>]<nowiki> has a perk that gives a 20% chance to choose what a new task will be from a Slayer master. There are a couple of tasks that should have priority over others. The last thing you want is having little to no points with your last item being an Edimmu pet which can be very hard to obtain.</nowiki></p>
