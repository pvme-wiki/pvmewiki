<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Kal'gerion Demons </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1155600607504257024.png?v=1 <nowiki>kalgerion_demon</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be killed at two locations outside Daemonheim:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The Demonic Ruins in the Wilderness at level 47.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The Kal'gerion Demons resource dungeon in Daemonheim.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Kal'gerion Demons are primarily killed for the Kal'gerion battle commendation drops which are a requirement for the Trimmed Completionist Cape. They also drop Demon Slayer equipment (T60 Ranged power armour) and an Adrenaline Urn which are requirements for the Daemonheim slayer log and Ultimate Slayer title.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="stats"><nowiki>Stats</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1003947795541143632.png?v=1 <nowiki>dungeoneering</nowiki>]<nowiki> level: 90</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki> level: 99+</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Kills per hour:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Low Effort: ~340 at 120 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki> using T90 weapons and an upgraded cannon</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• AFK: ~325 kills at 120 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki> using T95 weapons</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> XP per kill: 1858.8</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> XP per hour: ~604k (AFK) &amp; 632k (Low Effort)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Due to the way Necromancy has been designed, kills per hour will increase as your Necromancy level (and hence accuracy and damage) increases.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Player-owned Slayer Dungeon (3 spawns)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Right click teleport on the Slayer Codex</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Option 0→9 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/976732922545586246.png?v=1 <nowiki>dgcape</nowiki>]<nowiki> will take you directly outside the resource dungeon entrance.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Teleport to Daemonheim using a Ring of Kinship and run South-West to the entrance.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="methods-necromancy"><ins><nowiki>Methods: Necromancy</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="afk"><nowiki>AFK</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The AFK method uses </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049230112849133589.png?v=1 <nowiki>holyaggroverload</nowiki>]<nowiki> but this method could also use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289831800882.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovl</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/648976643687317532.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovlsalve</nowiki>]<nowiki> in combination with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/925794592199147581.png?v=1 <nowiki>aggressionpotion</nowiki>]<nowiki>. Some notes for this method are:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137940996391452772.png?v=1 <nowiki>ruination</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Sustain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975765831248130079.png?v=1 <nowiki>demonhornnecklace</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1023152065431744542.png?v=1 <nowiki>ectoplasmator</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Have your </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/902209626412560395.png?v=1 <nowiki>scriptureofful</nowiki>]<nowiki> turned </nowiki><strong><nowiki>on</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/854475442164334592.png?v=1 <nowiki>icenihil</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="low-effort"><nowiki>Low Effort</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The resource dungeon is not AFK for this method as there are more spawns. It is safer than the Wilderness as there are less surrounding mobs such as Abyssal Lords and Abyssal Beasts which can overwhelm the player.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The &quot;Drag&quot; mechanic will bind the player without leaving a status icon indicating such and cannot be blocked. On rare occasions this will take you out of range of your target and cause you to stop attacking - simply re-click your target.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Rarely, this same mechanic will take you out of auto-refill range of you cannon. Simply click a Kal'gerion Demon on the opposite side of your cannon.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137941003895046284.png?v=1 <nowiki>sorrow</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137940996391452772.png?v=1 <nowiki>ruination</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/839903693837959228.png?v=1 <nowiki>reaverpouch</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1022562484378419284.png?v=1 <nowiki>reaverscroll</nowiki>]<nowiki> set to autofire every 10s</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Sustain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975765831248130079.png?v=1 <nowiki>demonhornnecklace</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/648976643687317532.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovlsalve</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1023152065431744542.png?v=1 <nowiki>ectoplasmator</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Alternatively, sustain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/648976643687317532.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovlsalve</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/928221126360969226.png?v=1 <nowiki>powderofpenance</nowiki>]</p>
