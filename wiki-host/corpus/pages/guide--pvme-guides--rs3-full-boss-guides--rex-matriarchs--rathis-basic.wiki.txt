</nowiki><h2 id="fight-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Fight Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You should attack with Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Any other style will only deal insignificant damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="attack-rotation"><ins><nowiki>Attack Rotation</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>3x </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>ranged</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Acid Bombardment</nowiki></strong><nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>ranged</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> →</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>5x </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>ranged</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>ranged</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Poison Absorption</nowiki></strong><nowiki> →</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>3x </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>ranged</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> → 5x </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>ranged</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> →</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Repeat</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="auto-attacks-ranged-example-melee-example"><ins><nowiki>Auto Attacks</nowiki></ins><nowiki> (</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/VpJQA2o7i9.mp4 <nowiki>Ranged example</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/VpJQA2o7i9.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/JC2ZIPyPYv.mp4 <nowiki>Melee example</nowiki>]<nowiki>)</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/JC2ZIPyPYv.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Rathis uses Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> auto attacks. If you are outside melee distance, the melee attack becomes a charge instead.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stay in melee distance unless moving briefly to dodge poison</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If low Defence </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250979987525.png?v=1 <nowiki>defence</nowiki>]<nowiki> level, pray against Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488317046812.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectrange</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1052551455078367313.png?v=1 <nowiki>protectfrommissiles</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> once you are comfortable surviving the mechanics</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="charge-example"><ins><nowiki>Charge</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/RGB4lLK499.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/RGB4lLK499.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> If you move out of melee distance, Rathis can charge at you, stunning </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289428492369.png?v=1 <nowiki>stunicon</nowiki>]<nowiki> you and dealing heavy typeless damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stand in melee distance of Rathis</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If moving (e.g., to dodge acid pools, move back into melee distance quickly to avoid the charge</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="acid-bombardment-example"><ins><nowiki>Acid Bombardment</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/Kyo7hSa0SR.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/Kyo7hSa0SR.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Rathis launches two acid orbs, with one aimed at the player. Each orb deals up to 3500 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage when it lands and leaves behind a </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Poison Pit</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Poison Pits are large green pools that remain in the arena until Rathis uses </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Poison Absorption</nowiki></strong><nowiki>. Standing in them deals poison </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/944649693500154006.png?v=1 <nowiki>poisonicon</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage every tick and applies </nowiki><em><nowiki>Corrosion</nowiki></em><nowiki>, increasing all damage taken by 20% per stack, up to 100%.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Poison Pits shrink when stood in, going from 5x5 → 3x3 → 2x2 → 1x1.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If avoiding them:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Step away from the landing tiles as the orbs arrive</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Move only as much as needed, then return to melee distance</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If shrinking them:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Keep Elder Overload Salve </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/648976643687317532.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovlsalve</nowiki>]<nowiki>, Venomblood </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289714229258.png?v=1 <nowiki>venomblood</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Super Antipoison </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841409588884406272.png?v=1 <nowiki>superantipoison</nowiki>]<nowiki> active to reduce poison damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Briefly stand in Poison Pits to shrink them before Poison Absorption</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Avoid building too many </nowiki><em><nowiki>Corrosion</nowiki></em><nowiki> stacks, as this makes all incoming damage more dangerous</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Do not risk dying just to shrink every pool</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="poison-absorption-example"><ins><nowiki>Poison Absorption</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/ihohKA14Pr.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/ihohKA14Pr.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Rathis absorbs any remaining Poison Pits, healing based on their size (you will see </nowiki><code><nowiki>Uber Heal</nowiki></code><nowiki> ‎appear over Rathis). A full-size Poison Pit can heal Rathis for up to 35,000 LP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This mechanic rewards you for shrinking Poison Pits during </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Acid Bombardment</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Continue the kill as normal, but expect it to take longer</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
