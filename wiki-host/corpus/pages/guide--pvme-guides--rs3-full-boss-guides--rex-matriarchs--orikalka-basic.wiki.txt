</nowiki><h2 id="fight-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Fight Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stay aware of nearby Matriarchs when moving, as only 3/4 Matriarchs share the main arena</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You should attack with Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Fire spells </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/543465116092334083.png?v=1 <nowiki>firesurge</nowiki>]<nowiki> give a +27% hit chance boost</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Any other style will only deal insignificant damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="auto-attacks-melee-example-magic-example"><ins><nowiki>Auto Attacks</nowiki></ins><nowiki> (</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/n77pITX1Ix.mp4 <nowiki>Melee example</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/n77pITX1Ix.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/ua9uchPcaZ.mp4 <nowiki>Magic example</nowiki>]<nowiki>)</nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/ua9uchPcaZ.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Orikalka uses Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> auto attacks.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If lower Defence </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250979987525.png?v=1 <nowiki>defence</nowiki>]<nowiki> pray against Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1052551452788269127.png?v=1 <nowiki>protectfrommelee</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> once you are comfortable surviving the mechanics</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="frost-storm-frostbite-example"><ins><nowiki>Frost Storm / Frostbite</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/icvqcL0erz.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/icvqcL0erz.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Orikalka spins in place, dealing up to 4500 Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage and applying </nowiki><em><nowiki>Frostbite</nowiki></em><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>While </nowiki><em><nowiki>Frostbite</nowiki></em><nowiki> is active, your global cooldown is doubled for 30 seconds. This makes your abilities feel much slower and lowers your damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If Frost Storm deals too much damage, use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmage</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1052551456835768351.png?v=1 <nowiki>protectfrommagic</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If you get </nowiki><em><nowiki>Frostbite</nowiki></em><nowiki>, either:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Keep fighting with abilities which don't care about the global cooldown (e.g., channels </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063111104757760.png?v=1 <nowiki>gconc</nowiki>]<nowiki> or damage-over-time abilities </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478063107908702482.png?v=1 <nowiki>combust</nowiki>]<nowiki>)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Clear it using the fissures from </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Pyroclastic Shock</nowiki></strong><nowiki> (see below)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="pyroclastic-shock-fissures-example"><ins><nowiki>Pyroclastic Shock / Fissures</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/fxCDXAXY4V.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/fxCDXAXY4V.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong><nowiki> Orikalka slams its tail, creating 3 fissures on the ground. Standing on a fissure deals rapid Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage, with the final hit dealing up to 6000 damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Avoid the fissures unless you are attempting to clear </nowiki><em><nowiki>Frostbite</nowiki></em><nowiki> (see above solution under </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Frost Storm</nowiki></strong><nowiki>)</nowiki></p>
