<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Scavenging </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1109429520995463288.png?v=1 <nowiki>scavenging</nowiki>]<nowiki> is an </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250946695292.png?v=1 <nowiki>invention</nowiki>]<nowiki> perk which provides players a chance to get </nowiki><strong><nowiki>uncommon</nowiki></strong><nowiki> or </nowiki><strong><nowiki>rare</nowiki></strong><nowiki> components </nowiki><em><nowiki>as a drop</nowiki></em><nowiki>, whenever they kill an NPC.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Each rank provides a 1% chance per rank for uncommon components to drop from a kill, with a 1% chance that they will be rare components instead.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Scavenging 4 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005934140669046824.png?v=1 <nowiki>scavenging4</nowiki>]<nowiki> on level 20 equipment provides a </nowiki><strong><nowiki>4.4%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> chance per kill, of activation, with a </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1%</nowiki></strong><nowiki> chance that the components will be </nowiki><strong><nowiki>rare</nowiki></strong><nowiki> instead of </nowiki><strong><nowiki>uncommon</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Check the wiki for more information about </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Scavenging <nowiki>Scavenging</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1109429520995463288.png?v=1 <nowiki>scavenging</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Killing monsters en masse for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1109429520995463288.png?v=1 <nowiki>scavenging</nowiki>]<nowiki> procs is called </nowiki><strong><nowiki>scav farming</nowiki></strong><nowiki>, a low effort/AFK way to obtain a variety of components.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="acquiring-scavenging-4"><ins><nowiki>Acquiring Scavenging 4</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ With Ancient Invention unlocked, scavenging 4 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005934140669046824.png?v=1 <nowiki>scavenging4</nowiki>]<nowiki> should be obtained by placing 9 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583434604724224008.png?v=1 <nowiki>preciouscomp</nowiki>]<nowiki> into an </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/697405383769194516.png?v=1 <nowiki>ancientarmourgizmo</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Prepare a few gizmos as the chance for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005934140669046824.png?v=1 <nowiki>scavenging4</nowiki>]<nowiki> is </nowiki><code><nowiki>1/15.4 at 137 Invention</nowiki></code><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Visit the wiki's </nowiki>[https://tinyurl.com/bdfskkd9 <nowiki>perk calculator</nowiki>]<nowiki> to calculate your chances at lower </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250946695292.png?v=1 <nowiki>invention</nowiki>]<nowiki> levels.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Make sure you use an </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1032540072169439242.png?v=1 <nowiki>extremeinventionpotion</nowiki>]</p>
