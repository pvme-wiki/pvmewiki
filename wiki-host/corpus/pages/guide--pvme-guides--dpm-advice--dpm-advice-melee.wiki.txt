<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• More expensive, weaker in the first minute, and needs a dual-wield switch to stall / cast </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879283879977.png?v=1 <nowiki>gflurry</nowiki>]</p>
