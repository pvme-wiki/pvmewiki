</nowiki><h2 id="summoning"><ins><nowiki>Summoning</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583430259064569856.png?v=1 <nowiki>Summoning</nowiki>]</h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Summoning allows you to summon helpful familiars for to aid you in combat via extra inventory, healing, or dealing damage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: This assumes you already have the charms needed from other PvM content. The list of familiars suggested below is only a recommendation.</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Check </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583430259064569856.png?v=1 <nowiki>summoning</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Calculators#Summoning <nowiki>Wiki calculators</nowiki>]<nowiki> for up-to-date pricing.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The weekly Familiarisation minigame can be beneficial when farming charms (triple charms for up to 40 minutes).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Charming potions add +1 charms dropped when killing things</nowiki></strong><nowiki>, and are tradeable on the GE - use them when gathering charms for training summoning!</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Arch-Glacor#Normal_mode <nowiki>Arch glacor 0-1 mechanics</nowiki>]<nowiki> is a low-level boss that drops a lot of blue charms.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

Then remember to quickly get a </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Full_slayer_helmet <nowiki>Full Slayer Helmet</nowiki>]<nowiki> for the big accuracy and damage boost it provides for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Make sure to use the highest available </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_Masters#Overview <nowiki>Slayer Master</nowiki>]<nowiki> to get the best tasks.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Early on, you can quickly train slayer by squishing Corrupted Scarabs at Menaphos.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• See the </nowiki><code><nowiki>soulobby</nowiki></code><nowiki> ‎FC for calls on when scarabs spawn.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="invention"><ins><nowiki>Invention</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250946695292.png?v=1 <nowiki>Invention</nowiki>]</h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Invention unlocks the ability to use perks on gear, which adds a huge amount of DPS, skilling utility and survivability.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: see </nowiki>[[PvME/Invention perks/Invention Basics|<nowiki>Invention Basics</nowiki>]]<nowiki> for more information.</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Suggested training methods</nowiki></strong><nowiki>:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 1-4: Complete the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Invention_tutorial <nowiki>Invention tutorial</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 4-27: Research max item level 5, and disassemble two items at level 5 (cheap options like crystal halberd, black salamander, etc.)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 27-60: Research max item level 10, then disassemble items once they hit level 10.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 60-99/120: Disassemble (at level 10) or siphon (at level 12) weapons and armour while doing PvM/bossing and the skill will passively level up over time.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Shattered Worlds can be used to powerlevel invention, at the cost of combat experience</nowiki></strong><nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• See </nowiki><code><nowiki>!shatteredworlds</nowiki></code><nowiki> ‎command for more information.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Item experience will be higher than AFK alternatives, but the combat experience will be significantly reduced.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="dungeoneering"><ins><nowiki>Dungeoneering</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1003947795541143632.png?v=1 <nowiki>dungeoneering</nowiki>]</h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Note: </nowiki><em><nowiki>As tokens are the limiting factor for most upgrades, the farming of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/762960358930907156.png?v=1 <nowiki>dungtoken</nowiki>]<nowiki> is the focus of this subsection.</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Dungeoneering </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1003947795541143632.png?v=1 <nowiki>dungeoneering</nowiki>]<nowiki> is a support skill that gives a plethora of </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Daemonheim_Rewards <nowiki>rewards</nowiki>]<nowiki> to enhance your skilling and pvm (e.g. </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Ring_of_vigour <nowiki>ring of vigour</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Charming_imp <nowiki>Charming imp</nowiki>]<nowiki>) that can be bought with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/762960358930907156.png?v=1 <nowiki>dungtoken</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ ‎ ‎</nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Dungeoneering#Preparing_for_a_dungeon <nowiki>Dungeoneering</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be trained by completing Daemonheim floors or by doing </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Elite_Dungeons <nowiki>Elite Dungeons</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="token-farming"><nowiki>Token </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/762960358930907156.png?v=1 <nowiki>dungtoken</nowiki>]<nowiki> farming</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>An efficient token </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/762960358930907156.png?v=1 <nowiki>dungtoken</nowiki>]<nowiki> farming method is killing minibosses in elite dungeons, which yields 5,000 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/762960358930907156.png?v=1 <nowiki>dungtoken</nowiki>]<nowiki> per kill.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Level 70+ stats and weapons are recommended.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Temple of Aminishi</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Tokens </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/762960358930907156.png?v=1 <nowiki>dungtoken</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be farmed in the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Temple_of_Aminishi <nowiki>Temple of Aminishi</nowiki>]<nowiki> (ed1) on the first level solo up to trio (trio recommended). Click </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Dungeoneering_token/Strategies <nowiki>here</nowiki>]<nowiki> for strategies.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Miniboss spawns here are random. You can stretch your minimap to check for the miniboss icons.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When all have been killed, restart the dungeon.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Cerberus Juvenile</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Alternatively, one can kill the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Cerberus_Juvenile <nowiki>Cerberus Juvenile</nowiki>]<nowiki> miniboss in the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Zamorakian_Undercity <nowiki>Zamorakian Undercity</nowiki>]<nowiki> (ed4) at the beginning, solo up to 5-man. You can safespot him by standing just outside the room.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• He always spawns.</nowiki></p>

Kill the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Chaos_witch_mender <nowiki>Chaos witch mender</nowiki>]<nowiki> after.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When Cerberus has been killed, restart the dungeon.</nowiki></p>
