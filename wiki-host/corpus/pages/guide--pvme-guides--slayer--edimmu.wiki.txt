</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Edimmu </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107277567008922.png?v=1 <nowiki>Edimmu</nowiki>]<nowiki> are undead slayer monster requiring 90 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> and 115 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1003947795541143632.png?v=1 <nowiki>dungeoneering</nowiki>]<nowiki> to kill. They are a </nowiki><strong><nowiki>task-only</nowiki></strong><nowiki> monster so players going for the Daemonheim log are strongly advised to prioritise picking this monster whenever </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975284344123953164.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayercape</nowiki>]<nowiki> procs. The player can only access the Edimmu area if on a slayer task or holding slayer wildcards.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Besides the default Edimmu tasks, you can also kill Edimmu for the following tasks:</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Undead</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Ghosts</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><nowiki>Location and How to Get There</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Edimmu are slayer monsters found within the Edimmu dungeon, which is located in the Meilyr district of Prifddinas and requires 115 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1003947795541143632.png?v=1 <nowiki>dungeoneering</nowiki>]<nowiki> to be assessed. The best way to get there would be the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/976732922545586246.png?v=1 <nowiki>dgcape</nowiki>]<nowiki> teleport straight to the dungeon, or by teleporting to the Meilyr district via a teleport seed.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="general-strategy"><ins><nowiki>General strategy</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Despite looking large, Edimmus are 1x1, with Elites being 2x2. This makes it viable to use strategies which involve stacking them up and then AoEing them down.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For both active and AFK methods, position your cannon as shown below.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If AFKing, stand in the position as shown below.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/diLpe7Xnjz.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="method-jacquelyn-tasks"><ins><nowiki>Method: Jacquelyn tasks</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>If going for pet, as each slayer task forces an Elite spawn, a good way to farm Elites is via the slayer master Jacquelyn. This is done by killing Edimmu during a Zombie or Ghost task which forces an Elite spawn every 15 kills with slayer introspection.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="method-active-ranged"><ins><nowiki>Method: Active Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]</ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><blockquote><nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="disclaimer"><nowiki>⚠️ Disclaimer</nowiki></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>This method may not work after the combat changes made in Q1 of 2026</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Know of a newer method/rotation? Got a video? Share it in </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/1020853673317908500 <nowiki>Suggestions (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki>!</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The active strategy for Edimmu involves aggroing as many as possible, luring them to a barrier and then chinning them down. This method also works with melee if using a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159341322240.png?v=1 <nowiki>noxscythe</nowiki>]<nowiki>, but ranged has a much easier experience. Additionally, less damage is taken using ranged as Edimmus primarily attack with magic.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Generally this method is only recommended for relatively ungeared players, as players with good magic equipment can AFK the task obtaining a slightly lower kph.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The kph of killing Edimmu actively is highly limited by their slow spawn timers.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="strategy"><nowiki>Strategy </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When a bunch of Edimmu are aggro'd onto you, step behind the barrier and wait for the Edimmu to fill in the space immediately before the barrier.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When Edimmus are in position, immediately enter to avoid losing Edimmu aggression and target the center Edimmu.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Rough ability priority with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641669268722810881.png?v=1 <nowiki>mechchin</nowiki>]<nowiki>: </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/959089455283728386.png?v=1 <nowiki>deadshotigneous</nowiki>]<nowiki> &gt; </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> &gt; </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535534127131394088.png?v=1 <nowiki>snap</nowiki>]<nowiki> &gt; </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/642713547142332416.png?v=1 <nowiki>shadowtend</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• It is easier to target the normal Edimmu around the Elite and chin them down together.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ A </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513195712201228289.png?v=1 <nowiki>Yak</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be a viable familiar for this task.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Store some food it in case you mess up Elite Edimmu spawns.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use it to store rare drop table drops, sharks, or unstackable drops (e.g. crystal seeds).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Winter Storage </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1032540068163895347.png?v=1 <nowiki>yakscroll</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be used to bank untradeable drops like attuned crystal weapon/armour seeds or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1015699014764728492.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloodnecklaceshard</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ While waiting for Edimmu to respawn, you can run around to aggro and kill stray Edimmu not tagged by the cannon.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/867678153635004447.png?v=1 <nowiki>ricocaroming</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/909338509234569216.png?v=1 <nowiki>aggression</nowiki>]<nowiki> are good options for mass-aggroing.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If done right, you will be able to kill at least 585 per hour.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This will vary a little depending on the number of Elite spawns.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334812807238.png?v=1 <nowiki>grico</nowiki>]<nowiki> is not needed for the method to work well.</nowiki></p>
