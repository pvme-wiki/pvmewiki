<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Stunning Telos takes some getting used to, but with enough practice and knowledge you can greatly enhance your performance from higher survivability to longer window of time to DPS. This guide will assume you primarily use Magic, but Melee can also tap into many of these fundamentals with some changes. Unfortunately, as Ranged does not have an equivalent to either Destroy or Asphyxiate, it cannot stun-lock Telos to the same degree as either Melee or Magic and therefore will not be included much in this guide.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="teloss-freedom"><ins><nowiki>Telos's Freedom</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="enrage-cooldowns"><nowiki>Enrage Cooldowns</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>150%-249%</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: 30 second cooldown between Freedom use.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>250%-999%</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: 20.4 second cooldown between Freedom use. On P5, when Telos uses Freedom he has stun immunity for 6 seconds, then can be stunned in that 14 second window.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>1000%-4000%</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: 9.6 second cooldown between Freedom use. On P5, when Telos uses Freedom he has stun immunity for 6 seconds, then can be stunned in that 4.2 second window.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="mechanics"><nowiki>Mechanics</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Procs whenever off cooldown and if he was bound/stunned between Freedoms.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Example: At 150% enrage, if you bound or stun Telos immediately after Freedom, Telos will always use Freedom exactly 30 seconds after the previous one, even if you didn't bind or stun Telos after that.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Right when Telos uses Freedom, his attack cooldown resets and he tries to use his next attack 2 ticks / 1.2 seconds later, even if he just used an attack.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If Telos Freedoms right after he uses an attack, Telos would attack in 2 ticks instead of the usual 4 ticks / 2.4 seconds.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="common-mistakes"><ins><nowiki>Common Mistakes</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="actions-to-avoid"><nowiki>Actions to Avoid</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Stunning as soon as Telos attacks makes Telos attack quicker and wastes the stun. Instead, you always want to make sure you stun right before he does any attack.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://youtu.be/jzrbLGTA1nk <nowiki>https://youtu.be/jzrbLGTA1nk</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki>[https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/jzrbLGTA1nk <nowiki>Embedded guide video</nowiki>]<nowiki>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Following up a Telos Freedom immediately with a stun. If you use a stun right after popping Freedom, you will waste either the entirety of (Impact </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809655873556.png?v=1 <nowiki>impact</nowiki>]<nowiki> / Backhand </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854302605333.png?v=1 <nowiki>backhand</nowiki>]<nowiki>) or waste most of Asphyxiate (</nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]<nowiki> / Destroy </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879330148352.png?v=1 <nowiki>destroy</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Deep Impact </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833139912724.png?v=1 <nowiki>deep</nowiki>]<nowiki> / Forceful Backhand </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879346794516.png?v=1 <nowiki>fbackhand</nowiki>]<nowiki>).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://youtu.be/B7eumx0U998 <nowiki>https://youtu.be/B7eumx0U998</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki>[https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/B7eumx0U998 <nowiki>Embedded guide video</nowiki>]<nowiki>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Using stuns immediately after another without waiting properly or just mistiming it. This can range from canceling Asphyxiate too early to following up Deep Impact with Impact too quickly to simply mistiming the follow-up stun. Be sure to follow these timings </nowiki><em><nowiki>perfectly</nowiki></em><nowiki> if you want the maximum stun uptime!</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="stun-applications"><ins><nowiki>Stun Applications</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="tricks"><nowiki>Tricks</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ All of the above stun combos can work from P1-P4 to maximize stun time. Sometimes it's more efficient to go maximum DPS as stated in the other guides, but in general these stun rotations do lead to the most damage possible before any special attacks/etc.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At the start of P2, P3, and P4 with Magic, there exists a special stun combo you can do. Then you do </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537338981747261446.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloodbarrage</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]<nowiki> (3 hit) → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833139912724.png?v=1 <nowiki>deep</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537338981747261446.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloodbarrage</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809655873556.png?v=1 <nowiki>impact</nowiki>]<nowiki>. This normally lasts as long as the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809655873556.png?v=1 <nowiki>impact</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833139912724.png?v=1 <nowiki>deep</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537338981747261446.png?v=1 <nowiki>bloodbarrage</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]<nowiki> combo, but as both you and Telos take one tick longer to start attacking, the overall stun uptime is still as long as possible with a perfect Freedom at the end.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="stunning-p5"><ins><nowiki>Stunning P5</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Starting at 250% and above, when Telos Freedoms he gets a 6 second stun immunity on P5. This further punishes bad stunning, but you can still work around this and stun Telos as long as possible.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>When the phase starts, Telos will always Freedom immediately, which turns out to be a waste for him as you can immediately follow up with stuns after getting inside the green beam and setting up your Sunshine </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921119381463070.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki>. Telos will Freedom again afterwards, but by this point your stuns will be on cooldown so once they come back, you are free to stun again.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>At 1000%+, you can no longer stun him as easily. As you only have a 4.2 second window to stun, you only have room for one long stun like Asphyxiate </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833072672778.png?v=1 <nowiki>asphyx</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Deep Impact </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833139912724.png?v=1 <nowiki>deep</nowiki>]<nowiki>. Fortunately with enough raw DPS you can circumvent that fine enough, but should you be lacking in DPS in some way, knowing how to stun properly can still make the difference between a death or a kill. In general, you want to stun Telos after he does three autoattacks after his Freedom. This works no matter if you're in the green beam or not.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://youtu.be/MbfruBRsOFU <nowiki>https://youtu.be/MbfruBRsOFU</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki>[https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/MbfruBRsOFU <nowiki>Embedded guide video</nowiki>]<nowiki>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>Pay attention to when the Freedom happens and when he gets stunned, which is three attacks after Freedom.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="target-cycling-at-telos"><ins><nowiki>Target Cycling at Telos</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Target cycling can be used to tag earlier on phase 1 and phase 5.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>For phase 1, you need to trigger GCD (using Devotion </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki>) wait 2 ticks then Surge </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> in (mid-gcd) and sun on the next tick. Wait 3 ticks then use Target Cycle and an ability. Be aware that if your first ability splashes, it can cause Telos' first auto to hit 2 ticks earlier.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>In phase 5, you can Target Cycle the tick after the font moves as in the animation below. Alternatively, just Target Cycle as soon as you see Telos' head pop up, or as soon as you see the yellow dot on the minimap. Your target range must be set to max for this to work.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="p1-walk"><nowiki>P1 Walk</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>If using deto/wm strategy for P1, it's important to make sure you are either south or west of Telos to ensure your deto wm hits are all on the same tick. You do this by checking walk direction with your combust at start, running to the edge of your sun in the opposite direction. Next, run out of your sun immediately after an ability and escape back 2 ticks later. More specifically:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If West walk, run to the east to drag Telos and escape back into your sun. When walking your second combust, click one square under Telos to walk it, then click on Telos again 1t later to stay on his west side.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If South walk, run to the north west of your Sun and run north before escaping/running back south.</nowiki></p>
