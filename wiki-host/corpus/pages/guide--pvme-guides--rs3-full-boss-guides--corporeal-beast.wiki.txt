</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The Corporeal Beast is a boss unlocked from completing the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Summer's_End <nowiki>Summer's End</nowiki>]<nowiki> quest. Summoning familiars cannot be used at this boss, however a legendary pet can be used here.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Auto Attacks</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Only uses magic attacks if you are outside of melee distance.
⬥ Uses magic and melee attacks if you are in melee distance.
    • Protection prayers only partially reduce damage rather than halve it
    • Magic attacks will do an AOE splash on their target in a 3x3 square.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Core</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Occasionally, a dark core will spawn and jump towards its target.
⬥ The core will do constant typeless damage while nearby.
    • Damage will stop when you step more than 1 square away.
    • Will heal the boss unless it is killed or you step away from it.
​
</nowiki></div>

</nowiki><h2 id="general"><ins><nowiki>General</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Any weapon that isn't a </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Spear</nowiki></strong><nowiki> will deal half damage, so </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Melee</nowiki></strong><nowiki> is the recommended style that should be used at this boss.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ It Is recommended to teleport out </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1032540079589163049.png?v=1 <nowiki>warsretreatteleport</nowiki>]<nowiki> after every kill to reset Abilities, Health and Prayer.</nowiki></p>
