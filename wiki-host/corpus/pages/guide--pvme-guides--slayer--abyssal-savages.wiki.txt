</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Abyssal savages serve as a good pre-105 slayer alternative, offering high combat experience and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/947871842520170576.png?v=1 <nowiki>abyssalflesh</nowiki>]<nowiki> drop rates.</nowiki></p>
