<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Vyrelord <nowiki>Vyrelords</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Vyrelady <nowiki>Vyreladies</nowiki>]<nowiki> are popular Slayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> monsters due to being AFK while having a profitable drop table, as well as providing cheap, passive Prayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Firemaking </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1011705727041163396.png?v=1 <nowiki>firemaking</nowiki>]<nowiki> XP when killed with a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/860891494977765446.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunspear</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Darkmeyer is unlocked after partial completion of </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/The_Branches_of_Darkmeyer <nowiki>The Branches of Darkmeyer</nowiki>]<nowiki> quest; however, the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Sunspear_(melee) <nowiki>reforged Sunspear</nowiki>]<nowiki> is acquired during the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/River_of_Blood <nowiki>River of Blood</nowiki>]<nowiki> quest.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Completing the Morytania elite achievements is recommended due to the worn effect of the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Morytania_legs_4 <nowiki>Morytania legs 4</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Cremating 250 vyre corpses is recommended as this will restore 5% prayer points per vyre killed.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="desirable-drops"><ins><nowiki>Desirable drops</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Vyres have a very rewarding loot table, filled with Herblore supplies, seeds, and alchables; particularly useful in Ironman mode </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/764086050959392778.png?v=1 <nowiki>iron</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Cremating 200 and 500 vyre corpses is required for the </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Shade-Shattering Ka-Bloom</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641342351532621824.png?v=1 <nowiki>achiev</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Ivan is Flailing</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641342351532621824.png?v=1 <nowiki>achiev</nowiki>]<nowiki> achievements respectively.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Every 50 vyre corpses cremated gives you +10 damage with all Sunspear variants </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/860891494977765446.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunspear</nowiki>]<nowiki>, capping out at 500 vyre corpses cremated (+100 damage).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Every vyre killed will restore 1% prayer points, increasing to 5% at 250 vyre corpses cremated.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Cremating 500 vyre corpses is a requirement for the master quest cape </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1091050888828813412.png?v=1 <nowiki>masterquestcape</nowiki>]<nowiki> and the trimmed completionist cape </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235223932958.png?v=1 <nowiki>comptrim</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><h2 id="pre-afk-setup"><ins><nowiki>Pre-AFK Setup</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Before starting your AFK session, refer to the map below to ensure the area is prepared properly.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Red areas are places vyres can occasionally get stuck, check these spots and kill any vyres that are roaming the area.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The green house holds the 2 vyres which are used for the flagpole lure to increase KPH with Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The blue house's door can be opened for more KPH, but as the door shuts every 5 minutes, keeping it open is only viable with an alt.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If there are more vyres in a house than is shown on the map, kill all the vyres in that house to reset their spawns.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/Jc8BPhNV9K.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
</nowiki><em><nowiki>Note: on server reset Vanescula Drakan will block the path for vyres trying to walk to you. This is fixed by talking to Vanescula and then walking under her.</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="flagpole-lure"><ins><nowiki>Flagpole Lure</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>You can optionally set up the flagpole lure which allows you to have one extra spawn to kill, this can be done solo and is only recommended with Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Once you're done fixing the spawns, go in the house highlighted green above.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Lure the vyres which spawn in that house out, then try to get them stuck on the flagpole.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This can be done by tagging both vyres then running north.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When they're almost out of the house, run to the player position highlighted in the previous section.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If done correctly, the vyre which spawns between the house highlighted green and the bank won't get stuck on the flagpole, and will instead make its way to you.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: setting up a flagpole lure solo may be seen </nowiki>[https://youtu.be/pQ_ZQLOPyKE?si=ekESrGxDbN_0LOP_&t=39 <nowiki>here</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></em></p>
