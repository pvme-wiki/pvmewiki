<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Camel_Warrior <nowiki>Camel Warriors</nowiki>]<nowiki> are one of the 4 Raptor Mobs, generally killed for the collection log. As such, they should </nowiki><strong><nowiki>only be killed on task where they are 5x more likely to drop the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/917025698479210528.png?v=1 <nowiki>camelstaff</nowiki>]</strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Camel warriors have one main mechanic - After taking some damage, they split into 3 mirages - Blood, Smoke and Shadow. Each has their own unique abilities. All attack with magic.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Shadow mirages - Drains </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> points after charging their overhead bar.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Blood mirages - Heal other mirages for ~900 HP per attack.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Smoke mirages - Cast the Confuse, Weaken and Snare spells on the player, reducing hitchance and damage by 5%, and freezing you in place for 10s respectively.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ After the mirages have been killed, the camel warrior re-appears with the HP it had before phasing, and can now be finished off.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Mirages can be bypassed by dealing a singular hit of more than 20,125 damage, before hitcaps apply.</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For example, if </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513201065513058306.png?v=1 <nowiki>tuskas</nowiki>]<nowiki> is used on task in </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854004678657.png?v=1 <nowiki>zerk</nowiki>]<nowiki> with 120 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki>, it will be capped at 15K, but in theory it would've dealt 30K damage, so the camel will not be phased. This will then allow the player to finish off the camel by dealing the remaining 5125 damage in 1 hit.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If the player fails to deal 5125 damage in the next hit, the camel will phase into mirages. This is why perks such as crackling </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073087662686249.png?v=1 <nowiki>crack4</nowiki>]<nowiki> and aftershock </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712074245202772009.png?v=1 <nowiki>as4</nowiki>]<nowiki> should be avoided when relying on methods that skip the mirage mechanic.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>All methods suggested below use tricks that bypass the mechanic entirely,</nowiki></strong><nowiki> as it is very time consuming and detrimental to kph to kill Camel warriors &quot;normally&quot;.</nowiki></p>
