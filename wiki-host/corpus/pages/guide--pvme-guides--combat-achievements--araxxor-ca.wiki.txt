</nowiki><h3 id="phase-1_1"><nowiki>Phase 1</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>After Araxxor has spawned, equip </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809234922934282.png?v=1 <nowiki>omniguard</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809230573441064.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulboundlantern</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Apply </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809121983336548.png?v=1 <nowiki>invokedeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1143642262165790911.png?v=1 <nowiki>haunted</nowiki>]<nowiki> and build to 5 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139453152169558046.png?v=1 <nowiki>residualsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki> and 12 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139452791878856805.png?v=1 <nowiki>necrosis</nowiki>]<nowiki> stacks. Do not attack the boss other than to apply the debuffs and obtain stacks.</nowiki></p>
