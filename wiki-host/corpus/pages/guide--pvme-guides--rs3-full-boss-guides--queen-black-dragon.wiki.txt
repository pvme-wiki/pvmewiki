</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>The </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Queen_Black_Dragon <nowiki>Queen Black Dragon</nowiki>]<nowiki> resides in the lowest level of the Grotworm Lair which can be found just west of Port Sarim. 60 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583430259064569856.png?v=1 <nowiki>Summoning</nowiki>]<nowiki> is required to be able to enter the arena via the Summoning portal. Before attempting the fight it is </nowiki><em><nowiki>highly</nowiki></em><nowiki> recommended to have at least level 80 in your preferred combat style (generally </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]<nowiki>) and access to super antifire potions.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: Completion of the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Song_from_the_Depths <nowiki>Song from the Depths</nowiki>]<nowiki> quest is recommended as well to reduce the amount of damage received.</nowiki></em></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>

</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div></div>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>
</nowiki><div><nowiki>



</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Phase 1</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ QBD uses </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> auto attacks
⬥ QBD send a fire wall.
    • </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> attacks only occur if the player is standing 2 tiles north from the platform artefact.
⬥ Phase ends when her HP is depleted and the middle artefact is activated.
    • QBD will spit out grotworms until you activate the artefact.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Fire Wall</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ The chatbox will say </nowiki><code><nowiki>The Queen Black Dragon takes a huge breath.</nowiki></code><nowiki>
⬥ Fire wall deals 750 typeless damage for each tick it hits you.
    • You can walk through it to only take 1 tick of damage.
⬥ Each firewall has a gap where you can stand to avoid damage.
    • This gap will either be the 5th, 9th (one left of the center artefact) or the 15th square.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Phase 2</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ All mechanics persist however with each Phase her fire wall gets stronger.
    • In Phase 1, the firewall will have 1 wall you have to pass through.
    • In Phase 2, it will have 2 walls.
    • In Phase 3, there will be 3 walls, and so on.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Tortured Souls</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ There will be an onscreen message saying </nowiki><code><nowiki>The Queen Black Dragon summons one of her Captured Souls.</nowiki></code><nowiki>
⬥ A Tortured Soul appears and releases a cloud that deals 900 damage.
    • The cloud can be made to hit a Tortured Soul or grotworm first.
    • This can be done by walking through the soul at the right time.
⬥ She summons 2 and 4 should in phase 3 and 4 respectively.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Phase 3</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Any Souls in the arena can now be used to heal QBD.
    • The chatbox will say </nowiki><code><nowiki>The Queen Black Dragon starts to siphon the energy of her mages.</nowiki></code><nowiki> and will heal her 200 each tick from each Tortured Soul.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Crystal Form/Hardened Carapace</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ P3 onwards QBD can change her form.
⬥ If QBD takes on the consistency of crystal.
    • Takes 25% less </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage and 25% more </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage.
⬥ If QBD hardens her carapace.
    • Takes 25% less </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage but 25% more </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> damage.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Time Stop</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ P4 onwards a Soul can appear in one of the corners of the arena saying 3 of the following:
    • </nowiki><code><nowiki>Time is short!</nowiki></code><nowiki>
    • </nowiki><code><nowiki>She is pouring her energy into me...hurry!</nowiki></code><nowiki>
    • </nowiki><code><nowiki>The spell is nearly complete!</nowiki></code><nowiki>.
⬥ Kill the Tortured Soul or QBD before the Tortured Soul says all three.
⬥ If all dialogues are said, you screen will freeze for a few seconds, unable to move, eat, or do anything until the special is over.
​
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Extreme Dragon Breath</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ A high hitting attack prompted by the chat box saying, </nowiki><code><nowiki>The Queen Black Dragon gathers her strength to breathe extremely hot flames.</nowiki></code><nowiki>
    • Can be mitigated by moving to the sides of the arena.
    • Standing in the middle will cause high damage.
    • This also is the special attack needed to brand the Royal Crossbow.
</nowiki></div>
</div>
</div>
