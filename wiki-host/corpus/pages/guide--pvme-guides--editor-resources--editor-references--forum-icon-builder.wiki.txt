<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="steps"><nowiki>Steps</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><strong><nowiki>Grab the background image</nowiki></strong><nowiki> of the boss you're looking for</nowiki></li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎a. If using </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/ <nowiki>https://runescape.wiki</nowiki>]<nowiki> then:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Do not right-click the image on the NPC's main page (it is low quality/size)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Instead, click on the image and download the full-size version</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><nowiki>Once added into the web app, you can </nowiki><strong><nowiki>move (by dragging) and zoom</nowiki></strong><nowiki> (by scrolling) the background image</nowiki></li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎a. Try to make the background image in the bottom-right</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎b. Scroll through forum images to get an idea of the style</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><nowiki>Download your file by clicking the </nowiki><code><nowiki>create icon</nowiki></code><nowiki> ‎button</nowiki></li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎a. Your icon should be a </nowiki><code><nowiki>.png</nowiki></code><nowiki> ‎file - if not, use a converter tool online first such as </nowiki>[https://cloudconvert.com/webp-to-png <nowiki>https://cloudconvert.com/webp-to-png</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><nowiki>Edit the relevant forum inside </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/689575078698287152 <nowiki>Bot test (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki> by typing </nowiki><code><nowiki>/forum edit</nowiki></code><nowiki> ‎and following the instructions</nowiki></li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><ol><nowiki>
</nowiki><li><strong><nowiki>Et voila!</nowiki></strong><nowiki> It's that simple.</nowiki></li><nowiki>
</nowiki></ol>
