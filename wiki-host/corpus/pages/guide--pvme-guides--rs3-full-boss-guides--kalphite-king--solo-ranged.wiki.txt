<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide assumes you are already familiar with the basic mechanics of Kalphite King, which can be found in </nowiki>[[PvME/Kalphite King/Kalphite King — Stuns And Mechanics|<nowiki>Stuns And Mechanics</nowiki>]]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Being on a Kalphite Slayer task </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> for slayer helm </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285340460449792.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayerhelm</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Genocidal </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689503091539705870.png?v=1 <nowiki>genocidal</nowiki>]<nowiki> is </nowiki><strong><nowiki>completely optional</nowiki></strong><nowiki>, but will result in slightly faster kills.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide assumes no green shields happen, but they are very rare and generally won't ruin kills.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="preset-and-relics"><nowiki>Preset and Relics</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://presets.pvme.io/?id=c6459333-cf59-48bf-a9c3-e5258185a5f8 <nowiki>Ranged</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​
</nowiki>[https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/preset-images/o/images%2Fc6459333-cf59-48bf-a9c3-e5258185a5f8.png?alt=media <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Notes</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/703581275453128714.png?v=1 <nowiki>ripperpouch</nowiki>]<nowiki> set the autofire rate to 1.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643846948570267648.png?v=1 <nowiki>sirenicbody</nowiki>]<nowiki> switch has </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073087864012840.png?v=1 <nowiki>ch4</nowiki>]<nowiki> to prevent stuns for almost the entire kill.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/565726489341984779.png?v=1 <nowiki>kwuarmsticks</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/565726489136463894.png?v=1 <nowiki>spiritsticks</nowiki>]<nowiki> increase damage to help ensure the rotation ends consistently at 13.2-14.4 seconds.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Toss the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/655341074235129858.png?v=1 <nowiki>vulnbomb</nowiki>]<nowiki> 1-2 ticks before clicking on Kalphite King, otherwise Melee phase might null your click.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>In the fight:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>r</nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334812807238.png?v=1 <nowiki>grico</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1446178203608809472.png?v=1 <nowiki>gloomfirebow</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> → ‎ ‎</nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061860371828929.png?v=1 <nowiki>deadshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/626466665848242186.png?v=1 <nowiki>sgb</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1446178203608809472.png?v=1 <nowiki>gloomfirebow</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> (</nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258832052231.png?v=1 <nowiki>escape</nowiki>]<nowiki> 2t later) → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061855732666439.png?v=1 <nowiki>snapshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061855732666439.png?v=1 <nowiki>snapshot</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>If you don't kill after the last </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061855732666439.png?v=1 <nowiki>snapshot</nowiki>]<nowiki>, finish with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/900758234527301642.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsporearrows</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061863723073636.png?v=1 <nowiki>piercingshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> and improvise.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>If you get Melee phase spawn, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/736298121704767538.png?v=1 <nowiki>adrenrenewal</nowiki>]<nowiki> after </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/626466665848242186.png?v=1 <nowiki>sgb</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> and using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1446178203608809472.png?v=1 <nowiki>gloomfirebow</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1257438999794946099.png?v=1 <nowiki>eofspec</nowiki>]<nowiki> over the first </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061855732666439.png?v=1 <nowiki>snapshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> is an option to help ensure a 13.2 second kill, but not necessary.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Post-fight building:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Target kalphite marauder + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/900758234527301642.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsporearrows</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061863723073636.png?v=1 <nowiki>piercingshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/900758234527301642.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsporearrows</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334812807238.png?v=1 <nowiki>grico</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/900758234527301642.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsporearrows</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061863723073636.png?v=1 <nowiki>piercingshot</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>These </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/900758234527301642.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsporearrows</nowiki>]<nowiki> ensure you can both skip gaining adrenaline at crystal for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994644354536837121.png?v=1 <nowiki>gdeathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> and avoid the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/736298121704767538.png?v=1 <nowiki>adrenrenewal</nowiki>]<nowiki> during the pre-build, saving both banking time and supplies. The harder </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/656844963522281473.png?v=1 <nowiki>dummy</nowiki>]<nowiki> build also makes it harder to gain adrenaline from </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334812807238.png?v=1 <nowiki>grico</nowiki>]<nowiki> if you get a slower kill if you time </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1478061866193518744.png?v=1 <nowiki>imbueshadows</nowiki>]<nowiki> wrong.</nowiki></p>
