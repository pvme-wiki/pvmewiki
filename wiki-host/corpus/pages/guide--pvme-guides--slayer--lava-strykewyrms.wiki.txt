</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>After the Wilderness Update, killing Lava Strykewyrms has become substantially safer. Despite that, their drops are still highly valued mainly due to Searing Ashes being the main ingredient for Aggression Potions </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/925794592199147581.png?v=1 <nowiki>aggressionpotion</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="stats"><nowiki>Stats</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> XP per kill : 1872</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Optimal kills per hour: ~520 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki>, 450 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Optimal </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> XP per hour : ~973K </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki>, 842K </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="desirable-drops"><nowiki>Desirable Drops</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Lava Strykewyrms have a valuable loot table containing: Searing Ashes, Onyx Bolts (e), Rune salvage, coins and potions.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This makes them one of the better tasks to do for gp.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Lava Strykewyrms also drop the Wyrm Spike </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1023150743982723143.png?v=1 <nowiki>wyrmspike</nowiki>]<nowiki>, Wyrm Heart </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1023150746721603625.png?v=1 <nowiki>wyrmheart</nowiki>]<nowiki>, and Wyrm Scalp </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1023150749091381248.png?v=1 <nowiki>wyrmscalp</nowiki>]<nowiki> at a rate of 1/6168 on task (1/12,288 off task), which are required for the Wilderness Slayer collection log.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><nowiki>Location and how to get there</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ They are located South of the Lava Maze (level 34-41 wilderness).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The quickest way to get there is via the obelisk teleport to the level 35 obelisk.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="general-strategy"><ins><nowiki>General strategy</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Place your cannon </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869283142350041098.png?v=1 <nowiki>cannon</nowiki>]<nowiki> between the 2 small lava pits as shown below. You will alternate between killing the strykes northwest and southeast of this spot, most of which can be hit by the cannon.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/LTV5mCPKc0.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="method-active-magic"><ins><nowiki>Method: Active Magic</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This method is can be done with low effort, only requiring the player to click on each spawn and letting Revo++ kill them (~250 KPH), or with high effort, using both dual-wield and 2H weapons and full manual (~450 KPH).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If 4TAA, stamp on mounds with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/694566917553520680.png?v=1 <nowiki>inquisitorstaff</nowiki>]<nowiki> equipped and input the auto when you see your character do the stomp animation.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="method-active-ranged"><ins><nowiki>Method: Active Ranged</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>If </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994189289623662702.png?v=1 <nowiki>bolg</nowiki>]<nowiki> is owned, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> is the best style for killing lava strykewyrms due to its high damage, range and mobility.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Upkeep </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1006119102814887957.png?v=1 <nowiki>perfectequilibrium</nowiki>]<nowiki> at all times.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Proc priority for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1006119102814887957.png?v=1 <nowiki>perfectequilibrium</nowiki>]<nowiki> if 3 stacks: </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513201065513058306.png?v=1 <nowiki>tuskas</nowiki>]<nowiki> &gt; </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/642713547142332416.png?v=1 <nowiki>shadowtend</nowiki>]<nowiki> &gt; </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994189289623662702.png?v=1 <nowiki>bolg</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> &gt; </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541270521708566.png?v=1 <nowiki>rapid</nowiki>]<nowiki> &gt; </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258425204770.png?v=1 <nowiki>snipe</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Don't let autos snipe </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1006119102814887957.png?v=1 <nowiki>perfectequilibrium</nowiki>]<nowiki> proc when using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/642713547142332416.png?v=1 <nowiki>shadowtend</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Stacked damage buffs of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285340460449792.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayerhelm</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994189299681607800.png?v=1 <nowiki>infernalpuzzlebox</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/971025696958853180.png?v=1 <nowiki>fularrow</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643505179378974748.png?v=1 <nowiki>reckless</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/902209626412560395.png?v=1 <nowiki>scriptureofful</nowiki>]<nowiki> allow you to easily hit hitcap with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1006119102814887957.png?v=1 <nowiki>perfectequilibrium</nowiki>]<nowiki> even outside of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921434633744455.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswift</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
