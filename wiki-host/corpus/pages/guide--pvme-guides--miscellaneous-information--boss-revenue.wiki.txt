[[PvME|PvME guide index]]

<span id="boss-profits"></span><p><nowiki>This guide displays calculated GP/kill and related values for many commonly farmed bosses. The source calculations use GE list prices. Prices in this captured edition do not update automatically. Drop rates are sourced from the Runescape Wiki.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="how-to-use-this-guide"><nowiki>How To Use This Guide</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>To calculate your expected hourly profits </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/698816156961603654.png?v=1 <nowiki>coins</nowiki>]<nowiki> for a given boss, take the listed GP/kill total and multiply it by your kills per hour (KPH). Any listed KPH rates below are based on the current meta farming methods, to allow for comparison between bosses.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>To use your own KPH values, visit the PvME spreadsheet </nowiki>[https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1OGM9MBUG2bQVbHxlm86Xfs60soFcjINSOrPv1WL3Vgw/ <nowiki>here</nowiki>]<nowiki>, make a copy, and edit KPH values in the &quot;Boss Revenue&quot; sheet.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: The numbers provided do not take into account supply costs, with the exception of Magister and Legiones where key costs have been accounted for.</nowiki></em></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
