</nowiki><h2 id="phase-one-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Phase One Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="pads"><nowiki>Pads</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ One green pad for each player in the battle will spawn at a random location</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> to stand on a pad before the falling spore hits the ground</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Players will be dealt 5-6k blight damage per pad missed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• A Powerburst of Vitality </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be used to survive missing (or skipping) pads</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> will also be required to survive skipping pads in groups of size 3 to 6</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> will not guarantee survival in a group of 7, at least one pad must be taken with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="personal-blight-bomb"><nowiki>Personal Blight Bomb</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After pads, all players will have a bar above their head</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> as the bar fills entirely to negate the 9-10k damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ ‎ ‎If you are not the base you can safely </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> after the first auto post the bar appearing above your head</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ ‎ ‎If you are the base, the timing is a little tight. A melee smack will come out right before the bomb explodes. While learning use both </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="volatile-rootlings"><nowiki>Volatile Rootlings</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At 0:21, 8 Volatile Rootlings </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/848803062684581929.png?v=1 <nowiki>roots</nowiki>]<nowiki> appear around the edges of the arena</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• It is recommended for each player to pre-place a set of two </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/662249620579155968.png?v=1 <nowiki>dommine</nowiki>]<nowiki> underneath rootling spawn points before starting the fight</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Both </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/662249620579155968.png?v=1 <nowiki>dommine</nowiki>]<nowiki> should be placed same-tick. If not, the second one will sometimes not explode</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ A second set of two </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/662249620579155968.png?v=1 <nowiki>dommine</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be placed while killing the remaining rootlings</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Rootlings have 20,000 hp in a duo and gain 12,500 hp for every additional player in the fight</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If not killed by around 1:30, they will become Blightbound Lashers </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/581180203062001664.png?v=1 <nowiki>blightlasher</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Lashers will attack with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> and can deal heavy damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ If two consecutive rootlings turn into Lashers, the second will heal to full</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="personal-roots"><nowiki>Personal Roots</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At 1:06, 2 random players will be given a text box informing that they have Blight Roots as well as a timer that counts down</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• There will be yellow arrows at the players' feet, on the minimap, and on the arena itself indicating where the roots will be coming from</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When the timer hits 0, the roots will launch towards the players' location in a triangle shape</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The player must move 1 or more tiles away from their pinpointed location and away from the roots to avoid damage and a stun</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> before the timer reaches 0 to ensure you won't be stunned</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="aoe-blight-bomb"><nowiki>AoE Blight Bomb</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At 1:32 and 1:49 (and throughout Phase 3, as well), up to 3 non-base roles will receive a bar above their head</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When filled entirely, an explosion will deal 3-4k damage (AoE)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be used for a heal</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Alternatively, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki>, or tank it</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Solak's auto attacks will speed up after these bombs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="arms-and-legs"><nowiki>Arms and Legs</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At 1:55 (1:06 for rootskips, 1:32 and 1:49 respectively for bomb skips), Solak will root his arms to the center of the arena</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Arms must be killed within 30 seconds</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• After both arms die, Solak will stand up, exposing his legs. Legs must be killed within 20 seconds</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Being too slow on either arms or legs means that Solak will stand up and spawn Blightbound Lashers </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/581180203062001664.png?v=1 <nowiki>blightlasher</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="core"><nowiki>Core</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After both the arms and legs have been killed, Solak will expose his core</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The core has 125,000 hp per person, and is exposed for 18 seconds</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If the core is not finished within this time, P1 mechanics will repeat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Once the core is dead, Phase 1 ends and Phase 2 begins</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="phase-two-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Phase Two Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="eruptions"><nowiki>Eruptions</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At the start of Phase 2, an eruption will spawn in each corner of the arena</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When killed, these eruptions will spawn a blue circle that will teleport everyone within up to the Anima Storm 5 seconds after the eruption dies</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="anima-storm"><nowiki>Anima Storm</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Persists from the beginning of Phase 2 until it is killed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Every 10 seconds, it will drop blight rain onto the arena hitting all players for 2k damage and will add a Blight Stack </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/851514430613487686.png?v=1 <nowiki>blight</nowiki>]<nowiki> to the player's debuffs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• It is destroyed by teleporting via the eruptions and spam clicking the storm in the sky</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• As the storm &quot;rains&quot; down onto the arena, a puddle of blight rain will begin to form in the center</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Stay away from the puddle as it hits for almost 2k damage per tick and will add a Blight Stack </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/851514430613487686.png?v=1 <nowiki>blight</nowiki>]<nowiki> for each hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ While in the sky, players can position themselves away from the center to ensure they return to the ground outside of the puddle</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="cleanseblight-stacks"><nowiki>Cleanse/Blight Stacks</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Phases 2 and 3, Merethiel can be clicked to activate a green circle on the ground at the south</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Blight Stacks </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/851514430613487686.png?v=1 <nowiki>blight</nowiki>]<nowiki> appear on the players' debuff bars</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• It will remove all blight stacks from players standing within it</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Players will do 1000 less damage per click to the Anima Storm per stack, and receive 1% more damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="blight-spores"><nowiki>Blight Spores</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ 6 auto attacks after all eruptions die, Blight Spore bombs will spawn all around the arena</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Spores will rise into the air, fall back to the ground, and explode</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The longer the mechanic has progressed, the more damage they will deal</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Towards the end of the mechanic, they will hit up to 8k and should be avoided at all costs</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="anima-rain"><nowiki>Anima Rain</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ 12 auto attacks after spores, five hits of Anima Rain will fall from the sky</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Players should click the golden sphere in the north to consume the Blessing of Nature</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Immediately before the first rain, players should press their extra action button to deploy the blessing</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ The Blessing of Nature shield dome lowers the Anima Rain damage by 75%</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Players can run outside of the shield and use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> for a heal</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="arm-climb"><nowiki>Arm Climb</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ 2 auto attacks after the last Anima Rain, Solak will pull and root one player to the ground</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The player should </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> from the roots and move away</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Failure to do so will result in being insta-killed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• After Solak slams his arm, a player should click the arm to climb up</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ This will deal a deadly blow to Solak</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• After the climb, Solak will hit the climber with a massive melee hit, up to 9k</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Climber should </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> this hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="phase-three-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Phase Three Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="charge-pads"><nowiki>Charge Pads</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At the start of Phase 3, 8 pads will spawn around the arena</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• As Solak stands on this pad, it will charge up (indicated by a blue ring)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ It takes 15 seconds for a pad to become fully charged</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When fully charged, Solak will stop attacking and begin to shake rapidly for 12 seconds</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ This makes for a good time to heal with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Random bombs can fall onto pads throughout the phase, tank, move away, or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• During this 12 second window, players may use their extra action button to access the Realm</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If all 8 pads are fully charged, P3 will end</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ This is not commonly done; instead, it is recommended to enter the realm and kill Erethdor</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="the-realm"><nowiki>The Realm</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Accessed on Phases 3 and 4 by using the extra action button</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If clicked during P3, the player will be in the Realm for 20 seconds before returning to the regular arena</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When in the Realm, food cannot heal you, but other sources of healing still work (e.g. excalibur)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• In Phase 3, the Realm is used to kill Erethdor (&quot;the Elf&quot;)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ The phase will end when Erethdor is killed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="aoe-blight-bomb-stun"><nowiki>AoE Blight Bomb &amp; Stun</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ AoE blight bombs also occur in Phase 3, and are dealt with the same as in Phase 1</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Throughout the phase, Solak will stun all players in the regular arena</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If he is not stunned back, players will be hit with a bleed that escalates in damage over time</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Weapons with Flanking </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073088296157185.png?v=1 <nowiki>flank4</nowiki>]<nowiki> will still work for this mechanic</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="phase-four-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Phase Four Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="realm-manifestations"><nowiki>Realm Manifestations</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Entering the Realm on P4 should not be done under normal circumstances, instead, prefer to No-Realm</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Manifestations will spawn inside of the Realm and attempt to consume Solak's mind</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If the mind is consumed, all players will be insta-killed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The HP of Solak's mind is the bottom of the two displayed on-screen</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The rate at which manifestations consume the mind increases the longer they live</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• More manifestations will spawn the faster Solak's HP bar is depleted</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ This means delaying the use of some high damage abilities can be useful</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Manifestations can be tanked by a player through entering the Realm</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Manifestations attack with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>Magic</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• While in the Realm, players can sacrifice their own HP to heal Solak's mind</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ This should be done by first using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306353778689.png?v=1 <nowiki>cade</nowiki>]<nowiki> and then clicking Solak</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="burst-of-nature"><nowiki>Burst of Nature</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Appears as a purple tornado surrounding Solak</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Deals a large hit every ~28 seconds</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Damage can be lowered tremendously by using stunning abilities</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809655873556.png?v=1 <nowiki>impact</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533833139912724.png?v=1 <nowiki>deep</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854302605333.png?v=1 <nowiki>backhand</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879346794516.png?v=1 <nowiki>fbackhand</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306563231790.png?v=1 <nowiki>bindingshot</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541275957657600.png?v=1 <nowiki>tight</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809142376058910.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulstrike</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809243143766118.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathguard90</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/537340400273195028.png?v=1 <nowiki>spec</nowiki>]<nowiki> will still reduce damage even with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073088296157185.png?v=1 <nowiki>flank4</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="the-no-realm"><nowiki>The No-Realm</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The general approach to succeeding on P4 is to ignore the Realm, and instead finish Solak before his mind is consumed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Enter the phase with a fairly high level of adrenaline</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Build up to 100%, and wait for Solak to rush forward, and then be pulled backwards</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ You can build on Solak before the pull-back, but he will be immune to damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Execute a strong ultimate ability rotation after Solak is pulled back</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Stunning abilities should be used early to reduce Burst of Nature damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><div id="block-phase1"><u><nowiki>Phase 1</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>Damage Solak down to his HP cap while handling mechanics that interrupt damage. Killing the Core will start Phase 2
</nowiki></div>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Start &amp; Pads</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Pre-place </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/662249620579155968.png?v=1 <nowiki>dommine</nowiki>]<nowiki>, if available
⬥ Start fight, </nowiki><strong><nowiki>use damage-boosting ultimate</nowiki></strong><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921119381463070.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921434633744455.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854004678657.png?v=1 <nowiki>zerk</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Use a few strong abilities within your ultimate, and then </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> to a pad
        ⬩ Alternatively, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]<nowiki> (duo) or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258786177064.png?v=1 <nowiki>reflect</nowiki>]<nowiki> (3-6 people)
⬥ Return to Solak after pads, and continue doing damage until ~0:50
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Rootlings, Roots &amp; Bombs</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ The team should split up and kill volatile rootlings
    • Place second set of </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/662249620579155968.png?v=1 <nowiki>dommine</nowiki>]<nowiki> if available
⬥ Handle personal roots at ~1:06
    • Ensure you warn others if they need to move to safety
⬥ Continue doing damage until ~1:32 and ~1:49 Blight Bombs
    • Negate as required with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • Keep doing damage between bombs
</nowiki></div>
</div>
<div><div><u><nowiki>Arms, Legs, &amp; Core</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Kill arms, while keeping fairly high adrenaline
    • If using necromancy, you should </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809121983336548.png?v=1 <nowiki>invokedeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809172335951933.png?v=1 <nowiki>threadsoffate</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809140476031057.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsap</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159435029592686642.png?v=1 <nowiki>volleyofsouls</nowiki>]<nowiki> to kill arms
    •Magers and rangers </nowiki><strong><nowiki>should </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921119381463070.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921434633744455.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> at the end of arms</nowiki></strong><nowiki>
⬥ Kill the legs, while building adrenaline for core
    • Meleers </nowiki><strong><nowiki>should </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854004678657.png?v=1 <nowiki>zerk</nowiki>]<nowiki> just before core appears</nowiki></strong><nowiki>
⬥ Dump everything you have into the core
    • Ensure somebody uses </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/655341074235129858.png?v=1 <nowiki>vulnbomb</nowiki>]<nowiki>

</nowiki><div><div><div><u><nowiki>Eruptions, Cleanse &amp; Storm</nowiki></u><nowiki>

⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> back to the destroyed eruption, go up and damage the Anima Storm
⬥ After returning to the ground, repeat with other Eruptions until the Storm is dead
    • There is generally no need to cleanse a second time
</nowiki></div>

<div><div><u><nowiki>Blight Spores, Anima Rain &amp; Arm Climb</nowiki></u><nowiki>
</nowiki></div>
<div style="white-space: pre-line"><nowiki>⬥ Meet at the very north of the arena between the torches
    • </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Use damage-boosting ultimate</nowiki></strong><nowiki> immediately </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921119381463070.png?v=1 <nowiki>sunshine</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/994921434633744455.png?v=1 <nowiki>deathsswift</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854004678657.png?v=1 <nowiki>zerk</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1159434908486357072.png?v=1 <nowiki>livingdeath</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ Damage Solak while avoiding Blight Spores
⬥ Use the blessing of nature when the Anima Rain comes
    • Run outside of shield and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki> one of the hits
⬥ Continue damaging until the Arm Climb
    • If rooted, use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki>
    • One player should climb, and mitigate large hit with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258844635148.png?v=1 <nowiki>res</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ If not phased after climb, continue damage and potentially mitigate another Anima Rain
    • </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Do not</nowiki></strong><nowiki> use a damage-boosting ultimate unless extremely low damage

⬥ Watch out for AoE Blight Bombs and stun when required throughout the phase
⬥ Charge first pad, and go up to the Realm

⬥ Continue to damage Solak, use a second damage-boosting ultimate if Solak is high HP
⬥ When Solak is at HP cap, go up to the Realm once more
