</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide assumes you are already familiar with the basic mechanics of Kalphite King, which can be found in </nowiki>[[PvME/Kalphite King/Kalphite King — Stuns And Mechanics|<nowiki>Stuns And Mechanics</nowiki>]]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Being on a Kalphite slayer task </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> is recommended as you gain the benefits of the Slayer Helm </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285340460449792.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayerhelm</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Genocidal </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689503091539705870.png?v=1 <nowiki>genocidal</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This guide assumes no Green Shields happen, you may opt to teleport out if one happens early into the fight.</nowiki></p>
