</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Abyssal_beast <nowiki>Abyssal beasts</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107328964014100.png?v=1 <nowiki>abyssalbeast</nowiki>]<nowiki> are a higher level alternative for Abyssal Slayer creature assignments. They are mainly killed for Slayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> tasks, the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_collection_log#Senntisten <nowiki>Senntisten collection log</nowiki>]<nowiki>, combat </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896050370281523.png?v=1 <nowiki>combat</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250946695292.png?v=1 <nowiki>invention</nowiki>]<nowiki> item experience, profit, and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1123586941057769552.png?v=1 <nowiki>eliteclue</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Abyssal beasts can be assigned as a Slayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> assignment by Mandrith, Laniakea, Morvran, Kuradal, Duradel, Sumona, and The Raptor.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Abyssal beasts specific tasks may be assigned by Mandrith.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Demon cluster tasks also work, they may be assigned by Laniakea, and The Raptor.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Laniakea assigns almost twice as many.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The level 35 Wilderness location yields the highest kph.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Abyssal beasts can be fought in the Senntisten Asylum, the Wilderness, or the Sunken Pyramid.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Senntisten Asylum</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/ZRpoxozL1P.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ If killing Abyssal beasts at the Senntisten Asylum, use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/870326852361789501.png?v=1 <nowiki>pontifexshadowring</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Go to the second room of the Senntisten Asylum and position yourself 5 tiles east of the west drain.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869283142350041098.png?v=1 <nowiki>cannon</nowiki>]<nowiki> 4 tiles east of you.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ If using an </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285651387613194.png?v=1 <nowiki>oldakcoil</nowiki>]<nowiki> place it 3 tiles north of you.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Level 35 Wilderness</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/5LslOc41ka.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ If killing Abyssal beasts at the level 35 Wilderness location, use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137735861937655900.png?v=1 <nowiki>portableobelisk</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Go just north of the level 35 Wilderness obelisk and position yourself 2 tiles north of the skeleton.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869283142350041098.png?v=1 <nowiki>cannon</nowiki>]<nowiki> 1 tile north, and 9 tiles east of the skeleton.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Sunken Pyramid</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/mGGioEKQkA.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ If killing Abyssal beasts in the Sunken Pyramid, use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1097517249499254875.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayercodex</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Relocating your house to Menaphos and using a teleport to house tablet also works.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place 5 Abyssal beasts in the small room, and position yourself 7 tiles south of the northern statue.</nowiki></p>
