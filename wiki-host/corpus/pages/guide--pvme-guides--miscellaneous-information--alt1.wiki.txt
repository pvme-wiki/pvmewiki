</nowiki><h2 id="alt1-overview"><ins><nowiki>Alt1 Overview</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="what-is-alt1"><nowiki>What Is Alt1</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Alt1 is a 3rd-party program that consists of several application overlays on your screen. They get information by reading your screen, and provide a bunch of QoL/useful features.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>A detailed explanation of Alt1's features can be found on the Alt1 website, but amongst its default features it includes:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>AFK Warden</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - the most common use for Alt1, its in-game activity detection overlays</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Clue Solver</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - solves all clue puzzles and displays output as an overlay to speed up clue solving</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>DgKey</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - an overlay of your Dungeoneering </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1003947795541143632.png?v=1 <nowiki>dungeoneering</nowiki>]<nowiki> map to keep track of gatestones, teammates, etc.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Stats Lookup</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - look up a player's stats ;stats;, quest points, etc.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Item Lookup</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - quickly find any item's page on the Runescape Wiki</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>D&amp;D Notifications</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - opt-in for D&amp;D notification alerts</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>XpMeter</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - track XP/hr rates in real time</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Droplogger</nowiki></strong><nowiki> - an alternative to using the endgame RuneMetrics drop log</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="how-it-works"><nowiki>How It Works</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Alt1 is designed to capture images of the NXT Client and parse them with an OCR (Optical Character Recognition) Library.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The captured data is then transformed into pixel data that can be used in Alt1 apps.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ More information can be found on the forums and in the Alt1 Toolkit discord.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="is-it-safe"><nowiki>Is It Safe?</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Alt1 is essentially a glorified Chromium browser that creates overlays over your game. Every Alt1 app is essentially a webpage.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Alt1 does not send any inputs or otherwise directly interact with your client beyond reading the pixels on the screen.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki><strong><nowiki>It is not a client, therefore you won't get banned for using it.</nowiki></strong></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="afk-warden"><ins><nowiki>AFK Warden</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/UG5psi4.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="what-is-afk-warden"><nowiki>What Is AFK Warden</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This is one of the most commonly used Alt1 apps, so will be covered in some detail here.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This reads the pixels on your screen to recognize your character, buff bars, chatbox and more in order to provide AFK alert notifications.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ It tracks a number of things, including:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• HP </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250887712902.png?v=1 <nowiki>Constitution</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Prayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> alerts</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Buff timers like </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/648976643687317532.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovlsalve</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/565726489404899368.png?v=1 <nowiki>LantaSticks</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Familiar duration </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583430259064569856.png?v=1 <nowiki>Summoning</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Loot beams </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/698816156961603654.png?v=1 <nowiki>coins</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• AFK lobby timers</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Seren Spirits and Blessings of the Gods</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Character stance (for AFK skilling alerts)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• XP drops, and more.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="clue-solver"><ins><nowiki>Clue Solver</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/EhTPOsI.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="what-is-clue-solver"><nowiki>What Is Clue Solver</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ This is a very useful app that looks at your currently open clue on screen, and tells you how to solve it.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ It handles all clue steps, including puzzle boxes, scans, compass clues, etc. With the exception of scan clues.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Have your clue visible on-screen clearly and click the 'Unlock Any' button to find a solution to your clue that will be displayed on the overlay.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
