</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Abyssal_lord <nowiki>Abyssal lords</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107327126904842.png?v=1 <nowiki>abyssallord</nowiki>]<nowiki> are the best abyssal creatures task for both experience and gold. They are mainly killed for Slayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> tasks, the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_collection_log#Senntisten <nowiki>Senntisten collection log</nowiki>]<nowiki>, combat </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896050370281523.png?v=1 <nowiki>combat</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250946695292.png?v=1 <nowiki>invention</nowiki>]<nowiki> item experience, profit, and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1123586941057769552.png?v=1 <nowiki>eliteclue</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643215216934518815.png?v=1 <nowiki>trisk</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Abyssal lords tasks may be assigned by Mandrith.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Abyssal demons tasks may be assigned by Mandrith, Laniakea, Morvran, Kuradal, Duradel, Sumona, and The Raptor.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Demon cluster tasks may be assigned by Laniakea, and The Raptor.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Laniakea assigns almost twice as many.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ For both abyssal demons and demon cluster tasks, each </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107327126904842.png?v=1 <nowiki>abyssallord</nowiki>]<nowiki> counts as 5 kills.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Abyssal lords can be fought in the Wilderness, the Senntisten Asylum, or the Sunken Pyramid.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Level 47 Wilderness</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/AL2LhIQoXZ.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ Use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1009419838764109834.png?v=1 <nowiki>wildernesssword</nowiki>]<nowiki> and teleport to the Wilderness herb patch.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Position yourself on the westernmost corner of the rock and walk 2 tiles north and 1 tile west.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869283142350041098.png?v=1 <nowiki>cannon</nowiki>]<nowiki> 1 tile west of the bottom skeleton.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If using an alt, position yourself as shown </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/nmGp4fMne5.png <nowiki>here</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Abyssal lords and beasts can be fought simultaneously at this location.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Do </nowiki><strong><nowiki>not</nowiki></strong><nowiki> use Necromancy </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki> at this location.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Senntisten Asylum</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/wogijlVST4.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ Use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/870326852361789501.png?v=1 <nowiki>pontifexshadowring</nowiki>]<nowiki> and teleport to Senntisten, then run north-east towards the asylum elevator.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Once inside run to the final room, a </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Gate_key <nowiki>gate key</nowiki>]<nowiki> unlocks a shortcut.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Sunken Pyramid</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/mGGioEKQkA.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ Use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1097517249499254875.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayercodex</nowiki>]<nowiki> and teleport to the Sunken Pyramid.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Relocating your house to Menaphos and using a teleport to house tablet also works.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place 5 abyssal lords in the small room, and position yourself 7 tiles south of the northern statue.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
