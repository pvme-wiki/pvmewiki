</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Nightmare <nowiki>Nightmare creatures</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107308516782111.png?v=1 <nowiki>nightmare</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be assigned as a Slayer assignment after purchasing the toggle for 50 Slayer points, this requires completion of the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Children_of_Mah <nowiki>Children of Mah</nowiki>]<nowiki> quest. They are mainly killed for Slayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> tasks, the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_collection_log#Other_Worlds <nowiki>Other Worlds collection log</nowiki>]<nowiki>, combat </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896050370281523.png?v=1 <nowiki>combat</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689509250946695292.png?v=1 <nowiki>invention</nowiki>]<nowiki> item experience, and profit.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Nightmare creatures can be assigned as a Slayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> assignment by Laniakea, Morvran, and Kuradal.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Nightmare Creatures are located at the Ritual site on Freneskae, accessible through the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/World_Gate <nowiki>World Gate</nowiki>]<nowiki> after completion of the Children of Mah quest.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Bring a Sixth-Age circuit </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/937093290245894154.png?v=1 <nowiki>sixthagecircuit</nowiki>]<nowiki> for a quick teleport.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="method-afk-magic"><ins><nowiki>Method: AFK Magic</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]</h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Killing Nightmare creatures with Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> will yield the highest kph but require expensive abilities and gear for optimal results. If wanting to camp Nightmares, this is the preferred setup.</nowiki></p>
