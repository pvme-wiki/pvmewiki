<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Tormented demons are classified as demons, and can be slain with no Slayer level </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> requirement. They can be assigned as a task after completing </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/While_Guthix_Sleeps <nowiki>While Guthix Sleeps</nowiki>]<nowiki> and purchasing the ability to have them assigned, for 50 Slayer points </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Besides the default Tormented demon task, you can also kill them for Demons cluster tasks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="method-1-sunken-pyramid"><ins><nowiki>Method 1: Sunken Pyramid</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Four Tormented Demon souls should be placed into a large room in the Sunken Pyramid, use Necromancy </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki> with a blood reaver familiar and weapon poison.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137941003895046284.png?v=1 <nowiki>sorrow</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137940996391452772.png?v=1 <nowiki>ruination</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Sustain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/975765831248130079.png?v=1 <nowiki>demonhornnecklace</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1023152065431744542.png?v=1 <nowiki>ectoplasmator</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289831800882.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovl</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/547603714681339915.png?v=1 <nowiki>superprayerrenewal</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689525476158472288.png?v=1 <nowiki>weppoison</nowiki>]<nowiki> are active at all times</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1494069423047442573.png?v=1 <nowiki>vampyrismaspect</nowiki>]<nowiki> is active at all times</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You do </nowiki><ins><nowiki>not</nowiki></ins><nowiki> need </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/925794592199147581.png?v=1 <nowiki>aggressionpotion</nowiki>]<nowiki> as the demons are naturally aggressive</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Do </nowiki><ins><nowiki>not</nowiki></ins><nowiki> use Darkness </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809209782698024.png?v=1 <nowiki>darkness</nowiki>]</p>

</nowiki><h3 id="location-and-how-to-get-there_1"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Guthixian Temple</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Teleport using the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/937093290245894154.png?v=1 <nowiki>sixthagecircuit</nowiki>]<nowiki> (option 4 &quot;Tormented Demons&quot;)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Stand in the south-east room, anywhere on the steps which captures the aggression of a Tormented Demon.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
