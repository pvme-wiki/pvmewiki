</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>Armoured phantoms are the highest tiered Zemouregal's undead, requiring 113 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> to kill. Even though their drops are worth less than that of other similar high-levelled slayer monsters such as abyssal lords </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107327126904842.png?v=1 <nowiki>abyssallord</nowiki>]<nowiki> and moss golems </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107335343542404.png?v=1 <nowiki>mossgolem</nowiki>]<nowiki>, they offer competitive XP rates and make for a fairly speedy task.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>They can be killed for following tasks:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Armoured phantoms, assigned by </nowiki><strong><nowiki>The Raptor</nowiki></strong><nowiki> and </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Laniakea</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Zemouregal's undead, assigned by </nowiki><strong><nowiki>The Raptor</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Undead cluster, assigned by </nowiki><strong><nowiki>The Raptor</nowiki></strong><nowiki> and </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Laniakea</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>

</nowiki><h2 id="afk-methods"><ins><nowiki>AFK Methods</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ AFK relies on only allowing </nowiki><strong><nowiki>one</nowiki></strong><nowiki> phantom to attack you at once by either:</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Standing in a corner, causing phantoms to pile up to be hit with AoE damage such as </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/902209626509025290.png?v=1 <nowiki>magmatempest</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809152043925505.png?v=1 <nowiki>spectralscythe3</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532879359377439.png?v=1 <nowiki>meteorstrike</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285651387613194.png?v=1 <nowiki>oldakcoil</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Using a </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Platypus <nowiki>platypus</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Tooth_creature <nowiki>tooth creature</nowiki>]<nowiki> pet to block phantoms. A cannon </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/869283142350041098.png?v=1 <nowiki>cannon</nowiki>]<nowiki> with the Restocking Cannon upgrade is not necessary for this, but increases kph. An aggression potion </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/925794592199147581.png?v=1 <nowiki>aggressionpotion</nowiki>]<nowiki> can be used instead, but yields less kph.</nowiki></p>
