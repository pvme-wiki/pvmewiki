<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="general-information"><ins><nowiki>General Information</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gregorovic is located in the Heart of Gielinor.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gregorovic has 200,000 LP in both Normal Mode and Hard Mode.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Level 80 Prayer </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> and 40 Sliske kill count are required to enter.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gregorovic can hit hard and accurately, making him one of the harder GWD2 bosses for learners.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode, Gregorovic is immune to poison and gains the </nowiki><em><nowiki>Join Me</nowiki></em><nowiki> Wight phase after reaching 0 LP.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="fight-mechanics"><ins><nowiki>Fight Mechanics</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gregorovic deals both </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/580168050121113623.png?v=1 <nowiki>range</nowiki>]<nowiki> auto-attacks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Force Ranged hits by standing at 2+ squares away where possible</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If using Melee, use halberd-range weapon e.g., Laniakea's Spear </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/839903893177106454.png?v=1 <nowiki>lanispear</nowiki>]<nowiki>, Dragon Rider Lance </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/643161541931171872.png?v=1 <nowiki>dragonriderlance</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Noxious scythe </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190159341322240.png?v=1 <nowiki>noxscythe</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode, flick between Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectmelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488317046812.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectrange</nowiki>]<nowiki> protection prayers</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="attack-rotation"><ins><nowiki>Attack Rotation</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Normal Mode: 3 auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Trick Knife</nowiki></em><nowiki> → 3 auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Summon Spirit</nowiki></em><nowiki> → 3 auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Glaive Throw</nowiki></em><nowiki> → Repeat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Hard Mode: 3 auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Trick Knife</nowiki></em><nowiki> → 3 auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Summon Spirit</nowiki></em><nowiki> → 3 auto attacks → </nowiki><em><nowiki>Glaive Throw</nowiki></em><nowiki> → Repeat</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• At 0 LP in Hard Mode, Gregorovic uses </nowiki><em><nowiki>Join Me</nowiki></em><nowiki>, then resumes his attack rotation after all Wight Hunters are killed</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="trick-knife-example"><ins><nowiki>Trick Knife</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/aFZzLQF.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/aFZzLQF.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gregorovic throws a blade after 3 auto attacks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ The blade hits players 3 times total</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In solo, all 3 hits will usually hit you</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray against Ranged </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488317046812.png?v=1 <nowiki>deflectrange</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1052551455078367313.png?v=1 <nowiki>protectfrommissiles</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513190158728953857.png?v=1 <nowiki>devo</nowiki>]<nowiki> if you have low Defence</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="summon-spirit-example"><ins><nowiki>Summon Spirit</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/BVvu3F8.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/BVvu3F8.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gregorovic summons a spirit that walks towards him, based on whichever mask you are nearest</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If it reaches Gregorovic, he gains a buff</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Spirit of Mania</nowiki></strong><nowiki> increases attack speed (south-west)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Spirit of Rage</nowiki></strong><nowiki> increases damage (south-east)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• </nowiki><strong><nowiki>Spirit of Delirium</nowiki></strong><nowiki> increases poison damage (north)</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ In Hard Mode, 2 spirits spawn and move faster</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solutions:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Kill or ignore the spirit, or;</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Force the Spirit of Delirium by standing in the north-west</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use either Irit Incense Sticks </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/690987265371144202.png?v=1 <nowiki>IritSticks</nowiki>]<nowiki> or Venomblood perk </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289714229258.png?v=1 <nowiki>venomblood</nowiki>]<nowiki> and completely ignore the spirit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="glaive-throw-example"><ins><nowiki>Glaive Throw</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/ZPw1hZR.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/ZPw1hZR.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gregorovic marks shadow spots on the floor, including under you</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Standing in shadows causes a large Magic hit</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Step onto a tile with no shadows</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="shadows-example"><ins><nowiki>Shadows</nowiki></ins><nowiki> </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/4ce8fSc.mp4 <nowiki>(Example)</nowiki>][https://img.pvme.io/images/4ce8fSc.mp4 <nowiki>Source video</nowiki>]</h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gregorovic summons shadows at 140,000 LP and 60,000 LP and may teleport to them</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Normal Mode: 2 shadows, then 3 shadows</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Hard Mode: 3 shadows, then 4 shadows</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Kill shadows with AoE abilities </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532878969438210.png?v=1 <nowiki>cane</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809149690916945.png?v=1 <nowiki>spectralscythe2</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533809995874304.png?v=1 <nowiki>tsunami</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306391265284.png?v=1 <nowiki>bombard</nowiki>]<nowiki> or simply ignore them</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Note: you may need to ret-arget Gregorovic if he teleports</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="join-me-hm-only"><ins><nowiki>Join Me (HM Only)</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At 0 LP, Gregorovic becomes invulnerable and turns you into a shadow clone</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ You will not be able to use damaging abilities</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Gregorovic heals while 6 Wight Hunters spawn over time</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Wight Hunters have 9,000 LP each</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Your auto attacks deal high damage and can bounce to nearby wights</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Surge </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> to a corner to stack the Wight Hunters in a line</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Click the closest Wight Hunter to deal damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Avoid eating solid food, as it delays your auto attacks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Note: you can use defensive abilities to build adrenaline if low </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541306475151390.png?v=1 <nowiki>anti</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258546970624.png?v=1 <nowiki>prep</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541259465392143.png?v=1 <nowiki>voke</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="final-phase-hm-only"><ins><nowiki>Final Phase (HM Only)</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Mechanic:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After all 6 Wight Hunters die, Gregorovic becomes attackable again, returning with around 65,000-95,000 LP</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Greg will return to his attack rotation</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><strong><nowiki>Solution:</nowiki></strong></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Deal damage &amp; handle mechanics normally again</nowiki></p>
