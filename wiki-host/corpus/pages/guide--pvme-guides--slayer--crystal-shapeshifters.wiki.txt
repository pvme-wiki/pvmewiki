</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Crystal_Shapeshifter <nowiki>Crystal Shapeshifters</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1004107281153150986.png?v=1 <nowiki>crystalshapeshifter</nowiki>]<nowiki> are a slayer monster found on </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Tarddiad <nowiki>Tarddiad</nowiki>]<nowiki> and are accessible via the World Gate (looks like the number 1), they can be assigned by Morvran or Laniakea after the completion of </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/The_Light_Within <nowiki>The Light Within</nowiki>]<nowiki> quest. Crystal Shapeshifters will change their attacking style to what your armour is </nowiki><strong><nowiki>weakest</nowiki></strong><nowiki> against (if this is equal, attack styles will be random).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h3 id="location-and-how-to-get-there"><ins><nowiki>Location and How to Get there</nowiki></ins></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If killing Crystal Shapeshifters in Tarddiad use option 2 on </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/937093290245894154.png?v=1 <nowiki>sixthagecircuit</nowiki>]<nowiki> to select the World Gate.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Make sure to attune the World Gate to Tarddiad.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If killing Crystal Shapeshifters in the Sunken Pyramid use a </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1097517249499254875.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayercodex</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Relocating your house to Menaphos and using a teleport to house tablet also works.</nowiki></p>

</nowiki><h2 id="method-tarddiad"><ins><nowiki>Method: Tarddiad</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Make sure you </nowiki><strong><nowiki>toggle on aggression</nowiki></strong><nowiki> by speaking to Angof on Tarddiad (north, near the camp fire).</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This makes the Crystal Shapeshifters permanently aggressive and eliminates the need for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/925794592199147581.png?v=1 <nowiki>aggressionpotion</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/ESHtyB6.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p>[https://img.pvme.io/images/vNye6IiODf.png <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]<nowiki>
⬥ If using </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> or </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki> then go east and stand just to the east of the bridge.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If you find yourself getting dragged around then simply return back to the position shown above when convenient.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If using Melee </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1096130867279171706.png?v=1 <nowiki>melee</nowiki>]<nowiki> then go northwest and kill them </nowiki>[https://img.pvme.io/images/TVYM0GfqAn.png <nowiki>here</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Pray </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583429936958930964.png?v=1 <nowiki>torment</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/583429936606347280.png?v=1 <nowiki>turmoil</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137941003895046284.png?v=1 <nowiki>sorrow</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Sustain </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1005450651347730533.png?v=1 <nowiki>prayer</nowiki>]<nowiki> with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/928221126360969226.png?v=1 <nowiki>powderofpenance</nowiki>]<nowiki> / </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1494069332584693810.png?v=1 <nowiki>penanceaspect</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Ensure </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841419289831800882.png?v=1 <nowiki>elderovl</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/547603714681339915.png?v=1 <nowiki>superprayerrenewal</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689525476158472288.png?v=1 <nowiki>weppoison</nowiki>]<nowiki> are active at all times</nowiki></p>
