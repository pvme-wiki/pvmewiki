</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>There are 4 Nodon dragonkin variants that can be killed for slayer: Nodon engineers, artificers, hunters, and guards. The Nodon dragonkin task can be assigned by Morvran or Laniakea and requires level 92 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/797896049548066857.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayer</nowiki>]<nowiki>. Additionally, players can kill Kerapac </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/875443920673337344.png?v=1 <nowiki>kerapac</nowiki>]<nowiki> in Normal Mode or Hard Mode, since the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285340460449792.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayerhelm</nowiki>]<nowiki> buffs (but not </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513201065513058306.png?v=1 <nowiki>Tuskas</nowiki>]<nowiki>) apply to the boss.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Currently there are only methods detailed for Nodon guards and artificers. If you have a different method for Nodon hunters or engineers, please make a post in </nowiki>[https://discord.com/channels/534508796639182860/1020853673317908500 <nowiki>Suggestions (external Discord)</nowiki>]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><strong><nowiki>Note</nowiki></strong><nowiki>: Dragon cluster tasks from Laniakea apply </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/798285340460449792.png?v=1 <nowiki>slayerhelm</nowiki>]<nowiki> and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/513201065513058306.png?v=1 <nowiki>Tuskas</nowiki>]<nowiki> buffs to Slayer monsters at Nodon front but not </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/875443920673337344.png?v=1 <nowiki>kerapac</nowiki>]<nowiki>. This also applies for </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/641339921814126594.png?v=1 <nowiki>dragonslayer</nowiki>]<nowiki>, </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689502927731163170.png?v=1 <nowiki>dragonslayerperk</nowiki>]<nowiki> and Dragonbane weaponry.</nowiki></em></p>
