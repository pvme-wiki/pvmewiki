</nowiki><h2 id="introduction"><ins><nowiki>Introduction</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>This is an </nowiki><strong><nowiki>advanced guide</nowiki></strong><nowiki> which assumes a strong underlying knowledge of all mechanics and information presented in ⁠</nowiki>[[PvME/Nex/Overview|<nowiki>Nex Basic</nowiki>]]<nowiki>.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>When Nex spawns for the first time in a custom instance, she will be praying Deflect Magic </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMage</nowiki>]<nowiki> for the majority of the kill. This is why Ranged-only kills are the meta for fast consistent kills.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><em><nowiki>Note: the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Ritual_of_the_Mahjarrat <nowiki>Ritual of the Mahjarrat</nowiki>]<nowiki> quest gives access to the </nowiki>[https://runescape.wiki/w/Stone_of_Jas_(status) <nowiki>Stone of Jas</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1006121672790773770.png?v=1 <nowiki>jasboost</nowiki>]<nowiki> boost which gives a 5% damage boost in GWD1 (including Nex). It can be reached via fairy ring code </nowiki><code><nowiki>D.K.Q.</nowiki></code><nowiki> ‎or master quest cape option </nowiki><code><nowiki>5</nowiki></code><nowiki>.</nowiki></em></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="blood-phase-notes"><nowiki>Blood Phase Notes</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Make sure you don't have </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712073087662686249.png?v=1 <nowiki>crack4</nowiki>]<nowiki> or 100% </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/712074245202772009.png?v=1 <nowiki>as4</nowiki>]<nowiki> that accidentally phases Nex.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="ice-phase-notes"><nowiki>Ice Phase Notes</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If you get caught in an Ice Prison use Freedom </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535541258240786434.png?v=1 <nowiki>freedom</nowiki>]<nowiki> then </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/787904334377648130.png?v=1 <nowiki>divert</nowiki>]<nowiki>, optionally also use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/654618235097972737.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofvitality</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="zaros-phase-notes"><nowiki>Zaros Phase Notes</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Nex cycles her overhead curses every 3 auto attacks during Zaros Phase, following this rotation: </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/615613924506599497.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulsplit</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMelee</nowiki>]<nowiki> → no overhead curse</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535614336207552523.png?v=1 <nowiki>disrupt</nowiki>]<nowiki> may be used if you are low on food.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ If Nex ends her Ice Phase with </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMage</nowiki>]<nowiki>, there is a chance it will carry over to Zaros Phase. She may also switch to </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195487926845462.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMage</nowiki>]<nowiki> during Zaros phase, improvise accordingly.</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ When Nex uses </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/544195488447201300.png?v=1 <nowiki>DeflectMelee</nowiki>]<nowiki>, there is a chance your attacks will be reflected back at you if you hit her.</nowiki></p>
