<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ </nowiki>[https://presets.pvme.io/?id=Ae9Q2oND5zxYU4ILNbI5 <nowiki>Necromancy preset</nowiki>]<nowiki> </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1148995625896120460.png?v=1 <nowiki>necromancy</nowiki>]<nowiki>
</nowiki>[https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/preset-images/o/images%2FAe9Q2oND5zxYU4ILNbI5.png?alt=media <nowiki>Guide illustration</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ As </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/689504724159823906.png?v=1 <nowiki>magic</nowiki>]<nowiki> Araxxor is desired, wearing a ranged weapon until Araxxor has spawned is required</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ It is recommended to have the </nowiki><em>[https://runescape.wiki/w/NopeNopeNope <nowiki>NopeNopeNope</nowiki>]</em><nowiki> perk from Player Owned Farms active</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Upkeep the </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/841409588954923049.png?v=1 <nowiki>kalgscroll</nowiki>]<nowiki> buff from your familiar</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Araxxor takes a few seconds to clear vulns on phase 1-3, making vuln bombs </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/655341074235129858.png?v=1 <nowiki>vulnbomb</nowiki>]<nowiki> usable on all phases</nowiki></p>

<nowiki>
</nowiki><h2 id="top-path"><ins><nowiki>Top Path</nowiki></ins></h2>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-1"><nowiki>Phase 1</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ After Araxxor has spawned, equip </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809234922934282.png?v=1 <nowiki>omniguard</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1138809230573441064.png?v=1 <nowiki>soulboundlantern</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ Apply </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1137809121983336548.png?v=1 <nowiki>invokedeath</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1143642262165790911.png?v=1 <nowiki>haunted</nowiki>]<nowiki> and build to 5 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139453152169558046.png?v=1 <nowiki>residualsoul</nowiki>]<nowiki> and 12 </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1139452791878856805.png?v=1 <nowiki>necrosis</nowiki>]<nowiki> stacks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Do not attack the boss other than to apply the debuffs and obtain stacks</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This phase is timegated so it can be used to set yourself up for phase 2 while you deal damage</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><h3 id="phase-2"><nowiki>Phase 2</nowiki></h3>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>⬥ At 59 seconds use </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/756236265472524418.png?v=1 <nowiki>powerburstofacceleration</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> → </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]</p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Trigger player movement on every tick along with your mobility abilities</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Clicking on an enlarged minimap may be helpful in facilitating movement and </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535532854281764884.png?v=1 <nowiki>bd</nowiki>]<nowiki> placements across the path</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The first </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/535533810004262912.png?v=1 <nowiki>surge</nowiki>]<nowiki> + </nowiki>[https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1049378668197195808.png?v=1 <nowiki>dive</nowiki>]<nowiki> should be executed when web burn reads 99%</nowiki></p>
<nowiki>
</nowiki><p><nowiki>​</nowiki></p>
