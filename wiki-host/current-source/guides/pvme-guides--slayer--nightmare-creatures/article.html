Nightmare Creatures

​

Introduction

Nightmare creatures can be assigned as a Slayer assignment after purchasing the toggle for 50 Slayer points, this requires completion of the Children of Mah quest. They are mainly killed for Slayer tasks, the Other Worlds collection log, combat / item experience, and profit.

​

⬥ Nightmare creatures can be assigned as a Slayer assignment by Laniakea, Morvran, and Kuradal.

​

Stats

⬥ level: 80

⬥ XP per kill: 1540

⬥ Optimal kills per hour: ~870 , ~803 , ~800 (All AFK)

⬥ Optimal XP per hour: ~1.34m , ~1.24m , ~1.23m (All AFK)

​

Desirable drops

⬥ Nightmare gauntlets

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Required for the Slayer collection log.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 1/2,000 on-task and 1/10,000 off-task.

​

Note: if you change the monetary value for custom loot to 5k this will allow you to loot everything relevant (No sharks or Muspah spines). This is achieved by going to Settings > Gameplay > Item Drops > Loot System > Monetary Value 5,000.

​

Useful Items and Unlocks

Owning the following increases the efficiency of your sessions, but is by no means mandatory
Items
⬥ Sixth-Age circuit     • Allows for an easy teleport to the World Gate. ⬥ Demon horn necklace     • Helps sustain your prayer. ⬥ Grace of the Elves     • Can be used as a swap when looting to automatically bank Muspah spines. ⬥ Weapon perked with Eruptive 4 Ruthless 3     • Mainly useful if camping Nightmares.
Other
⬥ Dwarven siege engine     • Make sure to have the Restocking Cannon ability unlocked.

​

Location and How to Get there

⬥ Nightmare Creatures are located at the Ritual site on Freneskae, accessible through the World Gate after completion of the Children of Mah quest.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Bring a Sixth-Age circuit for a quick teleport.

​

Method: AFK Magic

Killing Nightmare creatures with Magic will yield the highest kph but require expensive abilities and gear for optimal results. If wanting to camp Nightmares, this is the preferred setup.
