Presets and Relics

⬥ Necromancy preset

⬥ As Araxxor is desired, wearing a ranged weapon until Araxxor has spawned is required

⬥ It is recommended to have the NopeNopeNope perk from Player Owned Farms active

⬥ Upkeep the buff from your familiar

⬥ Araxxor takes a few seconds to clear vulns on phase 1-3, making vuln bombs usable on all phases
