Boss Profits

This channel displays calculated GP/kill and related values for many commonly farmed bosses. The calculations use GE list prices, and auto update as the GE does. Drop rates are sourced from the Runescape Wiki.

​

How To Use This Guide

To calculate your expected hourly profits for a given boss, take the listed GP/kill total and multiply it by your kills per hour (KPH). Any listed KPH rates below are based on the current meta farming methods, to allow for comparison between bosses.

​

To use your own KPH values, visit the PvME spreadsheet here, make a copy, and edit KPH values in the "Boss Revenue" sheet.

​

Note: The numbers provided do not take into account supply costs, with the exception of Magister and Legiones where key costs have been accounted for.
