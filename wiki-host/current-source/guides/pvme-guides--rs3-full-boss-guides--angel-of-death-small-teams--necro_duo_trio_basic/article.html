Necromancy Duo/Trio AoD Basic

Introduction

Nex, Angel of Death can be done in small Necromancy teams with relatively low requirements due to the strong multi-target damage and sustain tools available to the combat style. This guide will cover strategies applicable to duo and trio Necromancy teams that will work both for getting your first kill and for consistent farming of the boss.

Phase 2

⬥ Phase 2 will begin as Nex hits 2.1 million health and will be shortly followed by amalgamation spawns, which will be cleared with Invoke Death and threads

⬥ Depending on team size, minion lures will differ but the objective will be to pull them into the center such that scythe combo and threads will hit them all as well as Nex

⬥ In the meantime, all players should focus on getting Nex down to 1.8 million health to avoid getting another elements special attack, which will spawn blocking tiles that will prevent the minions from being lured properly

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If your team is having trouble skipping this elements special attack, ideally try to phase Nex during an elements special attack on phase 1 (on the jump but before reavers spawn)

⬥ Phase 3 involves using Necromancy's strong multi-target damage to clear out the minions while still keeping up damage on Nex

⬥ If minions are handled properly they should die early in phase 3. If not, continue using AoE abilities until they're finished.

⬥ Utilise flicking on minions to heal up on AoE attacks

Phase 3 Mechanics

Mechanics in phase 3 will happen in a semi-random order, with the primary restriction being that ice cannot happen first and the same mechanic cannot happen back-to-back

​

⬥ Smoke Lines: Simply place these away from the base and walk out

​

⬥ Ice: You go into a 'stasis' mode where will get cancelled if it returns to you and conjures don't attack anymore

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use and place the ice where the team can reach it but be careful not to hit others

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use abilities such as , to quickly destroy the ice

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Can still use conjure abilities during ice but as said before conjures won't attack

​

⬥ Pool: There must be >14k combined health split amongst all players standing in the pool, heal up as the special attack begins to meet this requirement

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 4.7k health each in trio, 7k health each in duo

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Can use to boost life points to take it solo or take it with others if unavailable
