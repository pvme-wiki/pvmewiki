Nex, Angel of Death Basic

Introduction

The aim of this guide is provide a basic overview of how to get consistent kills at Nex, Angel of Death with a low-tier geared team. While writing this guide, we aimed to keep each preset within the confines of tier 1 of #upgrade-order. However due to the endgame nature of the boss, this has not been totally possible. Keeping this in mind, we have instead aimed to provide presets that are as low-tiered as possible without consistent strategies becoming unviable.

​

Disclaimer

This guide is made with the idea of getting started at AoD, and does not cover meta strategies. For those please refer to the main guides in #angel-of-death-7s. For a detailed description of mechanics, please refer to #unknown-channel.

​
