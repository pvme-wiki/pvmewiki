Introduction

Soulgazers can be killed in the Wilderness, the stalker dungeon in Zamorak's hideout, and the Sunken Pyramid. The Sunken Pyramid is easier to loot in and has no cannonball cost, but the Wilderness spawns can drop

Useful Items and Unlocks

Owning the following increases the efficiency of your sessions, but is by no means mandatory
Items
⬥ Slayer Codex     • Once 50 souls are added, it can be used to teleport to the Sunken Pyramid ⬥ Fellstalk Incense Sticks     • Increases chance of spawning elite monsters ⬥ Seeker's charm     • Offered to the portal in the stalker dungeon     • Increases chances of elite soulgazers spawning ⬥ Deathtouched dart     • Optionally brought to kill Veil-Ripper Ozharakha if it spawns
Other
⬥ Restocking Cannon Ability     • Restocks cannon without user input when using Wilderness location

⬥ Both items are considerably rare and have a much higher chance of being dropped by Elite Soulgazers, including Veil-Ripper Ozharakha which has a guaranteed drop. Using Seeker's Charms is highly recommended to increase the rate of elites from 1/50 to 1/10
