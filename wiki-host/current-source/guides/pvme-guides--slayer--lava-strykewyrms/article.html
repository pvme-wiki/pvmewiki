Lava Strykewyrms

​

Introduction

After the Wilderness Update, killing Lava Strykewyrms has become substantially safer. Despite that, their drops are still highly valued mainly due to Searing Ashes being the main ingredient for Aggression Potions

​

Stats

⬥ XP per kill : 1872

⬥ Optimal kills per hour: ~520 , 450

⬥ Optimal XP per hour : ~973K , 842K

​

Desirable Drops

⬥ Lava Strykewyrms have a valuable loot table containing: Searing Ashes, Onyx Bolts (e), Rune salvage, coins and potions.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This makes them one of the better tasks to do for gp.

⬥ Lava Strykewyrms also drop the Wyrm Spike , Wyrm Heart , and Wyrm Scalp at a rate of 1/6168 on task (1/12,288 off task), which are required for the Wilderness Slayer collection log.

​

Location and how to get there

⬥ They are located South of the Lava Maze (level 34-41 wilderness).

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The quickest way to get there is via the obelisk teleport to the level 35 obelisk.

​

Useful Items and Unlocks

Items
⬥ Wilderness sword 4     • Automatically notes ashes when carried in the inventory. ⬥ Headhunter's thrill     • Since lava strykes cannot be poisoned, there is no glove slot that significantly increases kph. Thus, these can be used for longer tasks. ⬥ Portable fairy ring/Quest cape     • Provides a fast teleport to the Glacor cavern for the Stone of Jas buff ⬥ Portable obelisk/Obelisk shard     • Used to teleport to the level 35 Obelisk, the fastest way to get to lava strykewyrms. ​
Passive unlocks
⬥ Premier Artefact     • Can be used for longer tasks. ⬥ Stone of Jas buff     • Provides a 4% damage increase against lava strykewyrms. ⬥ Infernal Puzzle box     • Provides a 9% damage increase and 14% damage reduction in the wilderness at max rank. ⬥ Mobile perk on armour     • Lava strykes are spaced out and spawn moderately slowly. You will need to use movement abilities frequently while waiting for spawns.

General strategy

Place your cannon between the 2 small lava pits as shown below. You will alternate between killing the strykes northwest and southeast of this spot, most of which can be hit by the cannon.

Method: Active Magic

This method is can be done with low effort, only requiring the player to click on each spawn and letting Revo++ kill them (~250 KPH), or with high effort, using both dual-wield and 2H weapons and full manual (~450 KPH).

​

⬥ If 4TAA, stamp on mounds with equipped and input the auto when you see your character do the stomp animation.
