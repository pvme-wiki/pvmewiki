Creating AFK Methods

Note: This editor guide applies to AFK bossing, not slayer or low-effort methods.

​

Get your priorities straight

AFK methods must:

  1. Be safe (rare chance of death)

  2. Be consistent (every hour is similar)

  3. Be easy to follow (no questions needed in #pvm-help)

​

AFK methods may:

  1. Encourage maximum KPH

  2. Require minimum gear

  3. Have low upkeep costs

​
