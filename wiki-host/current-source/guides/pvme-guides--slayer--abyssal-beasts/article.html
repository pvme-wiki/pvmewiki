Useful Items and Unlocks

Owning the following increases the efficiency of your sessions, but is by no means mandatory.
Items
⬥ Slayer Codex     • Once 50 souls are added, it can be used to teleport to the Sunken Pyramid. ⬥ Portable obelisk / Obelisk shard     • Allows for an easy teleport to the lvl 35 wilderness obelisk. ⬥ Demon horn necklace and attuned ectoplasmator     • Helps sustain your prayer. ⬥ Dwarven siege engine or Kinetic cyclone     • Make sure to have the Restocking Cannon ability unlocked.
Other

Location and How to Get there

Abyssal beasts can be fought in the Senntisten Asylum, the Wilderness, or the Sunken Pyramid.

​

Senntisten Asylum

⬥ If killing Abyssal beasts at the Senntisten Asylum, use a

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place 4 tiles east of you.

Level 35 Wilderness

⬥ If killing Abyssal beasts at the level 35 Wilderness location, use a

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Go just north of the level 35 Wilderness obelisk and position yourself 2 tiles north of the skeleton.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place 1 tile north, and 9 tiles east of the skeleton.
