The Zamorakian Undercity

Introduction

​

Zamorak, Lord of Chaos is the fifth and final instalment in the Elder God Wars. This guide will primarily focus on clearing the dungeon segment, for help defeating the boss visit #unknown-channel.

​

General Notes for Senntisten Undercity + Zamorak

⬥ Lucky charms for the dungeon can be bought from Thoksdottir at Daemonheim

⬥ A LOT of mobs can be ignored throughout the dungeon, but note that Menders and Dark Wizards will heal mobs/drain your adrenaline

⬥ The only required minibosses are the Jailer on the second floor (who teleports to different floors after 50k damage dealt x Team Size) and Eterna being the second boss before Zamorak.

⬥ A Demon / Greater Demon slayer task will affect a great number of mobs present in the dungeon.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Kuradal offers the best chance for this task.

Dungeon Floor Overview

​

Mefis the Jailer

Mefis the Jailer is the first boss of the dungeon, this boss is a typical "mini-boss" styled encounter, dealing magic auto-attacks and low upfront damage to the player.

Mefis will teleport to different floors after (50k damage dealt X Team Size), (ex: see this link for locations)
Damage Scaling
• 1 Person: 50k Damage before the jailer teleports • 2 People: 100k Damage before the jailer teleports • 3 People: 150k Damage before the jailer teleports • 4 People: 200k Damage before the jailer teleports • 5 People: 250k Damage before the jailer teleports

​

Unlocking Eterna's Door

To unlock Eterna's doorway you must find two crystal pylon locations see Dungeon Floor Overview Section for the respective locations

​

Eterna

Eterna is the last boss of the dungeon, this boss has multiple high hitting mechanics and does alternating magic / ranged auto-attacks throughout the fight.

Phase 1
⬥ Deal damage until Eterna phases     • Eterna will spawn a single Chaos Demon Savage after phasing, kill them to move into phase 2.
Phase 2
⬥ Eterna re-appears with full HP along with spawning spiked-circles on the ground, deal damage until phase HP again to move on.     • Circles can be dealt with by using defensives or ⬥ Magic bombs will also spawn from the red portal in the center dealing low magic damage. ⬥ Two Chaos Demon Savages will spawn kill them to proceed to phase 3.
Phase 3
⬥ Eterna re-appears with all mechanics listed previously, deal damage until she is dead to move on to the next room. ⬥ The red portal in the center fires a spout into the player, dealing no damage and placing a bar over their head.     • When this bar depletes, a 5x5 area around the player will become engulfed in smoke, dealing more damage the longer you stand inside.
