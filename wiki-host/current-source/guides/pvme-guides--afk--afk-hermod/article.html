Necromancy AFK Method (55+ kph)

Overview

⬥ The following ARE REQUIRED for this method to work:     • Cutting corners will result in failure.     • A more extensive list can be found in #unknown-channel.
Items
⬥ Salve amulet (e) ⬥ Cinderbane gloves ⬥ Weapon poison+++ ⬥ Kwuarm incense sticks ⬥ Powder of Penance ⬥ Igneous Kal-Mor or Igneous Kal-Zuk
Abilities
⬥ Command Vengeful Ghost     • Only required for high-level setup with minion skips. ⬥ Spectral Scythe (Cast 3)     • Only required for low-level setup without minion skips.
Other
⬥ Darkness     • Only required for low-level setup without minion skips. ⬥ Holy Scarab     • Doesn't require scrolls. ⬥ Undead Slayer perk     • Ideally paired with regular dps perks.
