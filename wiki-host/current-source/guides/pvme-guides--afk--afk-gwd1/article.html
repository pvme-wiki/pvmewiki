Necromancy AFK Method

Overview

⬥ The following ARE REQUIRED for this method to work:     • Cutting corners will result in failure     • A more extensive list can be found in #unknown-channel
Items
⬥ Deathdealer robes     • Used for the passive ability to apply the Death Mark debuff ⬥ Attuned Ectoplasmator and Demon Horn Necklace     • To further support prayer renewal at K'ril     • Can be substituted for Essence of Finality for marginally faster kills
Talent Tree Unlocks
⬥ Tiers 1-2     • Conjure Skeleton Warrior and Command Skeleton Warrior ⬥ Tiers 5-6     • Darkness
Prayers
⬥ Pray Soul split and Sorrow     • You will need to periodically use your to restore prayer     • You can use at the cost of renewing prayer points more frequently     • You may also have to take a few sips of a prayer-restoring potion throughout the hour depending on damage taken     • For K'ril: use your overload or prayer restoration after entering the instance as your prayer will be drained
Other
⬥ Your Ripper Demon does not require scrolls ⬥ Slayer Helmet (preferably on Anachronia Stand)     • Provides bonuses against K'ril when on a Greater Demon task     • Provides bonuses against Kree when on an Aviansies task ⬥ Totem of Intimidation     • To reduce the 40kc entry requirement

⬥ The method is very similar for all four GWD1 bosses, with slight exceptions as listed above for Kree'arra and K'ril Tsutsaroth.

⬥ Gear can be downgraded but you should expect more manual intervention and a lower KPH.

⬥ You may have to manually attack the boss after entering the arena as auto-retaliate is not always consistent on the first spawn.
