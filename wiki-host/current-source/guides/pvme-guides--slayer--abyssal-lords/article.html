Location and How to Get there

Abyssal lords can be fought in the Wilderness, the Senntisten Asylum, or the Sunken Pyramid.

​

Level 47 Wilderness

⬥ Use a and teleport to the Wilderness herb patch.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Position yourself on the westernmost corner of the rock and walk 2 tiles north and 1 tile west.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place 1 tile west of the bottom skeleton.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If using an alt, position yourself as shown here.

⬥ Abyssal lords and beasts can be fought simultaneously at this location.

⬥ Do not use Necromancy at this location.

​

Senntisten Asylum

⬥ Use a and teleport to Senntisten, then run north-east towards the asylum elevator.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Once inside run to the final room, a gate key unlocks a shortcut.

​

Sunken Pyramid

⬥ Use a and teleport to the Sunken Pyramid.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Relocating your house to Menaphos and using a teleport to house tablet also works.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place 5 abyssal lords in the small room, and position yourself 7 tiles south of the northern statue.
