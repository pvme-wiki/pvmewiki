Tormented Demons

​

Introduction

Tormented demons are classified as demons, and can be slain with no Slayer level requirement. They can be assigned as a task after completing While Guthix Sleeps and purchasing the ability to have them assigned, for 50 Slayer points

​

⬥ Besides the default Tormented demon task, you can also kill them for Demons cluster tasks
