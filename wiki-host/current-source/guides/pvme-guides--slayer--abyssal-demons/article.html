Abyssal Demons

​

Introduction

Abyssal demons offer high experience rates while being completely afk. Being that they don't deal much damage, they're a great option for lower level players.

​

Stats

⬥ level: 85

⬥ XP per kill: 277.2

⬥ Optimal kills per hour: ~2400 , ~3075 , ~2310 , ‎ ‎~

⬥ Optimal XP per hour: ~665k , ~852k , ~640k , ~

​

Desirable drops

⬥ Abyssal Demons drop the Abyssal whip , wand and orb which are required for the Slayer Log. They also drop alchables and hard clues at a decent rate.

⬥ If killed in the Wilderness, they can also drop which can be helpful for Ultimate Slayer hunters.

​

Useful Items

Owning the following increases the efficiency of your sessions, but is by no means mandatory.
Items
⬥ Portable obelisk / Obelisk shard     • Allows for an easy teleport to the lvl 18 wilderness obelisk. ⬥ Demon horn necklace + Attuned Ectoplasmator     • Helps sustain your prayer.
Other
⬥ Armour perked with Demon Slayer     • +7% damage against demons. ⬥ Infernal Puzzle Box (tier 6)     • 14% damage reduction and 9% damage increase whilst in the Wilderness.         ⬩ This effect scales with the tier of your puzzle box.     • Tier 6 allows you to put it in your tool belt. ⬥ Restocking Cannon Ability     • Allows restocking without user input.

​

Location and How to Get there

The best spot for abyssal demons is located at the level 18 Wilderness obelisk (Graveyard of Shadows). The fastest way to reach the location is using the portable obelisk or an obelisk shard.

​

Level 18 Wilderness

⬥ Place on yellow tile as shown below (2E of stump NE of obelisk) and stand on red tile (2N and 2W of stump SE of obelisk).

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If your character gets walked off the marked tile, step back on it, and click the nearest demon.
