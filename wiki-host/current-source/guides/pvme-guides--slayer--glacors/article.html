Glacors

​

Introduction

Glacors are profitable monsters that can be fought on or off task effectively, usually for their Slayer collection log items, or due to consistent profit from making and opening if fought at the Elder God Wars Dungeon.

The ability to be assigned Glacors can be unlocked at any Slayer master after completion of Ritual of the Mahjarrat, which also unlocks access to the Glacor Cave.

Location and where to stand

Glacors can be found at the Elder God Wars Dungeon, Glacor Cave, and Frozen Plateau (Wilderness), but are best killed at the Elder God Wars Dungeon due to dropping and being generally easier to AFK.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The Glacor Front of the EGWD can be accessed quickly using a boss portal leading to Arch-Glacor.

There are two viable spots to kill Glacors, which each having pros and cons:

​

AFK Magic

Magic is the only viable style to AFK Glacors due to Dragon Breath and Greater Chain being able to hit the spawned Glacytes while targetting the Glacor.

​

Note: When starting your hour or killing too slow, you may take the aggro of 2 Glacors at once, be sure to click on the one your character can actually attack, as one will be unable to be damaged
