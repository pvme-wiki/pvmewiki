Sanguine Werewolves

​

Introduction

Sanguine werewolves are a low level Slayer mob found in Berylbrook, located in Havenhythe. They are mainly killed for the Havenhythe collection log.

​

Stats

⬥ level: 1

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Level 99 Slayer is required to access the Sunken Pyramid

⬥ XP per kill: 1,877.5

⬥ Optimal kills per hour: ~850 , ~575 (Sunken Pyramid)

⬥ Optimal XP per hour: ~1.52m , ~1.08m (Sunken Pyramid)

Location and How to Get there

Sanguine werewolves can be fought in Berylbrook or the Sunken Pyramid.

​

Berylbrook

⬥ If killing sanguine werewolves in Berylbrook, use a as shown above.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You may have to get them unstuck from the clothesline for the first kill, thus allowing them to get pulled from the side by the cannon.
