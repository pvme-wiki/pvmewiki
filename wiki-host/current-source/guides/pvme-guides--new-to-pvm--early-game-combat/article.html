Some key tips before you start

⬥ Hit chance i.e. your damage scaling when attacking monsters can be viewed as a percentage on the monster's HP bar. The higher the number, the greater your damage.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Wearing "off-style" armour e.g. a melee weapon with magic armour severely impacts your hit chance in a negative manner! Be sure to match your armour combat style with your weapon combat style.

⬥ Attack affects melee accuracy and its level is what determines what melee weapons you can equip. Strength determines your damage dealt with melee weapons.

⬥ The best defence is a good offence: weapon upgrades take precedence over armour upgrades!

⬥ You will level quickly in the early levels - if you feel like you can survive without issues, skip an armour upgrade and save your gold for the following one

⬥ When using Magic you should regularly upgrade your auto-cast to the highest available spell tier. You can find out a spell's damage level cap by checking its tooltip.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For example: Air strike's damage cap is at level 16 - you can upgrade to Air bolt at level 17.

Combat Training

If questing isn't your cup of tea - getting stuck into killing some monsters might be more your style:

​

⬥ Combat levelling focuses on killing monsters for xp

⬥ Upgrade your weapons to a higher tier when you can!

⬥ Include area-of-effect (AoE) abilities as they become available. Check ability tooltips.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Ranged : chinchompas turn non-AoE abilities into AoE attacks within 1 tile of the target

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Melee : halberds gives a longer range on AoE attacks

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Magic : ancient spellbook gives AoE basic attacks, but requires the Desert Treasure quest

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Necromancy : AoE tools are largely locked behind levels and Well of Souls upgrades

War's Retreat & Bossing

⬥ War's Retreat is a PvM hub that can be accessed at Level 60 or with a Total Level of 1000 north of Draynor lodestone

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This place can serve as a free, unlimited place to teleport to for easy bank access, restoring prayer and more as you increase your boss kill count

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• To unlock the teleport, it is recommended to kill 10 lower level bosses such as Ivar, King of Bones, King Black Dragon, or Giant Mole

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For more info on bossing see #bossing-path

​

⬥ Reaper tasks are a PvM activity that can be accessed at Level 50 with 45 Slayer by talking to Death, north of Draynor lodestone

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You earn Reaper points to spend on rewards by killing bosses in a quantity that Death assigns you. You will only be given bosses that you have the levels/unlocks to access.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You will also gain Slayer experience for completing the task

Quests

⬥ For a list of quests with useful PvM unlocks, see Quest Unlocks in #permanent-unlocks

⬥ One of the first useful unlocks to aim for is the tier 3 Infernal Puzzle Box , which prevents your adrenaline from draining when outside of combat

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You can find the box in The Zamorakian Undercity specifically here. Completing the Daughter of Chaos quest (40 Archaeology and 40 Divination ) unlocks tier 2.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• To unlock tier 3, complete the Civil War I miniquest, and the Gross Misconduct achievement

⬥ Two quests also worth mentioning are The Temple at Senntisten and Plague's End. The first unlocks curses, which are basically stronger prayers. The second unlocks better overloads and combination potions.

⬥ Note that quests are more rewarding the sooner you do them, due to how level XP scaling works
