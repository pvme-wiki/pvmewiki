Necromancy

⬥ Solo should be consistently sub 33.6 seconds if done correctly.

⬥ You wear before the hop so you don't overkill the brothers with the rotation.

⬥ At 24 seconds you will get a message that says "As there is no-one on one side of the shadow portal, it empowers the brothers to destroy everyone!"

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Once this happens you can then kill the brothers on the side you start at

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• They will hop over and be targetable at 27 seconds

⬥ You should never start on the side in which Karil is on as he will delay the kill significantly

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The ideal brother rotation is: West - Ahrim, Guthan, Verac. East - Karil, Dharok, Torag

⬥ Whilst waiting the 24 seconds you need to get 5 stacks
