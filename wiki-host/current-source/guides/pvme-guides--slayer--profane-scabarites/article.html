Profane Scabarites

​

Introduction

Profane Scabarites are slayer monsters requiring 87 to kill. They can be killed off-task, or with either a "Scabarites" or "Profane Scabarites" task for on-task bonuses.

​

Stats

⬥ level required: 87

⬥ XP per kill: 900

⬥ Optimal kills per hour: ~850

⬥ Optimal XP per hour: ~765k
