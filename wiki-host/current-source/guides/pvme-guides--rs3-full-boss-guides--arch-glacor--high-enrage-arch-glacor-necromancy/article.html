Arch-Glacor: Necromancy

​

Introduction

​

This guide is aimed at players looking to kill Arch-Glacor at 2500%-4000%. However, all information is applicable to lower enrages. Swap to ripper demon below 2500%.

​

This guide assumes strong underlying knowledge of all information presented in #unknown-channel.

​

The Arch-Glacor is not affected by Slayer tasks in hard mode.

​

A large portion of the preset below is built on sustaining high enrage kill streaks. Read the notes in the preset link if you wish to substitute some supplies for more food.

​
