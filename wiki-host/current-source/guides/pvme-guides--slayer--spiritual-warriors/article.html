Spiritual Warriors

​

Introduction

Spiritual warriors have four different variants, each resembling their respective god within the God Wars Dungeon. They're mostly similar, except that Armadylean spiritual warriors can't be attacked with melee

​

⬥ This guide will focus on killing spiritual warriors in the Sunken Pyramid, as this location yields the highest kills per hour

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Other methods of killing spiritual warriors, such as in the God Wars Dungeon will not be discussed

⬥ They can be assigned as a slayer assignment by Jacquelyn, The Raptor, Vannaka, Sumona, and Laniakea

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• They count as spiritual warriors, ghosts, and undead slayer tasks

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Armadylean spiritual warriors also count as aviansie slayer tasks

​

Stats

⬥ level: 68

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• level 99 Slayer is required to access the Sunken Pyramid

⬥ XP per kill: 231

⬥ Optimal kills per hour: ~1170 , ~1000 (All AFK)

⬥ Optimal XP per hour: ~270k , ~231k (All AFK)

​

Desirable Drops

⬥ Spiritual warriors have a valuable loot table containing: coins , salvage , super sets , and a variety of herb drops

⬥ Sealed clue scroll (hard)

⬥ Warpriest armour

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• They will drop warpriest armour for their respective faction, so they are a good source of god components

​

Useful Items and Unlocks

Owning the following increases the efficiency of your sessions, but is by no means mandatory

Abilities
⬥ Magma Tempest ⬥ Greater Chain ⬥ Greater Sunshine
Other

Location and How to Get there

The best place to kill spiritual warriors is in the Sunken Pyramid due to how spread out they are in other locations. You can teleport there with a teleport to house tablet, or with a Slayer Codex

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Spiritual warriors will only spawn as the version you captured

​

Method: Magic and Melee

⬥ Spiritual warriors can be AFK'd for extended periods with minimal inputs required

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• It is possible to AFK them with only one input every 10 minutes

⬥ Sacrifice is used to help sustain your life points

⬥ They're aggressive to you if you're not wearing any armour for their respective faction; however, using aggression potions will net you a higher kph and requires less inputs

⬥ If you're using melee you can't have Armadylean spiritual warriors

​

Where to stand

⬥ With your camera facing north, center yourself between the northern statue and the two green pots to the east
