This guide will focus on the final boss encounter, at enrages less than 100%, for help clearing the dungeon refer to #unknown-channel, for a more detailed guide and higher enrage strategies refer to #unknown-channel.

Special Attacks
These are mechanics which will happen as you go around charging pads.
Channelers
⬥ The message Zamorak begins to draw power and energy from something within Infernus. appears and a witch spawns in infernus which you must kill to charge the next pad.     • Press extra ability button.     • / and towards and kill witch.     • Press extra ability button to return to Zamorak. Then walk the next pad you want to charge. ​
Flames of Zamorak
⬥ Zamorak says The world will burn. and slams his wings into the ground, dealing 2 heavy melee hits.     • Step into melee distance of Zamorak, use (use defensives if low hp)     • Generally, the smoke will go to the North Eastern most player.     • If any black smoke is absorbed, use defensives to mitigate damage. ​
Chaos Blast
⬥ Zamorak says I will tear you asunder! and wraps his wings around himself, with a blue bar appearing overhead.     • He sends a red smoke after you after you stun him and deal a specified amount of damage. The longer he charges the attack the more powerful the red smoke hits.     • Stun Zamorak to disrupt the blue bar. ⬥ He then says Feel the rage of a god.     • Use / / before the red smoke hits you. ​ ⬥Fully channelled Chaos Blast releases with an auto that counts towards the 5 or 11 auto set. If a chaos blast is stunned and releases before the channel is finished, it will not release this auto and do the full set after the mechanic finishes.
Infernal Tomb
⬥ Zamorak says Step into the dark... meet your death., stunning you and sending to infernus while charging a blue bar.     • He sends a red smoke after you after you stun him. The longer he charges the attack the more powerful the red smoke hits.     • / to avoid stun, and as screen turns black use a stun.         ⬩ You will only be hit with a weak attack shortly after entering infernus if done correctly.     • towards and kill the demon by using a strong threshold and basic.     • Match overhead rune with one on top of a pad and / onto it.         ⬩ Matching the wrong rune will make you take heavy damage.     • Note: If you didn't stun properly while entering, stun Zamorak ASAP after exiting infernus and stack defensives to mitigate a more powerful Chaos Blast. Example clip of proper stun timing here. ​
Adrenaline Cage
⬥ Zamorak says Chaos, unfettered!, dropping all overhead prayers and hits the base with a few attacks soon after.     • Stand still. You will take additional damage when further away from the original tile where Zamorak cast the attack.     • Base as soon as possible. ​
Rune of Destruction
⬥ Zamorak says You're already dead. and summons a ritual circle with an inner circle, an outer ring and a space in the middle.     • A black sludge will travel along the space stunning and dealing small typeless hits to players.     • Standing in the inner and outer rings will deal small typeless hits to the player.     • Stay off the red, avoid black sludge.     • Use / if you are in the black sludge to avoid getting stunned. ​

Mechanics

⬥ Charge Pads

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The arena has 6 pads that you can stand on to charge.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• When it is full charged, Zamorak will stop attacking for a while and your adrenaline bar will be refilled. After this, you will gain an Edict while Zamorak gains a Hex depending on the pad charged.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• You will become unable to charge pads when Zamorak says HEED MY CALL. To continue charging the next pad, you must enter Infernus and kill the mage present.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ This happens after charging every pad.

Enrage-Based Mechanics

Zamorak's damage and HP scales with enrage, and additional mechanics are introduced at specific enrage thresholds:

​

⬥ 100% Phase 7 is introduced

⬥ 500% During Infernal Tomb, all players must be on their rune at the same time to exit Infernus

⬥ 500% During Channelers, Chaos Witch Channeler spawns with 50% adrenaline and the first ability is Asphyxiate

⬥ 750% During Infernal Tomb, demons will move faster towards the portal

⬥ 900% During Channelers, grey HP healing ramp-up no longer resets between phases

⬥ 1000% During Channelers, Chaos Witch Channeler spawns with 100% adrenaline and their first ability is Omnipower

⬥ 1000% On phase 7, red bar charge is no longer reset between cycle(s)

⬥ 1000% On phase 7, Zamorak's life points cap at 250,000 per player

⬥ 1470% The life points of chaos demons cap at 70,000 while on Infernus

⬥ 1500% During the Adrenaline Cage attack, the targeted player will have their Devotion deactivated

⬥ 1600% Zamorak's stagger after killing a rune on Phase 7 reduced from 10 to 5 ticks

⬥ 1740% For phases 1-6, Zamorak's starting life points cap at 1,600,000 per player

⬥ 3500% On phase 7, the life points the runes revive with are capped at 60,000
