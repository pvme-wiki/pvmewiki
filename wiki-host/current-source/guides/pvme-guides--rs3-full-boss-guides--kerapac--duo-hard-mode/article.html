Kerapac, the Bound - Duo HM Necromancy

Introduction

​

This guide assumes you are already familiar with the basic mechanics of Kerapac which can be seen here #kerapac-hm-basic and own BIS gear in Necromancy.

​

It is recommended to have a Nodon dragonkin slayer task as the genocidal boost does work with Necromancy. You may also use the premier Artefact to have a 10% chance to not decrease.
