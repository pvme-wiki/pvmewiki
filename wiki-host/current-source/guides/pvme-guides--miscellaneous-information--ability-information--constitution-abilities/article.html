Some general notes:

⬥ The game runs on an internal tick system: 1t (tick) = 0.6s (seconds).

⬥ The minimum time between abilities is called the global cooldown: 1 gcd = 3t = 1.8s.

⬥ Auto attacks will only give adrenaline if they hit (splash = no adrenaline gain).

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Debuff spells like Vuln or Enfeeble or Smoke Cloud do not give adrenaline.

Constitution

This section covers auto attacks, basics, thresholds, ultimate abilities and special attacks for Defence.

​

Autos

Default Auto
⬥ Cooldown: Based on Weapon Speed     • Fastest: 4t (2.4s)     • Fast: 5t (3.0s)     • Average: 6t (3.6s) ⬥ Takes on style of main hand weapon

​

Basics

Siphon
⬥ Cooldown: 100t (60s) ⬥ Only works on targets with more adrenaline than you

​

Sacrifice
⬥ Cooldown: 50t (30s) ⬥ Heal for 25% of your damage dealt     • Increased to 100% if killing hit

​

Storm Shards
⬥ Cooldown: 50t (30s) ⬥ Stacks delayed ability damage on target     • Up to 10 stacks     • Damage is triggered by using ⬥ Damage when triggered is dependant on weapon which applied the

​

Regenerate
⬥ Cooldown: 0t (0s) ⬥ Must be out of combat to use ⬥ Trades 10% adrenaline for 2% of max health per tick until either     • Health is full; or     • Adrenaline is empty; or     • Combat is initiated

​

Eat Food
⬥ Cooldown: 0t (0s) ⬥ Eats food

​

Tuska's Wrath
⬥ Cooldown: 200t (120s)     • 25t (15s) if used on non slayer target ⬥ If enemy is your active slayer target, it will do 100*(Slayer level damage)     • All bosses except The Magister are immune to this effect

​

Thresholds

Reprisal
⬥ Cooldown: 100t (60s) ⬥ Duration: 10t (6s), or is cast again ⬥ Stores incoming damage while active (up to 30,000) ⬥ Stored damage dealt to target at end of duration

​

Shatter
⬥ Cooldown: 200t (120s) ⬥ Unleashes all Storm Shards on target ⬥ Maximum hit of 30,000 ⬥ Damage is based on weapon which applied , but effected by damage boosts when is used

​

This ability requires an Essence of Finality with a stored weapon
