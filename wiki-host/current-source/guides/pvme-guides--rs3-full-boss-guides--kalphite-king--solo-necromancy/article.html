Solo Kalphite King

​

Introduction

This guide assumes you are already familiar with the basic mechanics of Kalphite King, which can be found in #unknown-channel

​

Being on a Kalphite slayer task is recommended as you gain the benefits of the Slayer Helm and Genocidal

​

This guide assumes no Green Shields happen, you may opt to teleport out if one happens early into the fight.

​
