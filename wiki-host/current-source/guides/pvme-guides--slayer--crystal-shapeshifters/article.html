Crystal Shapeshifters

​

Introduction

Crystal Shapeshifters are a slayer monster found on Tarddiad and are accessible via the World Gate (looks like the number 1), they can be assigned by Morvran or Laniakea after the completion of The Light Within quest. Crystal Shapeshifters will change their attacking style to what your armour is weakest against (if this is equal, attack styles will be random).
