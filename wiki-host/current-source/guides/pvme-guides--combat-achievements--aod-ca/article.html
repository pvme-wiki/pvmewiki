Base Tank P4 Prebuild

Base runs AoD west at 600k, down to Fumus pillar. The team will lower Umbra pillar down to 30k health and run up to Glacies pillar. The base will pull AoD to Umbra and wait for the team to be ready. Third bomb is usually a good time as it will enable base to re-overload and prep 5 stack volley. The base will prematurely and on call. Base can then use any ability to kill the pillar once they have seen the bomb runner barge the dummy and in position under Glacies Pillar.
