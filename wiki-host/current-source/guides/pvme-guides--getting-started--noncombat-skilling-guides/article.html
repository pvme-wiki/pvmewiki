Herblore

Herblore grants you access to untradeable potions such as Overloads (96+ for combat skill boosting, and adrenaline potions (84+ ) for more adrenaline on-demand.

Note: prices of supplies and potions fluctuate. Making certain potions may even be profitable. Do some research before training.

⬥ Wiki calculators for research.

⬥ Warbands are one option for relatively fast and free Herblore XP, but may require some time investment to learn, and are located in the Wilderness.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• unavailable for .

⬥ Herby Werby has no requirements and can be done after visiting Anachronia once. Buy ancient seeds to return weekly.

⬥ You can choose to wait for a DXP (Double XP) weekend to train.

⬥ Always make use of portable wells when making potions for the +10% experience boost.

​

⬥ Suggested training methods:

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 1-38: Do Warbands daily, Herby-Werby weekly or the highest level potion available

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 38-52: Prayer potions

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 52-66: Super Energy potions

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 66-84: Super Defence potions

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 84-88: Adrenaline potions

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 88-96: Make ~3000 of each extreme potion. Should that not be enough, just make some more adrenaline potions OR more extremes of equal amounts until you hit 96.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 96-99: Overloads

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 98-99: Supreme overloads

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ You can boost to 107 via pulse core once you get to 99 in order to make Elder overload potions. These are the best-in-slot potions, and very worth the cost.

Slayer

Slayer may be less xp/hr than other methods; but comes with other benefits like making some , unlocking new monsters to kill, collecting charms for Summoning while training your combat stats all at the same time.

​

⬥ If you choose to do Slayer, prioritise completing the Smoking Kills quest. This doubles your point rewards from tasks. Then remember to quickly get a Full Slayer Helmet for the big accuracy and damage boost it provides for .

⬥ Make sure to use the highest available Slayer Master to get the best tasks.

⬥ Early on, you can quickly train slayer by squishing Corrupted Scarabs at Menaphos.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• See the soulobby ‎FC for calls on when scarabs spawn.

⬥ Fast/expensive training method:

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Kill greater demon berserkers with Slayer wildcards to 115 . Safespottable and even at lower levels this is very high xp/h.

Invention

Invention unlocks the ability to use perks on gear, which adds a huge amount of DPS, skilling utility and survivability.

​

Note: see #invention-basics for more information.

⬥ Suggested training methods:

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 1-4: Complete the Invention tutorial.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 4-27: Research max item level 5, and disassemble two items at level 5 (cheap options like crystal halberd, black salamander, etc.)

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 27-60: Research max item level 10, then disassemble items once they hit level 10.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 60-99/120: Disassemble (at level 10) or siphon (at level 12) weapons and armour while doing PvM/bossing and the skill will passively level up over time.

⬥ Shattered Worlds can be used to powerlevel invention, at the cost of combat experience.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• See !shatteredworlds ‎command for more information.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Item experience will be higher than AFK alternatives, but the combat experience will be significantly reduced.

Token farming

An efficient token farming method is killing minibosses in elite dungeons, which yields 5,000 per kill.

Level 70+ stats and weapons are recommended.

​

⬥ Tokens can be farmed in the Temple of Aminishi (ed1) on the first level solo up to trio (trio recommended). Click here for strategies.
