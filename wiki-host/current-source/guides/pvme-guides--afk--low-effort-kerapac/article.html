Melee Low Effort Method

Overview

⬥ The following ARE REQUIRED for this method to work:     • Cutting corners will result in failure     • A more extensive list can be found in #unknown-channel
Items
⬥ Amulet of Souls ⬥ Abyssal scourge , Masterwork Spear of Annihilation or Ek-ZekKil ⬥ Jaws of the abyss ⬥ Cinderbane gloves     • Use weapon poison+++ / Kwuarm incense sticks ⬥ Scrimshaw of vampirism or the superior variant ⬥ Igneous Kal-Ket or Igneous Kal-Zuk
Abilities
⬥ Chaos Roar     • Method may be less consistent without it. ⬥ Greater Flurry if using
Other
⬥ Darkness ⬥ Ripper Demon , scrolls optional ⬥ Nodon dragonkin task     • Slayer helmet stand and Genocidal ⬥ Bonfire Boost and Ogre flask (thermal) ⬥ Enriched pontifex shadow ring passive is unlocked. ⬥ Penance powder or Penance aura
