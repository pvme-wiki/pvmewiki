Some general notes:

⬥ The game runs on an internal tick system: 1t (tick) = 0.6s (seconds).

⬥ The minimum time between abilities is called the global cooldown: 1 gcd = 3t = 1.8s.

⬥ Debuff spells like Vuln or Enfeeble or Smoke Cloud do not give adrenaline.

⬥ Some useful sheets and resources are given below.

Magic

This section covers auto attacks and spells, basic abilities, enhanced abilities, ultimate abilities, utility abilities and special attacks for Magic.

​

Autos and Spells

Barrages
⬥ It is an AoE:     • 3x3 AoE     • Secondary targets take reduced damage (0.7x) ⬥ Each element has different effects when cast:     • Smoke: Enemy hit chance reduced by 5% for 16t (9.6s)     • Shadow: Enemy damage reduced by 5% for 16t (9.6s)     • Blood: Heals you for 5% of damage dealt     • Ice: Enemy is bound for 16t (9.6s)

​

Blitzes
⬥ Single target version of Barrages ⬥ Effects last for 12t (7.2s)

​

Vulnerability
⬥ Increases damage dealt by 10% ⬥ Lasts 60s

​

Incite Fear
⬥ Every ability cast applies a stack:     • Caps at 5 stacks, and at max stacks uses no runes on ability casts     • Stacks are lost after 20s, refreshed upon applying another stack     • Each stack reduces Tsunami's cast cost by -12% adrenaline ⬥ Frost Surge:     • At 5 stacks, ability casts trigger Frost Surge; deals 10-50% ability damage     • Triggering it costs runes even at max stacks     • Frost Surge is a 5x5 AoE around your primary target which hits up to 8 extra targets in range for 10-50% ability damage each     • Frost Surge has a 12s cooldown, after an ability cast can trigger it again

​

Exsanguinate
⬥ Every ability cast applies a stack:     • Caps at 12 stacks, and at max stacks uses no runes on ability casts     • Stacks are lost after 20s, refreshed upon applying another stack     • Each stack increases basic ability damage by +1%

​

Smoke Cloud
⬥ Increases damage dealt with critical hits by 15% for Magic, 6% otherwise ⬥ Lasts 120s

​

Animate Dead
⬥ Applies a flat damage reduction:     • Reduction based on 10% of equipped magic tank gear's armour + 25% of your current Defence level after any buffs or debuffs     • Damage reduced cannot exceed 60% of the damage taken     • 60% cap is based off the damage after all other damage reductions such as deflect prayers ( / / ) on the hit ⬥ Lasts 12 minutes ⬥ Does not reduce / HP / adrenaline gain ⬥ A calculator can be found here

Magic
⬥ Cooldown: 0t (0s) ⬥ Applies auto-cast spell effect ⬥ Auto triggered during combat

Sonic Wave

Greater Sonic Wave
⬥ Cooldown: 25t (15s) ⬥ Applies Greater Flow to self for 15t (9s) ⬥ Next Magic ability uses 20% less adrenaline

Dragon Breath
⬥ Cooldown: 12t (7.2s) ⬥ It is an AoE:     • AoE is straight line to target + 3x3 around it     • Hits 5 targets, inclusive of main ⬥ It is affected by Kerapac's wrist wraps :     • Next to hit within 10t of the cast will have remaining hits instantly hit at 25% more damage     • Within 10t means: if is cast t0, has to hit within t0-9         ⬩ Latest you can cast is t7 ⬥ It is affected by the Dragonrider Amulet     • Boosts damage by 10%     • Has a 10% chance of applying a 3-hit DoT, each for 10% of max hit, rounded down (excludes boosts that do not normally boost bleeds)

​

Impact
⬥ Cooldown: 25t (15s)     • Charges: 2 ⬥ Stuns and binds for 3t (1.8s) ⬥ It is affected by :     • Increases Max by 40% per rank     • Increases Minimum by 8% per Rank     • No longer stuns or binds

​

Combust
⬥ Cooldown: 30t (18s) ⬥ It is a Burn:     • Not affected by     • Hits every 3t (1.8s), for a total of 10 times ⬥ Affected by :     • Increases damage by 10% + 3% per rank ⬥ Affected by modified by Kerapac's wrist wraps     • Next to hit within 10t of the cast will have remaining hits instantly hit at 25% more damage     • Within 10t means: if is cast t0, has to hit within t0-9         ⬩ Latest you can cast is t7 ⬥ Deals 40% increased damage if used within 15 seconds of

Chain
⬥ Cooldown: 17t (10.2s) ⬥ It is an AoE:     • Hits within a 5 square radius from SW tile of initial target     • Hits 3 targets, inclusive of main     • Does not hit things on initial target's SW tile ⬥ Secondary targets hit by take the next ability cast on your main target within 6s at 30% ⬥ Affected by Caroming     • secondary target damage increased by 5% + 5% per Caroming rank

​

Greater Chain
⬥ Cooldown: 17t (10.2s) ⬥ It is an AoE:     • Hits within a 5 square radius from SW tile of initial target     • Hits 7 targets, inclusive of main     • Does not hit things on initial target's SW tile ⬥ Secondary targets hit by take the next ability cast on your main target within 6s at 50% ⬥ Affected by Caroming     • secondary target damage increased by 5% + 5% per Caroming rank

​

Concentrated Blast
⬥ Cooldown: 9t (5.4s) ⬥ Provides a passive critical hit buff     • 5% increased crit chance for each hit ⬥ Channelled ability     • Hits a maximum of 3 times over 5t (3s)

Greater Concentrated Blast
⬥ Cooldown: 9t (5.4s) ⬥ Provides a passive critical hit buff     • 7% increased crit chance for each hit ⬥ Channelled ability     • Hits a maximum of 3 times over 5t (3s)

Ultimate Abilities

Omnipower
⬥ Cooldown: 50t (30s) ⬥ Adrenaline Cost: 60% ⬥ Affected by the Igneous Kal-Mej and Igneous Kal-Zuk     • Fires 4 hits     • Increases average ability damage from 460% to 540%

Tsunami
⬥ Cooldown: 100t (60s) ⬥ Adrenaline Cost: 100% ⬥ It is an AoE ⬥ Provides a Critical Adrenaline buff:     • For the next 30s, critical hits give 8% adrenaline     • This buff is applied on release of the ability ⬥ Adrenaline cost can be reduced through auto-casting Incite Fear     • Each stack reduces adrenaline cost by 12, down to 40% at 5 stacks
