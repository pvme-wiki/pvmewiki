Introduction

⬥ Blood Reaver binding contracts are a good alternative to bossing for generating GP and a necessary evil for ironmen who wish to pursue many of the strategies outlined in PVME.

​

⬥ The below method assumes you have already prepared or collected your blank Binding Contracts from either:

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Making them yourself after the Dragon Bye quest

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Getting from Ancient Caskets (in stacks of 50)

​

Positioning

⬥ Inside Nex's encampment, stand in the south-middle area

⬥ You will be dragged around as you are aggro'd

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Occasionally a Blood Reaver may walk away - you may wish to click them manually (or wait for it to wander back into range)

​

Useful Items and Unlocks

The majority of the below items and unlocks will increase your KPH, but are not mandatory:
Essential items:
⬥ Enchanted notepaper for noting the contracts
Non-essential items:
⬥ Demon Slayer (perk) ⬥ Demon slayer equipment ⬥ Damage-boosting auras like ⬥ Damage-boosting prayers     • Require extra prayer restoration from or
Poison-boosting:
⬥ Weapon Poison+++ ⬥ Cinderbane gloves ⬥ Kwuarm sticks ⬥ Blood reaver with ⬥ Vampyrism aura
