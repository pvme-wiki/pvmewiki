Elite Profane Scabarites

​

Introduction

Elite Profane Scabarites are slayer monsters requiring 87 to kill. They can be killed off-task, or with either a "Scabarites" or "Profane Scabarites" task for on-task bonuses.

​

Stats

⬥ level required: 87

⬥ XP per kill: 1,890

⬥ Optimal kills per hour: ~460 , ~618

⬥ Optimal XP per hour: ~869k , ~1.17m

​

Desirable drops

⬥ The Spectral Lens which is the Necromancy slayer helm component.

⬥ Killing 200 Elite Profane Scabarites while wielding the Primed keris upgrades it to a Consecrated keris

​

Location and How to Get there

⬥ Elite Profane Scabarites can be fought in the Temple of Isolation or the Sunken Pyramid.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The Temple of Isolation can be reached by taking the boat in the Scabarite Cavern.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Once inside the cavern, cross the bridge and take the boat to the temple area.

​

Temple of Isolation

⬥ If using Melee:

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Run up the hill and across the bridge.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place your cannon south-east of the bridge, just south of the fallen pillar.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Stand underneath your cannon.

⬥ If using Magic:

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Run up the hill and across the bridge.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Place your cannon at the top of the stairs, in the middle of the platform outside the door.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Stand underneath your cannon.
