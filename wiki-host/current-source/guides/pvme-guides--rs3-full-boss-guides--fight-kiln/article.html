The Fight Kiln

Introduction

The Fight Kiln is a wave-based encounter which requires completion of The Elder Kiln quest and a one-time sacrifice of a fire cape. There are 37 waves which become increasingly difficult as the fight progresses.

The Fight

Waves 1-9

There will be mostly rangers with an increasing number of magers and meleers so use Melee and switch to kill the others

⬥ Wave 2: Invulnerability crystal spawn

⬥ Wave 4: Restoration crystal spawn

⬥ Wave 5: Dill spawn

⬥ Wave 9: All rangers

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Use on the last ranger to have it for Jad

​

Wave 10

⬥ Jad spawns southwest

⬥ 1 ranger also spawns southeast

​

Wave 11-19

There will mostly be magers with an increasing number of meleers and rangers so use Ranged and switch to kill the others

⬥ Wave 14: Invulnerability crystal spawn

⬥ Wave 16: Restoration crystal spawn

⬥ Wave 19: Dill spawn (switch to Melee for Dill)

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Go northeast, kill the Dill then go southwest and killing all the magers, use remainder on Jad

​

Wave 20

⬥ Jad spawns southwest

⬥ 1 mager also spawns southeast

​

Waves 21-29

There will mostly be meleers with an increasing number of magers and rangers so use Magic and switch to kill the others

⬥ Wave 26: Invulnerability crystal spawn

⬥ Wave 28: Restoration crystal spawn

⬥ Wave 28: 6 Dills spawn with a mager (kill mager then swap to Melee)

⬥ Wave 29: and kill meleers, use remainder on Jad

Wave 30

⬥ Jad spawns southwest

⬥ 3 meleers spawn in other corners

Waves 31-33

How to get a specific cape

Use the indicated style for the entire listed wave, except when Melee is required to break Dill armour.
Hybrid (37% of total LP dealt with style 1)
⬥ Style 1: 1-9, 11-15, 22-24, 31-32 ⬥ Style 2: 10, 16-21, 25-30, 33-37
Tribrid (23% of total LP dealt with style 1)
⬥ Style 1: 1-5, 8, 11-13, 22-23, 31-32 ⬥ Style 2: 6-7, 9-10, 14-21 ⬥ Style 3: 24-30, 33-37
Quadbrid (17% of total LP dealt with style 1)
⬥ Style 1: 1-3, 8, 11-13, 22-23, 31 ⬥ Style 2: 28-30, 32-37 ⬥ Style 3: 17-21, 24-27 ⬥ Style 4: 4-7, 9-10, 14-16
