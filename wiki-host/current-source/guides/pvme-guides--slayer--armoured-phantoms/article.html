Armoured Phantoms

​

Introduction

Armoured phantoms are the highest tiered Zemouregal's undead, requiring 113 to kill. Even though their drops are worth less than that of other similar high-levelled slayer monsters such as abyssal lords and moss golems , they offer competitive XP rates and make for a fairly speedy task.

​

They can be killed for following tasks:

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Armoured phantoms, assigned by The Raptor and Laniakea

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Zemouregal's undead, assigned by The Raptor

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Undead cluster, assigned by The Raptor and Laniakea
