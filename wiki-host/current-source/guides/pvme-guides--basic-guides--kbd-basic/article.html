King Black Dragon Basic

Introduction

A powerful three-headed dragon residing north-west of the Edgeville lodestone. The King Black Dragon (KBD) is an iconic lower-level boss suitable for players with tier 50-70 gear. His attacks involve dealing with high-damage Dragonfire and other elemental breath attacks.

Tips and Tricks

The King Black Dragon's mechanics are mostly accounted for via good preparation such as bringing protection from dragonfire. During the fight you'll be largely focused on dealing good damage.

⬥ If you have access to , use it and stand in melee distance.

⬥ Don't forget to use any damage-boosting prayers you have available!
