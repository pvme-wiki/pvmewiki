Frost Storm / Frostbite (Example)

Mechanic: Orikalka spins in place, dealing up to 4500 Magic damage and applying Frostbite.

While Frostbite is active, your global cooldown is doubled for 30 seconds. This makes your abilities feel much slower and lowers your damage.

​

Solution:

⬥ If Frost Storm deals too much damage, use / or

⬥ If you get Frostbite, either:

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Keep fighting with abilities which don't care about the global cooldown (e.g., channels / or damage-over-time abilities )

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Clear it using the fissures from Pyroclastic Shock (see below)

​

Pyroclastic Shock / Fissures (Example)

Mechanic: Orikalka slams its tail, creating 3 fissures on the ground. Standing on a fissure deals rapid Melee damage, with the final hit dealing up to 6000 damage.

​

Solution:

⬥ Avoid the fissures unless you are attempting to clear Frostbite (see above solution under Frost Storm)
