Introduction

A being of pure chaos that resides to the west of the Rogues' Castle located in deep Wilderness. The Chaos Elemental is a beginner-friendly boss with simple mechanics meant to disorient the player more than harm them.

​

How to Get There

WARNING: THIS BOSS IS LOCATED IN THE WILDERNESS, BE SURE TO HAVE PVP TOGGLED OFF BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO FIGHT IT.

​

⬥ The level 50 Wilderness Obelisk is located east of the Chaos Elemental, easily accessed with the Portable Obelisk , Obelisk Shards, or any of the other Wilderness Obelisks.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• These options will teleport the player to a random Wilderness Obelisk unless they have the hard Wilderness area tasks completed.

⬥ The teleport levers located in Edgeville and Ardougne teleport the player to the Deserted Keep, located west of the Chaos Elemental.

Mechanics

Discord
The Chaos Elemental's basic attack, made up of green particles that deal either or damage to the player. His other attacks are variations of this move with different effects.
Madness
⬥ The Chaos Elemental launches a multicoloured attack. Upon hit, the player's main hand weapon and up to 3 other pieces of equipped gear will be unequipped and placed in their inventory.     • If the player's inventory is full, the Chaos Elemental will not use this attack     • Click to view an example of Madness.
Confusion
⬥ The Chaos Elemental launches an attack made of fire. Upon hit, the player will be teleported upwards of 20 tiles away from Chaos Elemental in a random direction.     • The player will stop attacking the boss after being teleported, be sure to click on the Chaos Elemental once this happens to resume attacking.     • Click to view an example of Confusion

Tips and Tricks

⬥ The hit of Discord is the most accurate, making it best to either Protect from Magic or Soul Split throughout the fight.

⬥ Prioritize using powerful threshold abilities, only using basic abilities to build up to stronger ones, or when better options are on cooldown.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For Ranged examples of powerful abilities are Snap Shot and Rapid Fire , with abilities like Dazing Shot and Piercing Shot being your weaker basics. Death's Swiftness is a very powerful ability, but should only be used near the start of the fight, if you find yourself with too much adrenaline.

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For Necromancy build up to abilities like Volley of Souls and Finger of Death with your weaker abilities, Soul Sap and Touch of Death , using your Basic Attack when all else is on cooldown.

⬥ If possible, keep your inventory full of items to avoid the Madness special attack, Chaos Elemental's drops can be used to fill any blank spaces.

⬥ An NPC named Edward, located outside the Dragonkin Laboratory, south-east of the level 50 Wilderness Obelisk, can be used as a bank to store any drops the player receives and resupply for longer trips.

⬥ Due to the Chaos Elemental being located in the Wilderness, all Wilderness based bonuses granted by the Infernal Puzzle Box work against this boss.
