Kalphite King — Solo Ranged

​

Introduction

This guide assumes you are already familiar with the basic mechanics of Kalphite King, which can be found in #unknown-channel

​

Being on a Kalphite Slayer task for slayer helm and Genocidal is completely optional, but will result in slightly faster kills.

​

This guide assumes no green shields happen, but they are very rare and generally won't ruin kills.
