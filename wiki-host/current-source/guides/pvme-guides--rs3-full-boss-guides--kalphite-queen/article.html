Kalphite Queen

Introduction

​

As a very old boss, Kalphite Queen is relatively unrewarding and relatively simple. Typically only done for the pet or collection log, there is no further reason for a high level account to do this boss. The only notable mechanic is her form change. She will spawn into the instance immune to and . After the first 40k damage is dealt, she will change forms to her flying form, which is immune to . More information available here: https://runescape.wiki/w/Kalphite_Queen

​

If doing the boss on a low level account for a Reaper task, bring your best and / equipment, along with some food and poison protection.

​

If farming the boss for log on a high level account, you can do it actively for 88+ KPH or AFK for 40-45 KPH.
