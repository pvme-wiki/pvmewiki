Flesh-hatcher Mhekarnahz

​

Introduction

Flesh-hatcher Mhekarnahz is a stalker boss located in the Wilderness and requires 40 to fight. Unlike other stalker creatures, 71 is not required. This encounter is aimed at lower levels and introduces movement as a mechanic which players must react to and avoid.

​

Attack Rotation

3 auto → 3x3 square → 4 auto → 3x waves → 4 auto

​

3x3 square → 4 auto → 3x waves → 4 auto → minions → repeat

​

Notes:

⬥ Bar fills up per each auto, Mhekarnahz does a mechanic when bar is full

⬥ After minions it will be 4 autos

​

Mechanics

Auto-attack
⬥ Does 4 auto-attacks between mechanics     • The start of the encounter only has 3     • Click to view an example of the auto-attack
Targeted 3x3 Square
⬥ Shoots a 3x3 attack towards the player that inflicts damage     • Getting hit will cause a short and     • Click to view an example of the targeted 3x3 square
Waves
⬥ Unleashes 3 consecutive waves which cover specific parts of the arena     • They occur back-to-back in a random order and deal typeless damage     • They cover the outer, middle, and inner sections of the arena respectively     • Click to view an example of the 3 waves
Minions
⬥ Spawns 3 warped flesh sacs with bars above their heads     • If a bar fills, spawns a warped seeker     • Boss is immune while minions are alive     • Click to view an example of the minions
