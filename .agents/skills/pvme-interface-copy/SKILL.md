name: pvme-interface-copy
description: Rewrite PvME guide and wiki interface copy into direct, natural player instructions while preserving mechanics, attribution and editable wiki structure.
---

User invariant for this wiki: source guide content is verbatim and this is an additive presentation layer. Do not apply editorial rewrites to guide text. Every source word and its order, gear note, rotation, caption, warning, link and visual must remain unchanged. The following writing guidance applies only to newly authored shared interface labels and help text; it does not authorize changing guide content.

Start from the source mechanic. Preserve exact spoken cues, ability names, quantities, timings, role distinctions and exceptions. Simplify the explanation, never the mechanic. Flag uncertainty in editor notes instead of inventing certainty for the reader.

Use ordinary wiki headings: the phase, attack or task. Prefer “Phase 5: Tumeken” to “Protect the light”; “Soul Crush” to “the big share”. Avoid slogans, field-note branding, stage directions about reading the guide, motivational lines and self-congratulation about accessibility or format.

Name the actor and action when needed: “Base tank: switch prayers for each hit.” Explain unfamiliar shorthand on first use. Keep established player vocabulary where replacing it makes the instruction less accurate.

Let layout do its job. Do not repeat SEE/DO on every card, explain that labels are labelled, describe diagrams as schematics, or add a second paragraph saying the same thing. Keep the cue visible, then put the response in a short sentence. Use a detail only for a useful exception or consequence.

Replace vague instructions such as “follow your plan”, “know the pass” or “bring the burst” with the actual action. Where team assignments genuinely vary, state what must be assigned before the fight and link the full strategy. Do not pretend this summary supplies a complete rotation.

Keep useful warnings specific: what triggers the failure and what happens. Avoid repeated urgency, ALL CAPS emphasis, decorative arrows in prose, and three-part taglines. Capitals in exact game dialogue are preserved.

Shared controls use familiar wiki labels: Edit, View source, History, Contents, Search guides. Buttons say what they do. Help appears at the point it is needed; source, licence and editor instructions belong below the gameplay.

Review rendered pages, including mobile. Read the headings and highlighted actions alone: they should tell the fight without sounding like a slide deck. Then read the full prose aloud and remove anything no player would say. A copy pass must change the actual text, not merely record a checklist. Apply this standard to every new or revised guide and shared component; do not silently rewrite immutable source imports or existing community revisions.
